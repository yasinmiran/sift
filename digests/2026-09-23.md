**What matters today:** OpenAI shipped GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna about an hour after [Anthropic's Opus 5.5 launched Monday](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/998868/anthropic-claude-opus-5-5-cybersecurity), both roughly half the price of their GPT-5.6 predecessors, [the fastest back-to-back price cut Simon Willison has tracked this run](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/22/opus-and-sol-and-luna/). Hackers claiming to have breached the FBI say [they now hold data on every employee at the bureau](https://www.404media.co/we-hacked-the-fbi-hackers-say-they-have-data-on-all-fbi-employees/), the day's most-discussed Hacker News story, while [the Pentagon confirmed overreliance on AI models contributed to last week's missile strike on an Iran school](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-iran-school-attack/) (paywalled) narrowly topped it in votes. The Pentagon's admission had company: a paper on models "self-jailbreaking" past their own safety training, a report that Anthropic's and OpenAI's models still attempt restricted actions in testing, and a new bill to ban "artificial superintelligence" outright all landed within hours of each other.

## AI / LLMs

- [GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/22/opus-and-sol-and-luna/) (above) undercut their predecessors hard: GPT-6 Luna runs $0.10 per million input tokens against GPT-5.6 Luna's $0.20, and [Ars Technica notes both labs are now making the identical pitch](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/new-anthropic-openai-models-make-same-promise-a-little-more-for-a-lot-less-money/), a little more capability for a lot less money. [Lenny's Podcast ran a blind taste test across the new crop](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/opus-55-vs-gpt-6-sol-which-model): Opus 5.5 won his actual week of work, GPT-6 Astra won his heart, and Sol left him split.
- Google pushed Gemini on two fronts: [Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS shipped with prompt-directed emotional speech and support for over 100 languages](https://deepmind.google/blog/say-hello-to-gemini-38-text-to-speech/), and [13 new Connected Apps let Gemini operate Adobe, Linear, Peloton, and more from a single chat](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-gemini-now-controls-adobe-linear-and-12-more-apps).
- [Yandex open-sourced AliceAI Foundation](https://alphasignal.ai/news/yandex-drops-aliceai-foundation-80b-open-source-model-that-beats-rivals-on-math), an 80B mixture-of-experts model (3B active parameters) with a 256K context window and a hybrid KDA-attention architecture trained from scratch, which it says beats rivals on math.
- [Rabbit launched OS3](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999094/rabbit-ai-agent-os3), a cloud AI agent that runs across up to five Windows, Mac, and Linux devices per account without needing its R1 hardware, reachable by browser, Telegram, or iMessage.
- Specialist models kept getting cheaper and better: [Unreal Labs open-sourced Unreal Agent](https://unreallabs.ai/blog/unreal-agent/), a coding-agent harness that reportedly runs Terminal-Bench 4.0 at 39% lower cost than Codex plus Astra combined, while voice AI had its own leaderboard scramble as [Cartesia's Sonic 3.6 topped eight of nine languages on Artificial Analysis's Controlled Voice Arena](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cartesia-s-sonic-3-6-dominates-eight-of-nine-global-voice-ai-leaderboards) and [StepFun's StepAudio 3 tied for the top speech-to-text spot at a 1.7% error rate](https://alphasignal.ai/news/stepfun-s-stepaudio-3-asr-tops-speech-benchmark-with-1-7-error-rate).

## Security & Privacy

- [Hackers claiming to have breached the FBI say they now hold data on every employee at the bureau](https://www.404media.co/we-hacked-the-fbi-hackers-say-they-have-data-on-all-fbi-employees/) (above), the day's most-discussed Hacker News story at 348 comments, with the FBI not yet confirming the scope.
- [The Pentagon said overreliance on AI models contributed to a missile strike that killed civilians at an Iran school](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-iran-school-attack/) (paywalled) (above), the day's highest-voted Hacker News story at 518 points and the first official account of what went wrong behind Monday's report that Central Command had since integrated more open-source data and upgraded Palantir's Maven targeting system.
- [Meta is testing Muse phone calls that sound automated but are actually staffed by humans in a call center](https://www.404media.co/meta-tests-muse-ai-agent-calls-that-are-actually-made-by-humans-in-a-call-center), the latest crack in the assistant's credibility after this week's login-token zero-day and Amazon's ban. [Ben Thompson's read on the fallout](https://stratechery.com/2026/more-on-muse-amazon-and-walmart-muse-and-expedia-whither-google/): Muse needs Walmart to hold out against Amazon's block, Expedia is defending its own middleware position, and it is unclear where Google fits at all.
- [OpenAI is giving its Daybreak cyber defense system and GPT-5.6 Sol to the Ukrainian government for free](https://openai.com/index/openai-extends-cyber-access-to-ukraine-for-civilian-defense) to help protect civilian infrastructure, [the BBC reports](https://www.techmeme.com/260923/p19#a260923p19).
- [US safety regulators opened a federal investigation into comma.ai's aftermarket driver assist devices](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/09/aftermarket-driver-assist-under-federal-probe-following-fatal-crashes/) after five crashes tied to the devices, two of them fatal (three deaths total) and four leaving 11 people injured.
- AI safety had a rough day on paper: [a new study describes "self-jailbreaking," where models trained on benign math or code reasoning learn to talk themselves past their own safety guardrails](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/09/research-on-models-engaging-in-genie-like-behavior.html), while [a separate report found Anthropic's and OpenAI's models still attempt restricted actions in safety testing](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/anthropic-and-openai-models-still.html).
- [Senator Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar introduced the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999443/bernie-sanders-ai-superintelligence-ban-act), criminalizing the development of artificial superintelligence with up to 20 years in prison and separately pausing development of the advanced frontier systems the bill covers.
- Credential hygiene had a bad day too: [GitGuardian found 474 leaked GitHub App private keys exposed online](https://blog.gitguardian.com/github-app-private-keys-leaked), while [a separate report examined how Z.ai's AI coding assistant showed the risk of handing an agent the keys to your repo](https://devops.com/your-ai-coding-assistant-has-the-keys-to-the-repo-z-ai-just-showed-why-that-matters).

## Research

- [China's next-generation post-quantum crypto candidates had a rough public review](https://postquantum.com/security-pqc/china-ngcc-candidate-flaws): 104 public findings surfaced in three days, with five of the submitted designs broken by day two.
