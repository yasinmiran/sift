**What matters today:** Politics and money set the tone, and by evening the money was about compute. An Anthropic-backed super PAC [spent $63M+](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p17#a261107p17) in the midterms and won 10 of 17 races, while the US moved to [cut MEXC off](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p16#a261107p16) from American finance. Later reports add [Anthropic's $3.4B generator buy](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p8#a261108p8) for its data centers and [OpenAI's $11B raise](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/07/openai-just-raised-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-below-the-1-4-trillion-it-first-sought/) at a $1.25T valuation. On the developer side, a report says one coding agent's permission shortcut left passwords in public repos.

## AI / LLMs

- **AI safety in the midterms**: Bloomberg says Anthropic-backed super PAC Public First [spent $63M+](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p17#a261107p17) and 10 of the 17 congressional candidates it backed won. Politico adds the [origin story](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p18#a261107p18): a small, wealthy network organized in Signal chats that beat the $100M super PAC Leading the Future. Why it matters: stricter-oversight candidates now have a proven funding model heading into AI legislation.
- **Nous Research Arete-27B**: Nous [released Arete-27B](https://nousresearch.com/arete), a post-trained version of Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B, an open-weights model you can run yourself. Update since the morning: Nous says the tune [cuts state-aligned political framing](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nous-research-s-arete-27b-strips-chinese-state-propaganda-from-qwen-down-to-0-9) from 22% to under 1% using 53M tokens of post-training, and argues the base model's bias layer is shallow. Why it matters: if the claim holds, a cheap tune can reshape a model's politics, so provenance of the base model says less than it used to. Nous's own numbers so far.
- **OpenAI raises $11B**: OpenAI [raised $11B at a $1.25T valuation](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/07/openai-just-raised-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation-below-the-1-4-trillion-it-first-sought/), below the $1.4T it first sought. Why it matters: the round closed under the opening ask, a data point on how much investors will pay for frontier labs.
- **Suno sued again**: rights holders of Elvis Presley's music and the publisher of Eminem's biggest records are [suing Suno](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029533/elvis-eminem-suno-ai-music-clone-lawsuit), claiming it generates music and lyrics that mimic the artists. The Presley suit invokes Tennessee's ELVIS Act, which bars cloning a singer's voice. Allegations only; Suno already faces other suits.
- **Apple wants an agentic Siri**: Bloomberg's sources say new CEO John Ternus is [pushing Craig Federighi](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p9#a261108p9) to move past Siri tweaks to agentic features, and 9to5Mac reports Apple aims to make Siri ["more like an agent"](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/08/apple-working-on-agentic-siri-ai-next-year-report) as soon as next year. Why it matters: Apple is signaling it will chase assistants that act, not just answer.
- **AutoTrust Labs JEV-27B**: a 27B "decision model" [scores option probabilities](https://alphasignal.ai/news/autotrust-labs-jev-27b-scores-decisions-in-one-token-across-37-benchmarks) in one token instead of writing free-form answers, and reports 97 on tool selection across 37 benchmarks. Vendor numbers only; treat as a claim until others reproduce it.
- **Benchmark skepticism**: MaxxBench [questions DeepSWE scores](https://maxxbench.unlap.ai/research/why-maxxbench/) under the line "Nice DeepSWE score. But is it true?", a push to check whether coding-agent leaderboard numbers hold up.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Claude Code secrets leak**: Apiiro says Claude Code's "Don't Ask Again" permission option [exposed passwords in 1,122 public GitHub files](https://apiiro.com/blog/coding-agents-leaking-secrets-github-dont-ask-again). Why it matters: a convenience setting that stops prompting also stops a human from catching secrets before they are committed.
- **GitHub PR overview**: GitHub put a [new pull request overview page](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-05-new-pull-request-overview-page-in-public-preview/) into public preview, a redesigned landing view for reviewing changes.
- **Prefetch waste in Datasette**: Simon Willison found Datasette's `rel=next` link header made Firefox [prefetch the next page](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/7/link-rel-next/) of rows on every table view, forcing the server to render pages almost nobody opens. The fix stops serving that header to Firefox for HTML pages.
- **pgvector vs dedicated vector DBs**: a 14-minute comparison covers [memory, filters and index builds](https://explainanalyze.com/p/pgvector-vs-a-dedicated-vector-database-memory-filters-and-index-builds), the practical test for whether Postgres is enough for your embeddings.

## Security & Privacy

- **MEXC and the Treasury**: the US Treasury is [moving to cut crypto exchange MEXC off](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p16#a261107p16) from the US financial system, alleging $16B+ in illicit flows including Russian and Iranian sanctions evasion.
- **Crypto braces for Democrats**: FT says the industry [expects a reckoning](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p4#a261108p4) after Democrats won the midterms and vowed to investigate the Trump family's ties to crypto.
- **Binance CFO exits**: Binance's CFO [stepped down](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-07-binance-cfo-steps-down-nine-days-after-record-hack-co-ceo-says-transition-was-planned-six-months-ago-435992) nine days after a record hack; the co-CEO says the transition was planned six months ago.
- **Coldframe honeypot**: a Show HN project [poses as a supermarket refrigeration controller](https://github.com/hedgerow-dev/coldframe), cloned from real Danfoss and Dixell interfaces, to watch what attackers try after recent incidents on industrial fridges. Single Python file, standard library only.

## Startups & Industry

- **SpaceX Starbase supercomputer**: public records suggest SpaceX is [building a "Starbase supercomputer"](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p14#a261107p14) in Texas, per The Information, though the election of Gina Hinojosa as governor may delay parts of the data center plans.
- **Southeast Asia data centers**: SEA is about 50% of APAC data center capacity under construction, and a Guardian-reported study says Malaysia and Thailand [paused 49 projects](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p15#a261107p15) and delayed approvals of 117 in September on power and water limits.
- **Anthropic buys generators**: FT sources say Anthropic bought commitments for 1.7GW of gas-fired generators from supplier OnePWR for [$3.4B](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p8#a261108p8), the largest known such purchase by an AI lab, going around traditional suppliers to get scarce equipment. Why it matters: power equipment, not chips, is now what labs fight over.
- **Inner Mongolia builds fast**: SemiAnalysis [says](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p7#a261108p7) Inner Mongolia is China's fastest-growing AI data center region, with an 800MW wind-powered campus where 120MW was built within a year and no public backlash. The contrast with the paused projects in Southeast Asia is the story.
- **GPU supply**: Engadget says [there is no end in sight](https://www.engadget.com/2309554/gpu-supply-problem-no-end-in-sight) for the GPU supply problem.
- **Apple's Singapore tax shelter**: FT reports filings show Apple saved [$2.7B in taxes](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p1#a261108p1) this year by routing up to $50B in profits through Singapore, ahead of the $2.6B saved via Ireland.
- **GenRobot AI**: the Beijing robot-data company [raised a $100M Series A+](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p2#a261108p2) for equipment and software that collects human demonstrations to train robots.
