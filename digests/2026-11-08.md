## AI / LLMs

- **AI safety in the midterms**: Bloomberg says Anthropic-backed super PAC Public First [spent $63M+](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p17#a261107p17) and 10 of the 17 congressional candidates it backed won. Politico adds the [origin story](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p18#a261107p18): a small, wealthy network organized in Signal chats that beat the $100M super PAC Leading the Future. Why it matters: stricter-oversight candidates now have a proven funding model heading into AI legislation.
- **Nous Research Arete-27B**: Nous [released Arete-27B](https://nousresearch.com/arete), a post-trained version of Qwen3.8-27B, an open-weights model you can run yourself. Why it matters: another sub-30B tune competing on price and local deployment.
- **AutoTrust Labs JEV-27B**: a 27B "decision model" [scores option probabilities](https://alphasignal.ai/news/autotrust-labs-jev-27b-scores-decisions-in-one-token-across-37-benchmarks) in one token instead of writing free-form answers, and reports 97 on tool selection across 37 benchmarks. Vendor numbers only; treat as a claim until others reproduce it.
- **Benchmark skepticism**: MaxxBench [questions DeepSWE scores](https://maxxbench.unlap.ai/research/why-maxxbench/) under the line "Nice DeepSWE score. But is it true?", a push to check whether coding-agent leaderboard numbers hold up.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Claude Code secrets leak**: Apiiro says Claude Code's "Don't Ask Again" permission option [exposed passwords in 1,122 public GitHub files](https://apiiro.com/blog/coding-agents-leaking-secrets-github-dont-ask-again). Why it matters: a convenience setting that stops prompting also stops a human from catching secrets before they are committed.
- **GitHub PR overview**: GitHub put a [new pull request overview page](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-05-new-pull-request-overview-page-in-public-preview/) into public preview, a redesigned landing view for reviewing changes.
- **Prefetch waste in Datasette**: Simon Willison found Datasette's `rel=next` link header made Firefox [prefetch the next page](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/7/link-rel-next/) of rows on every table view, forcing the server to render pages almost nobody opens. The fix stops serving that header to Firefox for HTML pages.
- **pgvector vs dedicated vector DBs**: a 14-minute comparison covers [memory, filters and index builds](https://explainanalyze.com/p/pgvector-vs-a-dedicated-vector-database-memory-filters-and-index-builds), the practical test for whether Postgres is enough for your embeddings.

## Security & Privacy

- **MEXC and the Treasury**: the US Treasury is [moving to cut crypto exchange MEXC off](https://www.techmeme.com/261107/p16#a261107p16) from the US financial system, alleging $16B+ in illicit flows including Russian and Iranian sanctions evasion.
- **Binance CFO exits**: Binance's CFO [stepped down](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-07-binance-cfo-steps-down-nine-days-after-record-hack-co-ceo-says-transition-was-planned-six-months-ago-435992) nine days after a record hack; the co-CEO says the transition was planned six months ago.
- **Coldframe honeypot**: a Show HN project [poses as a supermarket refrigeration controller](https://github.com/hedgerow-dev/coldframe), cloned from real Danfoss and Dixell interfaces, to watch what attackers try after recent incidents on industrial fridges. Single Python file, standard library only.
