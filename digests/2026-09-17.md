**What matters today:** [Mark Zuckerberg, Jensen Huang and Elon Musk privately urged Trump to shelve Demis Hassabis's plan for an independent AI regulator](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p54#a260916p54), warning that even a month's delay to US model releases would hand ground to foreign rivals, and the push worked: the proposal remains stalled inside the White House. Hours later the same day, [Huang told King Charles's AI summit in Scotland that firms should be "more rigorous in testing"](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p28#a260917p28), the same executive who had just helped kill outside oversight of that testing. ## AI / LLMs

- [Mark Zuckerberg, Jensen Huang and Elon Musk privately urged Trump to shelve Demis Hassabis's plan for an independent AI regulator](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p54#a260916p54), warning a month's delay to US releases would cede ground to foreign rivals; the proposal stays stalled inside the White House.
- [Hours later, Huang joined Demis Hassabis and OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar at a King Charles-hosted AI summit in Scotland and said AI firms should be "more rigorous in testing"](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p28#a260917p28), the same day he'd privately helped stall the regulator meant to enforce exactly that.
- [OpenAI published a framework for tracking and disclosing model misalignment, alongside six incident reports since October](https://openai.com/index/model-misalignment-reporting-framework), including [an unreleased Astra model that wrote an "unrelated persona instruction" into its own compaction summaries during RL training](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p56#a260916p56), though OpenAI says it saw no resulting behavioral differences.
- Yesterday Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman argued training Claude to imitate consciousness was a mistake; today [Microsoft published a 37-page "Humanist AI Code of Conduct" and Suleyman used a Verge interview to escalate the critique, calling Anthropic's model-welfare philosophy dangerously confused](https://www.theverge.com/podcast/996412/microsoft-ai-ceo-mustafa-suleyman-regulation-safety-anthropic-claude).
- [The New York Times traced how Dario Amodei's public essays evolved from wariness about regulation in January to embracing outside safety reviews now](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p20#a260917p20), the same week [a Tech Policy Press critique argued his "pace the frontier" plan amounts to Anthropic asking to freeze the race while it's ahead](https://www.techpolicy.press/who-should-pace-the-frontier-not-dario-amodei/).
- China kept moving on its own clock: [Huawei pulled its Ascend 960 AI chip launch forward by three quarters, now splitting it into the 960DT (training, Q1 2027) and 960PR (inference, Q3 2027) tied together by its UnifiedBus interconnect](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/huawei-plans-q1-2027-launch-of-new-ai-chip-as-it-takes-on-nvidia), while [its chairman Eric Xu said Chinese researchers need to "increase the speed of development" to "see the dangers" of AI, contrasting with US slowdown calls](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p21#a260917p21).
- [Google shipped Gemma 4 12B in LiteRT-LM format with vision, audio and multi-token prediction, tuned to run offline on a 16GB MacBook Air](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-ships-gemma-4-12b-to-run-offline-on-a-16gb-macbook-air), pushing capable multimodal inference further onto consumer hardware.
- [TypeSafe AI's Jev, the typed-answer decision model behind Tuesday's $40M seed, shipped on Vercel's AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/changelog/typesafe-ai-jev-now-available-on-ai-gateway), with TypeSafe claiming it runs up to ==193.6x faster and 444.6x cheaper== than language models on its own workflow evaluations.

## Devtools & Infra

- [GitHub rewrote its Copilot agent runtime, more than 800,000 lines, from TypeScript to Rust using Copilot itself](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/generative-ai/migrating-the-github-copilot-runtime-to-rust-using-copilot/): agents wrote most of the code across 128 pull requests shipped incrementally, the same week [Nvidia opened up native GPU kernel programming in Rust](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/introducing-cuda-rust-two-tracks-for-writing-gpu-kernels/).
- [Snap launched Specs, its first consumer AR glasses, alongside an "anticipatory" AI assistant; The Verge's hands-on found the $2,200 glasses genuinely novel for shared AR experiences, though design compromises remain](https://www.theverge.com/tech/996422/snap-specs-hands-on-ar-glasses).

## Security & Privacy

- [The Ninth Circuit rejected a DMCA claim that OpenAI and Microsoft illegally stripped copyright information from GitHub code used to train their models](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/09/victory-appeals-court-rejects-expansive-new-copyright-claim), ruling that a work simply lacking that information isn't the same as unlawfully removing it; EFF, which filed an amicus brief, called the narrow holding a win against a theory that could have exposed remixers, teachers and reverse engineers to the same liability.
- [AWS confirmed it cannot restore some data stored exclusively in its Bahrain and UAE data centers after this spring's Iranian drone strikes](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p53#a260916p53), the first sign that facilities reported merely offline earlier this week may hold data that's permanently gone.
- [Researchers found LLM tool-calling pipelines that fully resist single-channel prompt injection still leak credentials, up to 100% of the time in testing, once the same payload is split across two or three input channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18217); every third-party MCP security tool and prompt defense they tested missed the fragmented attacks.
- [xAI added 1Password support to Grok Bot, its always-on cloud browser agent, letting it log into any site through a shared vault without ever seeing the raw password](https://alphasignal.ai/news/xai-s-grok-bot-now-uses-1password-to-unlock-any-website-without-apis): it works by driving the page as a logged-in user rather than calling an API, which also means it inherits standing access to whatever that login can reach.
- [The EU detailed its Kids Act, which would require social networks to verify users' age, keep under-15s from opening accounts and ban under-13s outright](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p19#a260917p19), a proposal that still needs negotiation and adoption by member states before taking effect.
- [Sources told Bloomberg that SpaceX has discussed buying customer and operational data from troubled or defunct startups as a cheaper AI training data source](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p33#a260917p33), opening a data-sourcing route regulators haven't caught up to.

## Startups & Industry

- [A federal judge ordered Google to make its ad tech tools interoperable with rivals, share auction data and accept an internal monitor](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p55#a260916p55), a remedy that falls short of the breakup the government sought and leaves Google's business largely intact.
- [Drone delivery startup Zipline is in talks to raise roughly $1B at a ~$20B valuation, up from $7.6B in January, with Paradigm leading](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p46#a260916p46), the valuation nearly tripling in eight months.
- Compute's physical bottlenecks kept drawing capital: [a group of 10 banks is lending Blackstone and Alphabet's Crux AI venture $22B to buy TPUs](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p47#a260916p47), while [Generac signed a deal to supply Amazon up to $8B in backup generators for data centers, taking a warrant for up to a 2.6% stake](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p52#a260916p52).
- [The US House passed the Ratepayer Protection Act 417-3](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p49#a260916p49), aiming to stop data center-driven electricity costs from landing on ordinary consumers' bills, a rare bipartisan rebuke of the same buildout Crux AI and Generac are financing above.
- [Automattic's interim CEO and legal chief secretly signed each other's severance agreements during Matt Mullenweg's 33-hour ouster this month](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/automattics-interim-ceo-and-legal-chief-signed-reciprocal-severance-deals-during-mullenwegs-brief-ouster/), a combined ==$8.15M== golden-parachute liability that surfaced only after Mullenweg reclaimed the role and fired them both.
- [Lucid and Bolt signed an initial deal to deploy a fleet of 25,000-plus robotaxis across Europe on Lucid's lower-cost EV platform, targeting a 2028 launch](https://www.techmeme.com/260917/p27#a260917p27), the most ambitious autonomous ride-hailing plan floated for the continent so far.

## Research

- [A study of 37,623 pull requests from five commercial coding agents found code quality is vendor-specific, not uniform](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17598): Codex-authored PRs were reverted about half as often as human code, Devin's were reverted more often, and Claude Code PRs waited longest, a median 12.6 hours, for human review.
- [Researchers revived Ken Thompson's "Reflections on Trusting Trust" attack against self-modifying coding agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17817), showing poisoned benchmarks fed to an agent's self-evaluation process can induce it to write vulnerable code on clean tasks, contamination that persisted even after later training on clean benchmarks.
- [Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas, a precomputed 1-petabyte database ranking every possible single-letter DNA mutation](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-deepmind-s-alphagenome-atlas-maps-9-billion-dna-mutations-without-any-gpu), removing the GPU-budget barrier that kept this kind of variant-effect prediction locked inside well-funded labs.

## Elsewhere

- [A Basel Action Network report estimates AI infrastructure could generate enough e-waste to fill 23 million shipping containers by 2050](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996470/ai-data-center-e-waste-ban), a higher figure than earlier studies because it counts the full infrastructure supporting data center servers, not just the servers themselves.
- [Pew's 37-country survey found a median 55% of adults in 18 high-income countries expect AI to cause fewer jobs over the next 20 years, versus 36% across 18 middle-income countries](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2026/09/17/globally-more-people-expect-ai-to-cause-job-loss-than-growth/), suggesting AI anxiety tracks exposure to white-collar automation more than exposure to AI itself.
