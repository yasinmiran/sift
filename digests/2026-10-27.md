**What matters today:** OpenAI says it has [three new results in math, computer science and physics](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/), including a preprint claiming the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a long-held assumption about how hard certain problems are, is false. Cloudflare [disclosed that a GLM-5.3 model on Workers AI leaked other customers' prompt data](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/) in 155 long-prompt responses. Yesterday's Anthropic S-1 now has a financing sequel: Broadcom is [arranging $63B to fund Anthropic's chips](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p47#a261026p47).

## AI / LLMs

- **OpenAI's three results**: OpenAI says its models produced discoveries in [harmonic analysis, complexity theory and quantum field theory](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p48#a261026p48), with outside "shepherds" guiding the computer science and physics work, [per The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1019143/openai-eth-kaseya-science-math-physics). The claim to watch is the [Exponential Time Hypothesis preprint](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf): if it holds up, many "this can't be solved much faster" bounds lose their footing, and until experts check it, it is a claim, not a result.
- **GPT-6.1 Terra**: OpenAI pitches its fast mid-tier model on [visual reasoning and computer use](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/26/gpt-61-terra/), the repetitive form-filling and editing work that suits a cheaper model. Simon Willison places it in the same slot as Anthropic's Sonnet, which makes the choice against pricier models harder.
- **Cost per task is the new scoreboard**: Cursor added [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 47.7% on CursorBench for $0.60 to $0.98 per task](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cursor-adds-deepseek-v4-1-flash-hitting-47-7-for-under-1-per-task); NVIDIA's [Nemotron 3.5 Super VL scored 22.7% on ARC-AGI-1 at $0.03 per task](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nvidia-s-nemotron-3-5-super-vl-hits-22-7-on-arc-agi-1-at-0-03-per-task); a new benchmark has [Claude Opus 5.5 leading AI video coding at 10x the cost of rivals](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-code2video-aa-benchmark-shows-claude-opus-5-5-dominates-ai). Buyers now pick on price per solved task, not the top score alone.
- **Siri gets memory**: [iOS and macOS 27.2](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/10/apple-releases-ios-and-macos-27-2-with-new-siri-ai-features-new-music-logo/) let Siri recall past conversations and reach voicemails and Messages attachments, so the assistant can answer from your history, not only the current request.
- **OpenAI "dots"**: TechCrunch reports OpenAI [turned its coding tools into dots, an always-on personal assistant](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/how-openai-turned-its-coding-tools-into-dots-an-always-on-personal-assistant), extending agent tooling from developers to everyday use.

## Security & Privacy

- **Cloudflare GLM-5.3 leak**: between Aug 27 and Sep 9, [155 responses contained other customers' data](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/), all for prompts over 681,280 tokens. The cause: a 1,048,576-token context window on a cache pool that held only 681,280, plus an SGLang bug that, on over-length prompts, left stale cache from earlier requests in place. Cloudflare found no sign of deliberate exploitation, fixed it by enlarging the pool to about 2.04M tokens and patching the engine, and restored the model Sep 9.
- **Authenticator for Android**: Microsoft [patched CVE-2026-80097 (CVSS 8.6)](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p2#a261027p2), which let untrusted apps impersonate Microsoft apps and obtain delegated account tokens. Update the app.
- **Data center freight theft**: experts tell NBC that [over $100M of US data center equipment has been stolen since June](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p1#a261027p1), including two heists where robbers hit the escorts guarding $10M+ truckloads. GPUs are now cargo worth robbing.
