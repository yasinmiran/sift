**What matters today:** OpenAI says it has [three new results in math, computer science and physics](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/), including a preprint claiming the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a long-held assumption about how hard certain problems are, is false; Anthropic has since published [Lean proofs of its own refutation](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/16a5ce41304fcdaea4e0be7c132e1c361dc26da5/eth). Cloudflare [disclosed that a GLM-5.3 model on Workers AI leaked other customers' prompt data](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/) in 155 long-prompt responses. The AI buildout is still a financing story: Broadcom is [arranging $63B for Anthropic's chips](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p47#a261026p47) and Nvidia is [putting $3B into Meta's stock raise](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p3#a261027p3).

## AI / LLMs

- **OpenAI's three results**: OpenAI says its models produced discoveries in [harmonic analysis, complexity theory and quantum field theory](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p48#a261026p48), with outside "shepherds" guiding the computer science and physics work, [per The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1019143/openai-eth-kaseya-science-math-physics). The claim to watch is the [Exponential Time Hypothesis preprint](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf): if it holds up, many "this can't be solved much faster" bounds lose their footing, and until experts check it, it is a claim, not a result.
- **Update, Anthropic claims the same result**: Anthropic has put up a [repo of Lean 4 proofs](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/16a5ce41304fcdaea4e0be7c132e1c361dc26da5/eth) for an algorithm that solves 3-SAT (a classic hard logic puzzle) in sub-exponential time, which would refute the hypothesis. It says an internal Claude model found the result and AI models wrote the paper and the proofs. The Lean build rests only on Lean's standard axioms, but nothing machine-checks that the Lean statements match the paper's, so human review still decides it.
- **GPT-6.1 Terra**: OpenAI pitches its fast mid-tier model on [visual reasoning and computer use](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/26/gpt-61-terra/), the repetitive form-filling and editing work that suits a cheaper model. Simon Willison places it in the same slot as Anthropic's Sonnet, which makes the choice against pricier models harder.
- **Cost per task is the new scoreboard**: Cursor added [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 47.7% on CursorBench for $0.60 to $0.98 per task](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cursor-adds-deepseek-v4-1-flash-hitting-47-7-for-under-1-per-task); NVIDIA's [Nemotron 3.5 Super VL scored 22.7% on ARC-AGI-1 at $0.03 per task](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nvidia-s-nemotron-3-5-super-vl-hits-22-7-on-arc-agi-1-at-0-03-per-task); a new benchmark has [Claude Opus 5.5 leading AI video coding at 10x the cost of rivals](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-code2video-aa-benchmark-shows-claude-opus-5-5-dominates-ai). Buyers now pick on price per solved task, not the top score alone.
- **Genie 4**: Google launched a [world model powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p23#a261027p23) that generates interactive simulations with accompanying audio, for now as a preview for trusted testers. Moving from video you watch to worlds you steer is the point.
- **Apple stacks AI on new hardware**: [iOS and macOS 27.2](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/10/apple-releases-ios-and-macos-27-2-with-new-siri-ai-features-new-music-logo/) let Siri recall past conversations and reach voicemails and Messages attachments, and Apple added a [beta assistant built on Apple's and OpenAI's models](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p20#a261027p20) to Keynote, Pages, Numbers and Logic Pro, with MCP (a standard for plugging tools into agents) support in the first three.
- **OpenAI "dots"**: TechCrunch reports OpenAI [turned its coding tools into dots, an always-on personal assistant](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/how-openai-turned-its-coding-tools-into-dots-an-always-on-personal-assistant), extending agent tooling from developers to everyday use.
- **ChatGPT inside Oracle**: Oracle is [adding ChatGPT and Codex to its app stack](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p24#a261027p24) and widening its cloud with Google TPUs, OpenAI's Jalapeño chip and Cerebras hardware, so enterprise software buyers get OpenAI without a separate contract.

## Security & Privacy

- **Cloudflare GLM-5.3 leak**: between Aug 27 and Sep 9, [155 responses contained other customers' data](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/), all for prompts over 681,280 tokens. The cause: a 1,048,576-token context window on a cache pool that held only 681,280, plus an SGLang bug that, on over-length prompts, left stale cache from earlier requests in place. Cloudflare found no sign of deliberate exploitation, fixed it by enlarging the pool to about 2.04M tokens and patching the engine, and restored the model Sep 9.
- **Secret surveillance ruling**: Senators Wyden and Schiff say the administration is [withholding a secret court ruling](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/us-officials-want-section-702-renewed-before-damaging-court-ruling-goes-public/) that objected to search filters letting analysts pull Americans' messages without required approvals and records. The 180-day release window closed Sept 13 while officials push Congress to renew Section 702, which lapsed in June though collection continues under the court's March approval. [Wired's account](https://www.wired.com/story/the-us-government-is-hiding-a-secret-court-ruling-about-how-it-spies-on-americans/) drew 393 points on Hacker News.
- **Starlink users targeted**: Reuters sources say Trend Micro's TrendAI found Israeli adtech firm Discover-Tech [heavily targeted Starlink users in 100+ countries](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p12#a261027p12), likely to geolocate or surveil them. The company is alleged, not charged.
- **PHP2Shell exploited**: CISA [confirms attacks are now using the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-confirms-php2shell-vulnerabilities-now-exploited-in-attacks), and Wiz tracks [exploitation attempts and remediation trends](https://www.wiz.io/blog/php2shell-exploitation-so-far). If you run PHP apps, check your patch status.
- **AI bug hunter vs fuzzer**: Project Zero compared Google's two tools on Chrome's V8 engine: in six months the LLM-based BigSleep [found 45 vulnerabilities to Fuzzilli's 37](https://projectzero.google/2026/10/fuzzing-v-AI.html), with only 25% overlap (about 60% in its dedicated experiments). Neither replaces the other, so vendors should run both.
- **Quad9 leaves France**: after a court ordered it to block sites accused of infringing a sports broadcaster's copyright, the privacy DNS service [stops serving France on Nov 24](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/10/french-courts-site-blocking-orders-drive-quad9-out-france), EFF reports. Infrastructure providers are being made copyright police.
- **Authenticator for Android**: Microsoft [patched CVE-2026-80097 (CVSS 8.6)](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p2#a261027p2), which let untrusted apps impersonate Microsoft apps and obtain delegated account tokens. Update the app.
- **Data center freight theft**: experts tell NBC that [over $100M of US data center equipment has been stolen since June](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p1#a261027p1), including two heists where robbers hit the escorts guarding $10M+ truckloads. GPUs are now cargo worth robbing.
