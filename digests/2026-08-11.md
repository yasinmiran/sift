**What matters today:** [OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber, a Daybreak-program model that completes ==95%== of advanced exploit requests, up from 1.5% for its standard model, gated to vetted defenders through a new tiered partner program](https://openai.com/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows): a frontier lab openly building and metering offensive-grade cyber capability, betting that arming defenders first keeps the "cyber defense window" from closing. Elsewhere, [an unreleased Claude research model pushed a longstanding lower bound tied to the Riemann hypothesis from 41.6% to 67.2% using a multi-agent swarm running inside Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta), real progress rather than a solution, while Anthropic separately [locked in a 20-year, $9.1B compute deal with Riot Platforms for 191 MW of capacity in Rockdale, Texas](https://www.techmeme.com/260810/p48#a260810p48).

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber under an expanded Daybreak program, completing 95% of advanced exploit requests to arm defenders, up from 1.5% for its standard model](https://openai.com/index/expanding-daybreak-as-the-cyber-defense-window-narrows): [approved Daybreak partners can now use the frontier cyber models directly to deliver authorized, governed cybersecurity services to customers](https://openai.com/index/putting-frontier-cyber-models-in-more-trusted-hands), OpenAI's attempt to keep offensive-grade capability out of the wrong hands while still closing real vulnerabilities faster than attackers can find them.
- [An unreleased Claude research model pushed a longstanding lower bound connected to the Riemann hypothesis from 41.6% to 67.2%, using a multi-agent swarm running inside Claude Code](https://www.anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta): it didn't solve the hypothesis, Anthropic is explicit about that, but the swarm made genuine, unexpected progress on a related problem, a proof point for agentic setups doing original math research rather than just checking known results.
- [Anthropic made Claude Sonnet 5's introductory pricing permanent at $2/1M input and $10/1M output tokens, cancelling a planned September 1 increase](https://www.techmeme.com/260810/p42#a260810p42): holding the line on price rather than capturing margin, as Chinese open models keep pressuring the low end of the market.
- [Cactus released Needle 2, a 45M-parameter agentic LLM compressed into a single 14MB binary that runs a full session in 28MB of RAM](https://cactuscompute.com/needle): it hits 500 tokens/sec on a Raspberry Pi 5 and 400-1,500 tokens/sec on headsets like the Meta Quest 3S and Apple Vision Pro, built for tool calling and device control on phones, wearables and small robots rather than chat.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Vercel opened its Sandbox firewall's full egress-control features to every Sandbox](https://vercel.com/blog/a-sandbox-without-a-network-boundary-is-only-half-a-sandbox): it enforces domain and CIDR policy by inspecting the TLS handshake's SNI at the host, outside the microVM, and injects credentials at that same boundary so they never enter the sandbox itself; alongside it, [Vercel introduced Managed Images, versioned open-source base images that replace Sandbox runtimes and default to Ubuntu with Node.js, Python and coding agents preinstalled](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-sandbox-managed-images).
- [Cloudflare wrapped a week of daily launches under "Agents Week"](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agents-week-review-august-2026/): runtime and infrastructure primitives for agents, an "Agent Development Lifecycle" framework for taking agentic software from prototype to production, Zero Trust access extended from users and devices to agents themselves, and new tooling for tracking what agents are actually doing on the open web.
- [GitHub shipped a preview of the Copilot SDK for Java, a framework-agnostic, BYOK client library for building Copilot agent sessions from server-side Java](https://github.blog/engineering/using-the-github-copilot-sdk-for-java/): it works against any provider, OpenAI, Azure, Anthropic or OpenAI-compatible endpoints, without a Copilot subscription, and composes with Jakarta EE, Spring, and virtual threads.
- [H3-metal is a from-scratch native inference engine that runs MiniMax-H3's video and audio generation entirely on Apple Silicon](https://github.com/antirez/h3.c), quantizing and fusing kernels to cut a Mac's memory pressure from 36.4GB to 25.9GB and generate a clip in as little as 3.5 seconds on an M5 Max.

## Security & Privacy

- [EFF asked the Meta Oversight Board to stop Meta's pattern of removing posts and restricting accounts over ordinary discussion of prescription medication, abortion care and pregnancy experiences](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/08/meta-must-stop-silencing-reproductive-health-information), filed over a specific case involving an Instagram post about medication during pregnancy and childbirth.
- [Illinois passed HB5511, a law that puts operating-system vendors, Linux distributions included, on the hook for age verification](https://linuxstans.com/illinois-hb5511-operating-system-age-verification/): the bill topped Hacker News with 300+ points and 400+ comments, another sign age-verification mandates written with apps and platforms in mind are starting to reach into the base of the software stack.
- [EFF urged a court to dismiss the LDS Church's trademark lawsuit against the "Mormon Stories" podcast](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/08/dismiss-churchs-trademark-lawsuit-against-mormon-stories-podcast-eff-urges-court), arguing the church has a pattern of using trademark claims and the threat of expensive litigation to pressure critics and commentators into silence.

## Startups & Industry

- [Anthropic agreed to a 20-year, ==$9.1B== compute deal with Riot Platforms for 191 MW of capacity at a Rockdale, Texas campus](https://www.techmeme.com/260810/p48#a260810p48): Riot's stock jumped roughly 25% after hours on the news, another AI lab locking in power and compute years ahead rather than competing for it on the spot market.
