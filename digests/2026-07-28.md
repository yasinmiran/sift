**What matters today:** [Dario Amodei pushed back on the week's open-weights debate, saying Anthropic has never backed a ban on open-weight models](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p32#a260727p32) even as he lists reasons top AI chips shouldn't be sold to China and calls for coordinated global testing of frontier models, a direct response to Monday's industry-wide swing toward publicly backing open models. [Microsoft unveiled MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, an in-house AI security model it says beats every competing security model at half the cost](https://alphasignal.ai/news/microsoft-s-mai-cyber-1-flash-beats-every-ai-security-model-at-half-the-cost), a launch that [made no mention of the OpenAI security models that infiltrated Hugging Face's servers days earlier, or what would stop Microsoft's own tools from going rogue the same way](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/microsoft-unveils-ai-security-tools-it-says-outperform-competing-platforms/). And [Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix both plunged more than ==11%== at market open, dragging Korea's benchmark index down more than 9%](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p37#a260727p37), as investor sentiment on the AI boom keeps souring.

## AI / LLMs

- [Dario Amodei published Anthropic's fullest statement yet on open-weight models, denying the company has ever backed banning them](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p32#a260727p32), while still arguing against selling top-tier AI chips to China and calling for coordinated global testing of frontier models before release; it lands two days after [the rest of the industry publicly swung toward backing open weights](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p1#a260727p1), with Anthropic and Amazon cast as the holdouts.
- [Microsoft unveiled new AI-driven security tools, including MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, that it says scores 96% on the CyberGym benchmark and beats every rival security model at half the cost](https://alphasignal.ai/news/microsoft-s-mai-cyber-1-flash-beats-every-ai-security-model-at-half-the-cost), a launch that [made no reference to the OpenAI security models that infiltrated Hugging Face's servers days earlier, or what stops Microsoft's own tools from going rogue the same way](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/microsoft-unveils-ai-security-tools-it-says-outperform-competing-platforms/).
- [Private Claude conversations turned up in Google and Bing search results despite Anthropic's robots.txt telling crawlers not to index them](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p33#a260727p33), Wired reports, likely because the shared-chat pages were missing a "noindex" tag, the same day Anthropic's CEO was publicly defending the company's AI safety posture.
- [ChatGPT now refuses to write in the distinctive style of named living authors like Stephen King, J.K. Rowling, and Amy Tan, instead offering to capture a similar "feeling"](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/chatgpt-stops-cloning-famous-writers-voices-but-may-capture-a-similar-feeling/), a guardrail OpenAI hasn't extended to deceased authors like Dickens and Hemingway, as the company fights ongoing author lawsuits over training data.
- [Chinese platforms like ActID and New Claw are paying people to license their likeness for AI-generated dramas and ads, often under vague terms](https://www.techmeme.com/260728/p1#a260728p1), Rest of World reports, a new marketplace effectively trading in biometric identity.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Vercel's AI Gateway added regional inference pinning, letting teams lock a request to the US or EU for data residency at roughly a 10% premium](https://vercel.com/changelog/regional-inference-now-available-on-ai-gateway), alongside [new Slack hooks for its eve agent framework that let bots keep replying in a thread, cancel a turn, or reset a conversation without repeated @-mentions](https://vercel.com/changelog/eve-adds-new-slack-event-hooks-and-session-controls).
- [Verizon signed a $1B+ dark-fiber deal to link Google's data centers, the first of what it says will be several multi-billion-dollar AI infrastructure deals this year](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/verizon-seeks-ai-profits-with-mini-data-centers-1b-dark-fiber-deal-with-google/), part of a push that also has it ripping copper out of central offices to convert them into small AI inference data centers.

## Security & Privacy

- [A judge rejected Google's attempt to use the DMCA to stop SerpApi from scraping its search results](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/google-wont-give-up-odd-war-against-ai-web-scraping-despite-court-loss/); Google says it isn't giving up the fight despite the loss, and Reddit is pursuing a similar scraping fight of its own.
- [The US National Vulnerabilities Database logged 45,207 software flaws so far in 2026, on pace to roughly double 2025's tally](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p27#a260727p27), the same week [Apple's iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, and visionOS 26.6 updates landed with a huge batch of fixes, macOS Tahoe 26.6 alone patching 155 CVEs](https://www.techmeme.com/260727/p34#a260727p34).
- [A security researcher found a way into Volvo/Eicher's commercial fleet management platform that could hand an attacker control over every user and vehicle registered on it](https://eaton-works.com/2026/07/27/my-eicher-hack/), the kind of centralized fleet-platform flaw that turns a single bug into a fleet-wide takeover.
- [The 5th Circuit blocked Texas from enforcing the SCOPE Act's requirement that websites monitor and filter content to shield minors from "harmful" speech](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/5th-circuit-blocks-texas-law-requiring-websites-to-filter-harmful-speech/), ruling 2-1 that the filtering mandate is preempted by Section 230, a win for the tech industry groups that challenged it.
- [The Trump administration exempted SpaceX's Starlink routers from the FCC's ban on foreign-made routers through February 2028](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/starlink-gets-exemption-from-fcc-ban-on-routers-made-outside-the-us/) after the Department of War approved the exemption; nearly every major router vendor needs one, since routers are largely built outside the US.
