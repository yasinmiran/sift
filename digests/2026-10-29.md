**What matters today:** Alphabet's stock [fell 12%+ after its earnings, a record $500B wipeout](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p38#a261029p38), as AI spending pushed free cash flow ==negative== and Google admitted AI Mode is hurting classic search. DoorDash [confirmed it is buying Instacart for $17B](https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/doordash-to-acquire-instacart), and Mistral [released open weights for Large 4](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p30#a261029p30). Still running from the morning: a hacker claims a breach of DHS's Okta login and [leaked records on 41,000+ employees](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1021528/ice-cbp-data-leak), and the DOJ is [asking about Nvidia's $12.9B Hugging Face deal](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p57#a261028p57).

## AI / LLMs

- **Mistral Large 4 weights**: Mistral [opened the weights of Large 4](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p30#a261029p30) (a roughly 1T-parameter coding-focused model nicknamed Le Chonk) and says it rivals top open models like GLM-5.3 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index; Vercel says the [updated weights come with lower pricing](https://vercel.com/changelog/mistral-large-4-updated-weights-and-lower-pricing-on-ai-gateway) on its gateway, so the cost of an open frontier coder just dropped for anyone already calling it.
- **Poolside Laguna M.1**: Poolside [ships a 225B mixture-of-experts coding model with open weights and 262K context](https://alphasignal.ai/news/poolside-drops-laguna-m-1-a-225b-open-weight-coding-agent), a second open coding contender in one day.
- **Vercel Virtual Models**: AI Gateway now lets teams [save a model plus its settings under one custom ID](https://vercel.com/blog/ai-gateway-virtual-models), so swapping models, setting fallbacks or enforcing zero data retention happens centrally with no redeploy.
- **Google's AI Mode ads**: Google [expands ads in AI Mode to international markets](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p6#a261029p6) as core search ad growth slows; the Register notes the slowdown comes with ["lower monetization" in AI Mode](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p3#a261029p3), on revenue of $63.25B (up 12%, versus 14.5% a year earlier). This morning that was a growth story; by the close it was a sell-off (see Startups & Industry).
- **Anthropic S-1**: Following this week's IPO filing, The Verge reads the S-1 as [a case that Anthropic must keep growing and spending on compute to avoid going bankrupt](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1021476/anthropic-s-1-compute-recursive), which is the investor pitch behind the spending.

## Devtools & Infra

- **PyTorch 2.15**: The release [turns the rewritten NCCL backend (GPU-to-GPU communication) on by default](https://alphasignal.ai/news/pytorch-2-15-rebuilds-distributed-training-and-cuts-decoding-overhead) and speeds up NVFP4 (4-bit float) decoding on Blackwell GPUs.

## Security & Privacy

- **DHS employee leak**: A hacker calling themselves "Department of Peace" [claims access through DHS's Okta](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1021528/ice-cbp-data-leak) and exposed names, titles, phone numbers and offices of 41,000+ staff; Wired adds the data [reveals ICE and CBP facility locations and ties staff to surveillance tools](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p62#a261028p62), so the harm is operational, not just identity theft.
- **GitLab exploitation attempt**: Alpine Linux [published an incident post on attempted exploitation of CVE-2026-97229](https://alpinelinux.org/posts/GitLab-security-incident-CVE-2026-97229.html) against its GitLab; check your own self-hosted instances.
- **DDoS shift**: Cloudflare's Q3 report finds [short, low-bandwidth attacks up 208% while attacks above 1 Tbps fell nearly 69%](https://blog.cloudflare.com/ddos-threat-report-2026-q3/), which it ties possibly to law-enforcement takedowns of four IoT botnets, so defenses tuned for giant floods miss the new swarm of small bursts.
- **Searchjacked**: Socket reports [hidden browser extensions hijacking searches for 13 million users](https://socket.dev/blog/searchjacked); audit installed extensions.

## Startups & Industry

- **DOJ eyes Nvidia and Hugging Face**: Reuters sources say the DOJ [asked how Nvidia's $12.93B purchase of Hugging Face affects AI competition](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p57#a261028p57), putting the main open model hub under antitrust review.
- **Samsung's memory bet**: Samsung reports record Q3 [revenue of about $146.3B and operating profit of $80.5B](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p64#a261028p64), expects capex up ~60% to ~$63B with [HBM (AI memory) sold out through 2027](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p4#a261029p4), yet shares [fell as much as 6.6%](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p5#a261029p5) on the capex and a chip-unit miss. WSJ adds that [flash prices have quintupled](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p65#a261028p65), flipping leverage from Apple to suppliers like Kioxia.
- **Alphabet's record drop**: Shares [fell 12%+, a record $500B in market value](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p38#a261029p38), after negative cash flow amid AI spending; Alphabet also disclosed [$1T+ in purchase commitments and other obligations](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p39#a261029p39), so investors are now pricing the AI build-out as a liability, not just growth.
- **DoorDash buys Instacart**: This morning it was a report (DoorDash had [moved its earnings up](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p2#a261029p2)); now DoorDash [announced the ~$17B acquisition](https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/doordash-to-acquire-instacart), merging the two biggest US grocery and local delivery apps, which will draw antitrust attention.
- **Funding**: FuriosaAI [raised $620M at a $2.9B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p58#a261028p58) for power-efficient inference chips, with $280M from South Korea's growth fund, and Fragment [exits stealth with $56M](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p1#a261029p1) for AI procurement. Marvell is [reportedly in talks to buy an optical component maker](https://x.com/trendforce/status/2115661151585305061).
- **Microsoft**: It is [seeking $25B+ in its first US investment-grade bond sale since 2017](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p27#a261029p27) to fund AI capex, and The Verge says it is [offering voluntary buyouts to around 60,000 employees](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1022023/microsoft-exit-offer-layoffs-notepad), its largest such offer, to hold down operating costs while AI spending continues. Windows OEM and devices revenue also [fell 22%](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p61#a261028p61) in fiscal Q1.
- **Deals**: Thrive Holdings [agreed to take ADT private](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p31#a261029p31) at about $7.6B equity value (a ~46% premium). Cohere [closed a first tranche of a round of up to $5B](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p36#a261029p36), with Canada investing up to $1.14B. Serval [raised $150M at a $3B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p29#a261029p29) for IT-ops agents.
