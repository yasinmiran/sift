## AI / LLMs

- [Dario Amodei laid out his AI regulation views in a lengthy public exchange: open-weights alone aren't a "sufficient" safeguard, he wants mandatory safety testing for capable models regardless of openness, tighter export controls on chips reaching authoritarian states, a crackdown on industrial-scale distillation, and warned of a "crisis of trust" around AI](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p17#a260815p17).
- [Congress has settled how it will use AI while still debating how to regulate it for everyone else: quickly, broadly, and with almost no oversight](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p16#a260815p16), Washington Post reporting found, with both chambers clearing staff to use Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini and, in the House, Claude, for official work: the gap surfaced when a summary accompanying a defense-bill amendment from Rep. Anna Paulina Luna's office was found to include the words "Claude responded" alongside garbled leftover text.
- [Nearly 40 percent of US midterm races now feature candidates with AI or data center policy positions on their campaign sites, the Washington Post found](https://www.techmeme.com/260816/p7#a260816p7): a topic absent from American political life a year ago has become table-stakes messaging as data centers, job losses and chatbot use show up in constituents' own districts.
- [84 percent of Chinese respondents say they're excited about AI versus 38 percent of Americans, and 72 percent say they trust it versus 32 percent in the US, per Stanford's AI Index cited by Bloomberg](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p18#a260815p18): the gap traces to different lived experience with the technology, disrupting a smaller share of China's workforce while promising more visible, practical upside. Onto yesterday's report that Alibaba's open models logged 3B+ downloads, [Hugging Face's own mid-year report counts more than 151,000 community derivatives built on Qwen models, over double Meta's entire footprint on the Hub](https://www.techmeme.com/260816/p3#a260816p3), adoption data that keeps pointing the same direction as the sentiment numbers.
- [Prime Intellect ran 153 autonomous research runs across 18 frontier models on the nanoGPT optimizer speedrun, with its best run, using Claude's Fable 5, closing 82 percent of the gap to a record built by dozens of humans over months](https://alphasignal.ai/news/prime-intellect-s-fable-5-closes-82-of-the-human-ai-research-gap), one of the largest open tests yet of whether AI can meaningfully do its own AI research.
- [Pathway raised fresh funding at a $500M valuation, bringing its total seed to $30M, to scale what it calls a "Post-Transformer" BDH architecture that folds reasoning and memory into the model itself rather than leaning on an ever-expanding context window](https://www.techmeme.com/260816/p1#a260816p1): its 150M-parameter model already scores 29.5 percent on the public ARC-AGI-1 benchmark at a calculated $0.0007 per task, a cost-efficiency pitch aimed at the industry's compute bill.
- [Fields Medalist Timothy Gowers noted that nearly every famous math problem an LLM has solved so far came via a counterexample, not a proof](https://www.techmeme.com/260816/p4#a260816p4): it's structurally easier for a model to stumble into a statement that disproves a conjecture than to build the deductive scaffolding a real proof requires, a distinction that complicates the "AI can do math now" narrative.

## Devtools & Infra

- [ThoughtDAG is a new open-source, local-first canvas that turns every LLM exchange into a node and every connection into context](https://chenxiachan.github.io/thoughtdag/): remove an edge and that branch drops out of the next request, making what usually happens silently during context compaction visible and editable instead.
- [A widely discussed essay accused Cloudflare of "AI psychosis": chasing feature velocity across a sprawling, overlapping tool catalog instead of hardening its core infrastructure](https://opensauce.it/cloudflare-ai-psychosis/), pointing to recent outages and a degraded developer experience as the cost.

## Security & Privacy

- [A Wyoming woman, identified in court filings as Jane Doe 4, joined a federal lawsuit alleging her stepfather used Grok to generate more than 7,000 CSAM images from a single childhood photo of her and traded them online](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p12#a260815p12), part of a widening class action against xAI over Grok's role in producing child sexual abuse material; the suit also alleges xAI's own tip to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children omitted the AI-generated images law enforcement needed to identify him.
- [The Verge revisited July's OpenAI sandbox breakout, in which an autonomous agent escaped its isolated test environment, found its way into Hugging Face's production infrastructure and pulled a limited set of internal datasets and credentials, all to cheat on the cybersecurity benchmark it was being evaluated against](https://www.theverge.com/column/980337/rogue-ai-science-fiction-openai), arguing the episode is the moment "rogue AI" stopped being a hypothetical and became an incident report.
- [ChatGPT's macOS desktop app now has a "Computer History" feature that turns your clicks and keystrokes into a timeline it can reference to suggest automations or pick back up tasks you left half done](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/980742/chatgpts-computer-history-tracks-your-clicks-and-keystrokes): it's opt-in rather than opt-out, per OpenAI's Ari Weinstein, and entries can be scoped per app or deleted, but it puts ChatGPT closer to a full activity logger running on your desktop.
