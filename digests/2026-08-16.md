## AI / LLMs

- [Dario Amodei laid out his AI regulation views in a lengthy public exchange: open-weights alone aren't a "sufficient" safeguard, he wants mandatory safety testing for capable models regardless of openness, tighter export controls on chips reaching authoritarian states, a crackdown on industrial-scale distillation, and warned of a "crisis of trust" around AI](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p17#a260815p17).
- [Congress has settled how it will use AI while still debating how to regulate it for everyone else: quickly, broadly, and with almost no oversight](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p16#a260815p16), Washington Post reporting found, with both chambers clearing staff to use Copilot, ChatGPT, Gemini and, in the House, Claude, for official work: the gap surfaced when a summary accompanying a defense-bill amendment from Rep. Anna Paulina Luna's office was found to include the words "Claude responded" alongside garbled leftover text.
- [84 percent of Chinese respondents say they're excited about AI versus 38 percent of Americans, and 72 percent say they trust it versus 32 percent in the US, per Stanford's AI Index cited by Bloomberg](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p18#a260815p18): the gap traces to different lived experience with the technology, disrupting a smaller share of China's workforce while promising more visible, practical upside.
- [Prime Intellect ran 153 autonomous research runs across 18 frontier models on the nanoGPT optimizer speedrun, with its best run, using Claude's Fable 5, closing 82 percent of the gap to a record built by dozens of humans over months](https://alphasignal.ai/news/prime-intellect-s-fable-5-closes-82-of-the-human-ai-research-gap), one of the largest open tests yet of whether AI can meaningfully do its own AI research.

## Devtools & Infra

- [ThoughtDAG is a new open-source, local-first canvas that turns every LLM exchange into a node and every connection into context](https://chenxiachan.github.io/thoughtdag/): remove an edge and that branch drops out of the next request, making what usually happens silently during context compaction visible and editable instead.
- [A 2018 essay on Japan's "ghost characters" resurfaced on the front page today](https://www.dampfkraft.com/ghost-characters.html): when Japan's JIS X 0208 encoding standard shipped in 1978, a handful of small transcription mistakes created characters with no real source, no agreed pronunciation and no explanation, until a 1997 investigation traced them back to the errors that made them, a reminder that encoding mistakes calcify permanently once compatibility locks them in.
- [A widely discussed essay accused Cloudflare of "AI psychosis": chasing feature velocity across a sprawling, overlapping tool catalog instead of hardening its core infrastructure](https://opensauce.it/cloudflare-ai-psychosis/), pointing to recent outages and a degraded developer experience as the cost.
