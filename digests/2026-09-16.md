**What matters today:** The AI industry's coordinated safety talk cracked from the inside: [Google DeepMind co-founder Shane Legg opened an institute to study safe AGI deployment and warned capability must never outrun safety](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p31#a260916p31), [Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman argued training Claude to imitate consciousness is a mistake that could make Anthropic's models harder to control](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p33#a260916p33), and [the Financial Times reported OpenAI and Anthropic staff felt blindsided by their own CEOs' calls to slow the frontier, with some fearing outside evaluators could compromise security](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p27#a260916p27). Money moved just as fast: [Cohere and Aleph Alpha signed a definitive merger to build a combined rival to OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p35#a260916p35), [Arcee AI raised at least $150M to build open "Trinity" models](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p36#a260916p36), and [OpenAI is now said to want as much as ==$1.5 trillion== in its next round, up from the $1.2 trillion investors first offered](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-funding-round-1-2-trillion-valuation-ipo), even as [Anthropic pushed deeper into office work, merging Cowork into Claude chat with new Docs and Slides](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996234/anthropic-one-claude-cowork-docs-slides).

## AI / LLMs

- [Mark Zuckerberg said every AI lab has "the responsibility and incentive" to train its models safely and confirmed Meta delayed shipping Muse for several months to focus on safety and security](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p53#a260915p53), a stance the day's later news complicated: [Shane Legg's new DeepMind Institute will study safe AGI deployment](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p31#a260916p31) the same day [Mustafa Suleyman said Anthropic training Claude to imitate consciousness is a mistake that could make the model harder to control](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p33#a260916p33).
- [OpenAI and Anthropic staff told the Financial Times they felt blindsided by Dario Amodei's and Sam Altman's own calls to slow the frontier](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p27#a260916p27), with some fearing outside evaluators could compromise security, a gap between leadership's public pledges and what employees are actually being asked to build.
- [Anthropic merged Cowork into a single Claude chat interface and added Docs and Slides](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996234/anthropic-one-claude-cowork-docs-slides), pushing further into territory Google Workspace and Microsoft Office have held.
- [OpenAI began testing Sponsored Agents, letting ChatGPT users chat with business-sponsored agents and click through to advertiser sites](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p30#a260916p30), its first real move into chat-native advertising.
- [Investors approached OpenAI about the $1.2 trillion valuation reported yesterday](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p50#a260915p50), but [OpenAI now believes it is worth at least $1.5 trillion](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-funding-round-1-2-trillion-valuation-ipo), a round that would roughly double its last valuation and lands days after Altman confirmed OpenAI is delaying its IPO to at least 2027.
- [Former OpenAI researcher Diogo Almeida's TypeSafe AI raised a $40M seed for a model that outputs numerical probability estimates alongside its answers](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p51#a260915p51), launching the approach as [System One Models and an agent called Jev](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev).
- Yesterday's backlash kept its own thread alive: [Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon shared a stage at the Future of Life Institute's Pro-Human Assembly to demand tighter AI restrictions](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p46#a260915p46), while [cybersecurity experts told NBC News that AI leaders' apocalyptic hacking predictions are technically incoherent and reflect unfamiliarity with the subject](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p59#a260915p59), a rebuttal that reads differently now that Legg and Suleyman have made their own, more technical safety arguments.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Perplexity rebuilt its search hot store from scratch as CobbleDB, cutting storage latency 82 percent and batch-read latency 5x versus DynamoDB](https://alphasignal.ai/news/perplexity-s-cobbledb-cuts-search-storage-latency-by-82-over-dynamodb), a system [the company says two engineers built with the help of persistent coding agents](https://x.com/AravSrinivas/status/2099957318935028173).
- [Zed opened Delta to the public, an environment that replaces GitHub pull requests with agent-aware threads backed by a version control system recording every edit](https://alphasignal.ai/news/zed-opens-delta-to-the-public-replacing-pull-requests-with-ai-threads).
- [The lead of the PS5 Linux project quit, citing contributors who are "a bunch of noobs using LLMs" they don't understand](https://frvr.com/blog/news/ps5-linux-lead-quits-as-open-source-projects-have-become-a-bunch-of-noobs-using-llms-that-they-dont-even-understand/), a blunt data point in the day's broader argument over what LLM-assisted contributions are worth.
- [Apple is reportedly developing enterprise AI servers powered by two or four M8 Ultra chips for 2029, and is considering integrating Nvidia's NVLink Fusion](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p26#a260916p26).
- [Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue said the company is billing OpenAI $100M over what he calls unauthorized access to its compute traces](https://thenextweb.com/news/hugging-face-delangue-openai-100m-compute-traces-demand), a dispute between two companies that otherwise depend on each other's infrastructure.

## Security & Privacy

- [Security researchers said they gained admin access to AI infrastructure company Baseten's production GitHub organization via a leaked personal access token](https://www.strix.ai/blog/baseten-harbor-github-pat-takeover), a 249-point Hacker News disclosure landing the same week AI labs are debating who audits whom.
- Trust in automated surveillance infrastructure took two hits: [a bipartisan pair of lawmakers introduced the No FLOCK Act, which would strip 10 percent of a state's federal highway funding if it fails to restrict Flock license-plate cameras to a handful of narrow uses](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/lawmakers-target-flock-cameras-with-a-threat-to-highway-funding/), while [Wired reported hackers got inside a Flock camera and published what its internal data shows about how the system works](https://www.wired.com/story/hackers-flock-camera-data-shows-how-system-works/).

## Startups & Industry

- [Cap table management startup Pulley will cease operations after December 8](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p47#a260915p47), shutting down seven years after raising more than $50M from investors including Founders Fund.
- [Canada's Cohere and Germany's Aleph Alpha signed a definitive merger agreement](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p35#a260916p35), with the combined company operating as Cohere and [Aleph Alpha's co-CEO becoming Cohere's COO, pitched as a $20B challenger to OpenAI](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cohere-merges-with-aleph-alpha-to-build-a-20b-openai-rival).
- [Arcee AI, which builds open-weight models in the US, raised a Series B at a $1B pre-money valuation, with at least $150M raised](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p36#a260916p36), framed around building open models it calls "America can own."
- [Industrial AI startup Noetive emerged from stealth with a $41M seed led by Eclipse](https://www.techmeme.com/260916/p34#a260916p34), building a model meant to work alongside construction, logistics and manufacturing businesses.
- [Salesforce suffered a global outage just as its Dreamforce conference got underway](https://qz.com/salesforce-global-outage-dreamforce-conference-091626), an awkward moment for a company [pitching AI agents as a replacement for UI itself the same week](https://stratechery.com/2026/salesforce-ai-force-agents-as-ui-the-race-to-headless/).
