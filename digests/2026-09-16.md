## AI / LLMs

- [Mark Zuckerberg said every AI lab has "the responsibility and incentive" to train its models safely and confirmed Meta delayed shipping Muse for several months to focus on safety and security](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p53#a260915p53), a stance [CNBC read as siding with Nvidia's Jensen Huang against a mandated slowdown, favoring outside evaluators instead](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/meta-mark-zuckerberg-with-nvidia-huang-ai-safety-slowdown.html).
- [OpenAI held early talks with investors about a private round at a $1.2T valuation before its planned IPO](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p50#a260915p50), the same week [OpenRouter data showed users spending more on OpenAI's models than Anthropic's, the first time that's happened since February 2024](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p48#a260915p48).
- Backlash kept outpacing policy: [Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon shared a stage at the Future of Life Institute's Pro-Human Assembly to demand tighter AI restrictions](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p46#a260915p46) and [a new NYT/Siena poll found 61 percent of voters oppose building data centers for AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/995917/data-center-nyt-midterm-poll-september), yet [Congress's AI bills remain stuck on partisan lines](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p55#a260915p55) and [an analysis found only about 2.3 GW of the 20 GW of data center capacity inside restricted zones is actually delayed by local moratoriums](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p57#a260915p57).
- [Cybersecurity experts told NBC News that AI leaders' apocalyptic hacking predictions are technically incoherent and reflect unfamiliarity with the subject](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p59#a260915p59), a pointed rebuttal to the AI-risk framing driving this week's safety talk.
- [Former OpenAI researcher Diogo Almeida's TypeSafe AI raised a $40M seed for a model that outputs numerical probability estimates alongside its answers](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p51#a260915p51), launching the approach as [System One Models and an agent called Jev](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev).
- [IFM says its open-weight K2-Horizon 7B model beats GPT-5 on coding with a 70.6 SWE-bench score](https://alphasignal.ai/news/ifm-s-k2-horizon-7b-beats-gpt-5-on-coding-with-70-6-swe-bench-score), a single-benchmark claim worth taking with caution.

- [Google Research swapped chain-of-thought fan-out for a 53.9M-parameter diffusion retriever, cutting AI search latency 12 to 20x](https://research.google/blog/bypassing-inference-bottlenecks-accelerating-complex-ai-search-with-retrieve-for-train/).
