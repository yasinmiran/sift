**What matters today:** Apple closed its fall event with the foldable it spent years denying interest in: the [iPhone Duo](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/apples-long-rumored-foldable-becomes-reality-with-the-2000-iphone-duo/) ships October 23 with a 5.4-inch outer screen, 7.6-inch inner screen and Touch ID, starting at ==$1,999== for 256GB, undercutting the higher price many expected during an ongoing memory shortage. AI safety governance converged from three directions the same day: [Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon quit warning that frontier labs are "gambling with our lives"](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/anthropic-researcher-quits-with-a-warning-self-improving-ai-could-kill-us-all/) racing toward self-improving superintelligence, [Paul Christiano joined OpenAI's nonprofit board](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p56#a260909p56) saying the industry isn't on track to bring that risk down to an acceptable level, and [California signed two Anthropic- and OpenAI-backed AI safety-evaluation bills](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p51#a260909p51) into law.

## AI / LLMs

- [Jacob Coxon quit Anthropic](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/anthropic-researcher-quits-with-a-warning-self-improving-ai-could-kill-us-all/), two months before his equity would have vested, and used the exit to warn publicly that frontier labs "earnestly believe" self-improving superintelligence "could kill us all by the end of the decade"; Anthropic's own alignment lead Evan Hubinger agreed, putting personal odds of AI-caused extinction this decade above 10%.
- [Paul Christiano, the researcher who pioneered RLHF, is joining OpenAI's nonprofit board](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p56#a260909p56) and its Safety and Security Committee, and used the announcement to say the industry currently isn't on track to bring loss-of-control risk down to an "acceptable" level.
- [California Gov. Gavin Newsom signed two AI bills backed by both Anthropic and OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p51#a260909p51) regulating how outside groups evaluate AI systems for safety, the same week the industry's own people are warning that oversight isn't moving fast enough.
- [Cognition engineer Eric Lu drove up to 18 concurrent Devin agent sessions over three weeks](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cognition-s-devin-breaks-rsa-260-record-in-three-weeks-with-ai-agents) to rewrite most of the CADO-NFS factoring pipeline for GPUs and factor RSA-260, an 862-bit semiprime unbroken since the original 1991 RSA Factoring Challenge, for about $400,000 in compute, ==10x cheaper== than the prior public state of the art.
- [OpenAI mobilized 250+ people and its Daybreak cyber models](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-defense-factory-deploys-ai-agents-to-patch-hundreds-of-security-flaws) to hunt and patch vulnerabilities across its own stack, then open-sourced the effort as a "Defense Factory" reference architecture.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Kepler Computing emerged from stealth with $468M](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p55#a260909p55), claiming its 3D chip stacking and a proprietary material can raise HBM and SRAM density without relying on EUV lithography, aimed at the memory shortage now driving up prices industry-wide.
- [Epoch AI's new compute tracker estimates OpenAI's computing capacity grew 17x over the past two years](https://alphasignal.ai/news/epoch-reveals-openai-s-computing-power-grew-17x-in-two-years), outpacing the other labs it tracks (Google DeepMind, Anthropic, Meta, xAI).

## Security & Privacy

- [Proofpoint found at least four hacking groups, some tied to the Chinese government, using a near-identical exploit kit it calls BlueMoon](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/09/4-groups-caught-using-the-same-chrome-and-windows-exploit-kit/), chaining two Chromium bugs with a Windows kernel flaw that spans Windows 10 (2018) through Windows 11; all three were patched only in the past 24 hours, and Proofpoint suspects AI-assisted bug hunting sped up how fast the kit spread.
- [Read the Docs published a postmortem on a nearly ten-day DDoS attack](https://about.readthedocs.com/blog/2026/09/2026-ddos-attack/) that peaked at 5.5 million requests per minute, about 100x normal traffic: the botnet deliberately targeted URLs that bypass caching (404s, redirects) and shifted request patterns and IP pools every time the site tightened rate limits.
- [New Mexico, the only US state taking its Cambridge Analytica-era case against Meta to trial](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p57#a260909p57), opened by accusing the company of failing to protect Facebook user data and enabling targeted ads meant to suppress Democratic turnout.

## Startups & Industry

- Apple's event also refreshed the rest of the lineup: the [iPhone 18 Pro](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/apples-iphone-18-pro-adds-variable-camera-aperture-and-a-more-powerful-chip/) adds a variable-aperture camera and a faster chip (black finish returning), [Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/apple-watch-series-12-ultra-4-have-wearables-most-accurate-heart-rate-sensing/) add what Apple calls its most accurate heart-rate sensing yet, and [AirPods 5](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/apple-debuts-129-airpods-5-with-better-noise-cancellation-and-transparency-mode/) ($129) improve noise cancellation and transparency mode.
- On the services side, Apple's [new Health app adds an Apple Intelligence Insights tab and a daily "readiness" score](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p48#a260909p48), it [published a document on how new Siri listening features keep processing on-device](https://www.theverge.com/tech/992919/apple-siri-ai-audio-intelligence-privacy), and a new [AppleCare One Family plan](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p50#a260909p50) covers every eligible device across a 6-person Family Sharing group for $49.99/month.
- [WordPress.com owner Automattic placed CEO Matt Mullenweg on paid leave](https://www.theverge.com/tech/993022/wordpress-automattic-ceo-matt-mullenweg-leave-of-absence) after what he called a board "conspiracy": CFO Mark Davies and three board members voted him out over his objection and installed Davies as interim CEO.
- [Consensys plans to split into two independent companies](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p47#a260909p47): MetaMask, staying under longtime CEO Joe Lubin, and a separate Consensys focused on Ethereum and institutional infrastructure.
- [Jeffrey Katzenberg is teaming up with ex-OpenAI Sora lead Bill Peebles and ex-Dropbox CFO Sujay Jaswa](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p49#a260909p49) to launch a startup training AI video models specifically for filmmakers.
- [The DOJ is investigating whether Nvidia tried to dodge antitrust scrutiny of its 2025 deal with Groq](https://www.techmeme.com/260909/p53#a260909p53), which Groq has described publicly as a "nonexclusive licensing agreement."
- A lawsuit accuses [Amazon of denying pregnant warehouse workers bathroom breaks and chairs](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/lawsuit-amazon-refused-to-give-pregnant-workers-bathroom-breaks-and-chairs/), drawing a large Hacker News thread on top of the underlying claims.
- Nearly 1,900 unionized [Blizzard workers ratified a historic CWA contract](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/blizzard-workers-win-historic-union-contract-that-could-set-a-new-standard-for-game-developers) spanning multiple bargaining units at once: it bars Microsoft from automating away their jobs without union talks first, guarantees laid-off members a recall offer within 14 months plus four weeks' extra pay, and adds a three-day hybrid work week.

## Elsewhere

- [Suno released its first AI music model built with record-industry cooperation](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/991977/suno-releases-its-first-ai-music-model-made-with-record-industry-help), the clearest sign yet that at least one label camp would rather work with AI generators than keep suing them.
- [Muse, the band, quietly gave up its longtime four-letter @muse handle](https://www.engadget.com/2254419/muse-the-band-lost-its-social-media-handles-to-muse-meta-s-new-ai-agent/) sometime this summer, now posting as @museband and @Musetheband, after Meta claimed the name for its own new AI agent, also called Muse; nobody involved has explained exactly how the handoff happened.
