**What matters today:** SpaceX [caught the Starship upper stage with its launch tower](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/with-starship-catch-spacex-still-shows-it-can-dazzle-the-world/) at the end of Flight 15, the first time the ship itself has been caught on its return from orbit, though the booster catch planned for the same flight was called off. The Nvidia story keeps widening: Elon Musk and other tech executives [signed a petition asking Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/elon-musk-other-tech-execs-sign-petition-calling-on-nvidia-to-reinstate-jensen-huang). Yesterday the Washington Post reported how Marco Rubio's rewrite of a CIA report helped bring Huang down; today the same paper counts [the cost to the White House](https://www.techmeme.com/261114/p23#a261114p23) before the midterms.

## AI / LLMs

- **Open models on ordinary hardware**: ChatR Labs published compressed builds of Alibaba's image model Qwen-Image-2.1 that [run on 4 to 14 GB of graphics memory](https://alphasignal.ai/news/chatr-labs-shrinks-alibaba-s-qwen-image-2-1-to-4-gb-for-consumer-gpus), the range of a consumer card. VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo, a Vietnamese text-to-speech model built from scratch, does [instant voice cloning at 48 kHz](https://alphasignal.ai/news/vieneu-tts-v3-turbo-runs-vietnamese-voice-cloning-on-a-consumer-gpu) and runs in real time on a CPU. And Simon Willison's [datasette-llm 0.1a11](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/14/datasette-llm/) adds OpenAI-compatible endpoints, so the Datasette Agent can run on a model served locally by LM Studio, Ollama or llama.cpp. Why it matters: image generation, voice cloning and a data agent each now work without a cloud account or a data center GPU.
- **Agents as coworkers in the team chat**: [agentes.chat](https://alphasignal.ai/news/agentes-chat-turns-team-chat-into-an-open-source-ai-agent-control-plane) is an open-source, self-hosted Slack clone where Claude, Codex, Gemini and Grok agents join channels as teammates, with approvals, spending budgets and MCP tools (the standard way agents connect to outside services). Why it matters: it puts the agent's permissions and costs in the place the whole team already watches, where a private terminal session hides both.
- **Also**: the South China Morning Post asks whether Chinese AI companies can [curb the cyber risks of open-weight models](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3374332/can-chinese-ai-companies-curb-cyber-risks-open-weight-models-without-losing-their-edge) without losing their edge, since released weights cannot be recalled or patched once downloaded.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Small releases**: Kiri pitches itself as a [fast Docker Desktop alternative](https://runkiri.com) for Mac, Windows and Linux. Willison also shipped [datasette-vega 0.7](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/14/datasette-vega/), the first release of the charting plugin since July 2018, updated for the latest Datasette alphas and for Datasette Lite, the in-browser version, which just gained the ability to run JavaScript plugins. Why it matters: Docker Desktop is the default way to run containers on a laptop, so a faster replacement on all three platforms targets a tool most developers open daily.

## Security & Privacy

- **Job boards purge fake listings**: LinkedIn and Monster [took down more than 1,000 fake job postings](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/linkedin-monster-take-down-over-1-000-fake-job-postings-after-cbc-investigation-9.7415379) after a CBC investigation. Why it matters: the listings came down only once reporters found them, which says the platforms' own screening had let them through.

## Startups & Industry

- **A petition to bring Huang back**: Elon Musk and other tech executives [signed a petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/elon-musk-other-tech-execs-sign-petition-calling-on-nvidia-to-reinstate-jensen-huang). Forbes explains why he could lose the company in the first place: unlike Mark Zuckerberg, Michael Dell and Evan Spiegel, who hold majority voting power, Huang [had no super-voting shares](https://www.techmeme.com/261114/p20#a261114p20). Why it matters: without those shares the decision rests with the board and ordinary shareholders, and a petition from fellow executives has no formal weight with either.
- **The scandal becomes a midterm problem**: the Washington Post writes that the Nvidia affair [ties together data centers, China and corruption](https://www.techmeme.com/261114/p23#a261114p23), three issues voters already care about, and so is a political challenge for the White House. A Wall Street Journal piece is headlined on the [backdoor route Huang built](https://www.wsj.com/tech/jensen-huang-backdoor-ai-data-centers-a5e4499d) that keeps Beijing in the AI race (paywalled), and 23andMe's Anne Wojcicki tells Bloomberg the episode shows what happens when CEOs [think they are "untouchable"](https://www.techmeme.com/261114/p22#a261114p22). Why it matters: the story has moved from one company's leadership to an election issue, which gives officials a reason to keep pressing it.
- **Nvidia's pricing, questioned**: TechSpot reports an allegation that Nvidia [kept GPU prices high through circular financing deals](https://www.techspot.com/news/114558-nvidia-deliberately-kept-gpu-prices-high-through-circular.html), meaning money it put into customers came back as chip orders; it is a claim, not a finding. PCWorld adds the long view: Huang [built Nvidia on gamers](https://www.pcworld.com/article/3284206/jensen-huang-built-nvidia-on-gamers-ai-made-them-an-afterthought.html), and AI made them an afterthought. Why it matters: if the allegation holds, buyers outside those deals, gamers included, paid for it.
- **Data center tenants asked to backstop the power**: Bloomberg's source says developer Applied Digital has, since at least last year, [asked tenants such as Oracle and Meta for billions in guarantees](https://www.techmeme.com/261114/p21#a261114p21) to secure electricity before leases are final. Why it matters: the financial risk of grid power is moving from the landlord to the tech companies, and it lands before they have a signed lease.
- **Stablecoins as Latin America's dollar account**: dollar-pegged stablecoins are [taking a growing share of the region's crypto activity](https://www.techmeme.com/261114/p19#a261114p19), Bloomberg reports, used to store value and to send money across borders, including by people who know nothing about blockchains. Why it matters: this is crypto adopted for an ordinary need, holding and moving dollars, in places where local currency and bank transfers do both poorly.

## Elsewhere

- **SpaceX catches the ship**: the launch tower's "chopstick" arms caught the Starship upper stage at the end of Flight 15, the ==first time== the ship itself has been caught, and on a return from orbit; earlier catches were all boosters. Ars Technica's Eric Berger, at Starbase, describes the ship [falling horizontally out of the clouds](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/11/with-starship-catch-spacex-still-shows-it-can-dazzle-the-world/), lighting three Raptor engines to swing upright, and settling onto the arms by two nubs that each stick out about a foot. The caveat: SpaceX [aborted the planned catch of the booster](https://www.theverge.com/science/1033930/spacex-chopstick-starship-catch), so it did not recover the whole vehicle. Why it matters: full reuse needs both halves back, and each has now been caught, though not yet on the same flight.
