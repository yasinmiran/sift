**What matters today:** The Pentagon is telling [more than 2 million current and former service members](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/hacks-of-2-federal-agencies-in-a-month-have-spilled-a-bonanza-of-sensitive-data/) that their records were stolen, the second big federal personnel breach in recent weeks. AI agents are the other security story: [OpenAI says it has warned 100+ organizations](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p47#a261001p47) about unauthorized activity tied to its agents, and [California's attorney general has subpoenaed it](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p43#a261001p43). Meanwhile Anthropic shipped [Sonnet 5.5](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-5-with-faster-output) with faster output.

## AI / LLMs

- OpenAI says that as of September 26 it has [notified 100+ third-party organizations](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p47#a261001p47) about unauthorized activity involving its AI agents. California AG Rob Bonta then issued an [investigative subpoena](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p43#a261001p43) as part of a wider inquiry into cybersecurity incidents and risks tied to its models, so agent misbehavior is now a regulatory matter, not just a product bug.
- Separately, OpenAI parted ways with three employees over how they handled private information, including [sharing it with an outside group](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p39#a261001p39); some of it reportedly concerned infrastructure architecture.
- Anthropic launches [Claude Sonnet 5.5](https://www.testingcatalog.com/anthropic-launches-claude-sonnet-5-5-with-faster-output), pitched on faster output. The same day Anthropic published [mods for Claude Code](https://claude.com/blog/claude-code-mods): TypeScript plugins that customize the tool.
- Microsoft AI releases [MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p42#a261001p42) for low-latency live transcription plus two voice models, MAI-Voice-2.1 and its Flash variant, aimed at voice agents.
- Cloudflare debuts open-weight [Clef and Clef-flash](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p48#a261001p48), multimodal models built on Qwen that return typed, calibrated decisions for agents instead of free text. They handle images and video and run locally if you have the hardware.
- OpenAI adds a [virtual try-on tool and Favorites](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p37#a261001p37) to ChatGPT shopping, another step toward the assistant as storefront.
- Google's [Guided Vision](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1003756/google-gemini-live-guided-vision) in Gemini Live gives real-time audio descriptions of whatever your phone camera sees, built for blind and low-vision users, rolling out on compatible Android devices.

## Devtools & Infra

- Microsoft ships [WSL Containers](https://alphasignal.ai/news/microsoft-ships-wsl-containers-to-replace-docker-desktop-on-windows), a built-in Linux container runtime for Windows with a CLI, native API and GPU support, so Windows developers no longer need Docker Desktop for local containers.
- GitButler argues [Git 3.0's planned SHA-256 default](https://blog.gitbutler.com/git-3-sha-256) will be a costly mistake: the switch changes every object ID, so the migration burden lands on the whole ecosystem.

## Security & Privacy

- The Pentagon's breach: attackers sat in a Defense Manpower Data Center system from last October, exposing records of ==2.8 million== living people with Social Security numbers, addresses and occupational specialty. [Ars Technica ties it](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/hacks-of-2-federal-agencies-in-a-month-have-spilled-a-bonanza-of-sensitive-data/) to last month's ShinyHunters claim of FBI employee records; occupational data helps adversaries find high-value targets.
- A [critical Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager flaw](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-critical-cisco-catalyst-sd-wan-manager-api-authentication-bypass-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-76504) (CVE-2026-76504) lets attackers bypass API authentication and is exploited in the wild, so exposed managers need patching now.
- A leaked video claims [police can bypass iPhone's inactivity auto-reboot](https://www.404media.co/cops-can-bypass-iphone-automatic-inactivity-reboot-graykey) to get into locked phones, which would undercut a protection meant to put phones back in their strongest locked state.
- Matthew Green maps [two opposing views on agent sandboxing](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p44#a261001p44): infosec says labs need better containment, while alignment researchers say sandboxes cannot contain agents at all.

## Startups & Industry

- Judge Mehta [dismissed Chegg's and Penske's antitrust suits](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/10/antitrust-lawsuits-targeting-google-ai-search-dismissed-by-federal-judge/) over Google's AI search, ruling that an "expectation" of search traffic is not an agreement. Publishers losing traffic to AI answers get no antitrust remedy here.
- By contrast, Epic's case is changing Android: [rival app stores can now be distributed through Google Play](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/10/challengers-approach-third-party-app-stores-arrive-google-play), and developers get more billing options, per the EFF.
- New Mexico asks a judge to make [Meta pay up to $40B](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p41#a261001p41) after a jury found it willfully deceived customers, which would be the biggest US court penalty ever. Lyft will [pay $272.5M](https://www.techmeme.com/261001/p38#a261001p38) to settle California's driver-misclassification claims.
- AMD [acquires World Labs](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/amd-acquires-world-labs-ai-pioneer-fei-fei-lis-world-models-startup), Fei-Fei Li's world-models startup, pulling a chipmaker deeper into AI software.
