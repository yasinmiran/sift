**What matters today:** [Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate reached $65B by the end of July](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p31#a260817p31), up from $47B in May and roughly $9B at the end of 2025, a sevenfold jump in under a year as the company heads toward a possible IPO. The growth lands the same day [Situational Awareness, the AI-focused fund run by former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner, offered part of its $5B Anthropic stake at a 20% discount while scrambling for cash](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p37#a260817p37), and the same day [David Sacks publicly clashed with Amodei over AI policy](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p36#a260817p36), arguing Amodei's push for pre-deployment testing and regulator oversight would centralize power in Washington and the labs rather than prevent it. The day also brought a run of AI trust-and-safety news: OpenAI launched a teen mode for ChatGPT, Meta ran ads for a deepfake nudify app, and Microsoft Copilot gave up the exact prompt that let researchers hack it.

## AI / LLMs

- [David Sacks, Trump's AI advisor, publicly rebutted Dario Amodei's case for AI regulation](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p36#a260817p36): "Dario Amodei believes frontier AI is too powerful to distribute; we believe it is too powerful to centralize," arguing Amodei's preferred FAA-style pre-deployment testing would hand gatekeeping power to a federal bureaucracy working hand in hand with frontier labs.
- [OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens), a mode that automatically limits high-risk conversations about self-harm, eating disorders and violence and adds studying tools and parental controls; the rollout follows years of lawsuits and reporting tying chatbot use to teen mental-health harm.
- [Qwen3.8 27B's overthinking habit, flagged by Simon Willison, now has 1M+ downloads to go with it](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8-27b): the open-weights model ties OpenAI's cloud-only GPT-5.6 Luna at 52 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, but got there by generating 160 million output tokens in testing versus a 43 million median for comparable open-weight models, and Alibaba says it is one of its fastest-growing model releases yet.
- [Alibaba's Alipay launched an "all-in-one" platform for businesses to automate tasks with AI agents](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p9#a260818p9), and Alibaba's stock jumped 5%+ on the news, up 40%+ since June.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Cursor launched Origin, a native code hosting service with pull requests, reviews and CI baked into the editor](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p28#a260817p28), rolling out in early beta on all paid plans the same day GitHub suffered a worldwide outage; Origin can sync GitHub repos in both directions, with GitHub still treated as the source of truth for now.

## Security & Privacy

- [Wiz's autonomous "Red Agent" found and exploited a script-injection bug in Snowflake's GitHub Actions workflow that GitHub Copilot's own Autofix tool had introduced](https://www.wiz.io/blog/red-agent-snowflake-copilot-cicd-bug), gaining read access to Snowflake's internal Jira for five days before Wiz reported it; Snowflake patched and rotated credentials the same day, and GitHub disputes that Copilot authored the flawed commit at all.
- [Researchers got Microsoft 365 Copilot to reveal the exact guardrail mechanics that let them hack it](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/microsoft-copilot-reveals-secret-input-that-allowed-it-to-be-hacked/): Varonis simply asked Copilot why it refused certain requests, and its answers, gathered over repeated questioning, mapped out the confirmation gesture Copilot needed to auto-execute commands that could exfiltrate a user's data from a single link click.
- [Meta ran ads for a "nudify" app that included a pornographic deepfake closely resembling a prominent US politician](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p22#a260818p22); Apple removed the app from the App Store after Wired's report.
- [Google quietly let verified political campaigns bypass Gmail's spam filter more easily](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p20#a260818p20), after years of complaints from Republicans, who had previously sued Google over claims the filter was biased before the case was dismissed.
- [Brazil ordered Discord to suspend livestreams nationwide after a 13-year-old died by suicide during one](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p40#a260817p40), the country's data protection authority finding "robust evidence" Discord failed to keep minors from content promoting self-harm; the feature stays off until Discord shows adequate safeguards.
- [Pornhub's parent company Aylo agreed to pay $120M over six years to settle class actions alleging its sites hosted child sexual abuse material](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p35#a260817p35), covering anyone who was a minor in content uploaded between February 2021 and December 2024; the settlement includes new age-verification and content-review commitments and is not an admission of wrongdoing.

## Startups & Industry

- [Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate hit $65B by the end of July](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p31#a260817p31), up from $47B in May and $9B in late 2025, as the company heads toward a possible IPO later this year.
- [Situational Awareness offered part of its $5B Anthropic stake at a 20% discount as it scrambled for cash](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p37#a260817p37): the Aschenbrenner-founded fund had already dumped its entire public-equities book to Citadel in late July after a leveraged AI bet went bad, and word of the Anthropic-stake sale left it "being hunted" by traders.
- [The DOJ has spent nearly a year investigating Andreessen Horowitz](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p29#a260817p29) over whether partners Ben Horowitz and Martin Casado, who sit on the boards of rivals Databricks and Fivetran, violate a Clayton Act rule barring the same person from directing competing companies; no charges have been filed.
- [Crusoe is in IPO talks with at least four Wall Street banks including JPMorgan](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p34#a260817p34), tied to a pre-IPO fundraise that would value the AI data center builder at $35B, up from the roughly $30B investors were discussing in July.
- [Google plans to move all Pixel phone, watch and earbud manufacturing out of China starting in 2027](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p39#a260817p39), Nikkei Asia reports, shifting the sensitive early production-tuning work to Vietnam and India amid US-China tensions.
- [Google won a bankruptcy auction for a trove of Spirit Airlines data for $10M](https://www.techmeme.com/260817/p26#a260817p26), beating a $7.5M bid from AI company Mercor; the haul includes roughly 100 million de-identified emails and 500 million Teams messages for AI training, with all customer and loyalty data excluded.
- [Klarna's CFO and CMO plan to step down in early 2027, and the company lowered its full-year guidance](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p16#a260818p16) even as it swung to a profit and posted higher revenue for the quarter; shares fell 20%+.

## Research

- [DeepMind's AlphaEvolve found a way to multiply 4x4 complex-valued matrices using 48 scalar multiplications](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.16884), the first improvement on Strassen's 1969 algorithm in that setting in over 56 years; the evolutionary coding agent has now beaten specialized tools on 14 different matrix-multiplication targets, with real gains already showing up as 23% faster training kernels on Google's TPUs.

## Elsewhere

- [Opening arguments began Tuesday in a federal trial pitting Meta against a 29-state coalition led by California, Colorado, New Jersey and Kentucky](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/meta-attorneys-general-california-federal-trial-astronomical-consequences.html) over claims its apps were engineered to addict children; the states are seeking up to $1.4 trillion in penalties over a trial expected to run seven weeks, with Zuckerberg and Instagram's Adam Mosseri likely to testify.
- [YouTube will count a view the moment a video starts playing, from August 24](https://www.theverge.com/streaming/981105/youtube-video-view-counting-update), matching how TikTok and Instagram already count and applying to long-form video, podcasts and live streams alike; a new "engaged views" metric will keep governing creator payouts, so the public number gets bigger without changing what ads actually pay out on.
