## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI said it has been coordinating on AI safety with Anthropic and Google for several weeks and does not need an antitrust waiver to do it](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p27#a260915p27), a direct answer to this morning's "cartel" framing of the labs' slowdown pledge.
- [OpenAI backed a provision of the bipartisan FRONTIER Act that would require AI companies to embed outside evaluators in their safety process](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p22#a260915p22), moving the coordination from informal talks toward binding law.
- [Elon Musk called for top US and Chinese AI labs, three or four of the latter by his count, to let rivals run a "test harness" on each other's models](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p31#a260915p31), a harder-edged rival version of the same day's safety push.
- [A Mozilla report found the performance gap between US frontier models and the best Chinese open-weight models has closed to ==4.4 months==](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/exclusive-open-chinese-models-close-gap-with-silicon-valleys-frontier-ai-models/), at roughly a fifth of the cost, explaining why most routine workloads are shifting to open models.
- [At Dreamforce, Salesforce unveiled Koa, its own CRM reasoning model built with Nvidia on the Nemotron architecture to power Agentforce agents](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p23#a260915p23), the same day [Anthropic shipped a Claude plugin with 37 built-in Salesforce skills, letting sellers act on live pipeline and account data without leaving the chat](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-brings-claude-inside-salesforce-with-37-built-in-sales-skills).

## Devtools & Infra

- [Microsoft rolled out an emergency out-of-band update for Windows 11](https://www.theverge.com/news/995302/microsoft-out-of-band-windows-11-update-fix-issues) to fix bugs its record-breaking, nearly 1,000-flaw September patch Tuesday introduced in Hyper-V Linux VM folder shares, Remote Desktop Services and some USB audio devices.
- [Factory, maker of the "Droids" AI coding agents that switch models by task complexity, raised $200M at a $5B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p37#a260915p37), up from $1.5B in April, with Khosla Ventures, Blackstone and Marc Benioff among the investors.

## Security & Privacy

- [New Stripe data found AI companies face 4.3x more attempted fraud than startups overall](https://stripe.com/blog/what-stripe-data-shows-about-fraud-at-ai-startups), a gap Stripe attributes to the sector's fast customer growth and thin abuse history.
- [The US DOJ says several companies used Binance to relay roughly ==$1.5B== in payments for black-market Iranian oil bought by Chinese buyers](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p24#a260915p24); Binance says it took action once it learned of the activity.
- [AWS facilities in Abu Dhabi and Bahrain remain mostly offline more than six months after Iranian drone damage](https://www.techmeme.com/260915/p32#a260915p32), complicating the Gulf region's AI infrastructure ambitions.
- [Anthropic published a report on threat actors' attempts to misuse Claude for harm, covering activity disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/09/15/the-bad-guy-with-an-ai-named-claude/) across seven areas: cyber operations, influence operations, surveillance, scams and fraud, biological misuse, conventional weapons development and distillation; per Zvi Mowshowitz's read, most attempts failed outright, the same low-grade misuse pattern behind [this morning's report of AI agents flooding Mastodon admins with spam pitches](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/ai-agents-flood-the-internet-with-slop-infused-spam/).
- [Cybersecurity stocks, which led the S&P 500 this morning as AI fears escalated](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p42#a260914p42), stayed the day's live wire as new incidents piled up: Binance's sanctions-evasion relay, AWS's still-dark Gulf facilities, and Anthropic's own disrupted-misuse report above.
- [The US confirmed for the first time this morning that it has deployed weapons in orbit](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/09/for-the-first-time-the-us-military-confirms-it-has-deployed-weapons-in-orbit/), an escalation that sat alongside the day's other security news without a new development by evening.
