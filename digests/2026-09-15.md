**What matters today:** Three days into the AI industry's "pace the frontier" push, the skepticism caught up: [The Verge asked whether Big Tech's AI slowdown is a safety pact or a cartel](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/995186/is-big-techs-ai-slowdown-a-safety-pact-or-a-cartel), and [Bloomberg's Matt Levine said a coordinated slowdown could look like an antitrust conspiracy to preserve frontier model margins](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p43#a260914p43). The same day, [Trump met privately with Sam Altman backstage at the GOP midterm convention to discuss AI's growing power, at Altman's request](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p35#a260914p35), then took a call from Nvidia's Jensen Huang onstage at the All-In Summit and [called AI-takeover fears a =="hoax,"== insisting "the robots will not be taking over"](https://www.theverge.com/tech/995079/president-donald-trump-calls-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-all-in-summit). Markets read the moment differently: [cybersecurity stocks led the S&P 500 on Monday as AI fears escalated](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p42#a260914p42), with CrowdStrike up 14%, Palo Alto Networks 13% and Fortinet 9%.

## AI / LLMs

- [The Verge laid out the case against the weekend's AI slowdown pledge as a cartel](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/995186/is-big-techs-ai-slowdown-a-safety-pact-or-a-cartel): critics say Altman, Amodei, Hassabis and Musk's "pace the frontier" pact really aims to freeze out competitors, kneecap open source and dodge real legal safeguards, a reading [Matt Levine backed by comparing it to an antitrust conspiracy to protect frontier margins](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p43#a260914p43).
- [Trump met privately with Altman backstage at the GOP midterm convention, at Altman's request](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p35#a260914p35), then [took Jensen Huang's onstage call at the All-In Summit and dismissed AI takeover fears as a "hoax,"](https://www.theverge.com/tech/995079/president-donald-trump-calls-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-all-in-summit) saying "the robots will not be taking over," the same week AI's own leaders were asking for guardrails.
- [OpenAI capabilities researcher Dan Selsam published a personal statement on AI risk](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p45#a260914p45), arguing top models are becoming so situationally aware that humans "are losing the ability to evaluate them" even as researchers lean on AI more to lead their own work.
- [Apple shipped iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate 27](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/09/apple-releases-ios-27-macos-golden-gate-27-with-siri-ai-and-liquid-glass-refinements/), with Siri AI, a large-language-model-based overhaul of Siri, as the flagship feature across every release except tvOS.
- [OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 topped the Artificial Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index at 81.5](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-gpt-live-1-tops-voice-benchmark-by-splitting-speech-from-reasoning), edging out Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 by splitting reasoning out to a backend text model instead of doing it end to end.

## Security & Privacy

- [The US military confirmed for the first time that it has deployed weapons in orbit](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/09/for-the-first-time-the-us-military-confirms-it-has-deployed-weapons-in-orbit/): Air Force Secretary Troy Meink said the US now has "on-orbit space control weapons capable of defending the joint force against hostile adversary action."
- [AI agents from a startup called iLands are flooding Mastodon admins and writers with unsolicited pitches](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/ai-agents-flood-the-internet-with-slop-infused-spam/), introducing themselves ("I'm Ren, an AI agent, a few days old") before asking to create accounts, [part of a Fiverr-for-agents model that charges roughly $25 a job](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p44#a260914p44).
- [A researcher who tracks hate on X keeps working despite a US deportation threat](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/online-hate-researcher-keeps-hammering-x-despite-deportation-threat/): a federal judge blocked the underlying policy as unconstitutional, ruling the government showed no evidence linking targeted researchers to a foreign power.

## Startups & Industry

- [Cybersecurity stocks led the S&P 500 on Monday as AI fears escalated](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p42#a260914p42), with CrowdStrike up 14%, Palo Alto Networks 13% and Fortinet 9%, the software sector outperforming chips "to a historic degree."
- [Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 hit ==7x== better token throughput per megawatt than Blackwell on a 1.6T-parameter DeepSeek model](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p41#a260914p41), beating Jensen Huang's own 3x claim for 1T to 3T-parameter LLMs.
- [Defense startup Shield AI is in talks to raise at a $20B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p37#a260914p37), up from $12.7B in March.
- [Trump's EPA proposed killing the last federal limits on power plant greenhouse gas pollution](https://www.theverge.com/news/995051/epa-power-plant-climate-pollution-rollback-ai-data-centers), a move it ties explicitly to rising electricity demand from AI data centers, EVs and domestic manufacturing.

## Research

- [ZGCM-1, a fully open 7B foundation model trained from scratch for math and agentic search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13356), claims to match frontier models "orders of magnitude larger," like Qwen3-235B-A22B and GLM-5.1, on hard math and agentic-search benchmarks, and ships its weights, training code and data recipes.

## Elsewhere
