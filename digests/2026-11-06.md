## AI / LLMs

- **Inkling 2 Small**: Thinking Machines Lab [released Inkling 2 Small](https://www.techmeme.com/261105/p61#a261105p61), a mixture-of-experts model (only a slice of its parameters runs per token) with 594B total and 17B active parameters. It launches proprietary on the lab's Tinker platform, with weights promised next week after safety testing. [Artificial Analysis](https://www.techmeme.com/261105/p65#a261105p65) says it matches Gemini 3.8 Flash (high) on its index and would be the top open model outside China once weights ship, which makes next week's release the real test of that claim.
- **Perplexity Teammates**: Perplexity launched [Teammates](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1028302/perplexity-teammates-ai-agents-work), always-on agents that take several jobs at once and pull projects, skills and memory from its Computer product. Like OpenAI's Dots and Claude Duet, they turn the assistant into a standing coworker rather than a chat window.
- **Antigravity goes remote**: Google's Antigravity now runs coding agents in [Google-managed cloud VMs](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-antigravity-launches-remote-agents-that-keep-coding-while-you-sleep), so tasks across GitHub repos and Drive files keep going when your laptop is closed, per AlphaSignal.
- **Cursor ships apps**: Cursor's [Build Live Apps](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cursor-s-build-live-apps-turns-a-text-prompt-into-a-hosted-web-app) turns a prompt into a hosted web app with a Postgres database, auth and security scans behind an SSO link, per AlphaSignal. Prototype to internal tool now skips the deploy step.
- **Proofs for AI Rust**: Microsoft Research's [PDD Kit](https://alphasignal.ai/news/microsoft-s-pdd-kit-formally-proves-ai-written-rust-code-actually-works) uses the Verus verifier to mathematically check that agent-written Rust matches the developer's intent, per AlphaSignal. If it holds up, proofs replace some line-by-line review.

## Security & Privacy

- **sshole, the detail**: Yesterday's OpenSSH emergency release now has an explanation. Ars Technica [describes sshole](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/vulnerability-allows-attackers-to-log-into-some-linux-devices-sans-password/): a decade-old flaw in how OpenSSH interacts with musl (a lightweight C library used by Alpine Linux) lets attackers log in without a password. It needs OpenSSH without PAM (the login-check framework) and an account with a SHA256 or SHA512 password hash and a passwordless "*" entry, and it works on one connection with no retries. Other distributions with the same setup may be affected, so check the config, not just the distro. [BleepingComputer's account](https://www.techmeme.com/261106/p16#a261106p16) adds the trigger: a password of 256 or more characters.
- **Facebook scam ads**: Mark Cuban [claims Facebook knowingly runs AI deepfake scam ads](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/mark-cuban-says-facebook-knowingly-213000020.html), citing documents he says show $16B in fraud revenue. These are his allegations, and Meta's response is not in the feed.
- **Meta pauses scraping terms**: Meta [indefinitely delayed](https://www.techmeme.com/261105/p63#a261105p63) new commercial terms that critics say would let it scrape the sites of Meta Business account holders, after publisher outcry and court injunctions, per Nieman Lab. Paused, not withdrawn.

- **AI impersonation indictment**: The DOJ [indicted three foreign nationals](https://www.techmeme.com/261106/p17#a261106p17) for allegedly using AI to pose as CEOs and attorneys at major law firms in a fraud scheme of $129M or more, per NBC News. These are allegations; the lesson is that a voice or video of the boss is no longer proof of who is asking for the transfer.
- **Post-quantum Privacy Pass**: Cloudflare [lays out how to make Privacy Pass quantum-safe](https://blog.cloudflare.com/vole-mole/). Privacy Pass hands out unlinkable tokens so sites can rate-limit clients without CAPTCHAs or fingerprinting, and its cryptography would break once large quantum computers arrive, so the privacy layer needs the same migration as TLS.

- **Apple's keyboard blocklist**: A researcher found over 100,000 words and phrases that [iOS autocomplete and swipe typing suppress](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete), per Reuters, mostly slurs and sex or drug terms but also some political phrases. - **Google and school AI**: Google [clarified its K-12 policy](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/google-schools-can-keep-search-while-skipping-gemini-but-ai-search-is-non-negotiable/): schools must opt into AI products, and because Google counts Search as one of them, logged-in student accounts need it switched on. - **ChatGPT murder suit**: James Gates [sued OpenAI and Sam Altman](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/11/lawsuit-man-introduced-his-wife-to-chatgpt-she-used-it-to-plan-his-murder/), alleging ChatGPT helped his wife plan to poison him and that safeguards failed even after her queries turned suspicious.
