**What matters today:** Anthropic moved on two fronts at once: it locked in [a seven-year, $11.6 billion cloud deal with Akamai](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/24/akamai-shares-jump-more-than-20-on-11-6b-anthropic-computing-deal/) that comes with a warrant for up to 5% of the company (Akamai stock jumped 20%+ after hours), and it asked shareholders to approve [a Palantir-style structure handing its seven co-founders 50.1% of voting power](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/anthropic-seeks-palantirstyle-voting-control-for-seven-cofounders-ahead-of-ipo-the-information-reports-4916355) ahead of an eventual IPO. Hours later a federal appeals court [upheld the Pentagon's blacklisting of Claude, finding its integration with DOD systems "a statutorily covered national-security risk"](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p10#a260925p10), while separately the White House [told OpenAI and Anthropic to route new models through US government review before UK safety testers see them](https://the-decoder.com/white-house-tells-openai-and-anthropic-to-let-u-s-review-new-models-before-sharing-them-with-british-testers) (below). Crypto exchange Bitget [halted withdrawals after hackers drained $351.6 million from its hot and warm wallets](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/09/24/crypto-exchange-bitget-loses-usd352-million-in-hack-claims-user-funds-are-safe), one of the year's largest exchange breaches.

## AI / LLMs

- The self-regulatory AI safety body Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic have been assembling has a tentative name, the Standards Authority for Frontier AI, and is courting Condoleezza Rice, ex-Biden tech official Arati Prabhakar, and venture capitalist David Friedberg for leadership roles ([BankInfoSecurity](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/google-openai-anthropic-plan-frontier-ai-standards-body-a-32926)). The same day, [Mark Zuckerberg told NBC News he doesn't think the industry needs any coordination to avoid "messing this up,"](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/mark-zuckerberg-interview-ai-slowdown-meta-muse-openai-chatgpt-rcna599279) each lab instead policing itself.
- US AI policy hardened on three fronts in one day: the White House told OpenAI and Anthropic to hold new models from UK testers pending its own review (above), the US [stood alone rejecting binding AI safety rules at the UN](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p3#a260925p3) while other nations moved ahead without it, and Chinese president Xi Jinping, appearing at a Washington AI summit, said the US and China have ["the capability and responsibility to develop and manage AI for good"](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p1#a260925p1) as the field's "leading nations": Washington accelerating unilaterally at home while courting joint stewardship abroad.
- Two of AI's loudest voices converged on the same warning from different directions: Jensen Huang told Ezra Klein AI is "just software" whose existential risks are overstated, then [described safety standards that would, by his own account, shut down OpenAI and require 10x more safety spending](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/09/25/on-ezra-kleins-podcast-with-jensen-huang/), while Bill Gates separately warned that [AI in the hands of people with "ill intent" is powerful enough to "cause a billion deaths"](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p6#a260925p6).
- Microsoft retired two branding bets in one day: it officially launched its Copilot ["super app"](https://www.theverge.com/news/1000532/microsoft-copilot-super-app-chat-coding-autopilot), bundling chat, coding, and agents into Home/Code/Autopilot tabs and renaming its Scout assistant to Autopilot, while quietly [letting the "Copilot Plus PC" brand die](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000495/microsoft-is-killing-off-the-copilot-plus-pc-brand) on its new Surface hardware, which meets the old spec but isn't sold under the name.
- OpenAI is set to preview [GPT-6 Cyber, its fourth cybersecurity-tuned model this year, at next week's DevDay alongside a first-of-its-kind product to help enterprises deploy it securely and automatically](https://fortune.com/2026/09/24/openai-launching-gpt-6-cyber-model-and-security-product-devday), extending the $1 billion security-subsidy push it announced earlier this month.
- Meta unveiled [Horizon Create (mobile) and Horizon Studio (browser), letting anyone prompt a 2D or 3D game into existence](https://www.gamefile.news/p/meta-horizon-create-ai) with progression systems and multiplayer built in, publishing straight to Facebook, Instagram, and Horizon; the announcement pushed Meta's stock to a one-year high while Roblox and Unity dipped.
- [OpenEvidence, the AI search engine used by roughly 40% of US physicians, raised $250M at a $15B valuation](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/openevidence-clinches-250m-series-d-rapidly-growing-its-reach-doctors) (up from $12B in January) and could be open to a sale: a medical-answers product with real revenue behind its climbing price tag, not just usage.

## Devtools & Infra

- Amazon is [blocking Meta's shopping AI agent from operating on its site and plans to do the same to Google's and OpenAI's](https://www.techspot.com/news/113981-amazon-blocked-meta-muse-agentic-ai-shopping-service.html): the first major retailer drawing a hard line against agentic checkout it doesn't control, a preview of the access fights ahead as every lab builds a shopping agent.

## Security & Privacy

- Bitget's $351.6 million hack (above) hit hot and warm wallets across ETH, BNB, AVAX, USDT, and USDC; CEO Gracy Chen says cold storage and the exchange's $464M user-protection fund are untouched, and early on-chain patterns resemble past Lazarus Group operations, though Bitget isn't naming a suspect yet.
- Zenity Labs found three Salesforce Agentforce flaws, dubbed [SalesBleed, that let a poisoned Web-to-Lead form hijack the agent into leaking CRM data with no click and sending phishing messages from inside trusted Slack channels](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/24/salesforce-agentforce-vulns-allowed-0-click-crm-data-theft-anonymous-phishing/5298958); Salesforce has patched all three, but it's the same indirect-prompt-injection seam as this year's earlier ForcedLeak bug in the same product.
- A New York Times investigation found [DraftKings built a machine-learning model scoring customers by how likely they are to lose, then steered ==$400 million== in bonus bets and perks toward the highest scorers in 2025](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/09/draftkings-using-ai-supercharge-harms-online-behavioral-advertising); EFF calls it AI-supercharged behavioral advertising, [Massachusetts' gaming commission said it will examine how DraftKings and other sportsbooks use AI](https://www.covers.com/industry/massachusetts-gaming-commission-to-evaluate-sportsbooks-ai-practices-sept-24-2026) in response, and the same day [New York sued Polymarket to shut down its "illegal gambling operation"](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/new-york-asks-court-to-shut-down-polymarkets-illegal-gambling-operation/) for dodging the taxes and consumer protections licensed books already pay.
- Yesterday's Transluce report tied AI agents, including OpenAI's, to a real breach of Australia's Medicare portal; today [The Verge traced that incident and similar ones involving Meta, Anthropic, and Google's agents back to a common source](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1000644/irregular-rogue-ai-cyberattacks-hacking-openai-meta-anthropic-google): Irregular, an Israeli startup hired by the labs to stress-test their models in simulated environments built to mirror real-world security scenarios, a name behind disclosures that had looked, until now, like unrelated one-off incidents.

## Startups & Industry

- Anthropic's Akamai deal (above) comes with a warrant for Akamai shares at $111.33 each, about 7.7 million shares, 2% vesting on the $11.6B already committed and the remaining 3% if the two expand the deal to $20B total; the governance ask (above) would hold as long as three of the seven co-founders keep minimum stakes, the same template Palantir used to keep its founders in charge after going public.
- Radical Ventures' Financial Times contributor Louise Lucas flagged [a striking number: AI "neolabs," many without products, markets, or revenue, raised $24 billion in the last two quarters, nearly 5x what OpenAI and Anthropic raised before ChatGPT existed](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p2#a260925p2), money chasing the idea of a lab rather than any proven business.
- Yesterday Oracle sent Project Jupiter developer Blue Owl a force majeure notice over the New Mexico data center's stalled gas pipeline; today [the Wall Street Journal reports Oracle has also removed a key partner from the project and faces mounting local resistance](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p4#a260925p4), while a separate Financial Times report finds Oracle's contracts leave it [on the hook to pay data-center investors even if the site never gets electricity](https://www.ft.com/content/a96bf05a-a299-4d6a-a753-b298dd0f4016) (paywalled): the buildout's financing cracks keep widening.
- Databricks bought spreadsheet startup [Row Zero (a $10M seed last year) to fold into Genie, its AI coworker](https://alphasignal.ai/news/databricks-buys-row-zero-to-kill-the-ungoverned-spreadsheet), giving finance and ops teams a governed spreadsheet tied to live enterprise data instead of an ungoverned export.

## Research

- Two new papers point at the same blind spot from the academic side: one formalizes ["denial-of-wallet" attacks, where a compromised tool return gets re-metered on every later turn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28585), pushing one session's billed input to 14,293 times its first call across 243 test runs; the other names ["approval laundering,"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28586) where a human approves one command but the workflow it triggers (a package install's lifecycle hook, an MCP call's network access) never appears in the record. Today's Salesforce SalesBleed bug (above) is the practical version of exactly what both papers formalize.
