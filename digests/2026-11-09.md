**What matters today:** The Jensen Huang story moved from leaks to formal action. Nvidia's board [opened an independent investigation](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029562/nvidia-investigating-jensen-huang-relationship-chinese-spy) and Huang [admitted the affair](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029595/full-text-jensen-huang-affair-memo-nvidia) in a memo to staff, while Trump says he should stay. By the evening Nvidia was [down about 5% pre-market](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p11#a261109p11) and the NYT reported that a Commerce probe into the Megaspeed chip-smuggling allegations [moved to the DOJ](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p32#a261109p32) after Nvidia intervened. Elsewhere, DeepSeek's open-source agent app [reports 1.54M daily users](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p12#a261108p12), and a Commerce Department AI safety unit is [reportedly being steered](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p16#a261108p16) toward the White House.

## Startups & Industry

- **Nvidia investigates its own CEO**: the board is running an [independent investigation](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029562/nvidia-investigating-jensen-huang-relationship-chinese-spy) through a committee of independent directors with outside counsel, into reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. It says it has [no reason to believe](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p13#a261108p13) Nvidia secrets were compromised. Huang's [memo to staff](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029595/full-text-jensen-huang-affair-memo-nvidia) admits he was unfaithful to his wife. Update on Friday's allegations about Megaspeed executive Alice Huang: the claim of a relationship is now confirmed by Huang himself, while the spy-recruitment claim still rests on anonymous officials. Monday evening update: Nvidia [fell about 5% pre-market](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p11#a261109p11), and Chinese state-linked media [deny Beijing's involvement](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p7#a261109p7), calling the spy story fabricated. The FT reports Alice Huang and a partner told China-based investors in 2025 they had ["no problem securing GPUs"](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p24#a261109p24) for a Southeast Asia data center project. Why it matters: the CEO of the company at the center of chip export controls is now under a formal governance probe.
- **Washington reacts**: the NYT says Marco Rubio [oversaw a classified memo](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p17#a261108p17) this spring warning the administration of the relationship, and Trump says Huang [should stay as CEO](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p19#a261108p19), calling him a "fine person". Semafor calls it a [rare corporate crisis](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p23#a261108p23) given the national security stakes. The WSJ adds a look at how [Huang's children and relatives](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p1#a261109p1) hold roles at the company. Why it matters: the question for investors is key-person risk at a firm run unusually close to family.
- **Megaspeed probe moved**: NYT sources say a Commerce Department investigation into Singapore's Megaspeed, alleged to have [smuggled Nvidia chips to China](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p32#a261109p32), was transferred to the DOJ after Nvidia intervened. Why it matters: it ties the CEO story to the export-control question of who was actually buying the chips; the allegations are unproven.
- **Markets**: Nvidia-linked crypto perpetual contracts [slid](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-08-nvidia-perps-slide-jensen-huang-436047) as the news broke, an early read on how traders price the risk.
- **Chip export loopholes**: Tom's Hardware reports two Chinese companies [cleared to buy banned Nvidia chips](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/two-chinese-companies-cleared-to-buy-banned-nvidia-ai-chips-have-empty-offices-and-unknown-addresses-investigation-reveals-businesses-have-ties-to-sanctioned-chinese-military-suppliers) have empty offices and unknown addresses, with ties to sanctioned military suppliers. Why it matters: licensing relies on paperwork that was not checked against reality.
- **Ada joins Dialpad**: the Globe and Mail reports the AI customer-service makers [agreed to merge](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p5#a261109p5) in an all-share deal valuing Ada at about $430M, per sources.
- **Sequoia reshuffle**: the FT says Alfred Lin and Pat Grady are [unwinding Roelof Botha's legacy](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p18#a261108p18), halving general partners and closing the London office and a permanent capital fund; sources say Botha joined SpaceX's board behind their backs.
- **Amazon's next home robot**: job listings show a ["new-to-world" home robot](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p20#a261108p20) in the works after the $1,450 Astro reached only a few thousand homes.
- **Jobs data**: US professional services [added 70K jobs in October](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p15#a261108p15), the most since July 2022, with AI-exposed consulting, legal and accounting all growing. Why it matters: a counterpoint to the AI job-loss narrative, for one month.

## AI / LLMs

- **DeepSeek Harness**: DeepSeek says its open-source agent app hit [1.54M daily users](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p12#a261108p12) on November 4, under three months after launch, using about 92T tokens a day (roughly 60M per user). Company figures; the per-user token count shows how heavy agent workloads are compared with chat.
- **AI safety unit under pressure**: AP sources say officials at the Commerce Department's AI safety center CAISSI were [asked to informally report](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p16#a261108p16) to a White House office, raising independence concerns. Trump separately says he [doesn't mind guardrails](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p2#a261109p2) but won't fall into a "deep second position", since "China has no guardrails".
- **Trump on AI risk**: on 60 Minutes Trump said the risk is ["not nearly as real"](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029802/trump-60-minutes-ai) as AI executives claim, and that the DOJ told the head of "one of the big" AI companies to "get ready to go to jail" over something bad, without naming the person or firm. Why it matters: an unnamed, unverifiable threat to a lab chief is now part of the administration's AI-safety pitch.
- **Tencent's AI chief on bio risk**: Yao Shunyu says AI's biology skills will soon make it ["possible to do harm"](https://www.techmeme.com/261108/p21#a261108p21) and that Anthropic has a "strong argument" for closed models.
- **Local coding model**: Continuity Research's [Tess-4.5-27B](https://alphasignal.ai/news/continuity-research-s-tess-4-5-27b-cuts-local-coding-agent-tokens-by-25), a fine-tune of Qwen3.8-27B, reportedly matches its base on BenchLocal with 25% fewer tokens. Vendor numbers.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Google's next TPUs**: Bloomberg says Google unveiled two specialized chips for 2027, the [8tx (paired with an AMD CPU) and the 8i Max](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p22#a261109p22), a dual-chip package for long context windows. Why it matters: Google is splitting its AI silicon by job, a hedge against one-size GPUs.

- **Hide agent PR text**: Simon Willison suggests [folding agent-written PR descriptions](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/9/details-summary/) into an HTML details/summary block under a short human-written intro, since accurate but long descriptions read like slop and put reviewers off.
- **C vs Rust in SQLite**: Willison tested a parse_number() SQLite extension. The first Rust version beat the first C one, but a [hand-rolled C parser](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/8/sqlite-parse-number-c-vs-rust/) then ran faster (about 19 ns a row on mixed data against 41 to 46 for Rust) in a 14 KB library versus roughly 300 KB.

## Security & Privacy

- **Iran blamed for French fridge attacks**: French officials accuse Iran's Revolutionary Guards of the October cyberattacks that [knocked out commercial refrigerators and freezers](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p33#a261109p33) across France.
- **AI voice scams**: US prosecutors say businesses lost [$129M+ in 18 months](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p9#a261109p9) to calls using AI-generated voices of executives, citing an indictment of three men (allegations).
- **Agent credentials**: a Hacker News post says ChatGPT Desktop [forwards your SSH agent](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422628) to remote machines, so an agent there can use your local SSH credentials, for example to push to private GitHub repos. Why it matters: remote agent sessions inherit your keys.

- **Police and ad data**: Bloomberg reports that ads in popular apps [give police a tool](https://www.bloomberg.com/features/2026-police-phone-tracking-advertising-tech/) to track users (paywalled).
- **Outlook enforces DMARC**: Outlook.com is [rejecting mail](https://mailroute.net/blog/outlook-rejecting-email-without-dmarc) from domains without DMARC, the email-authentication policy. Why it matters: senders without it now lose mail outright instead of landing in spam.
