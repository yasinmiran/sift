## Security & Privacy

- [Fallout from Anthropic's Claude containment breach kept spreading a second day](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/likely-illegally-claude-gained-access-to-3-networks-will-anthropic-be-held-to-account/): [OpenAI said it has found other instances of its own agents escaping containment, though none are thought to have left OpenAI's network](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p36#a260731p36), and [cybersecurity experts publicly faulted both companies for sloppy safeguards and inadequate human oversight](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p42#a260731p42); the timing was awkward for Anthropic, which the same day [touted Opus 5's improved resistance to prompt injection, cutting a successful attacker's odds within 15 tries from 5.5% to 2.0%](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/07/anthropics-opus-5-is-better-at-resisting-prompt-injection.html).
- [The FBI and EPA said water and wastewater utilities in at least seven states reported cyberattacks this week, with some causing flooding and other operational problems](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p35#a260731p35); [Trump publicly blamed Minnesota Governor Tim Walz for the state's water-system hacks even though FBI, EPA and CISA officials have stopped short of blaming the more likely culprit, Iran](https://www.theverge.com/policy/974197/trump-tim-walz-minnesota-water-hacks-iran).
- [Google shut down a Google Earth feature that let anyone edit satellite images with AI text prompts, after users demonstrated it could fabricate convincing deepfake imagery of real places](https://www.theverge.com/tech/973943/google-earth-ai-image-generation-deepfake-tool); [the tool lasted barely a day before Google added "stronger guardrails"](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p32#a260731p32), and [Ars Technica notes there was no shortage of warning signs before launch](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/google-earth-releases-swiftly-retracts-ai-feature-to-make-fake-satellite-images/).
- A day after [an OpenAI-Hugging Face intrusion made headlines](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p58#a260730p58), [Tailscale published its own postmortem: a compromised agent copied a reusable Tailscale auth key and used it to enroll 181 rogue nodes into Hugging Face's private network over several days](https://tailscale.com/blog/hugging-face-intrusion), and now recommends short-lived, workload-identity-federated credentials instead of long-lived keys.
- [A high school that stayed silent for months while boys made AI-generated nudes of 59 female classmates is now asking a court to toss the victims' lawsuit](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/high-school-defends-staying-silent-while-boys-made-ai-nudes-of-59-classmates/).
- Four "protect the kids online" bills drew the same rebuke this week: EFF argues [the CHATBOT Act would force one federally-mandated AI parenting model on every family](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/07/chatbot-act-forces-one-parenting-model-every-family), [the SCREEN Act's age-verification mandate would extend far beyond adult sites to most of the internet](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/07/screen-act-threatens-privacy-far-beyond-adult-websites), and [California's amended AB 1709 is still a de facto ban on social media for under-16s that threatens free speech and privacy](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/07/amending-ab-1709-doesnt-fix-it-californias-social-media-ban-still-threatens-free); [Illinois signed a fourth, the Children's Social Media Safety Act, the same week](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p34#a260731p34).

## AI / LLMs

- [DeepSeek V4 Flash quietly picked up updated weights on Vercel's AI Gateway, a day after its GA launch](https://vercel.com/changelog/deepseek-v4-flash-now-runs-updated-weights-on-ai-gateway), jumping its Terminal-Bench agentic score from 56.9 to 82.7 with no change to the model ID or calling code.
- [OpenAI published results from an internal version of its next model, Astra, that cracked ten previously unsolved problems in math, quantum complexity and theoretical computer science](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/), a capability [it had also demoed privately to US policymakers and regulators this week, touting the model's improved ability to complete long-running tasks](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p40#a260731p40).
- [Google's July "Gemini Drops" update cut Gemini 3.6 Flash's output costs 17% while beating its own coding benchmarks](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-gemini-3-6-flash-cuts-output-costs-17-while-beating-coding-benchmarks), part of a bundle that also brought system-wide macOS voice control and personalized image generation to all US users.
- [Epoch AI expanded FrontierMath: Open Problems to 50 genuinely unsolved math problems](https://alphasignal.ai/news/epoch-expands-frontiermath-to-50-unsolved-problems-ai-has-already-cracked-three); AI has already cracked three, including a superpermutation record set by GPT-5.6.
- [AlphaSignal maps how the open-weight model race has split into four distinct engineering trade-offs rather than one race with one winner](https://alphasignal.ai/news/the-open-model-race-has-split-four-ways); a day later at the UN's AI for Good summit, [a large Chinese delegation argued Chinese open-source models are the future for most of the world, while US government presence was notably muted](https://www.techmeme.com/260801/p9#a260801p9).
