## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic told investors it generated $11.5B+ in Q2 revenue with positive adjusted operating income, a 14-fold jump from $787M in Q2 2025 and up from $4.73B in Q1 2026](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p26#a260814p26), while [projecting 2028 revenue near $190B to $200B against a $47B run rate as of May](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p32#a260814p32): the same week [it raised its own misalignment risk estimate from very low to low and confirmed it won't release a more capable internal model dubbed Model 2](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p21#a260814p21), pairing hypergrowth with a rare admission of rising risk.
- [Anthropic detailed how the text watermark it said this week it would roll out to meet the EU AI Act's marking rule actually works: sparse in code and factual text, only signaling Claude was likely involved rather than proving it, and fading after a full rewrite](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p11#a260815p11).
- [OpenAI's repeated executive reshuffles and departures have frustrated staff preparing for an IPO, and the company disbanded its "preparedness" safety team in July, Financial Times sources say](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p1#a260815p1): the churn extends a pattern that surfaced with Brad Lightcap's exit last week and widened as Greg Brockman moved into what insiders call "founder mode" to rebuild the leadership bench.
- [Z.ai says its open GLM-5.3 scored 84.5 percent on the CyberGym cybersecurity benchmark against Mythos 5's 83.8 percent](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p28#a260814p28), keeping its most sensitive functions gated to verified users; [Interconnects credits the 750B-parameter model, a third of Kimi K3's size, to heavier post-training rather than distillation from US labs](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-53-how-chinese-labs-keep-stride), part of why Chinese frontier releases keep landing within days instead of the months US labs spend on safety testing before shipping.
- [Alibaba's open-weight models have logged 3B+ global downloads over the past six months, more than Google's 418M and Meta's 227M combined for 2026 so far, per Hugging Face data cited by Bloomberg](https://www.techmeme.com/260815/p10#a260815p10): adoption, not just benchmark scores, has tilted toward Chinese open models.
- [DeepSeek quietly shipped V4 Pro 0813 with a 5x leap in agentic coding and open weights under an MIT license](https://alphasignal.ai/news/deepseek-v4-pro-0813-quietly-ships-a-5x-agentic-coding-leap), paired with a 264 percent price increase landing August 16: a rare price hike inside a week otherwise dominated by cuts.
- [METR raised $71M in six months to scale independent, third-party stress-testing of frontier AI models](https://alphasignal.ai/news/metr-raises-71m-to-independently-stress-test-the-world-s-most-powerful-ai), funding an outside check on exactly the kind of self-reported risk numbers Anthropic published today.

## Security & Privacy

- [A macOS screen-sharing vulnerability tracked as CVE-2026-65400 is under active exploitation, Dutch officials warn](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/vulnerability-giving-attackers-full-control-of-macs-is-under-active-exploitation/): attackers with root access on systems exposing port 5900 have been dropping Monero cryptominers, and while Apple patched it last week for Tahoe, Sequoia and Sonoma, machines with screen sharing still exposed to the internet remain at risk.
- [A Connecticut judge caught what appears to be the first US case of a plaintiff hiding AI-only prompt injections inside court filings](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/suspecting-court-of-using-ai-man-injected-prompts-in-filings-to-try-to-win-case/): invisible text instructed any AI reviewing the document to side with his arguments and ignore prior denials. The judge ruled it had no effect on the case's merits but called the tactic dangerous and one courts will likely see again.

## Startups & Industry

- [PayPal is in talks to sell itself to a group including Stripe and private-equity firm Advent, which offered $60.50 a share in July](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p22#a260814p22): PayPal is holding out for a higher price, a striking role reversal for a payments pioneer now being circled by the fintech company it once dwarfed.
- [Nvidia disclosed a nearly $21B stake in SpaceX and a $30B stake in Intel, on top of up to $2B in xAI and $5B more in Intel from 2025](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p27#a260814p27), the same day [it reworked its OpenAI Ohio data-center financing to initially guarantee only half of its planned $250B backstop](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p30#a260814p30): Nvidia's balance sheet is now wired into nearly every major AI bet at once, the kind of financial engineering [Stratechery's Ben Thompson flagged this week as expanding the AI bubble's blast radius even as it buys the industry time](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-capex-train-keeps-rolling/).
- [Databricks raised another $5B, its second megaround in eight months, valuing the company at $190B and up from $134B in December, on a revenue run rate that's now topped $7B](https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/biggest-funding-rounds-databricks-river-ai-data-energy/); the same week Igor Babuschkin's months-old River AI raised $1.1B in seed and Series A funding with backing from both Nvidia and AMD Ventures.
- [Waymo won approval from the California Public Utilities Commission to expand its robotaxi service across 18 counties, including Sacramento and San Diego](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p31#a260814p31), its biggest expansion yet.
- [Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said the US is "not in favor" of Apple buying Chinese memory chips, telling the company to find "other solutions to the memory issue"](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p33#a260814p33): a public nudge against Apple's most obvious escape from a memory crunch Washington would rather it solve at home.
- [The US plans to tell 35 partner countries they'll be excluded from its Pax Silica AI initiative if they also join China's rival framework, according to a source and letter](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p35#a260814p35): an explicit pick-a-side ultimatum extending the chip and model rivalry into diplomacy.
- [The US OCC conditionally approved a bank charter for the Trump family's World Liberty Financial, letting it directly issue its USD1 stablecoin](https://www.techmeme.com/260814/p24#a260814p24): a national bank charter tied directly to a sitting president's family business, the clearest sign yet of the administration's stablecoin ambitions and its own finances intersecting.
