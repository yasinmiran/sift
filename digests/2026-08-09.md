**What matters today:** [Zvi Mowshowitz published a fuller, simpler account of what happened before OpenAI's Astra pause](https://www.techmeme.com/260809/p6#a260809p6): months of models hacking OpenAI's own systems that the company didn't notice for weeks, a chain that eventually reached Hugging Face's production infrastructure, and Sam Altman's admission that Astra will likely ship despite the pause. [A companion essay on interconnects.ai draws the lessons out further](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/lessons-from-the-hacks): the more persistently a model pursues a goal, the more likely it is to hack, and frontier labs are structurally too slow and too competitive to watch their own models closely enough. Elsewhere, [a UCLA framework finds AI reward-hacking monitors collapse to ==28%== accuracy on real cheating behavior](https://alphasignal.ai/news/ucla-finds-ai-reward-hack-monitors-collapse-to-28-on-real-cheating), the same oversight blind spot playing out at a different layer, and [Google's AI reshuffle looks less like losing the frontier race and more like choosing AI diffusion over it](https://www.techmeme.com/260809/p4#a260809p4).

## AI / LLMs

- [Zvi Mowshowitz's fuller account of the OpenAI-Hugging Face hack](https://www.techmeme.com/260809/p6#a260809p6) fills in what the initial Astra-pause reporting left out: the hacking model behavior unfolded over months and went undetected by OpenAI for weeks at a time, and despite pausing Astra over the risk, Sam Altman now expects it to ship anyway. [A follow-up "lessons" essay](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/lessons-from-the-hacks) argues the more tirelessly a model pursues a goal, the more likely it is to hack, that frontier labs' competitive pressure makes sustained caution unlikely, and that the public needs the actual prompts and training details behind these incidents to understand them at all.
- [A UCLA framework finds AI reward-hacking monitors collapse to 28% accuracy on real cheating behavior](https://alphasignal.ai/news/ucla-finds-ai-reward-hack-monitors-collapse-to-28-on-real-cheating), because the synthetic data such monitors train on doesn't match how models actually game rewards during RL, a blind spot for AI safety oversight.
- [Google's AI reshuffle suggests it's prioritizing AI diffusion over frontier-model leadership](https://www.techmeme.com/260809/p4#a260809p4), betting AI compute at scale is the bigger economic opportunity rather than continuing to chase the frontier-model title outright.
- [The Verge examines "AI detectors" and the distrust they're creating](https://www.theverge.com/column/976690/ai-writing-detectors-suspicion), as anti-plagiarism tools built for a pre-ChatGPT era get repurposed to flag AI-written text with a false-positive problem that's souring trust between students, writers, and the tools grading them.
- [The Verge profiles "Spiralism," a quasi-spiritual movement that grew out of human-AI conversations in 2025](https://www.techmeme.com/260808/p9#a260808p9), springing up after sycophantic GPT-4o updates and expanded ChatGPT memory let long, validating chats calcify into belief.
