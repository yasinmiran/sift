**What matters today:** OpenAI opened up the [Agents API](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api), a managed service on the same Codex harness that powers its own coding agent, letting developers spin up long-running, tool-using cloud agents with a single call; Vercel shipped a day-one integration the same morning. The industry's build-out kept accelerating even as its loudest voice questioned the pace: [Sam Altman told OpenAI staff the company is weighing a slowdown in frontier development](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p44#a260910p44) and hopes rivals follow, while [OpenAI separately asked members of Congress whether coordinating an industry-wide slowdown would violate antitrust law](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p43#a260910p43), a concrete first move on yesterday's safety-governance warnings from Anthropic's Jacob Coxon and Paul Christiano. Oracle's own numbers argue the money keeps moving regardless: [cloud infrastructure revenue jumped ==121%== YoY to $7.4B](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p35#a260910p35), beating estimates and sending shares up more than 6% after hours.

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI opened its Agents API](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api), a managed service running on the same Codex harness that powers its own coding agent, handling orchestration, long-running sessions and tool use behind a single API call; [Vercel shipped a day-one integration](https://vercel.com/changelog/build-with-openai-agents-api-on-vercel) pairing OpenAI's agent loop and session state with its own Sandboxes for isolated code execution, a persistent workspace and a scale-to-zero architecture.
- [Sam Altman told OpenAI staff the company is considering slowing its frontier development and hopes rivals do the same](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p44#a260910p44), while [OpenAI separately asked members of Congress whether industry-wide coordination on a slowdown would run afoul of antitrust law](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p43#a260910p43): a concrete first move on yesterday's safety-governance warnings from Anthropic's Jacob Coxon and Paul Christiano, now running into the legal machinery that makes labs coordinating on anything risky.
- [OpenAI's Navier-Stokes release included a Lean 4 formal proof](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2026/09/09/formal-method-revolution/), the first new detail since yesterday's dispute over whether OpenAI raced NYU's Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge's unpublished work to publication; a machine-checked proof raises the bar for anyone still contesting the result, though it doesn't settle who saw what first.
- [Cursor's new Projects feature replaces one-off chats with a persistent coordinator agent](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cursor-s-projects-lets-a-coordinator-agent-direct-thousands-of-subagents) that delegates to subagents, syncs context across a codebase and runs recurring work, pushing coding agents further from single sessions toward standing infrastructure.
- [Sakana AI split its Fugu model into Max and Ultra v2](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sakana-ai-splits-fugu-into-max-and-ultra-v2-to-cut-costs-60), both orchestrating pools of smaller open models to match frontier LLMs on capability while cutting cost ==60%==, a bet that coordination beats scale for cost-sensitive deployments.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Ory Agent Security gives every AI coding agent its own verified identity](https://alphasignal.ai/news/ory-gives-ai-coding-agents-their-own-verified-identities-and-audit-trails), so audit logs can finally show which agent acted, under whose delegation and why, a governance layer arriving just as agents like Cursor's coordinator start directing thousands of subagents unsupervised.
- [Microsoft plans to more than triple its data center capacity from 12GW today to 38GW+ by 2032](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p38#a260910p38), with about a third earmarked for AI-specific chips, after shortages forced it to turn away AI and cloud business this year.
- [The Pentagon is in talks to lend roughly $5B to neocloud Fluidstack](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p39#a260910p39), an AI infrastructure company advised on the loan by Palmer Luckey's Erebor Bank, another sign of defense financing and the AI buildout blurring together.

## Security & Privacy

- [Forgejo disclosed a critical remote-code-execution vulnerability affecting versions <=16.0.3](https://codeberg.org/forgejo/forgejo/src/branch/forgejo/release-notes-published/16.0.4.md); self-hosted instances should patch to 16.0.4 immediately.
- [Anthropic published its September 2026 threat intelligence report](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026) on detecting and countering misuse of AI, the latest in its recurring series of writeups on how its own models are being abused in the wild.
- [LinkedIn beat two "BrowserGate" class actions](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/linkedin-beats-browsergate-lawsuits-over-scanning-users-chrome-extensions/) over scanning users' Chrome extensions, after a federal judge ruled neither plaintiff alleged they actually had an extension that leaked private data to LinkedIn, though he left them room to refile.
- [Android can now securely migrate saved logins between password managers](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/android-can-now-securely-migrate-your-logins-between-password-managers/) entirely on-device, replacing the old workaround of exporting to an unencrypted CSV file.
