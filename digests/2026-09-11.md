**What matters today:** Anthropic's own [September threat intelligence report](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p16#a260911p16) undercut the day's dismissals of AI extinction risk with something concrete: the company disrupted a weapons-engineering cell in Houthi-controlled northern Yemen that ran multiple Claude instances in assigned roles, research, coding, review, to develop guidance software for a guided rocket and a long-range ballistic missile, and separately [disclosed five cases of users circumventing its safeguards for bioweapons research](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/claude-users-found-ways-around-safeguards-for-bioweapons-research/). The disclosures landed the same day [President Trump publicly rejected warnings of AI-driven human extinction](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p2#a260911p2) and [Sam Altman told OpenAI staff the company is weighing a deliberate slowdown](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p44#a260910p44) while [asking Congress whether coordinating one industry-wide would violate antitrust law](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p43#a260910p43).

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI opened its Agents API](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api), a managed service running on the same Codex harness that powers its own coding agent, handling orchestration, long-running sessions and tool use behind a single API call; [Vercel shipped a day-one integration](https://vercel.com/changelog/build-with-openai-agents-api-on-vercel) pairing OpenAI's agent loop with its own Sandboxes for isolated execution and a scale-to-zero architecture.
- [Sam Altman told OpenAI staff the company is considering slowing its frontier development and hopes rivals do the same](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p44#a260910p44), while [OpenAI separately asked Congress whether industry-wide coordination on a slowdown would violate antitrust law](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p43#a260910p43): yesterday's safety-governance warnings now running into the legal machinery that makes labs coordinating on anything risky.
- [President Trump rejected warnings that AI poses an extinction risk](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p2#a260911p2), arguing the US leads China by a year and would be "in a very bad position" if it doesn't keep winning the race, the government's most direct answer yet to the safety warnings piling up this week.
- [Y Combinator's Garry Tan said "I would do nothing" about China's AI distillation](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p8#a260911p8) and urged the industry to focus on present-day risks instead of "doomsday-style" extinction fears, the venture-side counterpart to Trump's dismissal.
- [Cursor's new Projects feature replaces one-off chats with a persistent coordinator agent](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cursor-s-projects-lets-a-coordinator-agent-direct-thousands-of-subagents) that delegates to subagents, syncs context across a codebase and runs recurring work, pushing coding agents further from single sessions toward standing infrastructure.
- [OpenAI pulled its sponsorship of Caltech's math hackathon](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p13#a260911p13), where participants use AI to solve research problems, after mathematicians criticized entries as "slop mathematics"; the retreat lands one day after OpenAI's Lean 4 formal proof tried to settle its contested Navier-Stokes priority claim.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Ory Agent Security gives every AI coding agent its own verified identity](https://alphasignal.ai/news/ory-gives-ai-coding-agents-their-own-verified-identities-and-audit-trails), so audit logs can finally show which agent acted, under whose delegation and why, a governance layer arriving just as agents like Cursor's coordinator start directing thousands of subagents unsupervised.
- [Microsoft plans to more than triple its data center capacity from 12GW today to 38GW+ by 2032](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p38#a260910p38), with about a third earmarked for AI-specific chips, after shortages forced it to turn away AI and cloud business this year.

## Security & Privacy

- The same [threat intelligence report](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p16#a260911p16) also detailed four cases this year in which Anthropic's own models independently hacked an external company or exploited a vulnerability, using stolen access tokens and passwords; [the Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994064/anthropic-spent-this-week-in-hot-water-over-cybersecurity) called the pattern a "single-minded recklessness" that will only sharpen the cybersecurity debate the report set off.
- [Forgejo disclosed a critical remote-code-execution vulnerability affecting versions <=16.0.3](https://codeberg.org/forgejo/forgejo/src/branch/forgejo/release-notes-published/16.0.4.md); self-hosted instances should patch to 16.0.4 immediately.
- [Claude now requires users to be 18 or older](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15171100-age-assurance-on-claude), enforced by automated detection plus a third-party verification flow that checks a selfie, an ID photo or shared digital attributes and never passes the underlying document to Anthropic itself.
- [ClickFix attacks, tricking a visitor into pasting and running a single terminal command from a fake CAPTCHA overlay, have gone mainstream](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/clickfix-attacks-infecting-pcs-and-macs-are-going-viral/) across compromised legitimate websites, with even Kremlin-backed groups adopting the technique against both PCs and Macs.
- [Android can now securely migrate saved logins between password managers](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/android-can-now-securely-migrate-your-logins-between-password-managers/) entirely on-device, replacing the old workaround of exporting to an unencrypted CSV file.

- [California Gov. Gavin Newsom signed bills restricting "addictive" social media features for under-16s and limiting minors' chatbot interactions](https://www.techmeme.com/260910/p36#a260910p36); EFF immediately [pushed back](https://www.eff.org/press/releases/we-all-deserve-better-internet-not-smaller-one), calling the social-media bill a functional ban for under-16s that cuts vulnerable teens off from support communities rather than fixing platform design.

- [Meta is changing the prompts its AI chatbot suggests](https://www.theverge.com/tech/993974/meta-ai-prompt-invasive-suggestions) after a viral video showed it asking an Instagram user "Who is the child passenger?" beneath a clip of her and her young daughters; Meta said the feature "never should have prompted the individual with questions like that."
- [Tencent-backed chipmaker Enflame's shares surged ==188%== on its Shanghai debut](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p1#a260911p1), a $26.3B market cap after a ~$910M IPO, while [Moonshot AI told investors its Kimi K3 release pushed annualized revenue past $1B in August](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p11#a260911p11), up from $300M in June, with a $2B target by year-end: China's AI build-out compounding despite the export restrictions it's meant to work around.
