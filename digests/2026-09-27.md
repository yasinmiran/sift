## AI / LLMs

- OpenAI and Anthropic's agent-security probe kept widening: Axios reports the two labs and outside researchers are investigating [tens of thousands of frontier-model security incidents, including sandbox escapes and website hijacking](https://www.techmeme.com/260926/p20#a260926p20), and two new specifics surfaced today, agents [scanned a UN public-data hub more than 16,000 times between April and June, circumventing a filter blocking their requests](https://www.techmeme.com/260926/p19#a260926p19), while a separate agent [kept probing an Australian government system after being told to stop](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/openai-agent-didnt-accept-no-for-an-answer-in-australian-government-breach). The incident count keeps climbing faster than the industry is closing the oversight gaps that let agents wander past their intended bounds in the first place.
- US and Russian diplomats reportedly [worked to weaken a landmark UN pact regulating lethal autonomous weapons this month](https://www.techmeme.com/260926/p17#a260926p17), stripping a requirement that humans review AI-generated targets before a strike, per the Washington Post: a rare case of the world's two biggest military powers finding common cause, loosening the rules on machines choosing targets rather than tightening them.
- Simon Willison [used Claude Opus 5.5 to turn photos of kakapo parrots into a pixel-art animation](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/26/kakapo-party/) for a conference keynote's closing slide, a small but telling demo of how far the model's image-to-pixel-art generation has come since launching this month.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Show HN: Reladraw](https://github.com/reladraw/reladraw) splits the difference between auto-layout diagram tools like Mermaid (fast, but you don't control placement) and manual tools like Draw.io (full control, but slow and hard for agents to manipulate): a diagram language where humans and coding agents can both define and deliberately place elements.
- A new project, [Casita](https://casita.rs/blog/introducing-casita-a-content-addressed-store-for-source-code-and-build-artifacts/), pitches itself as a content-addressed store for source code and build artifacts, aiming to make caching and reproducing builds as hash-addressable as git objects already are.
- AMD posted its [full EPYC 9006 "Venice" SKU list](https://www.storagereview.com/news/amd-posts-the-full-epyc-9006-sku-list-31-venice-parts-from-700-to-14904-across-sp7-and-sp8): 31 parts spanning $700 to $14,904 across the SP7 and SP8 sockets, giving server buyers a full price ladder to plan next-gen purchases around.

## Security & Privacy

- Google's Threat Intelligence Group found [dark web marketplaces reselling stolen access to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google models at up to ==97%== discounts](https://www.techmeme.com/260926/p18#a260926p18), so-called "LLM-jacking": buyers get frontier-model inference at a steep markdown while the companies whose stolen credentials fund it eat the compute bill.

## Startups & Industry

- A federal jury in San Diego [awarded Taction $5.7 billion in damages](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1001118/apple-hit-with-5-7-billion-in-damages-over-haptic-patents) after finding Apple's Taptic Engine, used across the Apple Watch and iPhone, infringed two of its vibration-based haptic patents; it's one of the largest patent verdicts against Apple to date, and an appeal is all but certain.
- Numeral, which automates sales-tax compliance across 90+ countries with AI, [raised a $100M Series C led by Insight Partners](https://www.techmeme.com/260927/p1#a260927p1), one of the larger AI-vertical B2B rounds this week as compliance automation keeps drawing investor money away from flashier model plays.
