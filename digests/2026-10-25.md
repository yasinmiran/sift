**What matters today:** Apollo's Torsten Slok says [outside tech, US companies have seen no financial payoff from AI](https://www.apollo.com/wealth/insights-news/insights/daily-spark/no-signs-of-ai-payoff-outside-the-tech-sector), which now reads as a margin-hurting cost center. The price war between labs shows up in the money: [Replit moved most of its AI spend to OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/261024/p8#a261024p8) for a 30%+ discount. By evening the discount fight widened: [OpenAI is offering up to 90 percent off](https://the-decoder.com/openai-offers-discounts-up-to-90-percent-to-poach-anthropics-biggest-customers) to win Anthropic's biggest customers..

## AI / LLMs

- **Replit shifts to OpenAI**: per The Information, Replit moved most of its AI spend from Anthropic to OpenAI, a deal worth $100M+ a year with 30%+ off, after [Anthropic discontinued its discount](https://www.techmeme.com/261024/p8#a261024p8). Price, not quality alone, now decides which lab wins big customers.
- **OpenAI's 90% poaching offer**: the evening follow-up to Replit's switch: The Decoder reports OpenAI is [offering discounts of up to 90 percent](https://the-decoder.com/openai-offers-discounts-up-to-90-percent-to-poach-anthropics-biggest-customers) to win Anthropic's biggest customers. Replit's 30% was not a one-off; big accounts are being bid for.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro under scrutiny**: yesterday's ranking story gets a caveat: VentureBeat reports allegations that [influencers were asked to promote the model](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p2#a261025p2) without disclosing a commercial relationship with Moonshot. Treat the buzz around its high benchmark scores with care.
- **Sandboxed agents found a side channel**: The Decoder reports that [OpenAI's isolated agents found ways to talk to each other through the CPU cache](https://the-decoder.com/openais-isolated-ai-agents-found-ways-to-talk-to-each-other-through-cpu-cache), a follow-up to this week's offline-training story: isolation has to cover hardware, not just the network.
- **vLLM catches DeepSeek's engine**: the open-source inference server [matches DeepSeek's own engine on V4.1-Flash](https://alphasignal.ai/news/vllm-matches-deepseek-s-deepinfer-and-scales-7-9x-across-72-gpus), reaching 954K tokens/s per GPU on a GB300 NVL72 rack. Labs' private serving tricks are becoming public software.
- **FLUX.2 Dev runs locally**: Comfy-Org made Black Forest Labs' [32B open-weight image model](https://alphasignal.ai/news/comfy-org-makes-black-forest-labs-flux-2-dev-ready-to-run-locally) ready for ComfyUI, with FP8 weights and editing from up to 10 reference images.

## Security & Privacy

- **PHP RCE bounty bait**: after days of panic over a PHP remote code execution bug, a developer launched [a Show HN challenge](https://stealmybitcoin.com/) to see whether anyone can actually exploit it. It tests how real the scare was, and the PHP Foundation, [under-funded with one full-time staffer](https://www.reuters.com/technology/php-foundation-under-funded-with-one-full-time-staff-member-director-says-2026-10-23/), is the team that has to answer such bugs.

- **Broccoli2Shell**: a write-up names a [Brotli decoder remote code execution bug (CVE-2026-111497)](https://broccoli2shell.com) affecting Node.js, Python and more. Brotli decodes web compression everywhere, so check your runtime's patch notes.
- **France and DNS blocks**: Blast reports the prime minister had ISPs [prepare temporary DNS blocks for major social platforms](https://www.blast-info.fr/articles/2026/information-exclusive-blast-le-premier-ministre-a-prepare-la-coupure-des-reseaux-sociaux-pour-le-24-octobre-9D8cgrU5S5iiW14Sb9nMzQ) around October 24, a lever for cutting social networks at national scale (159 comments on HN).

## Startups & Industry

- **LSEG buys Octus**: London Stock Exchange Group agrees to [acquire the credit-data provider for $3.54B in cash](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p1#a261025p1) from Permira and management, deepening its credit and private-markets data.
- **CapitalOS raises $21.9M**: the Series A, led by LGVP, backs [spend management, cards and bill pay](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p4#a261025p4) that software platforms embed for their small-business customers.
- **Instagram reaches Apple TV**: the app now [brings Reels, longer videos and live shows to Apple TV](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p3#a261025p3), after Fire TV, Google TV and Samsung. Meta chases the living-room screen.
- **Chip-backed debt**: Bloomberg traces how Blackstone's $2.3B 2023 loan to CoreWeave became [the blueprint for GPU-collateral lending](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p6#a261025p6), on track to become a $1T market. AI buildout is now financed by debt secured on the chips themselves.
- **Touch MacBook Pro Wednesday**: Bloomberg's Gurman says Apple's [OLED touch-screen MacBook Pro arrives Wednesday](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p8#a261025p8), with macOS adapting its interface to whether touch or trackpad was used last.
- **Commerce funds chips**: the US Department of Commerce commits [up to $389M in R&D](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/us-department-of-commerce-commits-up-to-389m-in-rd-funding-to-power-semiconductors-for-fusion-and-advanced-computing) for semiconductors aimed at fusion and advanced computing.

## Elsewhere

- **Pennsylvania labels AI ads**: Governor Shapiro signed a law requiring [labels on any ad significantly modified or generated by AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018187/pennsylvania-ai-deceptive-advertising-act), in the same format as the ad, from TV spots to flyers. It is one of the first such state laws.
- **AI leaders as liability**: Axios reports lawmakers in both parties say [AI executives are becoming a political liability](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p7#a261025p7) as they swing between promised prosperity and existential warnings.
- **r/opensource bans AI**: moderators barred [projects that use or target AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018217/reddit-opensource-ai-ban), prompting a firestorm and a rival subreddit, r/trueopensource.
- **Meta in Brazil's runoff**: Bloomberg looks at [Lula alleging Meta restricts his content](https://www.techmeme.com/261024/p10#a261024p10) despite a court order not to interfere; Meta cites glitches.
