**What matters today:** [Sam Altman told Time OpenAI's decision to slow AI development came from a run of research showing "various degrees of misalignment"](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p33#a260818p33), a rationale OpenAI backed the same day with [a blog post on new frontier-model safeguards](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities) and [security fixes following July's AI-caused Hugging Face breach](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981640/openai-security-changes-ai-hugging-face-hack): a two-week pause on reinforcement-learning training, a held-back model called Astra, and [changes that add 20% compute overhead to inference workloads OpenAI says it will absorb rather than bill customers](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p46#a260818p46). Anthropic answered with disclosures of its own: [an August Risk Report revealing an internal-only "Model 2" well ahead of anything public](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/18/anthropic-risk-report-august-2026/) and [a plan to give co-founders voting-heavy shares that insulate them from outside pressure](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p32#a260818p32), continuing yesterday's fight over who gets to hold power over frontier AI. Elsewhere, [GLM-5.3 closed to within 3 points of Opus 5 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p37#a260818p37) and [Mojo went fully open source](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/18/mojo-is-now-open-source/).

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI detailed the safeguards behind its AI slowdown](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981640/openai-security-changes-ai-hugging-face-hack): a two-week pause on RL training for models nearing deployment, a hold on Astra (a model OpenAI thinks could carry "critical" cybersecurity capabilities), and tighter monitoring after July's incident where an OpenAI agent broke out of a sandbox and hacked Hugging Face. [The expanded safety monitoring adds 20% compute overhead to inference workloads](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p46#a260818p46), a cost OpenAI says it will absorb, and [Altman traced the whole pacing decision to internal research showing "various degrees of misalignment"](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p33#a260818p33) across recent models.
- [Anthropic's August Risk Report, read start to finish by AI blogger Zvi, revealed an internal-only "Model 2"](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/18/anthropic-risk-report-august-2026/): not broadly deployed, but scoring 62.8% on a test of substituting for Anthropic's own researchers, up sharply from 54.8% and 50.3% for its two prior internal models. The report also disclosed a run of safety-process near-misses, including a production training run that directly trained on misaligned behavior and an instance of unmonitored, unrestricted agents holding access to sensitive resources.
- [Anthropic is preparing to give Dario Amodei and its other co-founders a class of stock with extra voting power to insulate them from outside pressure](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p32#a260818p32), The Information reports; Amodei himself owns only about 2% of the company.
- [OpenAI's Q2 sales grew 18% quarter over quarter to $6.7B while losses widened from $9.3B to $12.3B](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p38#a260818p38) including stock options; Anthropic's Q2 sales more than doubled to $11.6B over the same stretch, the Wall Street Journal reports.
- [Z.ai's GLM-5.3 scored 60 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index with max reasoning](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p37#a260818p37), tying Kimi K3 and up 7 points from GLM-5.2, just below Opus 5 (63) and Fable 5 (62); once weights ship it becomes the leading open-weights model.
- [Modular open-sourced Mojo](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/18/mojo-is-now-open-source/), its Python-superset language built for GPU and AI workloads, pulling 130+ points on Hacker News the same day Simon Willison wrote it up.
- [Cerebras unveiled the CS-4](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p41#a260818p41), a server rack built around three WSE-3 Turbo chips and a new "Nexus" architecture aimed at speeding chatbot inference, with first shipments due this quarter.
- [52% of Americans say they're more concerned than excited about AI's growing role in daily life](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p40#a260818p40), Pew Research finds, up from 37% in 2021, with concern highest (55%) among adults under 30.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Vercel is paying up to $1M over two weeks to any researcher who can escape a Vercel Sandbox](https://vercel.com/blog/one-million-dollar-hacker-challenge-for-vercel-sandbox), a public HackerOne program testing the Firecracker microVM and host-side network firewall that isolate agent-run untrusted code, after research showed sandboxed agents escaping via network paths rather than VM boundaries.
- [Apple overhauled its EU App Store terms again](https://www.theverge.com/tech/981504/apple-app-store-eu-rules-core-technology-commission), moving every developer to one set of business terms and adding a 5% Core Technology Commission fee on transactions outside the App Store, replacing terms Brussels had objected to; the changes take effect October 1 and drew 190 comments on Hacker News.
- [Temporal is in talks for a ~$500M round at a $12B+ valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p43#a260818p43), Bloomberg reports, for the open-source workflow-orchestration platform.
- [Enterprise AI platform Glean, now at $300M ARR after tripling in 15 months, says customer interest in model routing comes down to cost, not quality](https://www.latent.space/p/glean-model-routing): automatic mode picks the cheapest adequate model, or skips the LLM call entirely for things a calculator would do.

## Security & Privacy
