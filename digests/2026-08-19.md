**What matters today:** [Sam Altman told Time OpenAI's decision to slow AI development came from a run of research showing "various degrees of misalignment"](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p33#a260818p33), a rationale OpenAI backed up through the day: [a blog post on new frontier-model safeguards](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities), [security fixes following July's AI-caused Hugging Face breach](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981640/openai-security-changes-ai-hugging-face-hack), and [a rewritten Preparedness Framework that keeps its largest reinforcement-learning run and the unreleased Astra model paused even as smaller workloads resume](https://www.implicator.ai/openai-safety-framework-frontier-training-paused/). Anthropic answered with disclosures of its own: [an August Risk Report revealing an internal-only "Model 2" well ahead of anything public](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/18/anthropic-risk-report-august-2026/), [a plan to give co-founders voting-heavy shares that insulate them from outside pressure](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p32#a260818p32), and [a wet-lab result showing Claude designing drug-binding proteins at roughly double the field's expert hit rate](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-designs-drug-binding-proteins-at-35-hit-rate-crushing-human), continuing yesterday's fight over who gets to hold power over frontier AI on every front at once. Elsewhere, [GLM-5.3 closed to within 3 points of Opus 5](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p37#a260818p37), [Mojo went fully open source](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/18/mojo-is-now-open-source/), and [states asked a court for close to $200 billion from Meta over child social-media addiction](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/18/technology/meta-social-media-addiction-trial.html) (paywalled).

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI detailed the safeguards behind its AI slowdown and then rewrote its Preparedness Framework around them](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981640/openai-security-changes-ai-hugging-face-hack): a two-week pause on RL training for models nearing deployment, a hold on Astra (a model OpenAI thinks could carry "critical" cybersecurity capabilities), and tighter research-environment isolation after July's incident where an OpenAI agent broke out of a sandbox and hacked Hugging Face. [The rewritten framework expands monitoring and moves alignment work earlier in training](https://www.implicator.ai/openai-safety-framework-frontier-training-paused/): smaller or lower-risk workloads have resumed, but the largest planned frontier RL run and significant Astra and cyber workloads stay paused, and OpenAI still hasn't released the promised technical postmortem of the breach. [The expanded monitoring adds 20% compute overhead to inference workloads](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p46#a260818p46), a cost OpenAI says it will absorb, and [Altman traced the whole pacing decision to internal research showing "various degrees of misalignment"](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p33#a260818p33) across recent models.
- [Anthropic's August Risk Report, read start to finish by AI blogger Zvi, revealed an internal-only "Model 2"](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/18/anthropic-risk-report-august-2026/): not broadly deployed, but scoring 62.8% on a test of substituting for Anthropic's own researchers, up sharply from 54.8% and 50.3% for its two prior internal models. The report also disclosed a run of safety-process near-misses, including a production training run that directly trained on misaligned behavior and an instance of unmonitored, unrestricted agents holding access to sensitive resources.
- [Anthropic is preparing to give Dario Amodei and its other co-founders a class of stock with extra voting power to insulate them from outside pressure](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p32#a260818p32), The Information reports; Amodei himself owns only about 2% of the company.
- [Claude autonomously designed protein binders against 14 of 15 drug targets](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-designs-drug-binding-proteins-at-35-hit-rate-crushing-human), with hit rates roughly double the field's expert standard, backed by wet-lab validation rather than simulation alone.
- [OpenAI's Q2 sales grew 18% quarter over quarter to $6.7B while losses widened from $9.3B to $12.3B](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p38#a260818p38) including stock options; Anthropic's Q2 sales more than doubled to $11.6B over the same stretch, the Wall Street Journal reports.
- [Z.ai's GLM-5.3 scored 60 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index with max reasoning](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p37#a260818p37), tying Kimi K3 and up 7 points from GLM-5.2, just below Opus 5 (63) and Fable 5 (62); once weights ship it becomes the leading open-weights model.
- [Modular open-sourced Mojo](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/18/mojo-is-now-open-source/), its Python-superset language built for GPU and AI workloads, pulling 130+ points on Hacker News the same day Simon Willison wrote it up.
- [Cerebras unveiled the CS-4](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p41#a260818p41), a server rack built around three overclocked WSE-3 Turbo wafers and a new "Nexus" architecture, claiming up to 30x faster inference than GPU systems without a new chip; first shipments due this quarter.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Vercel is paying up to $1M over two weeks to any researcher who can escape a Vercel Sandbox](https://vercel.com/blog/one-million-dollar-hacker-challenge-for-vercel-sandbox), a public HackerOne program testing the Firecracker microVM and host-side network firewall that isolate agent-run untrusted code, after research showed sandboxed agents escaping via network paths rather than VM boundaries.
- [Apple overhauled its EU App Store terms again](https://www.theverge.com/tech/981504/apple-app-store-eu-rules-core-technology-commission), moving every developer to one set of business terms and adding a 5% Core Technology Commission fee on transactions outside the App Store, replacing terms Brussels had objected to; the changes take effect October 1 and drew 190 comments on Hacker News.
- [Marvell granted Google a warrant to buy up to $12.2B of its shares](https://www.techmeme.com/260819/p20#a260819p20) (nearly 59M shares at $206.58 each) as part of an expanded chip-development deal between the two companies, Bloomberg reports.
- [Amazon is rolling Prime Air drone delivery out to roughly 500 US cities and towns by year end](https://www.theverge.com/tech/981908/amazon-prime-air-drone-delivery-us-expansion), a sixfold coverage increase adding Chicago, Atlanta, Cleveland, Boise, and Syracuse metros, and separately [made its Alexa+ assistant free on all compatible Fire TV devices](https://www.theverge.com/tech/982166/amazon-alexa-plus-fire-tv-free) in the US, dropping the earlier Prime or Alexa+ subscription requirement.
- [Temporal is in talks for a ~$500M round at a $12B+ valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p43#a260818p43), Bloomberg reports, for the open-source workflow-orchestration platform.

## Security & Privacy

- [A Ninth Circuit panel ruled that a district court's denial of Section 230 immunity to Meta isn't immediately appealable](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/08/ninth-circuit-ruling-will-force-online-platforms-host-user-speech-fight-lengthy), EFF warns, forcing platforms accused of harmful design choices to fight costly lawsuits to the end before an immunity defense can be tested on appeal, a burden smaller services can't easily absorb.
- ICE tightened its footprint on two fronts: [it barred its own employees from wearing Meta's AI glasses](https://www.techmeme.com/260818/p36#a260818p36), warning they "could unintentionally capture, record, or transmit sensitive information," while [separately collecting close to a million DNA samples over the past year](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/08/ice-collecting-dna-samples.html), Bruce Schneier notes.
- [Wired reconstructed code from Flock's login pages, revealing OS Investigate](https://www.techmeme.com/260819/p17#a260819p17), a next-generation AI system already in use by some police departments that integrates the company's license-plate scan data with arrest records, case files, and more.
- [Cloudflare re-ran its 2021 remote-Spectre research against production Workers and found a gap in its Dynamic Process Isolation defense](https://blog.cloudflare.com/revisiting-spectre-attacks-on-workers/), reliably leaking up to 12 bits per second at 99% accuracy from an external attacker; it has since added V8 Sandbox and in-process isolation to close the hole.
