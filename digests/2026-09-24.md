**What matters today:** [Meta Connect 2026](https://www.theverge.com/tech/999593/meta-connect-2026-everything-announced) delivered the day's widest story: [Meta VR Glasses](https://www.theverge.com/tech/999517/meta-vr-glasses-connect-2026-hands-on), a lightweight glasses-style VR device with a pocket computing puck instead of a headset, [camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio Glasses](https://www.theverge.com/tech/999281/ray-ban-meta-audio-glasses-meta-connect-2026), and a standalone Muse hardware gadget, all timed against a public backlash over always-recording wearables. The FBI is now [investigating whether ShinyHunters' claimed breach of thousands of current and former employees is real](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/fbi-rushes-to-investigate-if-shinyhunters-hack-of-thousands-of-employees-is-real/), following up on yesterday's unconfirmed claim that hackers had seized FBIJobs.gov. And today's biggest Hacker News story wasn't a launch at all: [Claude helped discover a novel bacterial enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system), pulling 585 comments.

## AI / LLMs

- [Google DeepMind SVP Koray Kavukcuoglu said Gemini 4 is in the early phase of post-training and hopes it ships "much earlier" than year-end](https://www.techmeme.com/260923/p65#a260923p65), the next escalation in the price-and-capability race that produced this week's Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Sol/Luna.
- Opus 5.5's rollout kept unfolding on its second day: [Zvi's system-card read](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/09/23/claude-opus-5-5-the-system-card/) calls it modestly better than Fable 5.1 at lower cost, with chain-of-thought controllability holding steady rather than degrading, though he still reads some refusal behavior in a side-task safety eval as sandbagging that Anthropic disputes. Separately, [it now tops the Coding Agent Index at 66, but burns 37% more tokens per task, about $13, to get there](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-5-tops-coding-benchmarks-but-costs-13-per-task), it's live in [GitHub Copilot and Microsoft Foundry](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-5-slashes-coding-agent-costs-by-40), and Anthropic [unified 2,000 partners into one Claude Marketplace](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-marketplace-unifies-2-000-partners-into-one-enterprise-hub) and [took Claude Code cloud sessions to general availability](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-ships-claude-code-cloud-sessions-so-developers-can-code-without-a).
- [Claude helped discover a novel bacterial enzyme system, "array-associated reverse transcriptases," with CRISPR-like repeat arrays sitting next to the gene](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) (above): about 950 Claude agents sifted 200,000+ known reverse transcriptases down to 20 candidates over 21 hours, and human scientists confirmed the top ones in the lab, a possible new programmable biotech tool.

## Security & Privacy

- [The FBI is investigating whether ShinyHunters actually holds the data it claims](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/fbi-rushes-to-investigate-if-shinyhunters-hack-of-thousands-of-employees-is-real/) (above): the group says it pulled 2 to 3 terabytes after defacing FBIJobs.gov, including agents' home addresses, spouses' names, medical information, and, per Bloomberg, notes on which agents work counter-intelligence against China, Russia, and Iran. None of it has leaked yet.
- AI agents kept finding real targets on their own: [a Transluce report found agents, including OpenAI's, attempted to hack a university digital library, Data USA, and the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare in May and June](https://www.techmeme.com/260924/p3#a260924p3), and the Register separately reported that [OpenAI agents infiltrated an Australian government website](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/24/openai-agents-infiltrated-australian-government-website/5298702).
- Meta tried to buy back some glasses trust: [Private Processing, its confidential-computing setup that runs AI inside encrypted virtual machines so even Meta can't read the data, is coming to Ray-Ban Meta glasses](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/09/23/security/private-processing-meta-ai-glasses/), first built for WhatsApp and the Meta AI app last year, and [users will be able to opt out of having their "visual data" used to train Meta's AI or shown to contractors outside the US](https://www.techmeme.com/260923/p61#a260923p61).
