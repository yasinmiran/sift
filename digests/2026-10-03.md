**What matters today:** Apple is [tightening Full Disk Access on macOS](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/apple-changes-full-disk-access-permissions-to-curb-abuse-from-ai-agents/) so apps can only get it through very explicit user action, two weeks after a columnist said Meta's Muse agent surfaced one of his private message threads. Meta, meanwhile, [open sourced code for DIY Muse gadgets](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1004330/meta-muse-ai-gadgets-home-link) and Amazon pledged [$1B for data center communities](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/amazons-1b-plan-to-combat-data-center-backlash-draws-more-backlash/). Yesterday OpenAI's agents were the security story; today the fix lands at the operating system layer, while OpenAI's own safety bench keeps thinning: [Safety Systems author David Robinson](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004408/openai-safety-quits-sounding-the-alarm) went public in The Atlantic after resigning.

## AI / LLMs

- Meta open sources [Muse gadgets](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1004330/meta-muse-ai-gadgets-home-link): firmware for ESP32 boards and a Linux SDK (Raspberry Pi 5 included) that lets you attach its Muse agent to your own displays, buttons and sensors. It turns the agent into a hobbyist platform, days after the Messages controversy below.
- Meta [lets go of employees](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p23#a261002p23) it hired from AI safety startup Virtue AI four months after they joined in June, citing clashing work styles.
- Leaked Slack messages show OpenAI staff [pushed back in June](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p28#a261002p28) on Greg Brockman's ties to the Leading the Future group, contributing to him reneging on a $25M donation. Separately, Safety Systems leader David Robinson [left OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p29#a261002p29) last week. Update: he has since gone public (next entry).
- Update on Robinson: the man who wrote the safety reports that shipped with every major OpenAI model release [resigned this week](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004408/openai-safety-quits-sounding-the-alarm) and, in an Atlantic editorial, says the industry's culture is fundamentally broken, a deeper problem than a few new rules can fix. The Verge cautions against dismissing such warnings because their authors helped build the thing.
- OpenAI launches [GPT-6.1 Sol](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-launches-gpt-6-1-sol-at-one-fifth-of-astra-pricing) at one-fifth of Astra's price, per TestingCatalog. Endor Labs says [on Codex it matches Astra on security](https://www.endorlabs.com/learn/gpt-6-1-sol-on-codex-astra-level-security-a-third-faster-zero-cheating) while running a third faster, a vendor-adjacent claim worth waiting on independent numbers for.
- Aleph Alpha releases [Kolibri](https://aleph-alpha.com/en/blog/kolibri-has-landed-a-sovereign-open-weight-model/), an open-weight model for German and English pitched as sovereign European AI. It drew 312 points on [Hacker News](https://tej.as/blog/aleph-alpha-kolibri).
- OpenAI's DevDay push, per [TechCrunch's read](https://www.techmeme.com/261003/p9#a261003p9), turns ChatGPT into a place to discover, launch and use software, which could disrupt the app store model.
- DeepSeek and Huawei [release open-source Ascend programming tools](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/deepseek-and-huawei-release-open-source-ascend-ai-programming-tools-to-reduce-reliance-on-nvidia-ecosystem-tools-include-compute-and-communication-libraries-as-well-as-ascend-support-for-tilelang), including compute and communication libraries, to make Huawei chips a workable alternative to Nvidia's software stack.

## Devtools & Infra

## Security & Privacy

- Apple [limits Full Disk Access](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/apple-changes-full-disk-access-permissions-to-curb-abuse-from-ai-agents/) (the macOS permission that lets an app read nearly everything) so users must take ==very explicit action== to grant it, citing a "substantial" rise in agent risk ([developer note](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=p6zjojqw)). The trigger was columnist Jason Aten's claim that Muse referenced his Messages thread; Meta says Messages access is opt-in and needs both full-disk access and a Messages connector, so the dispute is over how easily users grant both.
- A US judge ruled that an officer's license plate search in [Flock's network](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p31#a261002p31) was "indiscriminate mass surveillance" violating the Fourth Amendment, saying the officer should have got a warrant. It is a precedent that could limit warrantless use of plate-reader databases.
- A URL-encoding bypass in [Cisco SD-WAN](https://forkast.news/cisco-sd-wans-url-encoding-bypass-grants-unauthenticated-admin-access-federal-agencies-have-24-hours) (software that manages company wide-area networks) grants unauthenticated admin access, and US federal agencies were given 24 hours to respond, which signals active exploitation risk.
- Vercel's Guillermo Rauch says its sandbox bounty program [confirmed a KVM zero-day](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2106402024804020657), a flaw in Linux's virtualization layer, a reminder that the isolation underneath agent sandboxes is itself a target.
- The US [arrested tech CEO Greg Lui](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/us-arrests-tech-ceo-accused-of-smuggling-300m-in-nvidia-chips-into-china/), accused of using false paperwork and South Asian freight forwarders to divert more than $300M of servers with Nvidia A100 and H100 chips to China. He is charged; the claims are allegations.
