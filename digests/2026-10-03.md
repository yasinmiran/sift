**What matters today:** Apple is [tightening Full Disk Access on macOS](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/apple-changes-full-disk-access-permissions-to-curb-abuse-from-ai-agents/) so apps can only get it through very explicit user action, two weeks after a columnist said Meta's Muse agent surfaced one of his private message threads. Meta, meanwhile, [open sourced code for DIY Muse gadgets](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1004330/meta-muse-ai-gadgets-home-link) and Amazon pledged [$1B for data center communities](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/amazons-1b-plan-to-combat-data-center-backlash-draws-more-backlash/). Yesterday OpenAI's agents were the security story; today the fix lands at the operating system layer.

## AI / LLMs

- Meta open sources [Muse gadgets](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1004330/meta-muse-ai-gadgets-home-link): firmware for ESP32 boards and a Linux SDK (Raspberry Pi 5 included) that lets you attach its Muse agent to your own displays, buttons and sensors. It turns the agent into a hobbyist platform, days after the Messages controversy below.
- Meta [lets go of employees](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p23#a261002p23) it hired from AI safety startup Virtue AI four months after they joined in June, citing clashing work styles.
- Leaked Slack messages show OpenAI staff [pushed back in June](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p28#a261002p28) on Greg Brockman's ties to the Leading the Future group, contributing to him reneging on a $25M donation. Separately, Safety Systems leader David Robinson [left OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p29#a261002p29) last week.
- DeepSeek and Huawei [release open-source Ascend programming tools](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/deepseek-and-huawei-release-open-source-ascend-ai-programming-tools-to-reduce-reliance-on-nvidia-ecosystem-tools-include-compute-and-communication-libraries-as-well-as-ascend-support-for-tilelang), including compute and communication libraries, to make Huawei chips a workable alternative to Nvidia's software stack.
- Meta's Muse Spark worked with mathematicians through its chat interface on six papers, [five answering previously open questions](https://alphasignal.ai/news/meta-s-muse-spark-helped-mathematicians-solve-five-open-research-problems), per AlphaSignal.
- Neuralink [pretrains a foundation model](https://x.com/neuralink/status/2106066173917856070) on 50,000 hours of implant data, applying the LLM recipe to brain-computer interfaces.
- Reka open sources [RIDM](https://alphasignal.ai/news/reka-releases-ridm-to-read-hidden-controls-from-any-video), which infers keyboard-and-mouse style actions from raw video, giving world-model training a way to label action data.

## Devtools & Infra

- Zig [0.17.0 ships](https://ziglang.org/download/0.17.0/release-notes.html), a long release-notes read that also led the front page tonight.
- Supabase [raises $150M](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p30#a261002p30) led by Singapore's GIC and agrees to acquire Turso, a database built for AI agents, so the Postgres vendor is buying into agent-native storage.
- Prime Intellect launches [Prime Inference](https://alphasignal.ai/news/prime-intellect-launches-prime-inference-serving-600b-tokens-daily), already serving 600B tokens a day, using prefill/decode disaggregation (splitting the two phases of generation across machines) and NVFP4 KV compression.

- Nvidia [adds a 64 GB DGX Spark](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p26#a261002p26) at $4,999, $1,000 above the 128 GB model's launch price: the memory shortage now shapes local-AI hardware.

## Security & Privacy

- Apple [limits Full Disk Access](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/apple-changes-full-disk-access-permissions-to-curb-abuse-from-ai-agents/) (the macOS permission that lets an app read nearly everything) so users must take ==very explicit action== to grant it, citing a "substantial" rise in agent risk ([developer note](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=p6zjojqw)). The trigger was columnist Jason Aten's claim that Muse referenced his Messages thread; Meta says Messages access is opt-in and needs both full-disk access and a Messages connector, so the dispute is over how easily users grant both.
- A US judge ruled that an officer's license plate search in [Flock's network](https://www.techmeme.com/261002/p31#a261002p31) was "indiscriminate mass surveillance" violating the Fourth Amendment, saying the officer should have got a warrant. It is a precedent that could limit warrantless use of plate-reader databases.

The pledge drew fresh criticism, and Amazon itself acknowledges backlash is driving local moratoriums.

Discovery near a megamerger.

## Research
