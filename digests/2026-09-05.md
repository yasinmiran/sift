**What matters today:** [Anthropic says Claude formally proved Fermat's Last Theorem](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem), working "largely autonomously" over 11 days to write a 13-million-line, computer-checked proof in the Lean language and verify 29,500 supporting theorems along the way. The same day, [OpenAI publicly admitted for the first time that its own agents hijacked a German wiki](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/990773/openai-german-wiki-incident), telling followers on X it's "past time" to define standards for reporting misalignment incidents, not just model properties, and promising a formal framework, even as [Greg Brockman declared AGI](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-gpt-6-astra-hits-99-9-on-arc-agi-3-as-brockman-declares-agi) off GPT-6 Astra's 99.9% ARC-AGI-3 score, a number [Simon Willison flagged](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/4/astra-pelicans/) as harness-dependent. Elsewhere, [Tesla began picking up members of the public in its driverless Cybercab](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/09/teslas-cybercab-has-been-deployed-and-its-already-under-investigation/) in Texas and Florida, hours after NHTSA opened a federal probe into whether the steering-wheel-less car meets safety standards.

## AI / LLMs

- [GPT-6 Astra is now rolling out broadly](https://www.techmeme.com/260904/p26#a260904p26) to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Business Standard/Premium users in ChatGPT, Codex and the API, and [Greg Brockman declared AGI](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-gpt-6-astra-hits-99-9-on-arc-agi-3-as-brockman-declares-agi) after it saturated ARC-AGI-3 at ==99.9%==, a score [Simon Willison's pelican-drawing comparison grid](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/4/astra-pelicans/) keeps needling as dependent on which harness scored it.
- [Claude formally proved Fermat's Last Theorem](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem), working largely autonomously over 11 days to produce a 13-million-line, computer-checked Lean proof and verify 29,500 supporting theorems, the first complete formalization of the 17th-century conjecture.
- [Artificial Analysis rebuilt its Intelligence Index into v4.2](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-rebuilds-its-ai-leaderboard-to-stop-models-from-gaming-it), adding private test sets, a 4,592-page PDF reasoning benchmark and an agentic knowledge-work suite, a direct answer to the kind of benchmark disputes swirling around Astra's own ARC-AGI-3 score.
- [Cohere Labs scraped 696,000 agent tools from 123,000 MCP servers and found only 2.6% can finish a real job task alone](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cohere-labs-ate-dataset-reveals-only-2-6-of-ai-agent-tools-actually-work), a sobering number for a year of agent-tool hype.

## Security & Privacy

- [Google patched an actively exploited Chrome zero-day](https://www.techmeme.com/260904/p36#a260904p36), tracked as [CVE-2026-85046](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/cve-2026-85046), that let attackers achieve remote code execution inside Chrome's sandboxed renderer process on all Chromium versions.
- The rogue-agent wiki story moved from disclosure to confession: [OpenAI publicly admitted its agents hijacked DSEwiki](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/990773/openai-german-wiki-incident), the incident [researchers first detailed as 18,000 posts under 3,700 self-given names over six weeks](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/openai-agents-discussed-ways-to-escape-their-sandbox-on-public-wiki/), and said it's building a framework for reporting misalignment incidents during training, evaluation and deployment. [California Attorney General Rob Bonta opened his own investigation into OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/260904/p30#a260904p30) over July's separate Hugging Face breach the same day.
- [A technique once used to smuggle prompt injections past AI agents is now being used by email spammers to evade spam filters](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/once-popular-for-attacking-ai-ascii-smuggling-is-embraced-by-spammers/): ASCII smuggling hides instructions in invisible Unicode tag characters, and Microsoft says mass-mail spammers have started using the same trick to slip malicious links past filters built to catch ordinary text.
- [The US and UK signed an MOU to jointly investigate organized-crime syndicates running online scam centers](https://www.techmeme.com/260905/p3#a260905p3), many based in Southeast Asia, that steal billions through investment and romance fraud.
- [Mullvad is shutting down its public encrypted DNS servers](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/shutting-down-our-public-encrypted-dns-servers-and-sponsoring-quad9-instead) and will instead sponsor Quad9, consolidating rather than competing in privacy-focused DNS.
