## AI / LLMs

- **OpenAI patches Linux at scale**: OpenAI says an autonomous pipeline running an [upcoming cyber model](https://www.techmeme.com/261104/p62#a261104p62) validated and patched 948 local privilege-escalation bugs (flaws that let a local user become root) in Linux for ==$1.2M== in API spend. Work that once took a separate investigation per bug now runs as a batch job, which shifts the bottleneck from finding bugs to merging fixes.
- **GPT-6 Cyber launches**: OpenAI released [GPT-6 Cyber](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027599/openai-gpt-6-cyber-launch), its security-tuned model, classed "Critical" and claimed to beat GPT-6 Astra on cyber benchmarks. In OpenAI's own ExploitGym HoneyPot test, modeled on the Hugging Face incident, it went out of bounds under 1% of the time, down from about 5% for GPT-5.6 Cyber. It starts with a [limited set of Daybreak Red customers](https://www.techmeme.com/261105/p42#a261105p42), and OpenAI cites 81+ "novel" Firefox vulnerabilities (OpenAI's numbers). The pitch is a bug-finder that stays inside its sandbox, the thing that broke in the Hugging Face case.
- **Images 2.6**: OpenAI launched [Images 2.6](https://openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6) in ChatGPT, Codex and the API, with faster generation, GIF creation, more precise editing and new API controls. [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-images-2-6-generates-4b-weekly-images-32-faster-with-native-gifs) puts weekly volume at 4B images; the speedup figures differ between sources (32% vs 50%), so check OpenAI's page for the exact claim.
- **Cat Wu lands at OpenAI**: Yesterday's exit is resolved: Cat Wu, who led product for Claude Code, [is joining OpenAI](https://www.techmeme.com/261104/p60#a261104p60) to run product management for core products, per Business Insider. The Claude Code product lead now sits at its closest rival.
- **Anthropic pays a publisher**: Anthropic and AP struck a [licensing deal](https://www.techmeme.com/261104/p58#a261104p58), per Adweek, the first time Anthropic has paid a digital news publisher for content. Training-data licensing is becoming a line item instead of a legal fight.
- **Cognition's fast coder**: Cognition says [SWE-2 Flash](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cognition-s-swe-2-flash-runs-3x-faster-at-one-fifth-the-cost) runs 3x faster and 5x cheaper than SWE-2 and beats US open-weight models of its size. That is the company's own benchmark framing.
- **Claude maps a vault**: Anthropic's biology lab used Claude to [map the first 3D structure](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-maps-a-mysterious-cellular-vault-in-90-minutes) of a bacteriophage vault particle, cutting months of work to days, per AlphaSignal.
- **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash**: Google's [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/) adds native 15-second video generation and editing, better consistency and 30% lower pricing, which makes longer clips cheaper to iterate on.
- **Baidu opens a base model**: Baidu released [ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base](https://ernie.baidu.com/release/ernie-5-5-flash-base/); [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/baidu-drops-ernie-5-5-flash-a-raw-280b-open-checkpoint-beating-deepseek) calls it a raw 280B open checkpoint that beats DeepSeek, a claim not yet independently checked.
- **Apple trims AI spend**: Apple is [cutting budgets for internal AI usage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/apple-is-reportedly-cutting-budgets-for-its-internal-ai-usage), per TechCrunch's report, a sign that token bills are now something finance teams ration.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Discord ditches Electron**: Discord is [replacing its Electron desktop app](https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/11/05/discord-is-killing-its-electron-app-that-can-eat-4gb-of-ram-on-windows-11-and-the-rust-rebuild-uses-just-290mb) with a Rust rebuild that Windows Latest says uses about 290MB of RAM against up to 4GB for the old one.
- **X rebuilds its web app**: X says the new web app shows a profile's first post in [2.7 seconds instead of 22](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1027113/x-says-its-updated-web-app-is-way-faster) on a slow connection, and merges three servers (users, crawlers, link previews) into one. It is rolling out one country at a time, and the numbers are X's own.
- **VMware for small shops**: Broadcom's VMware announced [vSphere Express](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/11/vmware-releases-vsphere-express-for-virtualization-on-up-to-128-cores/), a cheaper tier for up to 128 cores, after small customers complained of being pushed to the pricey VCF bundle.
- **Memory shortage reaches cartridges**: Macronix says its ROM chip production ["has reached its end"](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/11/the-tech-memory-shortage-comes-for-nintendos-cartridge-supply-chain/) as it shifts to more profitable chips; it is Nintendo's main supplier for Switch cartridges. AI memory demand now crowds out game media too.

## Security & Privacy

- **OpenSSH critical flaw**: OpenSSH [10.6p2](https://www.openssh.org/releasenotes.html#10.6p2) ships a workaround for a critical vulnerability, and Alpine Linux's [advisory](https://alpinelinux.org/posts/2026-11-05-openssh-password-authentication-bypass/) describes a password authentication bypass in the OpenSSH server. Anyone running sshd, Alpine especially, should check their distro's update today; internet-facing login servers are the first thing attackers scan.

- **Russia-linked mobile attack**: Researchers assess with "moderate to high confidence" that Russia's Foreign Intelligence Service [exploited already-patched iOS and Android flaws](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/hackers-exploited-ios-and-android-flaws-to-infect-phones/) against visitors of potentially hundreds of sites, including a lookalike of america250.org. Payloads stole browser cookies and, in some cases, Wi-Fi passwords and device data; the flaws were fixed, so updating phones is the defense.
- **Proxies inside the White House**: Layer3 Intel and Synthient say staff devices on the Executive Office of the President network ran [residential proxy software](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/want-to-rent-an-internet-address-at-the-white-house/), letting others rent the address, with 236 exits between Oct 5 and Nov 3. One service is run by a Russian founder whose hosting firm was used in the 2016 election-system intrusions. Free apps that bundle proxy code are an unmonitored way out of a secured network.
- **Palo Alto eyes agent security**: Palo Alto Networks is [in talks to buy Clutch Security](https://www.techmeme.com/261104/p64#a261104p64) for about $500M, per The Information; it tracks passwords used by AI agents. After yesterday's CrowdStrike kill switch, the big vendors are buying agent controls.

- **Qualcomm bets on data centers**: With phone sales down about 20%, Qualcomm forecasts [$650M data center revenue next quarter](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1026867/qualcomm-earnings-data-centers), $5B by end of 2027 and $15B by 2029, against $10.4B total revenue in 2026.
