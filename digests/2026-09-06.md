## AI / LLMs

- [Artificial Analysis's rebuilt Intelligence Index v4.2 returned its first scores](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-reshuffles-its-intelligence-index-to-separate-frontier): Claude Fable 5.1 leads at 57, GPT-6 Astra second at 55 (a 4-point gain over GPT-5.6 Sol), the first hard number to arrive since Astra's contested 99.9% ARC-AGI-3 score.
- [Zvi's roundup of the community verdict on Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/09/05/claude-mythos-5-1-and-fable-5-1-capabilities/) finds near-universal praise for writing quality and fewer intrusive safety refusals (Anthropic says biology-safeguard interventions on benign requests dropped 85%), alongside a 75% cut to cache-read pricing ($1 to $0.25 per million tokens) that Claude Code creator Boris Cherny says cuts a typical Claude Code session up to 38%.
- [LlamaIndex and Kaggle's new ExtractBench](https://alphasignal.ai/news/llamaindex-s-extractbench-catches-gpt-5-and-gemini-3-hitting-0-on-wide-schemas) grades document extraction across 370 enterprise files on missing fields, source grounding and duplicate records, and found GPT-5 and Gemini-3 both score 0% on its widest schemas, a sign leaderboard-topping models still fail basic structured-output work.
- [Meta's autonomous research agent AIRA₃ placed 8th of roughly 4,000 teams](https://alphasignal.ai/news/meta-s-aira3-beats-4-000-human-teams-to-win-kaggle-gold) in an Nvidia-run Kaggle contest to fine-tune Nemotron, gold-medal territory for an agent competing unassisted against human teams.

## Startups & Industry

- [Swiss Re expects global data-center insurance premiums to reach $20B-$30B a year by 2030](https://www.techmeme.com/260905/p11#a260905p11), with roughly 40% of US data-center capacity sitting in tornado-prone areas, per the Wall Street Journal, one more sign AI buildout risk is migrating onto insurers' books.
- [The data-center backlash is testing Texas's famously pro-business stance](https://www.techmeme.com/260905/p15#a260905p15), even as Wood Mackenzie finds the state has more data-center capacity under construction than any other, per the Financial Times.
- [The Seattle Times and Newsday sued OpenAI and Microsoft](https://www.techmeme.com/260905/p13#a260905p13), alleging the companies trained their models on the papers' journalism without permission; Microsoft and OpenAI are also funders of the Seattle Times, an awkward detail in an already crowded field of publisher AI lawsuits.
- [As researchers apply AI to decode animal communication, bioethicists warn the same tools could give humans new ways to manipulate or exploit other species](https://www.techmeme.com/260905/p14#a260905p14), per Bloomberg, a caution note on a research area usually framed as pure upside.
