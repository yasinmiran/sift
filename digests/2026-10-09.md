**What matters today:** Anthropic's [Cyber Mission](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission) pairs a defense program for power and water systems with a free scanner for open-source code, whose reports ship with no human review. SpaceX bought low-band spectrum to [become a US mobile carrier](https://www.theverge.com/science/1008467/spacex-announces-plan-to-become-a-major-mobile-carrier), and the White House [suspended Microsoft](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p55#a261008p55) from a green card program. The evening added OpenAI's defense of its fired safety researchers, Mistral's 1T-parameter open-weights preview and Deno's move to Cloudflare.

## AI / LLMs

- **Anthropic launches its Cyber Mission with two programs.** A [Critical Infrastructure Defense Program](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission) puts Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research behind 11 founding security partners (including CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks and Dragos) to protect grids and water systems. Its [OSS Scanner](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008521/anthropic-open-source-oss-scanner) gives opt-in open-source projects free periodic scans, but reports arrive without human triage, and Anthropic expects a true-positive rate above 90%, so maintainers inherit the sorting work.
- **OpenAI stands by firing three safety researchers.** Yesterday the trio published an open letter; today OpenAI said Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak and Mikita Balesni [violated "clear policies on handling sensitive information"](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008604/openai-defends-decision-fire-safety-researchers), a "significant breach of trust" and not retaliation for speaking out. The researchers [dispute the misconduct claims](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/fired-openai-safety-researchers-dispute-misconduct-claims-warn-of-chilling-effect/), so this is now a public fight over what counts as a leak.
- **Mistral previews Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk":** a [1T-parameter model it claims tops any open model from the US or Europe](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mistral-debuts-large-4-le-chonk-a-1-trillion-parameter-text-output-model-with-high-benchmarks-planned-for-open-weights-release), with weights due October 27. The benchmark claim is Mistral's own until the weights let others test it.
- **Anthropic's dynamic workflows leave research preview** with a multiagent API type in which a lead agent [plans and orchestrates up to 1,000 subagents per run](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-now-orchestrates-1-000-agents-finding-66-of-70-bugs). AlphaSignal's headline cites 66 of 70 bugs found. Fan-out at that scale turns long jobs into a coordination problem.
- **Meta launched its Muse agent despite safety concerns,** the [NYT reports](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p9#a261009p9), after Zuckerberg saw rival Instinct's similar agent gain traction. The Verge asks whether [Instinct can survive Muse](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1008254/instinct-agent-ai-hands-on-muse-dots) now that Big Tech sells much the same product.
- **SemiAnalysis counts 857 releases from nine Chinese AI labs** since 2021: [just 3.6% shipped with developer safety results, 1.1% at launch](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p14#a261009p14). It lands the day after OpenAI's researcher dispute, with the same question about who checks the labs.
- **Exa's new ATLAS benchmark finds [existing search evals are saturated and memorized](https://alphasignal.ai/news/exa-labs-atlas-exposes-how-ai-agents-fake-web-research-without-searching),** with top agents still missing a third of its answers. An agent that scores well by recall rather than by searching tells you little about real research.
- **Anthropic sets up a "presidential engagement" program for the 2028 US elections,** [offering AI policy education to candidates in both parties](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p1#a261009p1) through an in-house team. Yesterday it put $150M into science credits; today it starts building relationships with whoever runs the country next.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Deno is joining Cloudflare.** The Deno team will work on [simplifying self-hosting of Workers and Durable Objects](https://blog.cloudflare.com/deno-joins-cloudflare/) so the same primitives run outside Cloudflare's network. The runtime now has Cloudflare's resources behind it; the Hacker News thread below shows how developers took the news.
- **GitHub's new ModernBERT-based classifier [scores candidate secrets in under 2 ms](https://alphasignal.ai/news/github-s-modernbert-classifier-catches-leaked-passwords-every-two-seconds),** and could roughly double the credentials blocked before they enter repository history. Catching a leaked password at push time beats rotating it after.
- **Cloudflare adds [on-demand CPU and memory profiling](https://blog.cloudflare.com/workers-on-demand-profiling/) for Workers and Durable Objects:** request a profile of a live Worker from the Observability page, view it as a flamegraph, download it. It shows which function burns CPU in production instead of guessing from logs.
- **Simon Willison ships [ttok 1.0](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/9/ttok/),** his token-counting CLI, now defaulting to the GPT-5/GPT-6 tokenizer instead of GPT-4's. OpenAI has not confirmed GPT-6 shares GPT-5's tokenizer, though an experiment he cites counted identical tokens across seven models.

## Security & Privacy

- **Cryptographers weigh AI as a threat to public-key encryption.** Matthew Green puts a [15% chance on functionally losing confidence in today's public-key algorithms](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/9/matthew-green/), because AI surprises arrive far faster than standards can be replaced; an [Ethereum researcher argues AI may break encryption before quantum does](https://decrypt.co/380363/ethereum-researcher-ai-break-encryption-before-quantum). Migration takes years, so the preparation has to start before the surprise.
- **CISA, the FBI, NSA and international partners [warn of a China-based cybersecurity company](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/cisa-fbi-nsa-and-international-partners-warn-china-based-cybersecurity-company-enabling-threat)** enabling threat actors that target critical infrastructure worldwide. Defenders using vendors from that ecosystem should read the advisory itself.
- **An Iranian campaign used ChatGPT to plant fake articles in real US publications,** [the Washington Post reports](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/10/09/chatgpt-users-iran-planted-ai-generated-articles-us-news-media/). Influence operations now borrow the credibility of outlets instead of building their own.
- **A Ukrainian drone strike hit a major Yandex data center in Russia,** [The Record reports](https://therecord.media/yandex-russia-drone-ukraine), and [a second hit within 48 hours](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/yandex-takes-a-second-data-center-hit-in-48-hours-now-its-biggest-russian-site-is-damaged-23277) reportedly damaged its biggest Russian site. Cloud facilities are now plainly targets in the war.
- **Sen. Maggie Hassan says Trump Mobile apparently lacks FCC authorization** for its international calling service and has not filed a required robocall plan, [per her letter](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/trump-mobile-doesnt-seem-to-have-fcc-authorization-for-phone-service-senator-says/). It follows reports that hackers took data on 3,615 customers and that a vendor exposed customer data.
- **Empire Market co-creator Raheim Hamilton got ==40 years==** for facilitating $430M in illegal transactions on the dark web marketplace, [per BleepingComputer](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p54#a261008p54).

## Startups & Industry

- **SpaceX buys a low-band spectrum portfolio, including up to 14 MHz of paired 800 MHz,** to make Starlink Mobile [a major US carrier](https://www.theverge.com/science/1008467/spacex-announces-plan-to-become-a-major-mobile-carrier) once the FCC signs off. Low-band signal penetrates walls, which satellite-only service lacks; [AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon fell 6%+ after hours](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p48#a261008p48).
- **The administration suspended Microsoft and several other firms from a green card program,** and [Microsoft denies](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p55#a261008p55) JD Vance's claim it replaced thousands of US workers with foreign ones, saying most H-1B applications were for existing employees. [Ars adds](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/trump-administration-targets-microsoft-in-new-immigration-crackdown/) that Microsoft is barred from sponsoring foreign workers for US residency.
- **TypeSafe AI, maker of the Jev model, raised about $870M led by a16z at a $7.5B valuation,** [Bloomberg reports](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p13#a261009p13), only weeks after launch, with roughly a third of the Fortune 500 using Jev. Few startups go from launch to that valuation this fast.
- **Newcomer says OpenAI's annualized revenue is [$20B below the widely cited $70B figure](https://www.newcomer.co/p/competing-arr-estimates-and-fuzzy),** after FT reporting that sent AI stocks lower Thursday. Part of it is net versus gross accounting: OpenAI counts net, Anthropic counts sales through partners gross, so the two headline numbers were never comparable.
- **Global PC shipments fell 20.1% in Q3 to 62.7M units,** [IDC says](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p4#a261009p4), on higher prices and supply problems (HP down 30.9%, Dell 25%, Apple 11.3%). The same memory costs have [Apple telling suppliers to cut iPhone 18 Pro component output 15%-20%](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p3#a261009p3).
- **Meta bans ByteDance ads and paid marketing,** plus third-party campaigns linking to TikTok, [in the US, Canada, Egypt, Indonesia, Japan, Thailand and Vietnam](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p50#a261008p50). It turns a rival's ad budget off at the biggest distribution channel.
- **Tesla renames Full Self-Driving to Tesla Assisted Driving in Europe** after pushback from Germany, whose transport minister will now [back its EU-wide approval](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p18#a261009p18). A label change bought a political yes.
