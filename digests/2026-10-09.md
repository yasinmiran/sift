**What matters today:** Anthropic's [Cyber Mission](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission) pairs a defense program for power and water systems with a free scanner for open-source code, whose reports ship with no human review. SpaceX bought low-band spectrum to [become a US mobile carrier](https://www.theverge.com/science/1008467/spacex-announces-plan-to-become-a-major-mobile-carrier), sending AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon down after hours. The White House honored tech chiefs with medals the same day it [suspended Microsoft](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p55#a261008p55) from a green card program.

## AI / LLMs

- **Anthropic launches its Cyber Mission with two programs.** A [Critical Infrastructure Defense Program](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-cyber-mission) puts Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research behind 11 founding security partners (including CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks and Dragos) to protect grids and water systems. Its [OSS Scanner](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008521/anthropic-open-source-oss-scanner) gives opt-in open-source projects free periodic scans, but reports arrive without human triage, and Anthropic expects a true-positive rate above 90%, so maintainers inherit the sorting work.
- **Anthropic sets up a "presidential engagement" program for the 2028 US elections,** [offering AI policy education to candidates in both parties](https://www.techmeme.com/261009/p1#a261009p1) through an in-house team. Yesterday it put $150M into science credits; today it starts building relationships with whoever runs the country next.
- **Exa's new ATLAS benchmark finds [existing search evals are saturated and memorized](https://alphasignal.ai/news/exa-labs-atlas-exposes-how-ai-agents-fake-web-research-without-searching),** with top agents still missing a third of its answers. An agent that scores well by recall rather than by searching tells you little about real research.
- **Anthropic's Claude Science helped an astrophysicist [stitch decades of space telescope data into an all-sky UV map](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-science-fills-the-sky-s-missing-uv-third),** statistically filling the gaps in coverage. AlphaSignal calls it the first such map.
- **Midjourney is piloting a thinking step before image generation** on its alpha site, [aimed at prompt adherence and text rendering](https://alphasignal.ai/news/midjourney-tests-thinking-mode-to-cut-image-prompt-failures-by-80), with a headline claim of 80% fewer prompt failures. Treat the figure as Midjourney's own until outside tests appear.
- **Liquid AI's d1-3B returns [classifications and scores in one forward pass](https://alphasignal.ai/news/liquid-ai-s-d1-3b-makes-structured-ai-decisions-in-8-milliseconds-without),** about 8 ms with no text generated, on edge hardware. For routing and moderation decisions, skipping token generation removes most of the latency.

## Devtools & Infra

- **GitHub's new ModernBERT-based classifier [scores candidate secrets in under 2 ms](https://alphasignal.ai/news/github-s-modernbert-classifier-catches-leaked-passwords-every-two-seconds),** and could roughly double the credentials blocked before they enter repository history. Catching a leaked password at push time beats rotating it after.
- **Simon Willison ships [ttok 1.0](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/9/ttok/),** his token-counting CLI, now defaulting to the GPT-5/GPT-6 tokenizer instead of GPT-4's. OpenAI has not confirmed GPT-6 shares GPT-5's tokenizer, though an experiment he cites counted identical tokens across seven models.
- **AMD says it will bring FSR 4 upscaling to handhelds by year end,** but [not which devices](https://www.theverge.com/games/1008353/amd-will-bring-fsr-4-to-handhelds-by-the-end-of-2026), and it arrives alongside a new FSR 4-capable chip. Whether the Steam Deck generation gets official support is still open.

Defenders using vendors from that ecosystem should read the advisory itself.

Maggie Hassan says Trump Mobile apparently lacks FCC authorization** for its international calling service and has not filed a required robocall plan, [per her letter](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/trump-mobile-doesnt-seem-to-have-fcc-authorization-for-phone-service-senator-says/). ## Startups & Industry

- **SpaceX buys a low-band spectrum portfolio, including up to 14 MHz of paired 800 MHz,** to make Starlink Mobile [a major US carrier](https://www.theverge.com/science/1008467/spacex-announces-plan-to-become-a-major-mobile-carrier) once the FCC signs off. Low-band signal penetrates walls, which satellite-only service lacks; [AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon fell 6%+ after hours](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p48#a261008p48).
- **The administration suspended Microsoft and several other firms from a green card program,** and [Microsoft denies](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p55#a261008p55) JD Vance's claim it replaced thousands of US workers with foreign ones, saying most H-1B applications were for existing employees.
- **Trump gave Elon Musk, Jensen Huang, Lisa Su and Sergey Brin the [National Medal of Science](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p51#a261008p51),** and Satya Nadella and Michael Dell the [National Medal of Technology and Innovation](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p52#a261008p52). Microsoft's visa fight landed the same day, awkward timing for its CEO.
- **Meta bans ByteDance ads and paid marketing,** plus third-party campaigns linking to TikTok, [in the US, Canada, Egypt, Indonesia, Japan, Thailand and Vietnam](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p50#a261008p50). It turns a rival's ad budget off at the biggest distribution channel.
- **Flock Safety plans to cut [about 270 employees, 18% of staff](https://www.techmeme.com/261008/p53#a261008p53)** at the end of October, after a voluntary buyout in September.
