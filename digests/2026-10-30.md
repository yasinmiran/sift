**What matters today:** Binance says a hack [drained about $6.3B from its wallets](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p2#a261030p2), possibly the largest crypto theft ever, and TRM Labs [attributes it to North Korea's TraderTraitor](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p3#a261030p3); Binance pledges to cover every loss. The AI-offense story is the same shape: CrowdStrike says [China-linked hackers used an AI tool to target 200+ organizations in 11 days](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p1#a261030p1), and Epoch shows an open-weight model [turning unshipped Firefox fixes into working exploits](https://epoch.ai/publications/open-weight-models-patch-gap).

## Security & Privacy

- **Binance hack**: Binance [suspended all deposits and withdrawals](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p67#a261029p67) while Tether and Circle froze billions in coins, then said the theft was about $6.3B. TRM Labs says the funds moved across 10 blockchains, [mostly in 20 minutes](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p3#a261030p3), beating February 2025's $1.5B Bybit hack; 808bits reports the hot wallet [signed 50 EIP-7702 delegations (a feature that lets a wallet hand execution rights to a contract) three minutes before the drain](https://808bits.com/articles/binance-hot-wallet-eip-7702-delegations). By evening Binance said its follow-up found ["additional points of breach"](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p9#a261030p9) beyond a possibly compromised laptop and called it a "complex attack", and that it has [reserved $6.34B+ of company funds](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p14#a261030p14) for the fallout; Ars adds that [around $3B was frozen](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/hackers-steal-6-3-billion-from-binance-in-record-crypto-heist/) while Elliptic says the attacker converted about $3.3B into assets that cannot be frozen. Because Binance is covering losses, the damage lands on its balance sheet and on bitcoin and ether prices, [which fell](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/10/30/usd6-billion-binance-hack-sends-bitcoin-ether-tumbling-standard-chartered-sees-more-losses-ahead).
- **ARTEX espionage campaign**: CrowdStrike says the China-linked group VAPOR PANDA used an AI tool called ARTEX, driven by a DeepSeek model and another LLM, to [target 200+ organizations in 11 days and compromise at least nine](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p1#a261030p1); the [technical write-up](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/vapor-panda-uses-artex-to-scale-global-campaign) frames it as AI scaling a global campaign, so reconnaissance that took weeks now fits in days.
- **Patch gap**: Epoch reports GLM 5.3, an open-weight model, [built Firefox exploits from published but not yet shipped fixes](https://epoch.ai/publications/open-weight-models-patch-gap) across six releases for about ==$1,000== each. The window between a public fix and users receiving it is now cheap to attack.
- **Polymarket incident**: Polymarket [resumed trading after a breach at a third-party provider](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p60#a261029p60), which a source names as DebugBear, and says it will reimburse affected traders.
- **Claude Code extension backdoor**: This morning's report of a [trojanized Claude Code extension with a persistent SSH backdoor](https://opensourcemalware.com/blog/trojanized-claude-code-extension-plants-persistent-ssh-backdoor) grew into the day's biggest dev-tool scare: write-ups now describe a [backdoored Claude Code extension for VS Code released on the Cursor marketplace](https://doublepulsar.com/anthropic-hacked-to-deliver-remote-access-trojan-to-claude-developer-laptops-worldwide-1148a92baae2), and Cursor has posted a security notice. If you installed the extension from the Cursor marketplace, check Cursor's notice before trusting that machine.
- **Pentagon agent access**: Wired reports a defense contractor [gave a personal AI agent access to a secure Pentagon system](https://www.wired.com/story/a-defense-contractor-gave-their-personal-ai-agent-access-to-a-secure-pentagon-system), the insider-risk version of the agent-permissions problem.
- **Azure cross-tenant flaws**: binarysecurity details [five full cross-tenant compromises in Azure](https://www.binarysecurity.no/posts/2026/10/one-root-case), where a flaw lets one customer reach another's environment.
- **Northflank breach**: The cloud platform Northflank [posted a breach notice](https://status.northflank.com) on its status page.
- **GM data sale**: GM will [pay $220 million after selling customers' Smart Driver data](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/10/gm-to-pay-220-million-after-selling-customer-smart-driver-data/), a price tag on driving data sold without customers' understanding.

## AI / LLMs

- **Gemini 4 benchmarks**: Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 (a test of whether code would actually be merged) has [Gemini 4 Carbon at 40% against a best score of 45%](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-gemini-4-carbon-scores-40-on-cognition-s-toughest-coding-benchmark), and Artificial Analysis's new HLE-Diamond board [has Carbon on top at 69%](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-adds-hle-diamond-and-gemini-4-carbon-tops-at-69) with open-weight models more than 30 points behind. Frontier models cluster near 70% there, so the gap that matters is open versus closed.
- **ChatGPT credits for the API**: OpenAI now gives its $100, $200 and $500 subscribers [matching API credits](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/29/api-credits/), copying Anthropic's earlier Max and Team benefit, so a subscription doubles as prototyping budget on either side.
- **Token pricing**: The New Stack reports [Sam Altman nearly scrapped token pricing before backing off](https://thenewstack.io/openai-token-pricing-reversal), so per-token billing survives for now.
- **Open training stack**: The Institute of Foundation Models [released K2 Horizon 360 under Apache 2.0](https://alphasignal.ai/news/institute-of-foundation-models-opens-full-training-stack-behind-k2-horizon-360): 380 checkpoints, 13T training tokens and RL infrastructure, so outsiders can reproduce the whole recipe, not just run the model.
- **Droid 1.0**: Factory says its agent harness [cuts one 312-tool task from 36 requests to 2](https://alphasignal.ai/news/factory-s-droid-1-0-beats-openai-and-anthropic-agents-while-cutting-costs-51x) by calling tools programmatically (a vendor claim).
- **Claude API limits**: Anthropic [quadrupled the request size ceiling to 128 MB](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-quadruples-claude-api-request-size-to-128-mb-for-image-heavy-workloads) on Messages and Token Counting, with higher per-image resolution, which helps image-heavy workloads.
- **OpenAI's math push**: Sam Altman told a podcast he [sympathizes with mathematicians swamped by AI-generated results](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1023054/altman-cheeky-pint-ai-math-interview) but OpenAI will not stop, and hints at more discoveries to come.
- **Pope on AI**: Pope Leo XIV [called for AI to be "disarmed"](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1023188/pope-leo-disarm-ai) and urged UN members to take steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons at next month's Geneva meeting.

## Startups & Industry

- **Reddit goes logged-in only**: CEO Steve Huffman told shareholders Reddit is [moving to a logged-in-only experience](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/10/reddit-will-require-you-to-log-in-eventually-ceo-confirms/) for better ad signals, and on the call said Google's AI Mode ["does not drive traffic"](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1022848/reddit-ceo-google-ai-mode-traffic), with October search referrals down over 1 million a week. Yesterday's Alphabet sell-off had Google admitting AI Mode hurts classic search; today a major publisher confirms it from the receiving end.
- **Anthropic's S-1 and Taiwan**: A new detail from the filing: Anthropic says it [depends heavily on TSMC chips made in Taiwan](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p70#a261029p70) and warns of disruption from tensions with China, so its growth story carries a geopolitical single point of failure.
- **DeepSeek's round**: The Information says DeepSeek [doubled its nearly $16B round](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p71#a261029p71) because black-market Blackwell chip prices surged, with state-owned Chinese investors at 27%.
- **AI debt**: Microsoft plans to [raise $25B in debt](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/microsoft-25-billion-debt-offering.html) for its AI buildout, and Nvidia is reportedly negotiating to [guarantee more than $5B of construction debt](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115953126511689944) for Nscale's 460MW site. The buildout is increasingly financed by borrowing.
- **Amazon on shopping agents**: Andy Jassy told investors outside AI shopping agents are [still not great, nor more convenient than going to a retailer](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p63#a261029p63).
- **Chip demand**: Intel says its budget Wildcat Lake laptop chips are [sold out](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1022911/intel-wildcat-lake-sold-out-ram-shortage) as RAM shortages push buyers to cheaper machines, while Sandisk reports [revenue up 378% to $11B](https://www.techmeme.com/261029/p62#a261029p62) and an 8-for-1 split.
- **Meta under UK scrutiny**: The UK CMA [opened a probe](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p10#a261030p10) into whether Meta imposed exploitative terms on publishers and advertisers to use their content for AI, and London's High Court [rejected Meta, TikTok and X's challenge](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p15#a261030p15) to Ofcom's Online Safety Act information requests.
- **AI bubble politics**: House Democrats led by Reps Casar and Waters introduced a [resolution opposing an AI bubble bailout for Big Tech](https://progressives.house.gov/2026/10/reps-casar-waters-lead-democratic-resolution-opposing-a-i-bubble-bailout-for-big-tech-commit-to-protecting-american-people-instead), echoing the debt-financed buildout above, while FEC data shows [pro-regulation AI PACs outspending the industry side](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p13#a261030p13) in the midterms.
- **Defense and robotaxis**: Salesforce agreed to [acquire Rune](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p16#a261030p16), an AI military logistics startup, for its Missionforce defense unit, and Waymo will [build a purpose-built Toyota bZ4X robotaxi](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/1023112/waymos-next-robotaxi-is-a-purpose-built-toyota-bz4x) for a Tokyo service.

## Threads

- **AI as attacker**: ARTEX (state-linked espionage at scale) and Epoch's Firefox exploits (open-weight model, $1,000 each) show offense getting cheaper from both ends; the backdoored Claude Code extension, the Azure cross-tenant flaws and the Pentagon agent story show the agent tooling itself becoming the target.
- **Binance and Polymarket**: two exchange-scale security incidents in one day, one at a third-party provider and one in a hot wallet, with Binance covering losses, Polymarket reimbursing traders, and Binance's evening update widening the breach beyond one laptop.
- **Search traffic**: Reddit's "AI Mode does not drive traffic" extends yesterday's Alphabet story, and its logged-in-only move is the publisher response.
- **AI financing and supply**: Microsoft's $25B debt, Nvidia's Nscale guarantee, DeepSeek's black-market chip pricing and Anthropic's TSMC risk all describe the same constraint: compute is expensive and geopolitically exposed.
- **Regulators close in**: the CMA's Meta probe, Ofcom's court win and the House AI-bailout resolution all test how much leeway AI-era platforms get, as GM's $220 million payout shows the cost of selling data.
- **Credits and pricing**: OpenAI matching Anthropic's API credits and Altman keeping token pricing both show labs competing on how developers pay.
