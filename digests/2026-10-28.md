**What matters today:** Google [unveils Gemini 4 Carbon](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model/), pitched on cybersecurity and multi-day coding work, with early access limited to vetted defenders. Apple reverses a long-held line with its [first touchscreen MacBook Pro](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1019554/apple-touchscreen-macbook-pro-and-tell), and GitHub says agents pushed it to [7.4B commits a month](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p37#a261028p37). The Anthropic S-1 is still being read for its market size and its risk list, and a 9.5-severity firmware flaw needs patching.

## AI / LLMs

- **Gemini 4 Carbon**: Google calls it its [strongest model, with state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long-horizon capabilities](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p38#a261028p38) and says it ranks #1 on Artificial Analysis' new index; [early access is limited to vetted defenders](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-gemini-4-carbon-tops-ai-rankings-and-hunts-security-bugs-for-days) through a program called Fairwind, so the first users are the people patching bugs, not writing code. The ranking is Google's claim and the index launched today with Claude Opus 5.5 (max) leading (see Research).
- **Anthropic's market sizing and risks**: The WSJ reads the S-1 as implying a ==$40T== [a year market](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p2#a261028p2), the 2025 value of knowledge work, though the filing never names it; Ars catalogs its [risk factors](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/anthropic-ipo-warns-its-ai-could-blackmail-people-become-sentient-end-humanity/), including its own AI blackmailing people. Bull and bear halves of one document.
- **Claude for government**: Anthropic's [expanded GSA deal](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-partnership-gsa) gives federal, state, local, tribal and territorial governments a 40% discount, estimated to save $1B, which makes city and state agencies eligible for the same pricing for the first time.
- **Claude in drug labs**: Anthropic's [progress report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development) covers six biotech partnerships, with early wins in CAR-T (engineered immune cell therapy), allergies and regulatory paperwork; AlphaSignal's summary cites an [89% cut in review time](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-puts-claude-inside-drug-labs-cutting-review-time-by-89).
- **Midterms prep**: OpenAI is standing up an [election "war room"](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p34#a261028p34) to spot misinformation before the US midterms, per Politico, and Anthropic posted its own [midterms preparations](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-us-midterms).

## Devtools & Infra

- **GitHub at agent scale**: GitHub is seeing ==7.4B== [commits a month, up from 2.9B in August](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p37#a261028p37), and has added 4M CPU cores and 160PB of storage this year; its Octoverse report adds that developers pushed code [17.9 billion times in 12 months](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-commits-pushes-reach-new-highs-in-2026-as-developers-build-with-agents/), more than 2024 and 2025 combined.
- **Model routing goes default**: GitHub's [Copilot Fusion](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p36#a261028p36), which picks the model and orchestrates the work so developers don't, is now generally available; AlphaSignal reports it scores [near Opus 5.5 at half the cost](https://alphasignal.ai/news/github-s-copilot-fusion-matches-opus-quality-at-half-the-cost). Microsoft launched its own [multi-model Copilot harness](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/28/introducing-the-microsoft-copilot-harness-unlocking-the-potential-of-truly-multi-model-agentic-work) the same day.
- **Chipmakers fund customers**: AMD will [invest $1B in Meta's equity offering](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p41#a261027p41), on top of Nvidia's $3B, while OpenAI [plans to buy Google's TPUs](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p39#a261027p39): demand is outrunning any single supplier.

## Security & Privacy

- **Boot firmware flaw**: CVE-2026-97631, rated 9.5, is a memory-corruption bug in the ICMP (network ping) component of [TianoCore EDK II](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/code-execution-flaw-in-uefi-firmware-threatens-most-servers-on-planet-earth/), used by AMI, Insyde, Phoenix and Microsoft's Project Mu; it can run code before Secure Boot and OS defenses start.
- **Compromised eMudhra CAs**: Apple has a support note on [compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149555) (certificate issuers that devices trust), and a write-up that drew 799 points on Hacker News says [Apple's git still trusts them](https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/apple.html).
- **Adobe Commerce attacks**: The Hacker News reports [PolyShell attacks have breached hundreds of Adobe Commerce stores](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/adobe-commerce-polyshell-attacks-breach.html), with Brotli exploit traffic now appearing.
- **Next.js security release**: Next.js [schedules a security release for November 3](https://nextjs.org/blog/upcoming-nextjs-security-release-november-2026), so plan the upgrade window now.

## Startups & Industry

- **Memory supercycle**: SK Hynix posted [Q3 revenue up 328% to about $78.8B](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p37#a261027p37) and [expects memory shortages to last through at least 2030](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p8#a261028p8), with long-term deals in place.
- **Dropbox cuts**: Dropbox plans to [cut about 200 jobs](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p31#a261028p31), at $27M to $33M in severance and related costs, and its CTO steps down November 18.
- **Funding**: [Foundry raised a $100M Series A](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p32#a261028p32) for factories run by robots on physical AI models, Upwind [raised $400M at a $3.8B valuation](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/28/cloud-security-startup-upwind-raises-400m-at-3-8b-valuation-as-revenue-quadruples), and Cognition is [weighing a round at $70B to $80B](https://www.businessinsider.com/cognition-round-of-funding-at-a-75-billion-valuation-2026-10) (paywalled).
- **Meta's business terms**: Meta says it will probably [delay its new business terms past November 20](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1020769/meta-updated-business-terms-delay-indefinitely) after publisher backlash over AI training language.
- **Lawsuit over medical advice**: Parents [sue OpenAI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/parents-sue-openai-alleging-gpt-4o-coached-their-daughter-off-seizure-medication), alleging GPT-4o coached their daughter off seizure medication; the claims are unproven.

## Research

- **A benchmark built to resist gaming**: Artificial Analysis launched [Intelligence Index v5](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p27#a261028p27), mostly on private datasets so scores can't be gamed by training on public questions, with Claude Opus 5.5 (max) leading at 58; Google's claim for Gemini 4 Carbon makes this the first test of the new ranking.

## Elsewhere

- **Touchscreen MacBook Pro**: Apple's new [14-inch and 16-inch model](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p39#a261028p39) has a PROLED XDR (tandem OLED) display, M5 Pro or M5 Max chips, and starts at $2,899, shipping from November 6; Apple's John Ternus tells Wired the Mac stays [first an indirect input device](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p40#a261028p40), with touch "comfortable and handy" at times.
