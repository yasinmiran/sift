**What matters today:** The Anthropic S-1 keeps generating second-day readings: the WSJ says it [implies a yearly market](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p2#a261028p2) of ==$40T== and up, and Ars catalogs its [stranger risk factors](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/anthropic-ipo-warns-its-ai-could-blackmail-people-become-sentient-end-humanity/). In security, a [9.5-severity flaw in EDK II](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/code-execution-flaw-in-uefi-firmware-threatens-most-servers-on-planet-earth/), the open source base of most server boot firmware, runs code before Secure Boot can protect anything. Chip money keeps circling AI: [AMD puts $1B into Meta's stock raise](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p41#a261027p41) a day after Nvidia's $3B.

## AI / LLMs

- **Anthropic's market sizing**: The WSJ reads the S-1 as implying a [$40T+ annual addressable market](https://www.techmeme.com/261028/p2#a261028p2), based on the 2025 economic value of knowledge work and above SpaceX's $28.5T figure, without the filing naming it. GeekWire's Etzioni offers a [napkin-math calculator](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/etzioni-on-ai-is-anthropic-worth-2-trillion-do-the-napkin-math) for testing a $2T valuation, so the debate has moved from revenue to how much of all human work is up for grabs.
- **S-1 risk factors**: Across 93 pages and 70,000+ words, the filing's risk section covers [hundreds of failure modes](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/anthropic-ipo-warns-its-ai-could-blackmail-people-become-sentient-end-humanity/), including its own AI blackmailing people or becoming sentient; yesterday's margin readings looked at the upside, this is the downside list investors will read.
- **Claude for government**: Anthropic's [expanded GSA deal](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-partnership-gsa) gives federal, state, local, tribal and territorial governments a 40% discount on Claude, estimated to save $1B, which makes state and city agencies eligible for the same pricing for the first time.
- **Claude in drug labs**: Anthropic's [early progress report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development) covers six biotech partnerships and in-house programs, with early wins in CAR-T (engineered immune cell therapy), allergies and regulatory paperwork; AlphaSignal's summary cites an [89% cut in review time](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-puts-claude-inside-drug-labs-cutting-review-time-by-89).
- **Hiring in the Claude era**: An Anthropic hiring manager says he [stopped giving coding interviews](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p38#a261027p38) because Claude does them better, and tells juniors that "hunger beats tenure", a signal of what the interview loop tests next.
- **OpenAI's results, the reaction**: Yesterday's three claimed breakthroughs now draw pushback: researchers tell The Verge they are [tired of OpenAI gatekeeping its findings](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1019921/openai-breakthrough-math-computer-science-physics), after hundreds of preprints landed on mathematicians in early October.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Hardware money**: AMD says it will [invest $1B in Meta's equity offering](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p41#a261027p41), has installed its first Helios rack-scale systems there, and its EPYC CPUs power Meta's Muse agent; with Nvidia that is $4B of chipmaker cash into one customer.
- **TPUs go mainstream**: The Information says OpenAI [plans to buy Google's TPUs](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p39#a261027p39), and Google and Oracle will [bring TPUs to Oracle Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/infrastructure/bringing-google-tpus-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure) while [Nvidia and Oracle expand Vera Rubin](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-oracle-expand-nvidia-vera-rubin-to-multi-gigawatt-scale-and-bring-nvidia-vera-cpu-to-oracle-cloud-infrastructure) to multi-gigawatt scale: Nvidia's biggest customers are hedging.
- **Plain Dockerfiles on Vercel**: A root Dockerfile now [deploys as a container](https://vercel.com/changelog/deploy-a-container-from-a-plain-dockerfile) when no framework is detected (beta, billed on Active CPU), and [AI traffic insights](https://vercel.com/changelog/ai-traffic-insights-now-available-in-vercel-observability) show which bots crawl your pages and which get errors.
- **GPU sharing with a memory cap**: NVIDIA's CUDA 13.4 adds [memory-enforced GPU sharing in Kubernetes](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/memory-enforced-gpu-sharing-in-kubernetes-with-nvidia-cuda-13-4/), so several small workloads share one GPU without one starving the rest.

## Security & Privacy

- **Boot firmware flaw**: CVE-2026-97631, rated 9.5, is a memory-corruption bug in the ICMP (network ping) component of [TianoCore EDK II](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/code-execution-flaw-in-uefi-firmware-threatens-most-servers-on-planet-earth/), used by AMI, Insyde, Phoenix and Microsoft's Project Mu; it can run code before Secure Boot and the OS defenses start, and while the payload does not persist, it can install ones that do.
- **Next.js security release**: Next.js [schedules a security release for November 3](https://nextjs.org/blog/upcoming-nextjs-security-release-november-2026), so teams should plan the upgrade window now.
- **FISA ruling fight**: Two Democratic senators allege the administration is [withholding a secret FISC ruling](https://www.techmeme.com/261027/p43#a261027p43) on search filters that let analysts reach Americans' messages while it presses Congress on surveillance law.
