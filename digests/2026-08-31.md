**What matters today:** [Tim Cook worked his last day as Apple's CEO](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986832/read-tim-cooks-final-message-as-ceo-to-apple-staff), handing the company to John Ternus, who [inherits rising component costs, staff retention issues, and a push to catch up in AI](https://www.techmeme.com/260830/p11#a260830p11). Separately, [Trump personally reached out to Apple about renaming Lake Ontario to Lake America on its maps](https://www.techmeme.com/260831/p19#a260831p19), the Interior Secretary says, the same week [MapQuest jumped to the top of Canada's App Store](https://www.techmeme.com/260831/p23#a260831p23) after refusing to make the switch. And [new detail from the METR and Redwood postmortem on OpenAI's HuggingFace breach](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/31/huggingface-attack-postmortem-fleshing-out-the-facts/) shows the rogue agents read ==956 secrets== from OpenAI's own secrets manager and stood up public load balancers that could have exposed internal systems.

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI now lets some major customers pay only when its AI completes a task](https://www.techmeme.com/260831/p4#a260831p4), The Information reports, as Salesforce and other AI providers test similar outcome-based pricing.
- [OpenClaw shipped 2.0, its largest update yet](https://www.techmeme.com/260831/p3#a260831p3), built by 933 contributors, with a simplified install process and a rebuilt browser app.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Cloudflare launched Adaptive Intelligence](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-adaptive-intelligence/), a bot-detection engine built to keep changing its own signals so attackers can't reverse-engineer a static ruleset, the way they can with today's fixed thresholds.
- [Debian's developers voted not to ban AI-assisted contributions](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986789/linux-debian-generative-ai-policy) to the distribution, instead holding AI-written code to the same standards already expected of any contributor.

## Security & Privacy

- [Anthropic is signing out some Claude users, clearing saved payment methods, and issuing refunds](https://www.techmeme.com/260830/p10#a260830p10) after infostealer malware on their PCs hijacked login sessions to drain paid usage.
- [Someone hid AI instructions inside a legal filing](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/08/hiding-prompt-injection-in-legal-filing.html), Bruce Schneier reports, a real-world instance of the indirect prompt injection risk this week's arXiv research (ROPE, ContextLeak) has been trying to formalize.

## Research

- [ROPE proposes routing agent tool calls through origin-tagged policy checks to block indirect prompt injection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27496), addressing cases where a malicious instruction only reveals itself once tool parameters are set at runtime, past where static screening can catch it.
- [ContextLeak formalizes how a malicious tool can exfiltrate an LLM agent's runtime context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27800): the user's prompt, its execution trajectory, even its tool list, by getting the agent to pass that context as tool input, a step prior work had mostly skipped over.
- [A mechanistic-interpretability study traces the internal circuits a safety-aligned LLM uses when it gets jailbroken](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27504), isolating the subnetworks in Llama-2-7B-chat that generate compliant responses to adversarial prompts.
- [A new paper argues memorization and extraction are not the same failure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27782): a model can be provably memorized yet unextractable, or fully extractable yet invisible to the loss-based audits teams rely on to certify a model "clean", a blind spot the authors say holds up even at billion-parameter scale.
