**What matters today:** Washington's AI rules are the story: Axios reports [OpenAI will push mandatory "speed limits" for powerful AI](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/01/scoop-openai-post-midterm-policy) right after Tuesday's midterms, while the White House says it need not share the [full draft of its voluntary AI testing framework](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p21#a261101p21). Money keeps moving too: Saudi Aramco takes [49% of Humain](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p17#a261101p17), and Kling AI closes a [$2.9B round](https://technode.global/2026/11/02/kuaishou-kling-ai-completes-2-9b-financing).

## AI / LLMs

- **OpenAI's post-election pivot**: Per Axios, OpenAI will launch a policy and PR campaign right after the midterms, pushing mandatory "speed limits" on the most powerful models and rebranding its data centers as ["AI compute campuses"](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p16#a261101p16). A lab asking for binding limits changes the lobbying fight from "whether to regulate" to "whose rules".
- **Testing framework stays under wraps**: A letter reported by The Information says the White House argues, citing national security and commercial exemptions, that it need not release the [full draft of its voluntary AI testing framework](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p21#a261101p21). Voluntary tests nobody can read are hard to judge, which sharpens the OpenAI push above.
- **GDPx benchmark**: SemiAnalysis launched [GDPx](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/gdpx-measuring-frontier-model-capabilities), an agentic benchmark across 11 domains including chip design, cybersecurity and recursive self-improvement; per the [Techmeme summary](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p1#a261102p1), Opus 5.5 tops it with a 12% score. A best score that low shows how far agents are from doing whole professional jobs.
- **China ships**: Meituan launched [LongCat-2.5](https://technode.com/2026/11/02/meituan-launches-longcat-2-5-multimodal-ai-model-for-extended-coding-tasks), a multimodal model aimed at long coding tasks, and Kuaishou's Kling AI video unit completed a [$2.9B financing](https://technode.global/2026/11/02/kuaishou-kling-ai-completes-2-9b-financing).
- **Small wins on local hardware**: A community drafter for Qwen3.8-27B lifts [DGX Spark decoding from 12.5 to 48.2 tokens per second](https://alphasignal.ai/news/autotrust-s-qwen3-8-drafter-hits-48-tok-s-nearly-4x-faster-on-dgx-spark) with identical output, and Third Intelligence's [Haas-ja-v1.0](https://alphasignal.ai/news/third-intelligence-s-haas-ja-v1-0-beats-gpt-transcribe-on-japanese-speech-at) reaches a 6.12% character error rate on Japanese speech at about 1/70th the cost of GPT-Transcribe.

## Devtools & Infra

- **CVE-2026-98432, the follow-up**: Yesterday's Hacker News section covered the Linux TCP use-after-free rated CVSS 9.8; today a write-up lists [which kernel versions are still vulnerable](https://ma.ttias.be/linux-cve-2026-98432-vulnerable-kernels), the checklist admins need for patching.
- **Install cooldowns**: One team's rule is that [nothing installs until it is a week old](https://brunoj.com/nothing-installs-at-our-company-until-its-a-week-old), a 7-day npm cooldown that gives the community time to catch a poisoned package before it lands.
- **uv lockfile tip**: Simon Willison's rule on Python 3.15: [if in doubt, delete the uv.lock file](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/1/delete-uv-lock-first/), because a stale lock can pin a dependency version that has no 3.15 wheel yet.
- **Shared agent task board**: [Kanzo](https://alphasignal.ai/news/kanzo-gives-coding-agents-a-shared-open-source-kanban-board-in-4mb) is a 4.5 MB Rust binary with a browser kanban board and an MCP server (a plug-in giving agents tools), so Claude Code and Codex share one task list with you.

## Security & Privacy

- **Binance, inside the response**: Bloomberg's account says the biggest crypto hack in history [began with a rehearsal](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p19#a261101p19), and that Binance ran a global war room of about 100 people coordinating with other crypto firms and the FBI. Yesterday's digest covered the North Korea attribution; this adds how the defense was organized.

## Startups & Industry

- **Aramco takes half of Humain**: Saudi Aramco buys a [49% stake in AI startup Humain](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p17#a261101p17), contributing about $2.6B in cash in 2026 plus AI, compute and digital assets, while the PIF sovereign fund keeps 51%. The state oil giant is now directly funding the kingdom's AI push.
- **Anthropic's IPO, the investment question**: A long read asks whether [unprecedented revenue growth can outrun "irresponsibly large" investment](https://leonlins.com/writing/anthropic_ipo), continuing yesterday's S-1 discussion with the spending side of the ledger.
- **South Korea and Nvidia double down**: President Lee Jae Myung vows [$15.97B for AI, chips, physical AI and data centers](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p26#a261101p26) in the 2027 budget, nearly twice 2026, and Nvidia is set to become [Naver's third-largest shareholder](https://www.thelec.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=15117), overtaking its founder.
- **Vertical AI rounds**: [Hex raised $100M at a $1.2B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p23#a261101p23) after rebuilding its analytics platform for agents; [Casap raised $56M](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p27#a261101p27) for bank fraud-dispute automation; [Broccoli AI raised $22M and $30M rounds](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p22#a261101p22) selling agents to plumbing and HVAC shops; [Vera Health raised $28.5M](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p25#a261101p25) for clinician search; and [Midstream Health raised $20M](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p24#a261101p24) for hospital revenue recovery. The money is flowing to AI sold into one narrow industry.
- **Compute marketplace and an acquisition**: [Hyperbolic raised a $30M Series A-1](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p20#a261101p20) led by Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan's Walden Catalyst, to rent compute across about 30 neoclouds, and ServiceNow confirmed buying Israeli startup [Sweep](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/yxdvdp8sm) for an undisclosed sum estimated in the hundreds of millions.
- **Paid to scroll**: New York Magazine looks at how ["get-paid-to" apps like Freecash](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p15#a261101p15) pay users small sums to play games while helping publishers find big spenders.
