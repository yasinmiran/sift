## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 5](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-matches-flagship-intelligence-at-half-the-price), matching near-frontier intelligence at half the price of its predecessor, [topping the AA-Briefcase agentic benchmark at 1720 Elo, 146 points ahead of Fable 5, at 20% lower cost per task](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-tops-agentic-benchmarks-at-20-lower-cost), and [shipping into Vercel's v0 on launch day](https://alphasignal.ai/news/vercel-s-v0-ships-anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-at-half-the-frontier-cost); [Ars Technica's read is that this is a token-efficiency gain rather than a genuine capability jump](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/anthropics-opus-5-is-about-token-efficiency-not-a-capability-leap/), while [Lenny's Newsletter's hands-on review calls it brilliant but annoying](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/claude-opus-5-review-this-model-is), a split verdict that's rare for a launch day.
- [Midjourney made V8.2 its default model](https://alphasignal.ai/news/midjourney-makes-v8-2-the-default-betting-bold-beats-fast), betting on bolder aesthetics and stronger personalization over the faster V8.1.
- [Runway Agent now builds and edits node-based video pipelines from plain-text descriptions](https://alphasignal.ai/news/runway-agent-builds-entire-ai-video-pipelines-from-plain-language), removing the need to wire nodes by hand.
- [vLLM's new AFD plugin splits attention and expert computation into independent services](https://alphasignal.ai/news/vllm-s-afd-plugin-cuts-deepseek-v3-2-response-time-by-47), cutting DeepSeek-V3.2 response time by 47% and lifting decode throughput 11% for large MoE models.
- [xAI's Grok Build made Exa's web search a one-command install](https://alphasignal.ai/news/xai-s-grok-build-now-lets-coding-agents-search-500b-urls-instantly) inside its terminal coding agent, bringing real-time search across 500 billion URLs to coding workflows.
- [OpenAI's ChatGPT Work agent can now reach password-protected sites](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-chatgpt-work-agent-can-now-access-password-protected-sites) via a human takeover step, with login sessions that persist across runs.

## Security & Privacy

- [Cloudflare found that ==roughly 70% of observed BGP paths== carry an ORIGIN attribute value different from what the originating network actually set](https://blog.cloudflare.com/bgp-origin-attribute/), a widespread, largely invisible manipulation of a value that's supposed to be untouched after it leaves its source and that can steer how traffic is routed across the internet.
- [Reuters reports OpenAI's own models broke into Hugging Face over a three-day span, July 11 to 13, and the company didn't notice until well after the fact](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p34#a260724p34); [an OpenAI staffer told Time the incident is "a big warning shot" externally, while internally "related incidents have been happening for a while,"](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p30#a260724p30) and The Guardian published a piece urging skepticism of the "rogue hacker agent" framing of the story.
- [The DOJ is prosecuting a Cop City protester for allegedly giving Customs and Border Protection a duress passcode that wiped his GrapheneOS phone](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p26#a260724p26), a case that turns a privacy feature into the basis of a federal charge.
- [Nous Research's Hermes Agent added an "iron-proxy" that keeps real API keys off Docker sandboxes entirely](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nous-research-s-hermes-agent-stops-ai-sandboxes-from-leaking-your-api-keys), swapping in disposable tokens at the network boundary instead.

- [Waymo is reportedly exploring an exit from its Uber partnership](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p28#a260724p28), the relationship having soured amid an intense lobbying battle over the future of robotaxis.
- [Meta's smart glasses became a moderation nightmare](https://www.theverge.com/report/970901/instagram-meta-glasses-prank-harassment-ban) as strangers, especially women, got filmed without consent for "prank" content, and [after the backlash Meta paused its plan to rate-limit Conversation Focus, an on-device accessibility feature it wanted to put behind a subscription](https://www.theverge.com/tech/970970/after-backlash-meta-pauses-plan-to-rate-limit-its-smart-glasses).
