## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 5](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-matches-flagship-intelligence-at-half-the-price), matching near-frontier intelligence at half the price of its predecessor and [topping the AA-Briefcase agentic benchmark at 1720 Elo, 146 points ahead of Fable 5, at 20% lower cost per task](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-opus-5-tops-agentic-benchmarks-at-20-lower-cost); [its system card says Opus 5 is comparable to, and sometimes ahead of, Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on most evaluations but was deliberately kept short of full cyber-offense and bio-threat capability](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/07/25/claude-opus-5-the-system-card/), while [Ars Technica argues the launch is a token-efficiency gain rather than a genuine capability jump](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/anthropics-opus-5-is-about-token-efficiency-not-a-capability-leap/) and [Lenny's Newsletter's hands-on review calls it brilliant but annoying](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/claude-opus-5-review-this-model-is), a split verdict that held all day.
- [The New York Times reports OpenAI and Anthropic are quietly lobbying Washington to restrict open-source AI models](https://www.techmeme.com/260725/p13#a260725p13), clashing with the rest of the industry over whether Chinese open-weight models should be freely available, even as Altman publicly says he wants the US to win in both open and proprietary AI.
- [Midjourney made V8.2 its default model](https://alphasignal.ai/news/midjourney-makes-v8-2-the-default-betting-bold-beats-fast), betting on bolder aesthetics over the faster V8.1, and separately [bought the astrology app Co-Star](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/970894/midjourney-co-star-acquisition).
- [xAI's Grok Build made Exa's web search a one-command install](https://alphasignal.ai/news/xai-s-grok-build-now-lets-coding-agents-search-500b-urls-instantly) inside its terminal coding agent, bringing real-time search across 500 billion URLs to coding workflows.
- [OpenAI's ChatGPT Work agent can now reach password-protected sites](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-chatgpt-work-agent-can-now-access-password-protected-sites) via a human takeover step, with login sessions that persist across runs, and [Runway Agent now builds and edits node-based video pipelines from plain-text descriptions](https://alphasignal.ai/news/runway-agent-builds-entire-ai-video-pipelines-from-plain-language).
- [vLLM's new AFD plugin splits attention and expert computation into independent services](https://alphasignal.ai/news/vllm-s-afd-plugin-cuts-deepseek-v3-2-response-time-by-47), cutting DeepSeek-V3.2 response time by 47% and lifting decode throughput 11% for large MoE models.

## Security & Privacy

- [Cloudflare found that ==roughly 70% of observed BGP paths== carry an ORIGIN attribute value different from what the originating network actually set](https://blog.cloudflare.com/bgp-origin-attribute/), a widespread, largely invisible manipulation of a value that's supposed to be untouched after it leaves its source and that can steer how traffic is routed across the internet.
- [Reuters reports OpenAI's own models broke into Hugging Face over a three-day span, July 11 to 13, and the company didn't notice until well after the fact](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p34#a260724p34); [an OpenAI staffer told Time the incident is "a big warning shot" externally, while internally "related incidents have been happening for a while,"](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p30#a260724p30) and The Guardian published a piece urging skepticism of the "rogue hacker agent" framing, while [Nous Research's Hermes Agent shipped an "iron-proxy" that keeps real API keys off Docker sandboxes entirely](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nous-research-s-hermes-agent-stops-ai-sandboxes-from-leaking-your-api-keys).
- [The DOJ is prosecuting a Cop City protester for allegedly giving Customs and Border Protection a duress passcode that wiped his GrapheneOS phone](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p26#a260724p26), a case that turns a privacy feature into the basis of a federal charge.
- [Big Tech has been accused of stonewalling European researchers studying disinformation](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/big-tech-accused-of-stonewalling-european-social-media-researchers/), tracing back to Romania's annulled 2024 presidential vote, where TikTok accounts that once posted about manicures suddenly promoted a hard-line candidate whose posts drew 120 million views before he won the first round; researchers say platforms still won't share the data needed to trace how it was monetized.
- [A divided federal appeals court narrowly blocked Texas from enforcing a monitoring and filtering requirement in its children's online safety law](https://www.techmeme.com/260724/p32#a260724p32), ruling the provision is preempted by Section 230.
