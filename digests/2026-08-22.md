**What matters today:** [Canada suspended trade negotiations with the US and vowed to match its 50% tariffs "dollar for dollar,"](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgvyy4x2mvo) Prime Minister Mark Carney said, [blaming unfair last-minute changes to Washington's proposed terms](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/statements/2026/08/21/statement-prime-minister-carney-canada-us-trade-negotiations) just days after Trump had paused the duties and floated a "DEAL." [Apple is cutting more than 200 jobs across its Vision Pro and Siri teams](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983451/apple-layoffs-vision-pro-siri), largely shutting down the headset's gaming unit two years after the $3,499 device launched as its big spatial-computing bet, and [Anthropic's bankers have told potential investors the company could raise $100B+ in an IPO valuing it at $2T](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p32#a260821p32), more than SpaceX. [Nvidia's AVO agent system scored a perfect 100% across all 183 ARC-AGI-3 levels](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p24#a260821p24), up from a 30% bare-model baseline, and [TikTok agreed to pay $400M to settle a DOJ lawsuit over children's privacy](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983531/tiktok-settle-doj-lawsuit-coppa).

## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic's bankers told potential investors the company could raise $100B+ in its IPO, valuing it at $2T](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p32#a260821p32), a figure that would exceed SpaceX and cap a run of AI dealmaking that has moved tens of billions in equity and debt this week alone.
- Two ARC-AGI results landed the same day: [Nvidia's AVO agent scaffold pushed Claude Opus 5 from a 30% bare-model baseline to a perfect 100% across all 183 ARC-AGI-3 levels](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p24#a260821p24), while [DeepSeek's V4 Pro hit 90.5% on the older ARC-AGI-1 but stalled at 61.3% on ARC-AGI-2](https://alphasignal.ai/news/deepseek-v4-pro-hits-90-on-arc-agi-1-but-stalls-on-harder-puzzles), extra reasoning barely moving the needle once puzzles get harder.
- [DeepSeek quietly shipped an experimental vision model, V4-Flash-Vision-Exp](https://alphasignal.ai/news/deepseek-s-v4-flash-vision-exp-quietly-challenges-anthropic-s-opus-on), bolting image understanding onto V4-Flash at the same price and closing in on Claude Opus 4.8 on multimodal agent tasks.
- [Anthropic hired Amir Salek, who ran Google's TPU business until 2022, to join its compute team](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p23#a260821p23) as it lays the groundwork for building its own chips instead of relying solely on outside suppliers.
- [Anthropic opened Claude Mythos 5, its most capable cybersecurity model, to enterprise customers](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-opens-claude-mythos-5-to-enterprise-teams-for-hunting-code) for vulnerability scans and partner integrations, backed by $35M in open-source credits.
- OpenAI's leadership kept turning over: [President Greg Brockman's role expanded significantly, giving him control over product and scaling teams](https://www.techmeme.com/260822/p2#a260822p2) after a wave of executive departures, the same week [VP of sales Kaylin Voss resigned to return to Salesforce](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p21#a260821p21), a week after her boss Denise Dresser left.
- OpenAI trimmed cost and risk for developers on the same day: it [cut GPT-5.6 Sol's API prices by more than 20% for the next three months](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p26#a260821p26), to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, and [gave developers hard monthly spend caps that cut off traffic once hit](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-gives-developers-hard-spend-limits-to-stop-runaway-api-bills), letting teams attribute cost down to individual API keys.

## Devtools & Infra

- [DHH launched the Omacom Foundation, an $8M non-profit backing his Omarchy Linux distro](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p22#a260821p22), funded by Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Patrick Collison and others.
- [Cloudflare launched Bot Preference Sync](https://blog.cloudflare.com/bot-preference-sync/), which keeps a site's robots.txt automatically in sync with its dashboard-level Search, Agent and Training bot preferences instead of requiring a separately maintained static file.

## Security & Privacy

- [Researchers had two AI models generate about 700,000 candidate genomes for a bacteriophage virus](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/08/ai-is-learning-to-write-genetic-code.html), synthesized 285 of the most promising designs, and found 16 produced viable viruses, some more effective at killing E. coli than the natural original.
- [TikTok agreed to pay $400M to settle a DOJ lawsuit alleging it violated children's privacy law](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983531/tiktok-settle-doj-lawsuit-coppa) by collecting kids' data without parental consent and failing to delete accounts on request; $300M is due immediately, the rest tied to vacating a prior consent decree against predecessor Musical.ly.
- [A landmark trial over Meta's impact on children opened its first week](https://courthousenews.com/hook-harvest-hide-states-slam-metas-secret-strategy-on-first-day-of-jury-trial/), with California, Colorado, Kentucky and New Jersey arguing Meta's business model is "hook, hold, harvest and hide": designed to hook kids, hold their attention, harvest their data, then hide the harms from the public. Meta calls the claims unsubstantiated and points to teen privacy settings and Instagram's one-hour timer.

## Startups & Industry

- [Apple is cutting more than 200 jobs across its Vision Pro and Siri teams](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983451/apple-layoffs-vision-pro-siri), largely shutting down the headset's gaming unit and shrinking the group that makes its immersive video content, two years after the $3,499 device launched as Apple's "spatial computing" bet.
- [Canada suspended trade negotiations with the US and will match Trump's 50% tariffs "dollar for dollar,"](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvgvyy4x2mvo) Prime Minister Mark Carney said, days after Trump had paused the duties and announced a "DEAL"; the US tariffs apply to hockey sticks, building materials, liquor and certain clothing.
- [Amazon raised prices on its Echo, Fire TV, Kindle and eero devices by up to 60%](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983598/amazon-price-increase-echo-kindle-fire-tv), citing higher memory and storage costs; the base Echo Dot jumped from $49.99 to $79.99 and the entry Kindle rose nearly 37% to $149.99, with cheaper products hit hardest.
- [Shein is struggling to grow as it nears a much-delayed Hong Kong listing](https://www.techmeme.com/260822/p1#a260822p1) at a fraction of its once-$100B peak valuation, after abandoning earlier IPO attempts in New York and London under US and European pressure.

## Research

- Google Research published two new studies: [one shows how mobility data gives language models a deeper understanding of place](https://research.google/blog/how-mobility-gives-language-models-a-deeper-understanding-of-place/), and [another details a multi-agent system that mines wearable sensor data for statistically validated health biomarkers](https://research.google/blog/an-ai-tool-for-prioritizing-candidate-biomarkers-from-wearable-sensor-data/), [finding 66 health signals wearables had been missing](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-biomarker-discovery-framework-finds-66-health-signals-wearables-always).
