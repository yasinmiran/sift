**What matters today:** [Apple is cutting more than 200 jobs across its Vision Pro and Siri teams](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983451/apple-layoffs-vision-pro-siri), largely shutting down the headset's gaming unit and shrinking its immersive-video team two years after the $3,499 device launched as the company's big "spatial computing" bet. [Anthropic's bankers have told potential investors the company could raise $100B+ in an IPO that would value it at $2T](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p32#a260821p32), more than Elon Musk's SpaceX, the same day it [hired Google's former TPU chief Amir Salek to help build its own chips](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p23#a260821p23) and [opened its Claude Mythos 5 cybersecurity model to enterprise customers](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-opens-claude-mythos-5-to-enterprise-teams-for-hunting-code). ## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic's bankers told potential investors the company could raise $100B+ in its IPO, valuing it at $2T](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p32#a260821p32), a figure that would exceed SpaceX and cap a run of AI dealmaking that has moved tens of billions in equity and debt this week alone.
- Two ARC-AGI results landed the same day: [Nvidia's AVO agent scaffold pushed Claude Opus 5 from a 30% bare-model baseline to a perfect 100% across all 183 ARC-AGI-3 levels](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p24#a260821p24), while [DeepSeek's V4 Pro hit 90.5% on the older ARC-AGI-1 but stalled at 61.3% on ARC-AGI-2](https://alphasignal.ai/news/deepseek-v4-pro-hits-90-on-arc-agi-1-but-stalls-on-harder-puzzles), extra reasoning barely moving the needle once puzzles get harder.
- [Anthropic hired Amir Salek, who ran Google's TPU business until 2022, to join its compute team](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p23#a260821p23) as it lays the groundwork for building its own chips instead of relying solely on outside suppliers.
- [Anthropic opened Claude Mythos 5, its most capable cybersecurity model, to enterprise customers](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-opens-claude-mythos-5-to-enterprise-teams-for-hunting-code) for vulnerability scans and partner integrations, backed by $35M in open-source credits.
- [OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Sol's API prices by more than 20% for the next three months](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p26#a260821p26), to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Cloudflare launched Bot Preference Sync](https://blog.cloudflare.com/bot-preference-sync/), which keeps a site's robots.txt automatically in sync with its dashboard-level Search, Agent and Training bot preferences instead of requiring a separately maintained static file; crawlers that want the benefit of a "Disallow Training" exception must now also give site owners URL-level visibility into what they crawled.
- [DHH launched the Omacom Foundation, an $8M non-profit backing his Omarchy Linux distro](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p22#a260821p22), funded by Michael Dell, Jack Dorsey, Patrick Collison and others.
- [AWS Glue 6.0 shipped with 30% lower pricing and full Apache Iceberg v3 support](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-glue-6-0-now-available-with-30-lower-price-and-full-apache-iceberg-v3-support/), including a new VARIANT data type for querying semi-structured data without flattening schemas, on a modernized Spark 4.1 runtime.

## Security & Privacy

- [Researchers had two AI models generate about 700,000 candidate genomes for a bacteriophage virus](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/08/ai-is-learning-to-write-genetic-code.html), synthesized 285 of the most promising designs, and found 16 produced viable viruses, some more effective at killing E. coli than the natural original, a capability Bruce Schneier calls exciting and terrifying in the same breath.
- [OpenAI urged California to amend SB 53 to expand safeguards](https://www.techmeme.com/260821/p29#a260821p29), including monitoring of frontier models during training, following recent autonomous AI agent hacks.
- [TikTok agreed to pay $400M to settle a DOJ lawsuit alleging it violated children's privacy law](https://www.theverge.com/tech/983531/tiktok-settle-doj-lawsuit-coppa) by collecting kids' data without parental consent and failing to delete accounts on request; $300M is due immediately, the rest tied to vacating a prior consent decree against predecessor Musical.ly.
