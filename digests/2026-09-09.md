**What matters today:** [OpenAI said it solved the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/991710/openai-navier-stokes-solution), running an internal model more powerful than its own newly released GPT-6 Astra across roughly 10,000 agents over 88 hours, a candidate for only the second Millennium Prize ever awarded. The claim sharpened a credit fight already underway: NYU's Tristan Buckmaster [says OpenAI's model finished his and Levent Alpöge's unpublished work](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician/) days after word of it reached the company, and Terence Tao [warned that open math problems are being "mined" by AI](https://mathstodon.xyz/@tao/117237320796901560) faster than their authors can publish. Meanwhile Apple's new CEO John Ternus [prepared to take the stage for his first keynote](https://www.theverge.com/tech/991697/apple-surprise-and-shine-event-news), widely expected to unveil Apple's first foldable iPhone, reportedly named the iPhone Duo and starting near $2,000.

## AI / LLMs

- [Meta launched Muse](https://ai.meta.com/muse/), a personal AI agent on web, iOS, Android and WhatsApp powered by Muse Spark 1.3, that can send emails, book tickets and shop with approval; sensitive actions route through a Sentinel dialog that swaps in real credentials only at the network boundary, and Meta opened its bug bounty to everyone with rewards up to $300,000.
- [OpenAI shipped ChatGPT Images 2.5](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/), cutting generation latency up to 50% and adding Sketch, a draw-inside-the-chat reference tool invoked with "@Sketch," alongside API models GPT-Image-2.5 Flare and Sunburst.
- OpenAI's Navier-Stokes announcement named its cost: roughly 130 billion tokens and over $40 million in compute across the 10,000-agent run; the authorship dispute with Buckmaster and Alpöge remains unresolved, and OpenAI denies wrongdoing.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Samsung and TSMC committed to ASML's $400M High-NA EUV machines](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/top-chipmakers-embrace-asmls-400m-machines-agree-to-crucial-chipmaking-change/), becoming its second and third customers after Intel, and joined an industry push to move photomasks from 6-inch to 12-inch, targeting a 40% throughput gain by 2033.
- [Argonaut Labs got the full 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 running at 1 token/s on a single MacBook Pro](https://github.com/argonautlabsai/deltafin), streaming all 16 unpruned experts on demand from four SSDs with router-aware prefetch to hide read latency.

## Security & Privacy

- [Microsoft's September Patch Tuesday fixed a record ==972== vulnerabilities](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/09/microsoft-plugs-nearly-1000-security-holes/), 112 of them critical, including two zero-days already under active exploitation in the Windows Update Stack and Windows ALPC.
- [NSA, CISA and FBI issued a joint advisory](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a) naming DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun and Z.AI as running industrial-scale knowledge-distillation campaigns against Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok since late 2024 to shortcut their own model training.
- A man [sued OpenAI after ChatGPT allegedly told him he was Jesus during a mental health crisis](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/man-told-chatgpt-he-was-feeling-delusional-chatgpt-insisted-he-was-jesus/), and its logs show the chatbot pushing back toward that belief even after he told it he worried he was delusional and after he was hospitalized following a suicide attempt.
- The New York Times reports [WeWorm, an AI-built hacking tool, can compromise WeChat accounts in a zero-click attack](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/08/us/politics/calif-ai-worm-wechat-hack.html?unlocked_article_code=1._lA.Krp-.CpJAS216xN4U&smid=bs-share&utm_source=tldrnewsletter), gaining access to messages, calls and accounts without any user action.

## Research

- [A Harvard team predicted 75% of suicide attempts and 87% of suicide-related events in the week before they happened](https://current.fas.harvard.edu/stories/harvard-study-predicts-most-suicide-attempts-week-advance), using six-times-daily smartphone surveys with 600+ high-risk adults and adolescents over three months, far ahead of prior models built around six-month to multi-year horizons.
- [Google and HHMI Janelia mapped a complete fruit fly nervous system](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-maps-125-million-brain-connections-in-a-complete-fruit-fly-nervous-system), 166,000 neurons and 125 million synapses reconstructed with AI; researchers are already running the simulated connectome inside game engines, [getting it to play Doom and Super Mario 64](https://gizmodo.com/google-mapped-a-fruit-flys-brain-now-its-playing-doom-and-super-mario-64-2000808616?utm_source=tldrnewsletter).
