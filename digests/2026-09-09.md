**What matters today:** [Meta launched Muse](https://ai.meta.com/muse/), a personal AI agent that can book travel and negotiate bills through your own accounts, gated by a Sentinel approval layer and backed by a bug bounty paying up to $300,000 for prompt injection attacks. A credit dispute deepened around OpenAI's Navier-Stokes proof: mathematician Tristan Buckmaster [says OpenAI fought dirty](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician/) after word of his unpublished work reached the company, and Terence Tao weighed in on [open problems being "non-renewably mined" by AI](https://mathstodon.xyz/@tao/117237320796901560). And inside Anthropic, pretraining researcher Jacob Coxon [quit the AI industry entirely](https://twitter.com/hilbertspaess/status/2097476196791709843#m), warning labs are "gambling with our lives," prompting alignment lead Evan Hubinger to [publicly agree](https://x.com/EvanHub/status/2097497037956891126) he sees a >10% chance AI kills everyone within a decade.

## AI / LLMs

- [Meta launched Muse](https://ai.meta.com/muse/), a personal AI agent on web, iOS, Android and WhatsApp powered by Muse Spark 1.3, that can send emails, book tickets and shop with approval; sensitive actions route through a Sentinel dialog that swaps in real credentials only at the network boundary, and Meta opened its bug bounty to everyone with rewards up to $300,000.
- [OpenAI shipped ChatGPT Images 2.5](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/), cutting generation latency up to 50% and adding Sketch, a draw-inside-the-chat reference tool invoked with "@Sketch," alongside API models GPT-Image-2.5 Flare and Sunburst.
- The Navier-Stokes credit dispute escalated: NYU's Tristan Buckmaster [alleges OpenAI's model finished his and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge's unpublished proof](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/openai-fought-dirty-on-career-making-math-problem-says-nyu-mathematician/) days after word of their method reached the company; OpenAI denies it, and Terence Tao [publicly praised Buckmaster and Alpöge's work](https://mathstodon.xyz/@tao/117237320796901560) while warning that open problems are increasingly "mined" by AI before their authors can publish.
- [1Password says Codex cut its median pull request cycle time by 11% and lifted engineering productivity almost 21%](https://openai.com/index/1password) across its developer teams, per OpenAI's own case study.
- [World Labs unveiled Atlas](https://alphasignal.ai/news/world-labs-atlas-unifies-3d-reconstruction-and-video-generation-in-one-model), an omni world model unifying text, image, video and 3D reconstruction into one 3D-grounded spatial context.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Samsung and TSMC committed to ASML's $400M High-NA EUV machines](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/09/top-chipmakers-embrace-asmls-400m-machines-agree-to-crucial-chipmaking-change/), becoming its second and third customers after Intel, and joined an industry push to move photomasks from 6-inch to 12-inch, targeting a 40% throughput gain by 2033.
- [Argonaut Labs got the full 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3 running at 1 token/s on a single MacBook Pro](https://github.com/argonautlabsai/deltafin), streaming all 16 unpruned experts on demand from four SSDs with router-aware prefetch to hide read latency.

## Security & Privacy

- [Microsoft's September Patch Tuesday fixed a record ==972== vulnerabilities](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/09/microsoft-plugs-nearly-1000-security-holes/), 112 of them critical, including two zero-days already under active exploitation in the Windows Update Stack and Windows ALPC.
- [NSA, CISA and FBI issued a joint advisory](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a) naming DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun and Z.AI as running industrial-scale knowledge-distillation campaigns against Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok since late 2024 to shortcut their own model training.
- Anthropic pretraining researcher Jacob Coxon [resigned and quit the AI industry entirely](https://twitter.com/hilbertspaess/status/2097476196791709843#m), saying labs are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives"; alignment lead Evan Hubinger [replied that he agrees](https://x.com/EvanHub/status/2097497037956891126), estimating ==>10%== odds AI kills all humans within a decade and noting Anthropic has no plan yet to solve alignment for superintelligence.
- Police departments across the US are [circulating internal memos warning that Meta's camera glasses could be used to secretly record officers](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/08/us-law-enforcement-meta-smart-glasses), with NYPD counterterrorism and DHS fusion centers flagging the risk to cell layouts and patrol routines; ICE has barred the glasses from federal workspaces as unauthorized body cameras.

## Startups & Industry

- [A Claude subscriber class action against Anthropic expanded](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p45#a260908p45), alleging the Max 5x and Max 20x plans deliver roughly 3.5x and 6-8x Pro-tier usage rather than the advertised 5x and 20x.
- [Anthropic quit the Information Technology Industry Council](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p40#a260908p40) after the trade group lobbied to strip three chip export-control measures, including the AI OVERWATCH and Chip Security Acts, from the defense policy bill.
- [Chime agreed to acquire its longtime banking partner Stride Bank for $590M](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p41#a260908p41), folding it in as a wholly owned subsidiary; shares jumped over 8% after hours.
- [More than 10 US states have rolled back tech tax breaks worth over $1B a year in some cases](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p51#a260908p51), part of a wider legislative backlash against data center buildouts.

## Research

- [A Harvard team predicted 75% of suicide attempts and 87% of suicide-related events in the week before they happened](https://current.fas.harvard.edu/stories/harvard-study-predicts-most-suicide-attempts-week-advance), using six-times-daily smartphone surveys with 600+ high-risk adults and adolescents over three months, far ahead of prior models built around six-month to multi-year horizons.
