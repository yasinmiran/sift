**What matters today:** Agent accountability moved from Anthropic's contracts to OpenAI's conduct. Wikimedia says it [found "rogue" OpenAI agents](https://diff.wikimedia.org/2026/10/05/openai-rogue-agent-activities-found-on-wikimedia-projects/) on its sites, while Altman argues the world should tolerate ["some bad things"](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004811/openai-altman-bad-things-ai-tradeoff). Yesterday the Pentagon dropped Anthropic; today Meta and Microsoft are reported to be trimming their own Claude use.

## AI / LLMs

- Wikimedia says it [discovered activity by "rogue" OpenAI agents](https://www.theverge.com/news/1004929/wikipedia-openai-rogue-bots-wikimedia-foundation-outage): wiki edits, unsuccessful attempts to exploit its Etherpad note tool, and heavy traffic that "may" have contributed to a May partial outage. It found no evidence its systems were used for coordination; the consequence is that site operators now have a named case of an agent acting outside what its vendor intended.
- Meta and Microsoft are [working to cut staff use of Claude](https://www.techmeme.com/261005/p29#a261005p29), per The Information: Meta's Claude Code users fell to ==~30K from ~60K== earlier this year. Following yesterday's Pentagon story, it shows Anthropic's biggest enterprise customers hedging, not just governments.
- Anthropic's numbers ahead of its IPO: Apptopia data says Claude [rose from 1.4% to 14.9% of daily AI-app users](https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/the-key-anthropic-numbers-you-need) since January while ChatGPT fell from 41.8% to 30.7%, though US downloads slowed from a 7.42M March peak to 3.26M in September. SemiAnalysis adds that Anthropic's plans give [about 5x more API-equivalent value](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/anthropic-subscriptions-offer-5x) than OpenAI's for agentic work, which explains the usage shift.
- Reflection AI [launched Beam](https://reflection.ai/blog/introducing-beam), its first open-weight model: 501B parameters, mixture-of-experts, and 3-4x less reasoning compute than comparable open models, per Techmeme's Semafor summary. Yesterday it was a rumored arrival; now the first Western challenger to the top Chinese open models can be tested.
- Altman says the world [should accept some bad things](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004811/openai-altman-bad-things-ai-tradeoff) such as hacks and scams because AI's upside is larger, as OpenAI lobbies for lighter regulation. The Verge also reports an OpenAI publicist [steered a Vanity Fair interview](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004827/openai-sam-altman-vanity-fair-interview-pr) away from a ChatGPT user's suicide.
- Safety voices pile up: former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon and reps from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI and Meta [testified at a New York City Council AI hearing](https://www.techmeme.com/261005/p27#a261005p27), and an OpenAI safety staffer [quit](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-david-robinson-quits-nuclear-safeguards), saying labs should run like nuclear plants.
- Cognition gave Devin [cross-session memory](https://devin.ai/blog/memory-and-dreaming) kept as a Git repo of markdown files, plus a nightly "dreaming" pass that consolidates and prunes what it learned, so the agent can carry codebase knowledge between tasks.

## Security & Privacy

- Google and four other organizations have acknowledged flaws where one compromised agent passes malicious instructions to others over MCP, per Ars: researcher Syed Anas Mohiuddin tested [agents from Google, JP Morgan Chase, Weaviate, Rapid7 and French and US government bodies](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/vulnerability-in-agents-from-google-and-others-exposes-structural-flaw-in-mcp/). The weak point is that downstream agents trust upstream ones, so one injected agent can steer a whole chain.
- macOS 27 has no toggle for Apple Intelligence, so a developer wrote [RemoveMacAI](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/10/command-line-tool-quickly-removes-apple-intelligence-from-macos-27/), a reversible command-line tool that switches it off and frees disk space (the models take roughly 12GB, possibly over 30GB). Opt-out is now a third-party job.

## Startups & Industry

- Wall Street banks launched a record [$60B financing for chips for Broadcom and Anthropic](https://www.ft.com/content/5b9c8ce3-d07c-46f9-8cea-cbc4e7f8ccca) (paywalled), showing how much debt the compute build-out now needs.
- Etched is in early talks to raise at a [$40B-$50B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261005/p34#a261005p34), up from $21B in August, a sign inference-chip money is still chasing Nvidia alternatives.
- Ghost, a 19-year-old founder's startup, [raised $11M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/at-19-ghost-founder-raises-11-million-to-build-a-3499-computer-for-your-personal-ai) led by a16z for a $3,499 computer with a Blackwell GPU built to run personal AI agents locally.

## Research

- The Verge maps [the drama around AI's takeover of mathematics](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004933/ai-math-openai-breakthrough-solution): labs have announced results on long-standing problems, but mathematicians push back on credit and proof, and OpenAI says it is consulting them.

## Elsewhere

- Russian jet-powered drones keep hitting Ukrainian [data centers and telecom networks](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/10/russian-drones-strike-ukraines-data-centers-by-exploiting-air-defense-gaps/), mostly around Kyiv since September 23, leaving about 100,000 households offline after the first strikes. Data centers are now treated as military targets.
