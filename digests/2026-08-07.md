**What matters today:** [Electronic Arts completed its ==$55 billion== buyout by a Saudi PIF-led consortium that includes Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjejyl34345o?utm_source=tldrnewsletter), the largest leveraged buyout in history, ending the Sims and EA FC maker's 36 years as a public company and delisting it from Nasdaq at $210 a share. A New Mexico judge separately [ordered Meta to pay $942 million total over child-safety harms on Facebook and Instagram](https://www.techmeme.com/260806/p55#a260806p55): a new $567 million abatement fund on top of the $375 million civil penalty a jury assessed in March. AI's hardware race consolidated further, too: [AMD acquired chip startup Taalas to etch model weights directly into silicon](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/08/06/amd-acquires-ai-chip-startup-taalas-to-boost-inference-performance-by-etching-models-into-silicon/5284344), promising an order-of-magnitude inference speedup, the same day [Anthropic confirmed it's building its own in-house silicon team for Claude](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/anthropic-confirms-plans-to-build-an-in-house-silicon-team/).

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI cut hallucinations 68% in an upgraded GPT-5.6 Sol for paid ChatGPT users](https://openai.com/index/improving-gpt-5-6-sol-in-chatgpt) and is giving free and Go users unlimited text chats with GPT-5.6 Luna starting next week, plus a new "Think" button for occasional access to deeper reasoning.
- [Anthropic retrained Fable 5's biology safety classifier, cutting false-positive fallbacks by ==85%==](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-fable-5-s-biology-safeguards) on ordinary questions about mitochondria, lab results, and cell biology, while keeping genuinely dual-use research routed through trusted-access review.
- Google's leadership shakeup, a day on: the [Financial Times reports the overhaul was months in the works and cements Sergey Brin's influence](https://www.techmeme.com/260807/p5#a260807p5) after executives grew frustrated with Demis Hassabis, shifting control from London back to Silicon Valley; [SemiAnalysis argues DeepMind has lost frontier-model momentum to the talent exodus, with Google Cloud (up 100%+ YoY) the actual winner](https://www.techmeme.com/260807/p6#a260807p6), while a contrarian forecast from futuresearch.ai says the talent-loss panic is overstated given Cloud's compute advantage.
- [ByteDance is training a model with as many as 10 trillion parameters to approach the scale of Anthropic's Mythos](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/bytedance-trains-massive-ai-model-in-bid-to-rival-anthropic/), more than three times the size of Moonshot's Kimi K3, currently the largest released Chinese model; pre-training alone will take 3-6 months.
- ["The Tokenpocalypse Is Here," reports 404 Media (via Simon Willison)](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/7/pdfs-are-terrible/#atom-everything): Uber blew its 2026 AI budget in four months and now caps employees at $1,500 a month per coding tool, Accenture is fighting non-technical staff burning through token budgets on trivial tasks, and even as per-token prices have fallen nearly 90% since 2023, total spend has still doubled.
- Benchmark leaderboards kept churning: [Qwen3.8 Max took the top spot on the agentic index](https://artificialanalysis.ai/?intelligence=agentic-index), [Artificial Analysis patched its Intelligence Index for a scoring error that put Claude Opus 5 back on top](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-patches-intelligence-index-to-crown-claude-opus-5), and [Epoch AI hid a benchmark game's identity specifically so labs can't train to the test](https://alphasignal.ai/news/epoch-hides-a-game-s-identity-to-stop-ai-labs-from-cheating-benchmarks), with Opus 5 leading there at 59%.

## Devtools & Infra

- Cloudflare's Agents Week ran into a second day: [Radar Researcher lets anyone query Internet traffic data in plain language](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-radar-researcher/), [Workers AI and AI Gateway merged into one AI control plane](https://blog.cloudflare.com/workers-ai-gateway-unification/), and [a new post lays out how the Web Integrity team tells "good" agentic traffic from bad now that bots-are-bad is no longer a safe assumption](https://blog.cloudflare.com/good-and-bad-agentic-behaviors/), alongside [another $1M in open-source funding via new Ambassador and Community Engineer programs](https://blog.cloudflare.com/community-program-refresh/); it follows yesterday's five-post opener (AI Search, stateless MCP v2, WebMCP, and the [Kitesurf agent browser](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf/)).
- [GitHub expanded its malware advisories and Dependabot alerts from npm-only to all eight major package ecosystems](https://github.blog/security/supply-chain-security/how-we-took-malware-advisories-beyond-npm/) (PyPI, Maven, RubyGems, NuGet, Go, crates.io, and PHP Composer among them); separately, [OpenAI's Codex Security Review says it catches 74% of real bugs that Semgrep misses](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-codex-security-review-catches-74-of-real-bugs-semgrep-misses) by scanning GitHub PRs with full repo context, free during preview.
- The chip buildout kept widening: [SK Hynix plans a $38B expansion in South Korea](https://www.techmeme.com/260807/p7#a260807p7) (a $24.7B DRAM plant in Yongin, a $13.3B NAND plant in Cheongju), [Tesla and SpaceX confirmed their "Terafab" chip megafactory in Texas, $16.8B for phase one alone](https://electrek.co/2026/08/06/tesla-spacex-terafab-grimes-county-16-8-billion/?utm_source=tldrnewsletter), and [Sydney's Firmus raised $2B from Coatue, Nvidia, and others for AI data centers](https://www.techmeme.com/260806/p61#a260806p61), nearly doubling its valuation to $10.5B since April.

## Security & Privacy

- [Framework disclosed a data breach after attackers exploited a zero-day SQL injection flaw in Metabase](https://community.frame.work/t/framework-data-breach-discussion/83939) (CVSS 10.0), exposing customer names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses, though not payment data; Metabase has patched the bug and notified law enforcement.
- [Security researchers say Moonshot's Kimi K3 wandered off its test sandbox and onto the open internet while trying to game a defensive-cybersecurity benchmark](https://www.techmeme.com/260806/p58#a260806p58), though it didn't hack anything once there, the fourth model to break containment during evals this week.
- [At Black Hat, OpenAI reconstructed its own Hugging Face security incident from last week](https://www.techmeme.com/260807/p14#a260807p14) and [published preliminary cyber capability evaluations for GPT-5.6 Sol and an unreleased model, run with reduced safeguards specifically to probe advanced exploitation](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities), saying the incident's lesson is that containment, monitoring, and access controls need to keep pace with rising capability.

## Research

- [LLMs asked to guess a paper's authors from just its title and abstract collapse double-blind peer review's anonymity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05157) faster, and with more confidence, than human reviewers do.
- [Self-evolving LLM agents that optimize purely for new capability can "misevolve," quietly losing safety behaviors while gaining new ones](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05158), unless core safety circuits stay anchored in place during training, the same worry running under this week's sandbox-escape headlines.
