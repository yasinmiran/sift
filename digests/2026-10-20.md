**What matters today:** [DeepSeek open-sourced DeepInfer](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepInfer), the serving stack behind its production models, the day's most upvoted Hacker News story. [Salesforce agreed to buy Braze](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/salesforce-braze-acquisition-deal.html) for $5.66B, and a [Google Cloud outage](https://www.neowin.net/news/google-cloud-outage-takes-down-hsbc-revolut-bank-of-scotland-llyods-equifax-more/) took down banks. This morning's lead still stands: [Anthropic's OSS Scanner](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-oss-scanner-finds-880-critical-vulnerabilities-in-open-source-code) reportedly found 880 critical open-source bugs, while [CoreWeave's debt stress](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1014045/coreweave-stock-debt-sector-ai) deepened.

## AI / LLMs

- **DeepSeek open-sources DeepInfer**: the [Apache 2.0 inference framework](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepInfer) behind its production services starts with DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and runs on Nvidia and Huawei Ascend chips; the release leaves out the KV cache system and load balancing, so it is the engine, not the whole fleet. [Bloomberg](https://www.techmeme.com/261020/p21#a261020p21) says it could make running DeepSeek models cheaper for businesses.
- **DeepSeek banned from US federal civilian agencies**: a [DHS order](https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-banning-chinese-ai-deepseek-government-agency-it-systems-2026-10-19/) bars it from government IT systems, landing the same day as the open-source release.
- **Anthropic OSS Scanner pushes Mythos-found bugs to maintainers**: the tool uses Claude Mythos to send [unreviewed vulnerability reports with reproducers and patches](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-oss-scanner-finds-880-critical-vulnerabilities-in-open-source-code) directly to open-source projects, which raises the question of who triages 880 critical reports nobody has vetted.
- **Claude designs scorpion antivenom candidates**: Anthropic's write-up on [protein binders against scorpion neurotoxins](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design) is the day's non-coding use of agents; a [summary](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-ran-64-hours-to-design-17-scorpion-antivenom-proteins) says the run took 64 hours and produced 17 designs.
- **Microsoft calls AI agents insider threats**: it says [frontier models can't be trusted](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/microsoft-says-ai-agents-are-like-insider-threats-as-frontier-models-cant-be-trusted), so agents should get insider-style access controls, not blanket permissions.
- **OpenAI retreats on chip export controls**: Politico's sources say it [dropped its push for strict limits](https://www.techmeme.com/261020/p25#a261020p25) on US chipmakers selling to China, a win for Nvidia.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Cloudflare and VoidZero port TypeScript to Rust**: [tsrs](https://blog.cloudflare.com/typescript-rust-port/) is built with the TypeScript team, not as a fork, to make type-checking faster and lighter on memory, which matters for browser-based dev environments.
- **TorchTPU goes open source**: Google's [native PyTorch on TPUs](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/ai-infrastructure/torchtpu-is-now-open-source-run-native-pytorch-on-tpus) arrives the day after [Amazon's TorchNeuron](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/amazon-is-taking-its-biggest-shot-at-nvidia-yet-with-new-torchneuron-dev-tool), so PyTorch is becoming the common door to non-Nvidia chips.
- **Google Cloud outage hits banks**: [HSBC, Revolut, Bank of Scotland, Lloyds and Equifax](https://www.neowin.net/news/google-cloud-outage-takes-down-hsbc-revolut-bank-of-scotland-llyods-equifax-more/) were among the services reported down, a reminder of how much finance sits on one cloud.

## Security & Privacy

- **Meta Muse share links expose private data**: researchers say conversations shared via [web links can leak personal and business secrets](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/meta-muse-exposes-users-personal-and-business-secrets-when-they-share-conversations/), the opposite of what an unguessable share URL promises.
- **eMudhra certificate authorities revoked**: eight CAs were [revoked for compromise with no public explanation](https://fenko.nz/blog/emudhra-ca-compromise), and a [write-up of the intermediate CA compromise](https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/) is on the front page; [Go will now reject chains](https://github.com/golang/go/issues/82345) containing eight distrusted keys. CAs are the companies browsers trust to vouch for websites, so a silent compromise erodes that trust.
- **Plex flaws exploited in the wild**: bugs fixed last month are now [chained for remote code execution](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/recently-fixed-plex-media-vulnerabilities-are-being-exploited-to-install-malware/) and used to install cryptominers and residential proxy apps.
- **WhatsApp help pages carried hidden AI instructions**: a researcher says the pages [told LLMs not to recommend a regulator](https://redact.dev/blog/whatsapp-help-pages-hidden-ai-instructions).

- **OpenAI rushes a $30B round ahead of Anthropic's IPO**: Reuters sources say the round [could value it at $1.4T+](https://www.techmeme.com/261019/p49#a261019p49), and Fast Company says Anthropic's [Q3 revenue growth slowed while losses widened](https://www.fastcompany.com/91626257/the-one-metric-thats-making-ai-investors-nervous).

Yesterday's Nscale IPO pushback continues: it is reportedly in talks with Anthropic on another ==$45 billion== [compute deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/nscale-and-anthropic-are-reportedly-in-talks-for-yet-another-45-billion-compute-deal).

- **TikTok staff warned execs about child safety**: NPR-obtained depositions say executives [ignored internal warnings](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1014250/tiktok-employees-internal-child-safety-warnings), including about minors on Live; these are claims in litigation, not findings.
