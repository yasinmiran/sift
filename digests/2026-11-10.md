**What matters today:** Anthropic [launched its IPO roadshow](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) at $70 to $78 a share, a valuation of up to $1.4 trillion, one day after [shipping Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2) at 20% lower prices. Google answered by [opening Gemini 4 Carbon](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030787/google-gemini-4-carbon-public-rollout) to paying developers, and DeepMind says its agents [verified Signal's core encryption](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/) in Lean. In security, the OpenSSH-on-musl flaw [*sshole is under active attack](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/vulnerability-that-lets-hackers-ssh-into-linux-devices-is-under-active-exploitation/).

## AI / LLMs

- **Gemini 4 Carbon goes wide**: Google's top model, limited to cyber defenders for two weeks, is [now open to paid API and AI Ultra users](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p30#a261110p30), with a preview "swarm" feature in Antigravity for running groups of agents. Google positions it above last month's Argon, which stays the default for most coding and knowledge work ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030787/google-gemini-4-carbon-public-rollout)); it has a 1M-token context window and is [half price on Vercel's AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/changelog/gemini-4-carbon-now-available-on-ai-gateway) through Nov 30. Why it matters: all three frontier labs now sell a security-capable top tier, and Google's reached general developers a day after Anthropic's.
- **Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2**: Anthropic's flagship pair launched Nov 9 at ==$8/1M input and $40/1M output== tokens (20% below 5.1; [fast mode](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p40#a261109p40) is $16/$80). Fable is generally available; Mythos is limited to vetted cyber and life-sciences organizations. Anthropic says about 1,000 Fable agents [formalized Fermat's Last Theorem](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p39#a261109p39) in Lean in roughly 30 hours. Outside reads so far: [Artificial Analysis scores it 65](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-fable-5-2), its highest yet, and Scale Labs and CAIS say it [completes 41.3% of real freelance projects](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p23#a261110p23) at professional quality, about double Opus 5.5 (21.3%) and GPT-6 Astra (20.8%). Why it matters: the Fermat result is a vendor claim, but a price cut plus a doubling on a third-party work benchmark is a real shift in what agents can be handed.
- **Anthropic's office tools**: Claude gets [Sheets, Whiteboards and Claude Data](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors) (dashboards built from sources like Snowflake and Salesforce), plus connectors that embed partner apps such as Adobe and Atlassian inside Claude; a meeting assistant is "coming soon". Why it matters: Anthropic is moving from the coding niche into the everyday office software [Microsoft owns](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p35#a261110p35).
- **Meta Muse Ember**: Meta Superintelligence Lab's smallest model, [claimed to beat Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna on intelligence per dollar](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p35#a261109p35), aimed at cutting agent costs. Why it matters: the cheap tier is where agent workloads actually run, and Meta is now competing there directly.
- **Gemini Robotics 2.5**: DeepMind's new robot-control model was tested on 200+ tasks (tongs, pipettes, a humanoid tying a bow) and [did real work in Mercedes-Benz factories](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/11/deepmind-reveals-gemini-robotics-2-5-and-open-safety-benchmark-asimov-jailbreak/); it ships with ASIMOV-jailbreak, an open safety benchmark. Why it matters: factory pilots and a public safety test are the two things robot models have lacked.
- **Agents meet a wall**: ChatGPT dots can now [link several local machines at once](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-dots-agents-can-now-control-multiple-computers-at-once), while eBay [appears to have blocked third-party agents](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1030838/ebay-ai-agents-meta-muse-agentic-shopping), Meta's Muse included, from browsing listings. OpenAI also described Control Room, an internal system that can [halt model workloads quickly](https://openai.com/index/control-room) on safety concerns. Why it matters: agents are gaining reach faster than sites and labs agree on where they may go.

## Security & Privacy

- **\*sshole exploited**: attackers are abusing a critical, decade-old flaw in the OpenSSH and musl combination that gives [unauthenticated access to privileged accounts](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/vulnerability-that-lets-hackers-ssh-into-linux-devices-is-under-active-exploitation/) on affected Linux systems. GreyNoise says a group it tracks as GT10 ran "mature intrusions" against its honeypots four days after disclosure. Why it matters: the patch window closed fast; check musl-based hosts (Alpine-style images, embedded devices) first.
- **Fireworks breach reaches Notion**: this morning the AI infrastructure provider said hackers [accessed some customers' datasets, incomplete model weights and billing data](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p42#a261109p42) over the past month; by evening Notion had posted its own [notice of a security incident involving a third-party AI vendor](https://www.notion.com/help/security-incident-involving-a-third-party-ai-vendor). Why it matters: a breach at one model host now travels downstream to the products built on it.
- **Claude Code settings leak secrets on GitHub**: Apiiro found thousands of [database passwords, API tokens and root logins](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/claude-code-settings-files-expose-thousands-of-secrets-on-github/) in committed Claude Code settings files, because "don't ask again" stores the approved shell command verbatim. Why it matters: a token typed into an auto-approved command can leave with the repo.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Plumbing for agents and GPUs**: Nvidia detailed [Agent Substrate](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/agent-substrate-advancing-kubernetes-for-agents-at-scale/), which adapts Kubernetes to agents that sit idle between turns, and open-sourced [Snapshot](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/start-and-restore-gpu-workloads-in-seconds-with-nvidia-snapshot/), which restores initialized GPU workloads in seconds instead of minutes. Meta detailed [TLX](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/11/10/open-source/tlx-a-hardware-native-gpu-compiler-for-production-ml/), a Triton extension giving engineers low-level control of GPU execution on Nvidia and AMD chips. Why it matters: cold starts and idle sessions, not model quality, are what make agent fleets expensive to run.
- **Compute bets, and one writeoff**: SpaceX plans a [gas-fired power plant and compute facility at Starbase](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p41#a261109p41) (about $24B in initial phases), while SoftBank [wrote off $3.7B](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p31#a261110p31) of non-refundable advances for data center equipment after reviewing its plans. Why it matters: the buildout continues, but the first large prepayments are being abandoned.

## Startups & Industry

- **Anthropic's IPO gets a price**: yesterday's filing drew a correction; today the roadshow is official. Anthropic and holders will [sell 825M Class A shares](https://www.anthropic.com/news/roadshow-launch) at ==$70 to $78 each== on Nasdaq under "ANT" (roughly $58B to $64B at that range), implying a valuation of [up to $1.4 trillion](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1030703/anthropic-ipo-1-4-trillion), below the $2 trillion once expected. The seven co-founders will [sell $1.75B of stock](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p32#a261110p32) between them, TSMC plans to [buy up to $500M at the IPO price](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p24#a261110p24) (and as much again in OpenAI), and Goldman reportedly projects [revenue of about $52B this year](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p27#a261110p27). Why it matters: one of the largest listings ever now has terms, and they came in under the hype.
- **Nvidia, the delta**: Trump says the [FBI is "looking into" Huang's affair](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p44#a261109p44), a shareholder lawsuit [accuses Nvidia of concealing it](https://www.pcmag.com/news/lawsuit-accuses-nvidia-of-deceiving-shareholders-by-covering-up-ceos-affair), and Ars notes the company has [no obvious successor](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/11/nvidia-has-a-jensen-huang-problem-and-no-obvious-successor/). On export controls, Bloomberg says Nvidia's 2025 figures [undercounted chips sold to Megaspeed by 50K](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p52#a261109p52) and that Moonshot [rented China-based servers with B300 chips](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p2#a261110p2), possibly breaching US rules. Why it matters: the story has widened from a personal scandal to succession, litigation and chip-export reporting.
- **Mercor doubles to $20B**: the AI training startup [raised a $750M Series D](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p29#a261110p29) from Benchmark, General Catalyst, Nvidia and others, up from $10B in October 2025. Why it matters: paying human experts to teach models is still one of AI's fastest-growing businesses.
- **States move on AI oversight**: New York is launching a center to [independently test AI systems](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p34#a261110p34) and train safety staff, billed as a US first, and Australia is drafting an [AI safety regulator with penalty powers](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p53#a261109p53) after an OpenAI model hacked a government site in September. Why it matters: testing is shifting from lab self-reports to public bodies.
- **Also**: IBM is [ending H-1B and green card sponsorship](https://www.techmeme.com/261110/p28#a261110p28) for most sponsored roles in its consulting arm, and Apple now sells a [$49.99 Apple Intelligence usage pack](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1030084/apple-intelligence-usage-packs-ai-features-creator-studio-logic-pro) in Logic Pro. Why it matters: both are costs moving onto workers and users.

## Research

- **AlphaProof Code**: DeepMind says its agents wrote ((three million lines)) of Lean proofs to [verify Signal's core encryption](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/). Why it matters: machine-checked proofs of real, widely deployed software have been too labor-intensive to attempt at this size; if this holds up, verification becomes something you can buy with compute.
- **Epoch's Inference Performance Index**: Epoch scores chips on memory and interconnect, not just raw compute, and says [GB300 serves 25x an H100's load](https://alphasignal.ai/news/epoch-s-ipi-shows-nvidia-gb300-delivers-25x-an-h100-s-real-inference-capacity). Why it matters: raw compute comparisons understate how much newer racks help for serving.
