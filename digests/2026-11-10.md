**What matters today:** Anthropic [shipped Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2) on Monday at 20% lower prices than 5.1, claiming a formalized proof of Fermat's Last Theorem along the way. Meta answered the same day with the cheap [Muse Ember](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember). In security, the OpenSSH-on-musl flaw [*sshole is now under active attack](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/vulnerability-that-lets-hackers-ssh-into-linux-devices-is-under-active-exploitation/), and the Nvidia saga gained an [FBI angle](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p44#a261109p44).

## AI / LLMs

- **Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2**: Anthropic's flagship pair launched Nov 9, priced at ==$8/1M input and $40/1M output== tokens (20% below 5.1; [fast mode](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p40#a261109p40) is $16/$80), with outputs up to 30% faster. Fable is generally available; Mythos is limited to vetted cyber and life-sciences organizations. Anthropic says about 1,000 Fable agents [formalized Fermat's Last Theorem](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p39#a261109p39) in Lean in roughly 30 hours and solved two open black hole physics problems. Why it matters: these are vendor claims, but a price cut on the top tier changes what long-running agent jobs cost.
- **First outside reads on Fable 5.2**: [Artificial Analysis scores it 65](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/claude-fable-5-2), the highest on its index so far, and [Cursor reports](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cursor-s-claude-fable-5-2-tops-cursorbench-at-60-with-20-cheaper-tokens) 60% on its own CursorBench. [Perplexity wired it into Search and Computer](https://alphasignal.ai/news/perplexity-integrates-anthropic-s-fable-5-2-topping-web-research-benchmarks-at) and [Vercel added it to AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/changelog/claude-fable-5-2-now-available-on-ai-gateway) the same day. Why it matters: independent numbers matter more than launch charts, and these are early.
- **Meta Muse Ember**: Meta Superintelligence Lab's smallest model, [claimed to beat Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna on intelligence per dollar](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p35#a261109p35), aimed at cutting agent costs. Why it matters: the cheap tier is where agent workloads actually run, and Meta is now competing there directly.
- **OpenAI Control Room**: OpenAI describes an internal system that [tracks model workloads across its research infrastructure](https://openai.com/index/control-room) and can halt them quickly on safety or security concerns. Why it matters: a rare look at how a lab plans to stop a runaway job.
- **Agents get more reach**: ChatGPT dots can now [link several local machines at once](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-dots-agents-can-now-control-multiple-computers-at-once), routing tasks per computer, and Google Antigravity added a [Gemini Live voice mode](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-antigravity-lets-developers-control-coding-agents-with-their-voice) to steer agents and approve permissions out loud. Why it matters: agents are moving from one terminal to a fleet you manage by talking.

## Security & Privacy

- **\*sshole exploited**: attackers are abusing a critical flaw in the OpenSSH and musl combination that gives [unauthenticated access to privileged accounts](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/vulnerability-that-lets-hackers-ssh-into-linux-devices-is-under-active-exploitation/) in seconds on affected Linux systems. GreyNoise says a group it tracks as GT10 ran "mature intrusions" against its honeypots four days after disclosure, using a backdoor that looks AI-generated. Why it matters: the patch window closed fast; check musl-based hosts (Alpine-style images, embedded devices) first.
- **Claude Code settings leak secrets on GitHub**: Apiiro found thousands of [database passwords, API tokens and root logins](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/11/claude-code-settings-files-expose-thousands-of-secrets-on-github/) in committed Claude Code settings files. Choosing "Yes, and don't ask again" stores the approved shell command, secrets included, in the local settings file, which is normally gitignored but sometimes gets committed. Why it matters: auto-approved commands are persisted verbatim, so a token typed into a command can leave with the repo.
- **Fireworks breach**: the AI infrastructure provider says hackers [accessed some customers' datasets, incomplete model weights and billing information](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p42#a261109p42) over the past month; two customers told Forbes they have largely moved off it. Why it matters: hosted fine-tuning data and weights are now a target in their own right.
- **Binance heist laundering**: Elliptic says the thieves behind the $6.3B Binance theft [created at least six memecoins on NEAR](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p36#a261109p36) to launder about $430,000. Why it matters: small amounts, but it shows the trail moving to newer chains.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Vercel's week**: [build disk space rises to 64 GB](https://vercel.com/changelog/build-disk-space-on-vercel-increased-to-64-gb), Sandbox snapshots are [up to 10x faster](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-sandbox-snapshot-creation-is-now-up-to-10x-faster), and its [November AI Gateway index](https://vercel.com/blog/ai-gateway-production-index-november-2026) says decision models take 26% of requests and Anthropic wins new teams 2:1 (Vercel's own traffic data). Why it matters: platforms are tuning for agent workloads, long builds and fast sandboxes.
- **SpaceX and Mavria bet on power-hungry compute**: SpaceX plans a [gas-fired power plant and super-compute facility in Starbase](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p41#a261109p41) with about $24B in initial phases, and Mavria [raised $130M at a $1B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261109/p37#a261109p37) for data centers shipped by truck. Why it matters: electricity, not chips, is becoming the bottleneck for AI buildout.
