## AI / LLMs

- [An AI-supervised remote entrance exam for UNAM, Mexico's largest university, went badly wrong for its nearly 160,000 applicants](https://arstechnica.com/culture/2026/08/an-ai-supervised-remote-exam-went-so-badly-that-58000-students-must-retake-it/): where 3.5% of test takers scored 100 or higher between 2021 and 2025, 16.3% did this year under the lockdown-browser, AI-proctored format, and ==58,000== students must retake it.
- [Meta doubled the training efficiency of GEM, the generative ads recommendation foundation model behind Instagram and Facebook, to 20-25% Model FLOPs Utilization while scaling training compute 4x in 12 months](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/08/03/ml-applications/training-gem-at-llm-scale-meta-ads-recommendation-foundation-model/), via custom attention kernels and mixed ultra-low-precision training, evidence recommendation systems, not just chatbots, are now getting LLM-scale infrastructure investment.

- [Apple briefly pulled Telegram from the App Store on Monday over CSAM content, restoring access about 20 minutes later](https://www.techmeme.com/260803/p39#a260803p39) after Telegram said it removed the content and banned the user.

- [Show HN: Nightcrawler runs a fully autonomous pentesting agent entirely on a rooted Android phone](https://github.com/garagehq/nightcrawler/), using a local 1.2B-parameter model for recon, exploitation, and reporting with no cloud connectivity, 27 exploit playbooks, and a 24,956-entry CVE database on board.

## Research

- [The first dynamic security assessment of internet-facing MCP servers audited 414 of 640 confirmed production instances and found 68 reportable vulnerabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00150), including SQL injection and SSRF against cloud metadata services; 91.8% lacked OAuth authentication and 41.6% of confirmed servers disappeared within three days between measurement runs, pointing to deployment far outrunning security review.
- [A new taxonomy of attack vectors for multi-agent web systems includes a "Telephone Loop" attack that exploits cross-agent delegation to trap agents in cyclical tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00202), harmless against single-agent setups but compromising Claude Sonnet 4.5, GPT-5.2, and GPT-5.4 at an average 80% rate; only Claude Sonnet 4.6 resisted, catching it 92% of the time.

## Threads

- Texas's grid halt and Wall Street's enthusiasm pulled in opposite directions: the same day a state ordered a pause on new AI data center hookups, Amazon crossed $3 trillion and Palantir and Snap both beat Q2 estimates, the buildout's power problem and its market reward landing on the same day.
