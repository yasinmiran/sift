**What matters today:** The Claude Code extension scare from yesterday now has [an Anthropic statement](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p2#a261031p2): a malicious actor used access to Cursor's marketplace to publish a tampered copy, and Anthropic's own systems were not compromised. Money got pickier on the same day: OpenAI [trimmed its round to about $20B at a $1.25T valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p30#a261030p30), and [Intel and Reddit](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p24#a261030p24) both had their worst days in years.

## Security & Privacy

- **Claude Code extension, the update**: Yesterday's backdoor report is now pinned on Cursor's marketplace. Ars says version 2.1.313 was [live for nearly 12 hours and reached at most 5,000 users](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/anthropic-vs-code-extension-on-cursor-backdoored-for-nearly-12-hours/), and researchers say it went out through Anthropic's official account there; Anthropic [says it did not build or publish the copy](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p2#a261031p2), and neither company has explained how the account was reached. Anyone who ran 2.1.313 should treat that machine's credentials as exposed.
- **Wiz on a "Black October"**: Wiz [frames this month's incidents at AI developer platforms](https://www.wiz.io/blog/black-october-for-ai-developer-platforms) as a pattern, so the extension compromise reads as one of several attacks on the tools developers trust by default.
- **Shared AI opt-out**: OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic say they will adopt a [single robots.txt signal for AI training preferences](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1023763/openai-google-microsoft-ai-scraping-standard-opt-out), proposed at the IETF (the internet standards body) by an Anthropic standards lead. Publishers set one rule instead of one per crawler; [Google's note](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/10/ai-training-control) confirms support.
- **VPNs in Russia**: EFF says VPN apps are [again being pulled from Apple's App Store in Russia](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/10/why-vpns-are-worth-defending), cutting off a main route around state censorship.
- **Scribd notice**: A user posted an emailed [notice that Scribd had a security incident](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50312756) affecting its brands, including Slideshare and Everand; no scope is public yet.
- **VOLTA infostealer**: Talos describes [VOLTA, an infostealer that asks a language model to rank victims by how much they are worth](https://blog.talosintelligence.com/appraisal-by-ai-model-driven-victim-prioritization-in-the-volta-infostealer/), so attackers can spend effort on the richest machines first. Prioritizing targets used to take human time; now it scales with the malware.
- **Binance, the delta**: Yesterday's hack story has a payout: Binance says it has [covered 90% of the stolen amount](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p9#a261031p9) from over $6.34B of company funds, with the other 10% in less liquid tokens.

## AI / LLMs

- **Claude plan change**: Anthropic renamed Max 5x to "Max" ($100/month) and Max 20x to "Ultra" ($200/month), and [verified Ultra accounts lose the 5-hour session limit](https://latent.space/p/ainews-google-admits-ai-mode-hurts-search-revenue-15-500b-drop-worst-since-2004-ipo-ai-bear-market-c/); weekly limits stay. Heavy users get uninterrupted sessions.
- **Teen weapons limits**: OpenAI says ChatGPT will [give teens less weapons-related information, even for questions that look harmless](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p25#a261030p25).
- **Agents and food delivery**: DoorDash's CEO says outside AI agents have produced [only several hundred orders](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/10/doordash-says-outside-ai-agents-have-only-led-to-hundreds-of-orders-so-far/) so far, a reality check on agentic shopping.
- **Hallucination benchmark**: Artificial Analysis [cut AA-Omniscience to 3,000 questions](https://alphasignal.ai/news/artificial-analysis-aa-omniscience-v1-1-catches-ai-models-guessing-instead-of) and says it now exposes models that guess instead of abstaining.
- **Altman on RAM**: Sam Altman says it is [better for RAM to sit in data centers than in personal PCs](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1023820/sam-altman-ram-shortage-cloud-servers), a stance that lands badly amid a memory shortage.
- **Dartmouth provost**: Provost Santiago Schnell [resigned after a committee found undisclosed AI use](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/dartmouths-provost-warned-us-about-ai-cheating-but-his-own-ai-use-cost-him-his-job/) in a paper and an op-ed on AI cheating.
- **AI detectors reshape writing**: Bloomberg says detectors like Pangram are [pushing authors to drop em dashes, "not X, but Y" phrasing and words like "delve", or even add typos](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p8#a261031p8), so human prose bends toward whatever the detector scores as human.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Pydantic 3**: Pydantic V3 is in development, [V1 reaches end of life and a paid enterprise support tier arrives](https://alphasignal.ai/news/pydantic-3-drops-v1-support-and-adds-paid-enterprise-contracts); teams still on V1 need a migration plan.
- **Nesformer**: Drew Breunig [baked a working NES CPU emulator into the weights of Qwen3.5-4B](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/30/nesformer/), so Super Mario Bros runs, very slowly, inside a language model.

## Startups & Industry

- **OpenAI's raise shrinks**: The target fell from $30B at $1.4T to [about $20B at $1.25T](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p30#a261030p30), and the WSJ says only [about $11B is committed](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p31#a261030p31), less than half the original goal. It lands just before Anthropic's IPO, which [TechCrunch reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/anthropic-expected-to-receive-high-junk-rating-from-credit-agencies-following-ipo-report-says) is expected to draw a high "junk" credit rating.
- **Debt gets pricier**: S&P moved [Meta's outlook to negative](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p3#a261031p3) over capex and negative free cash flow, and the WSJ says [CoreWeave, Nebius and Nscale are effectively shut out of the bond market](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p4#a261031p4). Big Tech has now [committed over $3 trillion to AI build-out](https://thenextweb.com/news/big-tech-3-trillion-ai-spending-commitments), and the cheap money behind it is thinning.
- **Google's AI Mode**: Google's business chief said AI Mode's growth [came with lower monetization](https://latent.space/p/ainews-google-admits-ai-mode-hurts-search-revenue-15-500b-drop-worst-since-2004-ipo-ai-bear-market-c/), tying the sell-off to the summary-versus-source tension publishers keep raising.
- **Intel and Reddit**: Intel fell ((28.6%)), its [worst day since the 1970s](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p24#a261030p24), on weak guidance, and Reddit [dropped 29%](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p23#a261030p23) after a weak Q4 forecast.
- **Baseten**: Baseten is [raising about $2B at $26B and $30B valuations](https://www.techmeme.com/261030/p29#a261030p29), up from $13B in June, evidence that inference infrastructure still draws money.
- **Apple Intelligence in China**: Apple will [bring earlier Apple Intelligence features to China with iOS 27.3](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1023974/apple-intelligence-china-ios-27-3), beta November 4 and release in December, without the new Siri.
- **Meta's humanoid**: Meta built a prototype humanoid robot with China's Fourier in three months and is [building a second](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p10#a261031p10), The Information reports; Meta says it has no plans to sell the units, so this is research for now.

- **Hubble**: NASA is [again asking for ideas to boost Hubble's orbit](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/10/think-you-can-save-the-hubble-space-telescope-nasa-wants-to-know/); left alone it re-enters in the early-to-mid 2030s.
