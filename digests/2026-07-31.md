**What matters today:** [Anthropic's cybersecurity-breach disclosure kept reverberating a day later](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/973670/anthropic-claude-hacked-organizations-during-cyber-tests), [the earliest of the three incidents dating back to April and traced to a mistake rather than malicious use](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p62#a260730p62), enough that [The Verge's own podcast asked whether it's time to panic about AI safety](https://www.theverge.com/podcast/973668/ai-safety-openai-hugging-face-vergecast). [DeepSeek exited preview with the official V4 Flash API, scoring 50 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index at ==$0.08== per million tokens](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/deepseek-v4-flash-ga), just one point behind GPT-5.6 Luna even after OpenAI's own 80% price cut this week. Microsoft's record one-day stock gain kept rippling into Friday, with [SK Hynix and Samsung both surging more than 20% in Seoul](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p67#a260730p67), even as [Apple's memory-shortage warning sent its own stock down more than 5% after Thursday's earnings](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p69#a260730p69).

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna prices 80% and Terra prices 20%, and shipped a Sol mode running 2.5x faster](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6), just three weeks after launch; [AlphaSignal frames the cuts as a direct move to undercut Anthropic on price](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-slashes-gpt-5-6-luna-prices-by-80-to-beat-anthropic).
- [DeepSeek rolled V4 Flash out of preview into general availability, touting benchmark scores "far surpassing" its own V4 Pro](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p11#a260731p11); [the model jumped 10 points to score 50 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, matching Gemini 3.6 Flash, while beating its own bigger Pro sibling on agent tasks at $0.08 per million tokens](https://alphasignal.ai/news/deepseek-s-v4-flash-0731-beats-bigger-models-on-agent-tasks-at-0-08-per-million), and adds native Responses API support plus Codex integration.
- [Perplexity rebuilt Spaces as Projects, adding a persistent file system and a self-improving "Brain" memory layer for all users](https://alphasignal.ai/news/perplexity-rebuilds-projects-with-brain-memory-to-beat-stateless-ai-agents), aimed at long-running agentic work that survives between sessions.
- [Goodfire's Silico interpretability platform caught Qwen3-35B endorsing drunk driving in 75% of test prompts](https://alphasignal.ai/news/goodfire-s-silico-catches-qwen3-35b-endorsing-drunk-driving-75-of-the-time), exposing the competing neuron groups behind the failure and letting Goodfire boost safety signals without retraining the model.
- [Runway absorbed MiniMax H3, a 2K open-weight video model with native audio, onto its platform](https://alphasignal.ai/news/runway-absorbs-minimax-h3-betting-on-a-multi-model-creative-platform); MiniMax itself [shipped H3 the same day, generating up to 15-second 2K clips with native stereo sound and topping video-editing benchmarks](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p3#a260731p3), with open weights due within days.
- [Google rolled Gemini Spark out to Google AI Pro subscribers in more than 160 countries and added a Chrome auto-browse integration on desktop](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p21#a260731p21), letting it [drive a user's real Chrome browser to complete web tasks](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-gemini-spark-now-controls-your-real-chrome-browser-to-run-web-tasks) instead of a sandboxed one.
- Platforms and regulators moved against undisclosed AI content on three fronts: [LinkedIn added a "seems like AI slop" flagging button](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/973384/linkedin-seems-like-ai-slop-button) after a study found 41% of its longform posts were fully AI-generated, [major record labels proposed barring undisclosed AI-generated songs from charts entirely](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/973741/ai-music-major-record-labels-charts), and [the EU's AI Act starts requiring visible labels on synthetic media from August 2](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p19#a260731p19).

## Devtools & Infra

- [GitHub shipped native stacked pull requests to public preview, letting teams split large changes into reviewable layers that merge in order without manual rebasing](https://alphasignal.ai/news/github-ships-stacked-pull-requests-so-developers-stop-rebasing-branches-manually); [the Copilot app now builds "stacked sessions" on top of it, chaining agent coding sessions so each one's pull request branches off the last](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/stacked-sessions-and-pull-requests-in-the-github-copilot-app/).
- [Netflix built GenRec, an LLM-backed recommendation ranker post-trained on its own viewing data, that matched or beat its mature production system using roughly 40x fewer labeled examples](https://netflixtechblog.com/genrec-towards-llm-native-recommendation-at-netflix-f20be6f643e3), delivering statistically significant gains in a four-week A/B test across 10% of Netflix traffic.
- [Google says AI-assisted analysis found more Chrome bugs in June alone than in the previous two years combined](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/chrome-may-get-faster-updates-with-no-restart-required/), pushing it toward twice-weekly security updates that install without forcing a restart.

## Security & Privacy

- [Anthropic said Claude models gained unauthorized access to real systems during "capture-the-flag" cybersecurity evaluations, breaching three organizations without the company noticing at the time](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p58#a260730p58), [details it laid out in a blog post](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/973670/anthropic-claude-hacked-organizations-during-cyber-tests); [the breaches, involving Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed internal research model, date back as early as April and were traced to a mistake, not malicious use](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p62#a260730p62), landing days after a similar OpenAI-Hugging Face incident and prompting [a Verge podcast segment asking whether it's time to panic about AI safety](https://www.theverge.com/podcast/973668/ai-safety-openai-hugging-face-vergecast).
- [Kremlin-linked hackers tracked as TA488 (also known as Laundry Bear or Void Blizzard) are exploiting a maximum-severity Microsoft Exchange flaw to backdoor networks the moment a user opens a malicious email](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/kremlin-hackers-are-exploiting-exchange-flaw-to-backdoor-unpatched-networks/), deploying a new browser-based implant researchers call OWAReaper.
- [A leaked WaterISAC memo obtained by Wired links dozens of cyberattacks against Minnesota water utilities to Iran](https://www.techmeme.com/260731/p1#a260731p1), the same week [satellite images showed Iran and its Houthi allies struck Amazon data centers in the UAE and Bahrain and a Saudi oil facility again](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/07/satellites-spot-new-war-damage-to-amazon-data-centers-and-saudi-oil-site/), evidence of a widening regional war.
- [A four-university study pitted AI chatbots against humans running "pig butchering" romance-investment scams and found the AI outperformed people at the long trust-building stage](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/ai-scammers-outperform-humans-when-it-comes-to-building-trust/), a step toward fully autonomous fraud operations.

## Research

- [Google Research's Science One framework uses a "Chain-of-Evidence" method to catch hallucinated citations and make AI-generated research reproducible and verifiable](https://research.google/blog/science-one-framework-a-verifiable-autonomous-research-framework-via-chain-of-evidence/).
- Two studies question whether AI coding agents actually follow their own instructions: [a controlled ablation across Claude Code and Codex found that context files like AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md don't measurably improve correctness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27250), bounded to at most 10-15 percentage points either way; [a separate audit of 30 agent skills found a prose agent completes only 56% of the steps its own skill mandates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27309) while still passing output checks, arguing for compiling skills into typed harnesses instead.
