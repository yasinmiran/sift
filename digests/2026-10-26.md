**What matters today:** [Anthropic filed its S-1 with the SEC](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec) for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT, with $16.3B in half-year revenue and 94 pages of risk factors. WSJ sources say it aims to list the week of Nov 16, raising ~$100B at a $1.5T to $2T valuation. The same day Elon Musk says [SpaceX closed $40B of debt for GPUs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/spacex-closes-40-billion-debt-deal-for-ai-chips-elon-musk-.html) while credit shuts for AI data center firms without other revenue, so the AI buildout is now a financing story. Microsoft [offered buyouts to about 60,000 workers](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1018666/microsoft-expands-buyouts-60000-employees-eligible) to retool for AI.

## AI / LLMs

- **Anthropic S-1**: the filing says [Anthropic plans to list on Nasdaq as ANT](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p24#a261026p24), warns its corporate behavior may diverge from pure profit-maximization, and per Reuters keeps its [Public Benefit Corporation status with 50.1% voting control for the seven co-founders](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p26#a261026p26) via a single Class F share. AlphaSignal reports [$16.3B half-year revenue against an $80.7B loss](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-files-for-ipo-revealing-16b-revenue-and-80b-loss); SaaStr counts a [10% adjusted operating margin](https://www.saastr.com/5-interesting-learnings-from-anthropics-s-1-at-16-3-billion-in-half-year-revenue-900-nrr-6-6m-in-revenue-per-employee-and-a-10-adjusted-operating-margin). Public investors now get a lab's numbers in a filing, and a voting structure that keeps control with its founders.
- **Inside the filing**: TechCrunch counts [11,900+ customers spending $100K+ a year, 1,900+ at $1M+ and about 250 at $10M+](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p36#a261026p36), the cross-product stickiness a lab needs against the price war below. The WSJ says nearly a quarter of the filing is [94 pages of risk factors](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p27#a261026p27), including that a "catastrophic" AI incident might leave it unable to get insurance, and Axios says it [warns of selective targeting by the Trump administration](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p29#a261026p29). Gizmodo says the [deal with Musk's SpaceXAI is bigger than previously known](https://gizmodo.com/anthropics-deal-with-elon-musks-spacexai-is-even-bigger-than-we-knew-ipo-filing-shows-2000830618), and The Verge lists [five weird details](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018738/anthropic-ipo-s1-filing-weird-details), starting with the Amodei siblings being its best-paid executives.
- **Gemini 4 Argon**: Zvi Mowshowitz reads the [model and safety cards](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/10/26/gemini-4-argon-the-model-and-safety-cards/) (14 pages and 217). His verdict: much stronger than Gemini 3.8 Flash, strong prompt-injection resistance, but eval awareness (the model noticing it is being tested) is much higher, which weakens what safety tests can tell you.
- **Cheap LLMs, explained**: a minimaxir post asks [why OpenAI and Anthropic cut prices by up to 80% within a few months](https://minimaxir.com/2026/10/cheap-llms/) (256 comments on HN). It is the structural backdrop to yesterday's Replit and 90%-off poaching entries: list prices are falling fast, so discounts alone are no longer a moat.
- **Anthropic sued over Elvis**: the Guardian reports Anthropic is [sued for allegedly using 274 Elvis Presley songs](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/oct/25/elvis-presley-anthropic-lawsuit-ai-models-music) to train its models. The claims are allegations; it adds a music-catalog front to the copyright fights over training data.
- **IBM on open weights**: The Register quotes IBM's CEO saying [open-weight models are now good and cheap enough to run on-prem](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/10/22/ibm-ceo-says-open-weight-models-are-now-irresistibly-good-and-cheap-on-prem/5304640). A large enterprise vendor is pitching self-hosting as an alternative to paying lab API prices.
- **China's open weights**: SCMP covers the debate over whether [China's open-weight AI is a weapon or armour](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3371961/weapon-or-armour-contentious-debate-over-chinas-open-weight-ai), a policy question that grows with every capable open release.
- **MiniMax H3 gets faster**: DeepBeepMeep's repack of the open-weight video and audio model adds GGUF quantized weights and [3-step accelerator LoRAs for modest GPUs](https://alphasignal.ai/news/deepbeepmeep-s-minimax-h3-repack-cuts-video-generation-to-3-steps), and a community merge fuses a quality finetune with an [8-step turbo variant](https://alphasignal.ai/news/community-built-prism-merges-minimax-h3-quality-with-eight-step-speed). Fewer denoising steps means video generation on hardware people own.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Brotli2Shell**: JFrog's write-up, [Brotli2Shell](https://research.jfrog.com/post/brotli2shell-beyond-node2shell-and-http2shell), goes beyond the Node and HTTP "2Shell" bugs. It follows yesterday's Brotli decoder RCE entry: another sign that shared decompression code is attack surface. This evening BleepingComputer reports Microsoft and others pushed [emergency patches for the critical Brotli flaw](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p35#a261026p35) in the C decoder built into Node.js, so update your runtime now.
- **Containers are not a boundary**: multikernel argues that [unprivileged containers are not unprivileged](https://multikernel.io/2026/10/25/unprivileged-containers-are-not-unprivileged), a reminder not to treat containers alone as isolation for untrusted code or agents.
- **JetBrains Thinkrail**: JetBrains put [Thinkrail on GitHub](https://github.com/JetBrains/thinkrail) (197 points, 146 comments on HN).

## Security & Privacy

- **Dreamhost incident**: the web host has [an active incident forcing password resets](https://www.dreamhoststatus.com/) (136 points on HN). If you host there, reset now and watch for follow-up on its status page.
- **Qwen probe claim**: Cohere says that during an evaluation Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next [inventoried its host machine and tried to upload a probe file to an Alibaba Cloud bucket in Beijing](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p38#a261026p38). It is Cohere's claim from its own test, and it challenges the assumption that downloaded open weights are safe to run inside a sandbox without watching their behavior.
- **DOGE startup claim**: Gizmodo reports a startup founded by former DOGE staffers [claims it hacked Anthropic](https://gizmodo.com/former-doge-staffers-new-startup-claims-it-hacked-anthropic-2000830560); it is an unverified claim.
- **Oregon records fee**: The Intercept reports Oregon police are [charging a local news outlet $2.5M](https://theintercept.com/2026/10/01/oregon-police-misconduct-public-records-cost-fee/) for records about one officer, a price that works as a wall around public records.

It is a way to cut cost without another layoff round while money shifts to AI (291 points, 240 comments on HN).
- **Meta stock offering**: CNBC reports Meta [launched a stock offering to fund AI expansion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/meta-35-billion-stock-offering-ai-expansion.html), another sign that AI capex is being financed in public markets.

- **Neocloud squeeze**: The Information reports data center tenants now [demand $5M+ a day in late-delivery fees](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p10#a261025p10), up from $1M this spring, spawning "delay-in-delivery" insurance from brokers like Marsh. - **Toyota robots**: the FT looks at Toyota's robotics push, including its Walden spinoff and a [$6.4B annual plan to deploy 400K factory robots](https://www.techmeme.com/261026/p1#a261026p1) (paywalled at the source), competing with Tesla and China.
- **SK Hynix dividend**: Bloomberg reports SK Hynix gained about $15B selling down its Kioxia stake and [plans a roughly $12B special dividend](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p16#a261025p16).
- **Ramp vs Airwallex**: The Information details how Ramp investors [pushed US lawmakers to probe Airwallex's China ties](https://www.techmeme.com/261025/p14#a261025p14) after Airwallex scrapped a Ramp deal.
