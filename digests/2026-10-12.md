**What matters today:** Ledger confirmed a [hardware implant](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p17#a261011p17) in wallets sold by reseller CryptoBilis, tied to an $86M+ theft across hundreds of devices. Yesterday Nadella said to assume every model is compromised; today the Verge says he, Nvidia and others have [adopted "super intelligence"](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p10#a261011p10) branding in step with the White House. Amazon is in talks to buy Decart for about $7B, a deal [Anthropic passed on](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p11#a261011p11) in September.

## AI / LLMs

- **Amazon eyes Decart at ~$7B.** WSJ sources say Amazon is [in talks](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p11#a261011p11) for the startup, whose software eases switching between AI chips; Anthropic decided against buying it for about $6B in September. Chip portability is the prize.
- **"Super intelligence" rebrand.** The Verge says Musk and Benioff pledged to rename [SpaceXAI and AIForce](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p10#a261011p10) while Nadella and Nvidia adopted the term, framing it as Silicon Valley falling in line with Trump. Yesterday Nadella urged containment of models; today the branding story.
- **Google's enterprise agent.** Google [unveiled a Gemini agent](https://www.thelec.net/news/articleView.html?idxno=14513) for enterprise work, per The Elec.
- **Torvalds and Claude Code.** Phoronix reports Linus Torvalds is [working with Claude Code](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linus-Torvalds-Claude-Code) on a sizeable Linux kernel improvement.
- **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next goes local.** A 177B-parameter model [runs on a 64GB Mac](https://alphasignal.ai/news/atomic-chat-runs-a-177b-qwen3-8-flash-next-on-a-64-gb-mac) at 36 tokens/s via an SSD-paged table. Separately a community build [strips its refusals](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sc117-strips-qwen3-8-flash-next-refusals-without-touching-1-079-tensors) by transplanting 144 tensors into the quantized file, so open weights keep losing their guardrails.
- **Sakana's MASS.** One 27B model [plays its own agent team](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sakana-ai-s-mass-trains-one-model-to-run-its-own-agent-team), judge and teacher, lifting score per token up to 1.6x on research benchmarks.
- **Phones as local LLM hosts.** Qualcomm's CEO says AI companies [are asking](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p15#a261011p15) for phone chips that run 100B-parameter local models continuously by 2028.

## Devtools & Infra

- **GitHub retires Spokes.** The Register says GitHub is [rewriting its storage layer](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/10/09/so-long-spokes-github-rewrites-storage-to-restore-reliability-just-in-time-for-agentic-hordes/5302148) to restore reliability ahead of heavy agent traffic.
- **Mimic.** An open-source tool [captures mobile app traffic](https://alphasignal.ai/news/mimic-uses-claude-to-turn-app-traffic-into-python-clients) and has Claude generate a typed Python client, so any app becomes scriptable like a library.
- **Bridge Data Centres upsizes.** Bain-owned Bridge is [in talks](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p16#a261011p16) to lift a loan from $2.8B to about $6.6B, one of Asia's largest data center deals.

## Security & Privacy

- **Ledger wallet theft.** Ledger says the $86M+ theft [involved devices from CryptoBilis](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p17#a261011p17) and confirms a hardware implant: buy hardware wallets only from the maker.
- **AWS AgentCore.** The Register reports AgentCore's security was [undone by a prompt](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/09/aws-agentcore-security-undone-by-prompt-requesting-credentials/5302436) asking for credentials, another case of agent guardrails living in text.
- **College cannot cancel Flock.** Elgin Community College asked in October 2025 to remove its six cameras over [data sharing with Customs and Border Patrol](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1009411/elgin-community-college-flock-safety-cameras) and weak security; Flock refused, saying it had not breached the contract.

## Startups & Industry

- **Palantir on AI targeting.** CTO Shyam Sankar [answered questions](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p12#a261011p12) on the Iran school strike, AI targeting and the DOD dropping Anthropic.
- **Funding and deals.** Vitalize [raised a $31M Series A](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p13#a261011p13) for hospital staffing AI; Anchorage Digital [bought Routable](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p14#a261011p14), its largest acquisition by team size and revenue.
- **Chip supply chain.** Dongshan, a PCB maker valued at $39B, is [eyeing a $2B to $3B Hong Kong listing](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p1#a261012p1), and TSMC is [talking more seriously with Texas](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p2#a261012p2) about overseas fabs.

## Threads

- **Hardware you cannot trust.** Ledger's implant, AWS AgentCore's prompt-based credential leak and Elgin's stuck Flock cameras all show security failing at the layer buyers assumed was handled; the HN passkeys and fingerprinting essays argue the same about identity.
- **Chips are the strategic input.** Amazon's Decart talks (chip portability), Qualcomm's local-LLM ask, TSMC in Texas and Dongshan's listing all trace to who controls compute supply.
- **Agents everywhere.** GitHub's storage rewrite, Mimic, Gemini's enterprise agent and Sakana's MASS all assume agent traffic is the new default load.
- **Open weights lose their guardrails.** The Qwen refusal-stripping build and the 64GB Mac run sit beside Nadella's call for containment, the tension between local models and oversight.
