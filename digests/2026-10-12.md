**What matters today:** Ledger confirmed a [hardware implant](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p17#a261011p17) in wallets sold by reseller CryptoBilis, tied to an $86M+ theft across hundreds of devices. The evening added Microsoft's [Maia 300 chip](https://www.theverge.com/news/1009465/microsoft-maia-300-chip-improvements-rollout), its third-gen in-house AI silicon and a bid to depend less on Nvidia, and [cyberattacks on Danfoss refrigeration systems](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p27#a261012p27) that heated hundreds of French supermarkets to 50°C for about six hours. Amazon is in talks to buy Decart for about $7B, a deal [Anthropic passed on](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p11#a261011p11) in September.

## AI / LLMs

- **Amazon eyes Decart at ~$7B.** WSJ sources say Amazon is [in talks](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p11#a261011p11) for the startup, whose software eases switching between AI chips; Anthropic decided against buying it for about $6B in September. Chip portability is the prize.
- **"Super intelligence" rebrand.** The Verge says Musk and Benioff pledged to rename [SpaceXAI and AIForce](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p10#a261011p10) while Nadella and Nvidia adopted the term, framing it as Silicon Valley falling in line with Trump. Yesterday Nadella urged containment of models; today the branding story.
- **Torvalds and Claude Code.** Phoronix reports Linus Torvalds is [working with Claude Code](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linus-Torvalds-Claude-Code) on a sizeable Linux kernel improvement.
- **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next goes local.** A 177B-parameter model [runs on a 64GB Mac](https://alphasignal.ai/news/atomic-chat-runs-a-177b-qwen3-8-flash-next-on-a-64-gb-mac) at 36 tokens/s via an SSD-paged table. Separately a community build [strips its refusals](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sc117-strips-qwen3-8-flash-next-refusals-without-touching-1-079-tensors) by transplanting 144 tensors into the quantized file, so open weights keep losing their guardrails.
- **Sakana's MASS.** One 27B model [plays its own agent team](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sakana-ai-s-mass-trains-one-model-to-run-its-own-agent-team), judge and teacher, lifting score per token up to 1.6x on research benchmarks.
- **Phones as local LLM hosts.** Qualcomm's CEO says AI companies [are asking](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p15#a261011p15) for phone chips that run 100B-parameter local models continuously by 2028.
- **Microsoft Maia 300.** Third-gen in-house AI chip on TSMC's N2P process, pitched as its [primary inference system](https://www.theverge.com/news/1009465/microsoft-maia-300-chip-improvements-rollout); production workloads start in 2027. Racks hold 96 chips at about 167 kW, and Microsoft plans [multi-gigawatt deployments](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p29#a261012p29) from next year, so its inference spend shifts from Nvidia GPUs to its own silicon.
- **UK AISI gets its access back.** The White House [reversed course](https://the-decoder.com/white-house-reverses-course-and-restores-uk-ai-safety-institutes-access-to-frontier-models) and restored the UK AI Safety Institute's access to frontier models for testing.
- **Capital as the AI bottleneck.** Intel's Lip-Bu Tan and Broadcom's Hock Tan [warned in Singapore](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p18#a261012p18) that access to capital and rising borrowing costs could become AI's main constraint, and Hock Tan [likened speculative AI investors to casino gamblers](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p16#a261012p16).

## Devtools & Infra

- **GitHub retires Spokes.** The Register says GitHub is [rewriting its storage layer](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/10/09/so-long-spokes-github-rewrites-storage-to-restore-reliability-just-in-time-for-agentic-hordes/5302148) to restore reliability ahead of heavy agent traffic.
- **Mimic.** An open-source tool [captures mobile app traffic](https://alphasignal.ai/news/mimic-uses-claude-to-turn-app-traffic-into-python-clients) and has Claude generate a typed Python client, so any app becomes scriptable like a library.
- **Cloudflare buys Deno.** The runtime maker joins Cloudflare to [improve the Workers programming model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/10/cloudflare-acquires-deno-to-improve-its-workers-programming-model), which puts a JavaScript runtime author inside the edge platform.
- **Python 3.15.** [Released](https://lwn.net/Articles/1099602), per LWN.

## Security & Privacy

- **Ledger wallet theft.** Ledger says the $86M+ theft [involved devices from CryptoBilis](https://www.techmeme.com/261011/p17#a261011p17) and confirms a hardware implant: buy hardware wallets only from the maker.
- **AWS AgentCore.** The Register reports AgentCore's security was [undone by a prompt](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/09/aws-agentcore-security-undone-by-prompt-requesting-credentials/5302436) asking for credentials, another case of agent guardrails living in text.
- **College cannot cancel Flock.** Elgin Community College asked in October 2025 to remove its six cameras over [data sharing with Customs and Border Patrol](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1009411/elgin-community-college-flock-safety-cameras) and weak security; Flock refused, saying it had not breached the contract.
- **French supermarket cyberattack.** Sunday-night attacks on Danfoss-managed refrigeration [heated systems to 50°C for roughly six hours](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p27#a261012p27) across hundreds of French stores, forcing them to throw out food: physical damage from a vendor's remote management layer.
- **FBI-hacking arrest.** The FBI [arrested a suspect](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/10/fbi-arrests-suspect-in-fbi-hacking-case.html) tied to September's ShinyHunters theft of FBI data about its own agents.
- **Yandex outage and the bots.** Yesterday's drone strike on a third Yandex data center fed a chart showing Russian bot traffic collapsing; The Verge says it [probably was not the disinformation kind](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1009594/russian-bots-yandex-outage-drone-strikes-debunk), so it probably shows ordinary automated traffic going offline with the data center, not a troll farm switched off.

## Threads

- **Hardware you cannot trust.** Ledger's implant, the Danfoss refrigeration attack, AWS AgentCore's prompt-based credential leak and Elgin's stuck Flock cameras all show security failing at the layer buyers assumed was handled; the HN passkeys and fingerprinting essays argue the same about identity.
- **Chips are the strategic input.** Amazon's Decart talks (chip portability), Microsoft's Maia 300, Qualcomm's local-LLM ask, TSMC in Texas and Dongshan's listing all trace to who controls compute supply, while the Tans warn capital may be the real limit.
- **Agents everywhere.** GitHub's storage rewrite, Mimic, Cloudflare's Deno deal and Sakana's MASS all assume agent traffic is the new default load.
- **Open weights lose their guardrails.** The Qwen refusal-stripping build and the 64GB Mac run sit beside Nadella's call for containment, the UK AISI getting its model access back, and QGIS's AI ban: who gets to oversee, and who gets to opt out.
