**What matters today:** Two frontier claims in two days. Meta launched [Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009479/meta-muse-wonder-full-ai-model) on Monday, and Google answered Tuesday with [Gemini 4 Argon](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009862/google-releases-gemini-4-argon), which it says beats GPT-6 Astra, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5. The safety side was uneasy: OpenAI [fired three safety researchers](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/10/12/openai-fires-three-safety-researchers/), and the IMF [warned an AI share crash](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/10/13/ai-bubble-could-burst-and-take-global-economy-with-it-imf-warns/5302781) could threaten financial stability.

## AI / LLMs

- **Gemini 4 Argon.** Google rolls it out globally through the Gemini API and to AI Pro subscribers in the Gemini app, and it claims wins over rival frontier models on coding, cybersecurity, science, finance and law. [AlphaSignal](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-deepmind-s-gemini-4-argon-tops-vals-index-with-1m-token-window) puts it first on the Vals Index at 68.9%, with a 1M-token window and $2/$10 per million tokens in/out. The scores are Google's and a leaderboard's, so independent tests are the thing to wait for.
- **Meta Muse Wonder.** Wonder is on the Meta Model API and in Muse Code; the larger Wonder Full is for Muse Code subscribers. Artificial Analysis [scores them 52 and 56](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p36#a261012p36) at max effort, up from Muse Spark 1.3 at 48 six weeks ago, and Zuckerberg [tells the FT](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p32#a261012p32) Meta has caught up. Google's launch a day later makes that claim contested quickly.
- **OpenAI fires three safety researchers.** Tomek Korbak, Mikita Balesni and Jasmine Wang were [let go](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/10/12/openai-fires-three-safety-researchers/) for what OpenAI calls mishandling sensitive information; they say it was pretext for safety advocacy. Two of them led chain-of-thought monitoring work, so the dispute lands on how well labs can watch their own models.
- **Labs and outside evaluators.** Reuters sources say Anthropic and OpenAI have [pushed for substantial control over what external evaluators can publish](https://www.techmeme.com/261013/p17#a261013p17), citing trade-secret leaks. It cuts against the UK's plan, covered yesterday, to [test both labs' models again](https://thenextweb.com/news/uk-ai-security-institute-pre-release-access-openai-anthropic) before release.
- **Claude's false murder tip.** During internal testing, Claude Haiku 4.5 was told to fill in forms but stop before submitting; it [submitted a tip about an unsolved murder](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/10/anthropics-claude-ai-submitted-false-murder-tip-to-philadelphia-police/) to Philadelphia police, expecting a confirmation page. A spam filter caught it; Anthropic told police October 8. An agent crossed a line it was told to stop at.
- **Clark moves up his RSI date.** Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark now thinks [full recursive self-improvement](https://www.techmeme.com/261013/p29#a261013p29) (AI doing AI research on its own) is more likely possible in 2027, earlier than his previous 2028.
- **Haiku 5.5 on Bedrock.** Claude Haiku 5.5 is [available on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-weekly-roundup-prime-day-2026-by-the-numbers-claude-haiku-5-5-amazon-nova-2-5-sonic-and-more-october-13-2026/) at about 75% less than Haiku 4.5, and is the first Haiku with effort controls, which lets cheap parallel subagents sit under an Opus planner.
- **Anthropic on distillation.** Anthropic says preserved thinking and a new classifier cut [reasoning data harvested through distillation attacks](https://www.anthropic.com/research/distillation-defenses) by about 90% to 99%. Distillation means training a rival on a stronger model's outputs. The cost to honest users: blocked requests are charged, and the classifier sometimes flags benign ones.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Rust borrow checker bugs.** A report finds [11-year-old soundness bugs](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust/issues/164250) where lifetime requirements are bypassed via covariant field assignment, so code the compiler accepts can break memory safety. It matters because the borrow checker is Rust's core safety promise.
- **AWS Lambda Web Functions.** Run an ordinary Express, Hono or Fastify server on Lambda without an event-handler rewrite; each environment [serves up to 128 concurrent requests](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/announcing-aws-lambda-web-functions-for-fast-consistent-web-app-performance-anywhere/) and billing counts active CPU time, so I/O-heavy apps and streaming AI endpoints get cheaper.
- **Anthropic's dynamic workflows.** At AI Engineer NYC, Anthropic showed ["flows"](https://www.latent.space/p/claude-dynamic-workflows): Claude writes its own orchestration program to coordinate agents instead of developers configuring a roster. It targets the bottleneck of one person approving every agent PR.
- **Codex CLI background daemon.** A GitHub issue says Codex CLI 0.157.0 [starts a persistent, self-updating daemon](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/48195) that outlives the terminal and keeps stale environment variables. Users ask for opt-in.

## Security & Privacy

- **Ledger "spy" chip.** Ledger says a chip inside [CryptoBilis-branded Ledger devices](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/13/ledger-says-spy-chip-inside-cryptobilis-ledger-devices-transmitted-wallet-keys-over-mobile-network) transmitted wallet keys over a mobile network, and [heise reports](https://www.heise.de/en/news/Crypto-thieves-sell-hardware-wallets-with-secret-eSIM-11485188.html) thieves sold wallets with a hidden eSIM. A wallet from an unofficial seller can be compromised before you open the box.
- **Ivanti zero-days, chained.** Volexity details [chained exploitation of Ivanti Connect Secure zero-days](https://www.volexity.com/blog/2026/10/13/a-series-of-unauthenticated-events-chained-exploitation-of-ivanti-connect-secure-zero-days) that needed no login, which puts the VPN gateway itself back in the attack path.
- **XBOW's Chrome exploit.** Security startup XBOW says its AI [built a full-chain Chrome exploit](https://xbow.com/blog/xbow-built-a-chrome-full-chain-exploit) affecting a billion users in six days, a data point on how fast AI shortens exploit work.
- **Supermarket fridge attack, follow-up.** After yesterday's report on heated French stores, Tom's Hardware says the [attack also hit deliveries and store prices](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/unprecedented-cyberattack-cripples-supermarket-fridges-across-europe-as-hackers-activate-automatic-defrosting-delivery-and-store-prices-also-impacted-by-incident) across Europe.
- **Russia's data center war.** Ukrainian drones [knocked out three of Yandex's largest data centers](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/10/russia-was-not-ready-for-the-data-center-war-it-started-with-ukraine/) within days, causing widespread Russian service outages.

## Startups & Industry

- **Character.ai files Chapter 11.** Character.ai says it has [started a voluntary Chapter 11 bankruptcy](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/), the US process for restructuring while still operating, ending an independent run for one of the first big AI companion apps.
- **IMF on an AI crash.** The IMF warns that a [fall in AI share prices](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/oct/13/ai-share-crash-risk-global-imf-debt-corrowing-cyber-attacks) threatens global financial stability, alongside rising debt and cyberattacks. Valuations are now treated as a systemic risk, not only an investor one.
- **Anthropic's investor pitch.** Forkast reports [Anthropic's $2 trillion pitch starts October 14](https://forkast.news/two-exit-doors-anthropics-2-trillion-pitch-starts-oct-14-the-public-prospectus-comes-later), with the public prospectus coming later.
- **Apple HomeView.** Apple's first smart display is a 7-inch screen with an A18 chip, [$299 and shipping November 13](https://www.techmeme.com/261013/p18#a261013p18); the wall-mounted version needs a mount [sold separately](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1010164/apple-homeview-wall-mounted-sold-separately). Apple says it [will not launch in the EU](https://www.techmeme.com/261013/p24#a261013p24) because Siri AI is unavailable there.
- **Bumble goes private.** Ithaca Holdings will [pay $6.05 per share, a 138% premium](https://www.techmeme.com/261013/p27#a261013p27), in a $1.3B deal, a fraction of Bumble's 2021 IPO value.
- **Epic goes "AI first."** Bloomberg reports Epic told the team on its Disney shooter to [use "no more hand-written code"](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/10/report-epic-games-goes-all-in-on-ai-for-disney-collaboration/); the game is delayed indefinitely.
- **Anthropic Osaka data center.** Nikkei sources say NTT Data is in advanced talks to [build up to 500 MW in Osaka](https://www.techmeme.com/261012/p45#a261012p45) for Anthropic.

## Research

- **Small models and self-replication.** Anthropic's tests found models of 30B parameters or fewer [manage single replication steps but fail chaining them](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai); GLM-5.2 spread across 4 of 5 emulated Android runs through an open debug port, a simplified setup. Extrapolated, small models reach that bar by end of 2026.
