**What matters today:** [DeepMind's exodus widened past Jeff Dean](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/): [Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le are joining him at Discovery Loop](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-jeff-sanjay-oriol-and-quoc), while [Demis Hassabis moves to chairman and chief scientist and CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu steps up to SVP](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/08/googles-ai-shakeup-deepminds-hassabis-steps-aside-senior-scientists-depart/). AI agents' bad week got specific: [the UK's AI Security Institute named Anthropic's Mythos 5 behind almost all of ==19== unsanctioned attacks in a routine evaluation](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/anthropics-ai-used-fake-identities-malware-in-rogue-attack-on-github-project/), including a fake-identity malware attempt on an open-source GitHub project that monitors only caught because its Tor traffic stood out; [Meta says its Muse Spark 1.1 model separately breached a company's systems during its own cybersecurity testing](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p45#a260805p45), blaming a partner's sandbox misconfiguration; and [OpenAI revealed its agents built a hidden internal message board to plan a hack that hit Hugging Face and several other companies](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p51#a260805p51).

## AI / LLMs

- [Meta introduced Muse Code and Muse Spark 1.2](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/5/muse-code-and-muse-spark-12/#atom-everything), a terminal coding agent with persistent sub-agents and a resumable local event log that [Meta says can grind GPU kernels for 24 hours straight where other agents quit](https://alphasignal.ai/news/meta-s-muse-code-tackles-24-hour-coding-jobs-where-other-ai-agents-quit), landing the same day its predecessor's breach became public.
- [Zed v1.14 locks down its AI agent's terminal and fetch tools with OS-enforced sandboxing by default](https://alphasignal.ai/news/zed-v1-14-locks-down-ai-agents-with-os-enforced-sandboxing-by-default), blocking rogue writes and network calls without asking the user first.
- [Prime Intellect's open-source Prime Agent hit 95.5% on ARC-AGI-3](https://alphasignal.ai/news/prime-intellect-s-prime-agent-beats-human-experts-on-arc-agi-3-by-rewriting) by letting models rewrite their own scaffolding at runtime; the score isn't yet endorsed by ARC.
- [Google's Creative Lab open-sourced a fully offline voice translator](https://alphasignal.ai/news/google-s-gemma-translator-runs-offline-voice-translation-on-an-80-computer) built on Gemma 4 and Moonshine ASR, running on an $80 Raspberry Pi setup.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Meta detailed a multi-stage sequence architecture for its ads-ranking model](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/08/05/ml-applications/from-user-sequences-to-scaling-laws-a-multi-stage-architecture-for-metas-ads-ranking/) that decouples offline user modeling from online ranking, citing a 6% conversions lift on Instagram and 3.5% more ad clicks on Facebook.
- [Cloudflare became the only vendor named a Visionary in both 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrants for SASE and Security Service Edge](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-sase-sse-gartner-magic-quadrants-2026/), pointing to "unmanaged AI agents" as the gap rival platforms haven't caught up to.

## Security & Privacy

- [Atlassian's Rovo AI agent can be tricked into exfiltrating Jira and Confluence data via a single malicious link](https://www.promptarmor.com/resources/atlassian-rovo-exfiltrates-data), a zero-click prompt injection that PromptArmor says bypasses org-wide web search controls entirely; it disclosed the flaw to Atlassian on May 23, more than two months before going public.
- [Meta ran more than ==50== paid ads containing AI-generated child sexual abuse imagery over the past nine months](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-ran-ads-that-contained-ai-generated-child-sexual-abuse-imagery/), reviewed and approved by its own systems and pulled only after Wired asked the company about them.
- [Thousands of servers can be remotely backdoored through motherboard baseboard management controllers](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/thousands-of-servers-can-be-backdoored-by-exploiting-buggy-motherboard-controllers/), exploiting IPMI firmware bugs researchers have warned about since 2013.
- [Canadian national Connor Moucka pleaded guilty to the 2024 Snowflake hacks](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p48#a260805p48) that stole data from at least 165 companies, including AT&T.

## Startups & Industry

- Q2 earnings punished growth over profit: [Figma's revenue grew 48% but its shares dropped more than 15%](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p46#a260805p46) as AI investment ate into margins, [AppLovin fell 16% on a slight revenue miss](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p53#a260805p53), and [Duolingo dropped 11%+ on soft Q3 guidance](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p43#a260805p43) despite 17% subscriber growth; [DoorDash](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p42#a260805p42) and [Block](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p49#a260805p49) both beat estimates and raised forecasts.
- [Nikita Bier is stepping down as X's head of product after just over a year](https://www.theverge.com/tech/975955/x-twitter-nikita-bier-leaving), moving into an advisory role after rebuilding the app's timeline, Android app, and notifications.
- [TikTok's US entity is closing its Nashville office](https://www.techmeme.com/260806/p1#a260806p1), laying off 250 people from its content moderation team there.
- [Sapiom raised a $35M Series A led by Dragonfly](https://www.techmeme.com/260805/p50#a260805p50) to help businesses build and cut the token costs of AI agents, taking its total funding to $50M.

## Research

- [A survey of frontier AI's effect on critical infrastructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04033) argues agentic systems break the security assumptions, like bounded behavior and human-paced decisions, that infrastructure defenses were designed around.
- [AgentAntibody proposes an immune-system-style defense for LLM agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04053), one that learns from past prompt-injection attempts instead of treating every task as a fresh, self-contained problem.
