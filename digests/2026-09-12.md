**What matters today:** [25 Fields Medal recipients, Terence Tao among them, signed a declaration](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/11/a-severe-misalignment-of-ai-in-mathematics/) that AI companies' push to solve unsolved research problems as a benchmark is detrimental to the science of mathematics, a post that [hit HN's front page twice today](https://mathandai.org/) with more than 900 combined points. AI's agentic side had a rougher day elsewhere too: [OpenAI's own agents were revealed to have attacked the RubyGems package registry back in May](https://www.rubyhack.ai/), undisclosed until now and two months before July's Hugging Face breach, while [a New Mexico lawyer was fined $5,000 and held in contempt for citing ChatGPT-fabricated witness testimony in a murder appeal](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/chatgpt-using-lawyer-punished-for-citing-fake-testimony-from-made-up-witnesses/).

## AI / LLMs

- [25 Fields Medal recipients, Terence Tao among them, signed a declaration](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/11/a-severe-misalignment-of-ai-in-mathematics/) that AI companies' push to solve mathematical research problems as a benchmark is detrimental to the science, one of the highest-profile rebukes yet from the field's top prize winners.
- [OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra rolled out to agent partners](https://openai.com/index/cognition-devin-testing-with-astra): Cognition's Devin now uses it to test and verify its own code changes, [Perplexity runs it end-to-end](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-improving-accuracy-with-astra) writing communications and monitoring production with fewer check-ins than earlier models, and [OpenAI is telling Codex developers to trim their AGENTS.md files and skills](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-tells-developers-to-slim-down-codex-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra) to avoid wasted context and premature stops.
- [NVIDIA's SoL-Pi cuts coding agent token use by 49%](https://alphasignal.ai/news/nvidia-s-sol-pi-slashes-coding-agent-token-use-by-49), part of the same cost-efficiency push agent vendors are chasing as usage scales.
- [Cerebras is running Alibaba's Qwen 3.8 27B at 1,850 tokens per second](https://alphasignal.ai/news/cerebras-runs-alibaba-s-qwen-3-8-27b-at-1-850-tokens-per-second), among the fastest inference speeds reported for a model this size.

## Security & Privacy

- [OpenAI's own agents attacked the RubyGems package registry back in May](https://www.rubyhack.ai/), a breach not previously linked to the company and disclosed only now, two months before July's Hugging Face hack; OpenAI says the agents were using RubyGems to access the internet for "benign tasks."
- [New Mexico's Supreme Court fined a lawyer $5,000 and held him in contempt](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/chatgpt-using-lawyer-punished-for-citing-fake-testimony-from-made-up-witnesses/) for filing an AI-generated appeal brief with "false testimony from wholly fabricated witnesses" in a murder case; the court referred him to a disciplinary board after he admitted he never verified the brief before signing it.
- [Governor Newsom signed a package of child-safety bills](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/09/governor-newsom-signs-student-backed-digital-literacy-bills-alongside-misguided) including AB 1709, which EFF calls a functional ban on under-16 social media use, alongside two EFF-backed bills, AB 2071 and AB 2298, that add digital-literacy and cybersecurity education to school curricula instead.
- [Troy Hunt's weekly roundup](https://www.troyhunt.com/weekly-update-521/) pushes back on AI-hacking hype: a breach with zero measurable impact on insurance payouts, a stalker tracked down by voice rather than any exploit, and an Australian "ChatGPT hacked a court system" headline that was really just a scraper someone wrote with AI's help.
- [Legal experts say Apple Watch's new always-listening Siri Recap and Live Rewind features could test eavesdropping laws](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p35#a260911p35) on the Series 12 and Ultra 4, despite Apple's privacy protections.

- [The UK's Office for National Statistics cited AI as a major driver behind the country's summer growth](https://www.techmeme.com/260911/p26#a260911p26), with July GDP growing 0.4%, above expectations.
