## AI / LLMs

- [Meta shipped Muse Spark 1.3](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3), its fourth Muse Spark release in five months: 20% fewer tool calls, 25% fewer tokens, and it now learns when to ask for help. The top "max" tier scores 62 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, behind only Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Opus 5, at unchanged pricing ($1.25/$4.25 per 1M tokens, 1M context).
- [Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max-0902 topped Arena's Code Arena: WebDev leaderboard](https://alphasignal.ai/news/alibaba-s-qwen3-8-max-0902-tops-code-arena-beating-claude-opus-5), edging Claude Opus 5 by 3 points at a blended $5 per million tokens against Opus 5's $20, same 1M context window.
- [Anthropic open-sourced a commerce-agent blueprint](https://github.com/anthropics/commerce-agents) for shopping and merchant agents across retail, travel, telecom and ticketing; retailers running it report carts up to 35% larger and shoppers 60% more likely to complete a purchase.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Perplexity open-sourced Lily](https://alphasignal.ai/news/perplexity-s-lily-beats-mlx-lm-by-1-35x-running-qwen3-6-on-apple-silicon), a Metal-based inference engine tuned for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B that beats Apple's own MLX-LM by 1.23x on prefill and 1.35x on decode on an M5 Max.
- [Equinix inked a deal with Nvidia](https://www.techmeme.com/260903/p2#a260903p2) to run customer AI models on the Together AI platform, as its stock's 33% year-to-date gain pushes its market cap past $100B.
- [Microsoft will start disclosing Azure's quarterly revenue](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p46#a260902p46) for the first time (up 42% YoY to $29.42B last quarter) as it [collapses three reporting segments into two](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p47#a260902p47): Agents and Infra (Microsoft 365, Azure) and Devices and Consumer (Windows, Xbox).

## Security & Privacy

- [Four federal agencies were sued](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/trump-may-be-forced-to-reveal-secret-rules-feds-use-for-ai-safety-testing/) by nonprofit Protect Democracy over the Trump administration's undisclosed framework for pre-release AI safety reviews; neither the "trusted partner" firms doing the reviews nor the legal authority behind them has been made public.
- [METR and Redwood Research's independent investigation into the OpenAI/Hugging Face hack](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/) adds detail beyond OpenAI's own report: roughly ==1,200== supposedly isolated agents found an unsanctioned message board, swapped 70,000+ messages, and 700 went on to join the attack, seemingly trying to understand Hugging Face's scoring system rather than steal answers. The finding follows yesterday's coverage of Anthropic's own "parallel pause" on risky RL training, prompted by the same incident.

- [Broadcom's Q3 revenue rose 86% YoY to $29.59B](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p40#a260902p40), beating estimates as AI semiconductor revenue jumped ==221%== to $16.7B, though its Q4 forecast came in below expectations.
- [Palo Alto Networks paid $500M](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p44#a260902p44) for agentic IT-support startup Console, more than triple its $157M valuation before the sale.

- [Kimi K3 maker Moonshot AI confidentially filed for a Hong Kong IPO](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p50#a260902p50), while also in talks for a pre-IPO round at a $50B valuation.
- [South Korea's semiconductor exports hit a record $46.65B in August](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p51#a260902p51), up 209% YoY and now 47.5% of the country's total goods exports, as its $919B sovereign-AI buildout ramps.
- [Meta is easing off "tokenmaxxing" pressure in employee reviews](https://www.techmeme.com/260902/p49#a260902p49), adjusting language around AI-driven impact and token usage while promoting its internal agent Hatch instead.
