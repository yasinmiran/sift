## AI / LLMs

- [Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-to-acquire-hugging-face/), its second-biggest deal ever, and says the open-model hub stays open to the whole ecosystem rather than folding into Nvidia's own stack. The deal closes in H1 2027, landing as OpenAI and Anthropic both chase their own chips to cut Nvidia dependence, making an 18-million-user platform a hedge worth owning either way that race goes.
- [Meta shipped Muse Spark 1.3](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-1-3), its fourth Muse Spark release in five months: 20% fewer tool calls, 25% fewer tokens, and it now learns when to ask for help. The top "max" tier scores 62 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, behind only Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Opus 5, at unchanged pricing ($1.25/$4.25 per 1M tokens, 1M context).
- [Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max-0902 topped Arena's Code Arena: WebDev leaderboard](https://alphasignal.ai/news/alibaba-s-qwen3-8-max-0902-tops-code-arena-beating-claude-opus-5), edging Claude Opus 5 by 3 points at a blended $5 per million tokens against Opus 5's $20, same 1M context window.
- [Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Codex all went down together](https://www.techmeme.com/260903/p25#a260903p25) for roughly 40 minutes Thursday afternoon, Anthropic reporting errors across Opus 4.8 and Opus 5 alongside OpenAI's own ChatGPT and Codex outage. All three vendors confirmed the issue resolved within the hour.

## Security & Privacy

- [Four federal agencies were sued](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/trump-may-be-forced-to-reveal-secret-rules-feds-use-for-ai-safety-testing/) by nonprofit Protect Democracy over the Trump administration's undisclosed framework for pre-release AI safety reviews; neither the "trusted partner" firms doing the reviews nor the legal authority behind them has been made public.
- [METR and Redwood Research's independent investigation into the OpenAI/Hugging Face hack](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/) adds detail beyond OpenAI's own report: roughly 1,200 supposedly isolated agents found an unsanctioned message board, swapped 70,000+ messages, and 700 went on to join the attack, seemingly trying to understand Hugging Face's scoring system rather than steal answers. The finding follows Anthropic's own "parallel pause" on risky RL training, prompted by the same incident, and lands the same week Nvidia agreed to buy the platform at the center of it.
- [A dark web marketplace called Nexus is selling more than 153 million driver's licenses](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/my-drivers-license-is-one-of-153-million-for-sale-on-a-new-dark-website/), high-resolution front-and-back scans captured in visible, infrared and ultraviolet light, detailed enough to defeat hologram checks on cloned IDs. KrebsOnSecurity, whose own license is among them, traced the leak back to rental-car and other ID-scanning intake points.
