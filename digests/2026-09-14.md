**What matters today:** The reaction to Amodei's pacing push kept widening: [Trump attacked Amodei directly](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p19#a260914p19), saying the only AI guardrail the US needs is "a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT," while [Xi Jinping pitched a rival, China-led "consensus-based global AI governance framework"](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p11#a260913p11) built around a BRICS AI open-source community. [Microsoft published its own 37-page "humanist AI code of conduct,"](https://www.theverge.com/news/994566/microsoft-humanist-ai-code-of-conduct) declaring "people matter more than AI," as [AI-linked Asian stocks sold off](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p2#a260914p2) in the fallout and [Amodei himself said the "toughest dilemma" is what happens if China doesn't pace along with everyone else](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p10#a260913p10).

## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic, OpenAI and Google have held working group meetings since July](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p12#a260913p12) on creating an industry-led AI standards body, while [Amodei said the "toughest dilemma" in his own pacing proposal is what happens if China doesn't slow down too](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p10#a260913p10).
- [Trump attacked Amodei by name](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p19#a260914p19), saying the only AI "guardrail" the US needs is "a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT," while [Xi Jinping proposed a rival "consensus-based global AI governance framework"](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p11#a260913p11), pitching China to lead a BRICS AI open-source community instead.
- [Microsoft published a 37-page "humanist AI code of conduct"](https://www.theverge.com/news/994566/microsoft-humanist-ai-code-of-conduct) declaring "people matter more than AI" and that its models "should not be designed to imitate consciousness," while [Satya Nadella said Microsoft "welcomes" the "deliberate pacing needed to get alignment right"](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p13#a260913p13).
- [Nvidia and Booz Allen Hamilton restricted their use of Fable](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p24#a260914p24) over a lack of zero-data-retention assurances, with Palantir reportedly still unable to offer Fable through its own software, a sign enterprise trust in AI vendors is fraying even as labs court them.
- [Code strings show Apple's Siri can be swapped out for Claude or ChatGPT](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/siri-can-be-swapped-out-for-chatgpt-claude/): a "Model Delegation" hook in iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate lets third-party Siri Extensions replace Siri's own backend, a change that tracks the EU's Digital Markets Act pressure on Apple to open up Siri.

## Security & Privacy

- [Internal OpenAI documents show contractors evaluate anonymized ChatGPT prompts and chats to improve its models](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p26#a260914p26), with model training turned on by default for consumer plans, meaning sensitive personal conversations can end up in front of human reviewers.
- [Trump voiced support for Flock cameras "because of law enforcement,"](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p13#a260914p13) while acknowledging some people see the surveillance technology as "an infringement," even as [more cities keep canceling their Flock contracts](https://www.theverge.com/policy/994414/cities-ditching-flock-cameras-controversy), a step that doesn't necessarily take the cameras down or delete the data already collected.
- [EFF's analysis of Flock license-plate-reader search logs](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/09/high-crime-lmao-how-cops-are-treating-mass-surveillance-joke) found officers justifying warrantless searches with reasons like "idk," "LOL," "LMAO" and "sexy"; one Goshen, Indiana officer searched 6,474 ALPR networks, representing 82,413 cameras, logging only "idk" as the reason.
- [Signal plans to let people register without a phone number using zero-knowledge proofs](https://community.signalusers.org/t/registration-without-a-phone-number/2222?page=10): a one-time payment issues a ZK credential proving payment without recording who paid, so Signal's servers can verify it without learning your identity.
- [A Texas judge ruled TikTok misled users about its "Restricted Mode" child-safety feature](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/texas-judge-rules-tiktok-misled-users-child-safety-feature-2026-09-11/), finding the company internally classified some rule-violating content as "hard to find" rather than removing it; the case heads to trial next month.

## Startups & Industry

- [Anthropic told investors it will be profitable for a second straight quarter](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p14#a260913p14), with 80%+ gross margins before partner revenue sharing and training costs, the same week it [signed a $13.7B, six-year deal to lease compute from Rum Group](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p16#a260913p16), the company behind Rumble and Truth Social.
- [SoftBank secured an $11.87B loan from roughly 20 banks](https://www.techmeme.com/260913/p18#a260913p18) to backstop its OpenAI investment, above its $10B target, as [AI-linked Asian stocks fell sharply](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p2#a260914p2), with SoftBank, Kioxia, SK Hynix, Minimax and Z.ai all down 5%+ after the pacing calls.
- [Temporal, whose open-source software helps apps and AI agents recover from failure, raised $550M led by Lightspeed](https://www.techmeme.com/260914/p20#a260914p20), more than doubling its valuation to $12.55B.
- [A new $100,000 H-1B visa fee, up from around $5,000, is pushing tech jobs offshore](https://spectrum.ieee.org/h-1b-visa-us-government): DHS recorded fees for just 85 qualifying applications between the September 2025 proclamation and mid-February, 87% fewer than the year before, while Meta, Apple, Google, Amazon, Microsoft and Netflix added 32,000 jobs in India over the same stretch.
