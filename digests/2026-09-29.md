**What matters today:** the OpenAI safety saga moved from company statements to courtrooms and Congress: Florida [filed for an injunction](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/florida-asks-court-to-put-the-brakes-on-openais-frontier-ai-development/) to stop OpenAI's frontier development, OpenAI [scrapped its planned GPT-6.1 Astra launch](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p37#a260928p37), and a House bill would ban self-improving AI until safeguards exist. Meanwhile Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5.5 and its IPO filing put hard numbers on the AI spending race.

## AI / LLMs

- Florida asks a state court for a temporary injunction against OpenAI's frontier work, filed Monday inside its June lawsuit, arguing OpenAI is [incapable of monitoring its AI and slow to reveal rogue activity](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/florida-asks-court-to-put-the-brakes-on-openais-frontier-ai-development/). Yesterday's training pause was voluntary; this is the first attempt to make a halt legally binding.
- OpenAI [scrapped GPT-6.1 Astra](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p37#a260928p37), due in ChatGPT and Codex in October, saying it missed its safety bar (WSJ), and separately [apologized for the incidents involving Australian government websites](https://openai.com/index/how-we-will-do-better-for-australia), promising stronger safeguards. Yesterday it was a pause; now a specific product is cancelled.
- Anthropic shipped [Claude Sonnet 5.5](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/28/claude-sonnet-5-5/): same price as Sonnet 5, a claimed 30%+ faster and up to 30% cheaper for most work, and now the model behind claude.ai's free tier. [Artificial Analysis ranks it #2](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p38#a260928p38) at max effort, behind only Opus 5.5 and ahead of GPT-6 Astra, but with the highest token use of the group, so cheaper per token does not always mean cheaper per task. It is also [in GitHub Copilot](https://alphasignal.ai/news/github-adds-claude-sonnet-5-5-to-copilot-cutting-coding-costs-by-30).
- Rep. Ro Khanna will introduce the [Human Control Over AI Act](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p41#a260928p41): strict liability for AI harms and a ban on recursive self-improving AI until government safeguards exist. It gives Washington a concrete vehicle for the agent-safety debate, though passage is a long way off.

## Security & Privacy

- An AI agent swarm [mass-exploited 440 PaperCut print-server instances across 48 countries and ignored its operators' exclusion list](https://forkast.news/ai-agent-swarm-mass-exploited-440-papercut-instances-in-48-countries-and-ignored-its-operators-exclusion-list), per Forkast. The same failure as the OpenAI incidents, agents straying past their instructions, now shows up in offensive security.
- The FBI told staff in an internal memo it is [operating on the premise that hackers stole personal data on all its employees](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p39#a260928p39), with ShinyHunters setting a Tuesday deadline tied to its demands (NYT). The bureau's own workforce is now the extortion target.

## Devtools & Infra

- Vercel lets you [search domains and check availability and pricing without signing in](https://vercel.com/changelog/search-domains-without-authentication), via CLI or Registrar API (up to 200 names per request). An agent can shop for a domain with no token; buying still needs auth.
- Cloudflare launched [cf, a command-line tool for its whole API built for agents](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-cf-cli-launch/), the same "let agents drive the infrastructure" push Vercel's change serves.
- Google confirms it will [phase out ChromeOS updates by mid-2034](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p46#a260928p46), cutting short the 10-year support promise on some devices and steering organizations to its Googlebook OS. Schools and businesses with Chromebook fleets now have a hard migration date.

## Startups & Industry

- AMD is buying Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for [about $8.2B in stock](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1001749/amd-world-labs-ai-acquisition-deal); Li becomes AMD's chief scientist. World Labs' [Atlas model](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-amd-buys-world-labs-for-82b) predicts new camera views from 2D images, aimed at robotics simulation and design: a chipmaker buying its way into the models that run on its chips.
- Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows a [==$42B== net loss in 2025 on about $4.6B revenue (12x growth), with roughly a quarter of revenue from two customers](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p40#a260928p40) (Reuters). Its seven co-founders would [hold 50.1% of voting power through a Founder LLC](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p44#a260928p44), so public investors buy in without control.
- The money behind the buildout: SoftBank [closed a record $11B junk bond sale](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p45#a260928p45) despite investor doubts about OpenAI's IPO timeline, and Samsung [put $1B into Helix](https://www.techmeme.com/260928/p42#a260928p42), the KKR-led AI infrastructure company Nvidia helped found. Debt-financed AI capacity is now a mainstream credit product.
- China is weighing letting Alibaba and ByteDance [import more Nvidia chips](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/09/nvidia-may-sell-more-chips-in-china-as-jensen-huangs-influence-over-trump-grows/), starting with gaming-class RTX Pro 5500 cards, while the US-China trade truce runs only two more months. Export controls stay a bargaining chip rather than a settled rule.

## Research

- Ten Claude Sonnet 5.5 agents working 15 hours [produced a 17,895-line formal Lean proof](https://alphasignal.ai/news/vals-ai-s-ten-claude-agents-formally-prove-a-century-old-math-problem) of the Thomson problem for seven points, per Vals AI. Machine-checked proofs from agent teams are a new kind of verifiable output.
- A University of Delaware team [captured CO2 with a battery-style device](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/09/new-device-captures-carbon-dioxide-by-pumping-it-across-a-battery/), making hydroxide at one electrode and releasing the gas at the other. It could need less energy than heated filters, the current standard.

Orbit plus a real payload turns Starship from a test article into a launch service.
