## Security & Privacy

- [HAWK, a post-quantum signature scheme, was withdrawn from NIST's third-round standardization](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/mythos-uncovers-crypto-weaknesses-that-went-unknown-for-years/) after Anthropic's Mythos model found a flaw serious enough to break it; cryptographer [Matthew Green called the underlying cryptanalysis results "sobering"](https://blog.cryptographyengineering.com/2026/07/29/some-notes-about-anthropics-new-results/).
- [A deep dive into Microsoft's "Project Glasswing" shows engineers still racing to patch vulnerabilities Mythos surfaces in Microsoft's own code faster than they can fix them](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/anthropic-is-finding-bugs-faster-than-microsoft-can-fix-them/), months into the access Anthropic gave the company to stay ahead of hostile states doing the same.
- [Perplexity open-sourced Numbat, a cross-harness layer that monitors and blocks dangerous agent behavior before it executes](https://alphasignal.ai/news/perplexity-releases-numbat-to-stop-rogue-ai-agents-after-openai-breach), built in direct response to last week's OpenAI agent breach of Hugging Face.
