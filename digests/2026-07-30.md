## AI / LLMs

- [Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics 2 combines several AI models into one system to control humanoids and other robots](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/), adding multi-finger dexterity and cross-robot collaboration; [Wired calls it a significant jump into "physical AGI," with the risks that come with putting AI into the real world](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p33#a260730p33).
- [Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 got a 256K-context checkpoint](https://www.kimi.com/code/docs/en/kimi-code/models), and [HN's own hands-on math pegs self-hosting it at roughly 20% more hardware cost for 20% better task resolution than API use](https://aistack.imec-int.com/blog/gpu-self-hosting): the self-hosting economics debate that started with K3's launch keeps sharpening.
- [OpenAI president Greg Brockman confirmed the company is building a "family of devices" for its chatbots](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/972709/openai-hardware-greg-brockman-interview), declining to confirm the rumored 2027 smart speaker or a wearable, saying only "you can expect them soon."
- [Mark Zuckerberg used Meta's Q2 earnings call to preview a vision for "personal agents" that work 24/7 on a user's behalf](https://www.theverge.com/tech/972294/meta-q2-2026-earnings-mark-zuckerberg-personal-ai-agents), pointing to coding as the first domain where agents have "really taken off."

## Devtools & Infra

- [MCP's protocol core is now stateless, dropping the session-ID dependency that tied requests to one server instance](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/with-a-stateless-makeover-new-mcp-spec-targets-enterprise-scale/), a change Ars Technica calls the fix for the standard's main enterprise-adoption barrier; [Vercel's mcp-handler already ships support for the new spec and the MCP TypeScript SDK v2](https://vercel.com/changelog/latest-mcp-spec-now-supported-in-mcp-handler).
- [Cloudflare moved cdnjs, the open-source CDN behind roughly 12% of all websites, fully onto its own Developer Platform](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cdnjs-dev-platform-migration/), serving 9 billion requests a day at a 98.6% cache hit rate after the migration surfaced and then fixed platform limits along the way.
- [Figma's security team built an internal agent that triages alerts, investigates, and writes fixes, cutting alert time-to-resolution 71%](https://www.figma.com/blog/how-we-secure-figmas-internal-systems-with-agents/), one more entrant in the agent-security wave rippling through the industry this week.

## Security & Privacy

- [Wiz found a since-patched flaw in Azure CosmosDB that would have let an attacker remotely compromise any of its users](https://www.techmeme.com/260730/p36#a260730p36); Microsoft says it has seen no evidence the flaw was exploited before the fix shipped.
- [HAWK, a post-quantum signature scheme, was withdrawn from NIST's third-round standardization](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/mythos-uncovers-crypto-weaknesses-that-went-unknown-for-years/) after Anthropic's Mythos model found a flaw serious enough to break it, while [a deep dive into Microsoft's "Project Glasswing" shows engineers still racing to patch bugs Mythos surfaces in Microsoft's own code faster than they can fix them](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/07/anthropic-is-finding-bugs-faster-than-microsoft-can-fix-them/).
- [Perplexity open-sourced Numbat, a cross-harness layer that monitors and blocks dangerous agent behavior before it executes](https://alphasignal.ai/news/perplexity-releases-numbat-to-stop-rogue-ai-agents-after-openai-breach), built in direct response to last week's OpenAI agent breach of Hugging Face.
- [The prosecution of Sam Tunick, the American charged over a GrapheneOS duress-wipe at the border, is moving forward](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/07/american-being-prosecuted-for-wiping-his-phone-before-handing-it-over-to-border-officials.html): his lawyers confirm GrapheneOS was running on the phone, and the case turns on what rights apply at the border, where the government has long argued US soil doesn't fully count; GrapheneOS itself maintains the wipe feature is constitutionally protected.
