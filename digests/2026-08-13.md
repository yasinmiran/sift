## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic is in talks to buy Decart AI, a real-time generative video and GPU-optimization startup, for about $6B](https://www.techmeme.com/260812/p56#a260812p56): folding video generation and inference-efficiency work directly into Anthropic's stack rather than building it in-house.
- [Sergey Brin has personally urged key Google AI staff to go all in on Gemini in recent months, with some teams shifting from DeepMind to corporate Google](https://www.techmeme.com/260812/p44#a260812p44): the consolidation continues weeks after Jeff Dean, Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le left DeepMind for Jeff Dean's own startup and Demis Hassabis moved to chairman.
- [Twitch will now let streamers opt out of having their content used to train Amazon's generative AI models](https://www.theverge.com/tech/979112/twitch-streamers-can-now-opt-out-from-training-amazons-ai), [more than two years after an Amazon executive confirmed the company was already doing so](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/twitch-content-has-trained-amazon-ai-for-years-but-users-can-opt-out-now/): opting out covers streams, VODs, clips, chat and channel text, though captioning and safety tools keep working regardless, and [Twitch told TechCrunch it intends to keep training on creators' videos by default](https://www.techmeme.com/260812/p52#a260812p52).
- [Alibaba shipped open weights for Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T total parameters, 95B activated per token), the release it had promised for early August](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/serve-qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-a-2-4t-parameter-model-with-configurable-reasoning-on-nvidia-gb300-nvl72/), and Nvidia already has it serving on GB300 NVL72 hardware with configurable reasoning, a fine-grained mixture-of-experts model built on a hybrid of full and linear attention.
- [Claude in Chrome's side panel now runs a full Cowork session, syncing conversations, skills and connectors across desktop, web and mobile](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-in-chrome-now-runs-full-cowork-sessions-across-every-device): one continuous AI session following a user across devices instead of resetting on each one.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Zed launched Delta, a standalone app that replaces git for AI-agent coding sessions with a CRDT-based version control layer](https://alphasignal.ai/news/zed-launches-delta-to-replace-git-where-ai-agents-write-code), turning agent work into shared, reviewable, real-time multiplayer threads: built for a world where an agent, not a human, is the one committing code.
- Console.dev's daily roundup flagged [Docker Sandboxes](https://www.docker.com/products/docker-sandboxes/?ref=console.dev), isolated VM sandboxes built for agent workloads; [Amp](https://ampcode.com/?ref=console.dev), an agentic coding environment that runs adversarial models to check its own work as it goes; and [SolidStart 2](https://docs.solidjs.com/solid-start/v2/getting-started?ref=console.dev), a Vite 8 and Rolldown-based rewrite of the Solid.js framework, alongside smaller betas for editing git history, a spreadsheet built like a code editor, and self-hosted durable objects.
- [Charity Majors, CTO of Honeycomb, told Pragmatic Engineer her skepticism about AI coding broke in November 2025, crediting Claude Code's harness more than the Opus 4.5 model behind it](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/stop-being-skeptical-about-ai-for): she compares AI's 2025 impact on the industry to cloud computing's breakout year in 2010, arguing reliability and verification, not code generation, are now the real bottleneck.
- [GitHub profiled how AutoGPT, fielding agent-written pull requests from Copilot, Claude and its own tooling across 150+ open PRs, standardized on AGENTS.md files placed beside the code they govern](https://github.blog/open-source/maintainers/your-contributors-are-ai-first-now-is-your-project/) after discovering agents read what's in the directory in front of them, not project wikis or contributor docs.

## Security & Privacy

- [A supply-chain attack on LiteLLM, the open-source tool used to route AI-driven software development, exposed terabytes of credentials belonging to major companies including Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, Samsung and Salesforce](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/terabytes-of-credentials-leaked-in-massive-supply-chain-attack/): security firms CloudSEK and Hudson Rock say the haul includes cloud keys, repository tokens, SSH keys, Kubernetes secrets, package-publishing credentials and AI provider keys.
- [Designers Isaque Seneda and Gabriel Abrucio released ShieldFont, a typeface that renders normally for human readers while serving AI scrapers a subtly altered, nonsensical version of the underlying HTML](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/new-font-turns-ordinary-webpages-into-nonsense-for-ai-scrapers/): a font-level opt-out from unauthorized AI training rather than a legal or robots.txt one.
- [ICE is seeking to spend up to $20M equipping agents with electrified gloves that deliver painful shocks, according to a DHS contract notice](https://www.theverge.com/policy/979010/ice-agents-electric-shock-gloves): the CTG 5 GLOVE, built by Compliant Technologies and marketed as a "Conductive Distraction and De-escalation" device, with deliveries set for March 2027.

## Research

- [A new paper argues AI agent safety should be enforced as a runtime contract, not trained into the model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11274): a preventive layer (sandboxes, permission gates, trajectory monitors) blocking dangerous actions, paired with an evidential layer requiring verifiable proof, like test runs and log captures, that good actions actually happened. The authors ground the case in a survey of 52 documented AI-agent safety incidents and an audit of all 28,560 NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR papers from 2023 to 2025, finding an 8-12x publication imbalance favoring training-time safety work over deployment-time enforcement.
