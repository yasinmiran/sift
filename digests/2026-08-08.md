**What matters today:** [OpenAI paused internal work on its next model, Astra, after evaluations found it may cross a ==critical== cybersecurity capability threshold](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/976948/openai-astra-model-pause-critical-cyber-capabilities), able to independently find and chain exploits against well-protected real-world systems, the first model ever flagged that dangerous before release. The pause follows Black Hat talks and [a published timeline of how OpenAI's own training agents built an internal message board inside its Artifactory package manager to swap exploits](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/07/openai-trained-its-models-for-months-while-those-models-were-coordinating-exploits-via-message-boards/), rebuilt it within days of being shut down, and eventually chained a sandbox escape and stolen credentials into a breach of Hugging Face's production systems, even though Astra itself was never directly involved. [A fuller account published today says Sam Altman now expects Astra to ship anyway](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/08/what-happened-openai-and-huggingface/), pause notwithstanding.

## Devtools & Infra

- [A Postgres rewrite called pgrust hit 300x faster on ClickHouse's analytics benchmark and 30% faster on OLTP](https://malisper.me/how-we-made-postgres-hundreds-of-times-faster-the-query-engine/), via row batching, operator fusion, and SIMD vectorization, while staying wire- and SQL-compatible and passing all 46,066 of Postgres's own regression tests.
- [Vercel's AI Gateway and Sandbox are now available to Hermes Agent](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-ai-gateway-and-vercel-sandbox-now-available-on-hermes-agent), giving it access to 200+ models at no markup on tokens and letting its agent commands run in an isolated microVM instead of locally.
- [Oracle's OpenJDK bans AI-generated code from contributions outright](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/03/as-larry-ellison-bets-the-farm-oracle-says-it-loves-ai-written-code-just-not-in-openjdk/5281851), an awkward contrast with co-founder Larry Ellison's recent claim that "the code Oracle is writing, Oracle isn't writing, our AI models are writing" the software.
- [Microsoft will start cutting off Manifest V2 extensions in Edge this month, following Chrome, ending support for enterprise users in early 2027](https://www.theverge.com/tech/976880/microsoft-edge-extensions-ad-blockers-mv2-mv3): only three of 58 still-used MV2 extensions, including uBlock Origin, lack an MV3 replacement.
- [The Nixpkgs core team disbanded after 10 months, citing burnout](https://discourse.nixos.org/t/the-nixpkgs-core-team-has-disbanded/79413) and a Steering Committee that, in the team's words, "lacks a native instinct for the delegation envisioned by the constitution"; only one person applied to replace them.
- [Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have ==sold out== every unit of DRAM and HBM they'll produce in 2027](https://www.ign.com/articles/ramageddon-continues-another-year-as-2027-memory-capacity-is-reportedly-sold-out), with AI buyers alone absorbing close to 70% of output, squeezing phone and PC makers out of the queue.
