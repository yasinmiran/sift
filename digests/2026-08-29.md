**What matters today:** [OpenAI notified SpaceX it will wind down the contract supplying OpenAI models to Cursor, with a proposed shutoff date of November 12](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex), saying it "cannot be confident" SpaceX will keep using the technology within OpenAI's terms of service after SpaceX's $60 billion acquisition of the coding assistant closed. [A federal appeals court separately ruled that Kalshi's sports-event contracts are gambling, not swaps](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/kalshi-cant-evade-nevada-gambling-laws-by-calling-bets-swaps-court-rules/), rejecting the prediction market's bid to preempt Nevada's gaming laws and creating a split with an earlier appeals ruling that regulators say now needs the Supreme Court to settle. [Z.ai released GLM-5.3's weights on Hugging Face under a new license that drops the old MIT terms](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p25#a260828p25), requiring any company with $10 billion or more in yearly revenue to pass a Z.ai security review before hosting the model.

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI will cut Cursor's direct model access on November 12](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-cuts-cursor-s-access-to-gpt-models-after-spacex-takeover), citing distrust of SpaceX following the $60 billion takeover; Cursor's SpaceX ownership has already strained other partnerships this month.
- [Z.ai put GLM-5.3's weights on Hugging Face but dropped the MIT license](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p25#a260828p25), now requiring $10B+ revenue providers to clear a Z.ai security review before hosting it, a departure from the fully open terms rivals like DeepSeek still use.
- [Anthropic gave Claude 48 hours and one GPU to fix ten alignment failures planted in small models](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-beats-human-researchers-fixing-ai-alignment-at-4-an-hour), then tested whether it could align a frontier successor, beating human researchers at a fraction of the cost.
- [Stripe's Link Wallet lets an AI agent complete online checkout end to end](https://alphasignal.ai/news/stripe-s-link-wallet-lets-ai-agents-buy-things-on-your-behalf), using single-use virtual cards that still need per-purchase approval.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Claude Code's desktop app now supports a /resume command that picks up any terminal session](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-code-finally-bridges-terminal-and-desktop-sessions), preserving the full conversation and context between CLI and desktop.
- [GitHub Copilot can now fix bugs from inside Slack and Teams conversations](https://alphasignal.ai/news/github-copilot-invades-slack-and-teams-to-fix-bugs-during-meetings), moving agentic coding into the chat apps engineers already use during meetings.
- [Nvidia's TensorRT Model Connect deploys an open model from checkpoint to inference in two commands](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/deploy-an-open-model-from-checkpoint-to-inference-in-two-commands-with-nvidia-tensorrt-model-connect/), collapsing a setup that normally spans several tools.

## Security & Privacy

- [CXMT sued the Pentagon over its "Chinese military company" designation](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p29#a260828p29), arguing the memory chipmaker isn't affiliated with China's military and its chips aren't built for military use.
- [Cities are terminating Flock license-plate camera contracts at a record pace this August](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/cities-terminate-flock-contracts-at-record-pace-in-august/), and [Texas Governor Greg Abbott ordered state agencies to pause funding for Flock cameras](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p27#a260828p27) after an investigation found one agency had steered $30 million-plus into building out the state's network.

## Startups & Industry

- [Kalshi lost its bid to treat sports-event contracts as federally preempted swaps](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/kalshi-cant-evade-nevada-gambling-laws-by-calling-bets-swaps-court-rules/): a panel of three Trump-appointed judges ruled unanimously that Nevada can enforce its gambling laws against the prediction market.
- [AI cloud provider Lambda raised about $1 billion in private short-dated debt to buy Nvidia GPUs](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p18#a260828p18) that it will then lease to Microsoft, a financing structure that keeps compute flowing without Microsoft owning the hardware.
- [Meta is running full-page ads in US newspapers pressuring TikTok and YouTube to adopt teen-safety protections comparable to its own](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p21#a260828p21), turning its own child-safety settlements into a competitive cudgel.
- [Google is expanding how far its AI Mode results push traditional search links down the page](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986364/google-search-ai-overviews-auto-expand), continuing a shift that has already reshaped traffic for publishers and SEO-dependent businesses.

## Research
