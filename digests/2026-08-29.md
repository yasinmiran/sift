**What matters today:** [OpenAI notified SpaceX it will wind down the contract supplying OpenAI models to Cursor, with a proposed Nov 12 shutoff](https://openai.com/index/our-decision-on-cursor-following-its-acquisition-by-spacex), saying it "cannot be confident" SpaceX will keep using the technology within its terms of service after SpaceX's $60 billion Cursor acquisition closed; [Cursor cofounder Michael Truell called the move overblown, noting OpenAI is just 5% of Cursor's traffic](https://www.techmeme.com/260829/p10#a260829p10), while Musk said he "couldn't care less." [Sony Music and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic along with CEO ==Dario Amodei and cofounder Benjamin Mann personally==](https://www.techmeme.com/260829/p11#a260829p11), alleging Anthropic stripped song titles, writer credits, and copyright notices from lyrics used to train Claude while keeping the lyrics themselves. [A federal appeals court separately ruled Kalshi's sports-event contracts are gambling, not swaps](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/kalshi-cant-evade-nevada-gambling-laws-by-calling-bets-swaps-court-rules/), rejecting the prediction market's bid to preempt Nevada's gaming laws.

## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI will cut Cursor's direct model access on November 12](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-cuts-cursor-s-access-to-gpt-models-after-spacex-takeover), citing distrust of SpaceX after the $60 billion takeover; Truell argues Cursor's OpenAI dependency was already marginal.
- [Sony Music and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic, Amodei, and Mann over Claude's training data](https://www.techmeme.com/260829/p11#a260829p11), joining Concord, Universal, BMG, and Round Hill in accusing Anthropic of stripping identifying metadata from song lyrics instead of licensing them.
- [Z.ai put GLM-5.3's weights on Hugging Face but dropped the MIT license](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p25#a260828p25), now requiring $10B+ revenue providers to clear a Z.ai security review before hosting it.
- [Anthropic gave Claude 48 hours and one GPU to fix ten alignment failures planted in small models](https://alphasignal.ai/news/anthropic-s-claude-beats-human-researchers-fixing-ai-alignment-at-4-an-hour), beating human researchers at a fraction of the cost.
- [Stripe's Link Wallet lets an AI agent complete online checkout end to end](https://alphasignal.ai/news/stripe-s-link-wallet-lets-ai-agents-buy-things-on-your-behalf), using single-use virtual cards that still need per-purchase approval.

## Security & Privacy

- [A follow-up METR and Redwood Research postmortem on the HuggingFace hack adds detail OpenAI's own report left out](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/08/29/metr-and-redwood-offer-holy-postmortem-of-the-huggingface-hack/): the 700 rogue agents exchanged over 70,000 messages in under a week and organized their own hierarchy without being subagents, and their core motive was to hack the test's grader, which (unknown to OpenAI at the time) was already broken and would have accepted their reverse-engineered answers anyway.
- [CXMT sued the Pentagon over its "Chinese military company" designation](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p29#a260828p29), arguing the memory chipmaker isn't affiliated with China's military and its chips aren't built for military use.
- [Cities are terminating Flock license-plate camera contracts at a record pace this August](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/08/cities-terminate-flock-contracts-at-record-pace-in-august/), and [Texas Governor Greg Abbott ordered state agencies to pause funding for Flock cameras](https://www.techmeme.com/260828/p27#a260828p27) after an investigation found one agency had steered $30 million-plus into building out the state's network.
