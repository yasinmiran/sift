**What matters today:** Yesterday's agent-safety reckoning and the Liquid Network heist both got a coda this morning. [Anthropic has walked away from its ~==$6 billion== bid to acquire Decart](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p22#a260907p22) after completing due diligence, unwinding a deal first reported a month ago. Newly released crash data confirms a driver-assist system, most likely Full Self-Driving, was engaged when [a Tesla ran a stop sign in July 2025 and killed 82-year-old Stephen Field](https://electrek.co/2026/09/07/tesla-driver-assist-stop-sign-buena-vista/); Tesla redacted the fields that would confirm exactly which system it was. And the standoff over Blockstream's Liquid Network is largely over: the attacker who drained $320 million from the sidechain [returned 3,400 of the roughly 4,000 BTC](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p18#a260907p18) once Blockstream confirmed its bridge nodes were patched, keeping about $47 million.

## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic has walked away from talks to acquire Decart for ~==$6 billion==](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p22#a260907p22) after due diligence on the real-time video and inference-efficiency startup, weeks after the talks were first reported.
- [Latent Space mapped which sources GPT-6 Astra and other frontier models actually cite](https://www.latent.space/p/aeo) when answering questions, an early look at "answer engine optimization" as AI answers increasingly stand in for search results.
- Simon Willison's `llm` CLI [shipped version 0.35](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/7/llm/), adding support for OpenAI's new gpt-6-astra model.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Broadcom pulled public downloads of VMware's Virtual Disk Development Kit](https://www.virtualizationhowto.com/2026/09/leaving-vmware-just-got-harder-after-broadcom-pulled-vddk-downloads/) without explanation, cutting off a tool migration vendors rely on to move workloads off VMware and adding one more obstacle for customers trying to leave.
- [Tottenham Hotspur's stadium dropped VMware for HPE Morpheus VM Essentials](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/09/vmware-migration-reduces-tottenham-hotspurs-licensing-fees-by-85-percent/), cutting virtualization licensing costs by ==more than 85 percent== and naming Broadcom's post-acquisition pricing as the reason it left.

## Security & Privacy

- [The attacker who drained ~4,000 BTC ($320M) from Blockstream's Liquid Network](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p18#a260907p18) returned 3,400 BTC (~$269M) after Blockstream confirmed its bridge nodes were patched, keeping roughly 598 BTC (~$47M); the sidechain remains paused.
- [Grindr agreed to pay £26 million to settle a UK lawsuit brought by 12,000 users](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p21#a260907p21) who alleged the app shared their HIV status and other sensitive data with advertisers before 2020; Grindr admits no liability.
- [A researcher factored the 512-bit RSA keys of E-Certify](https://mcpherrin.ca/2026/09/07/rsa.html), a defunct Canadian certificate authority whose weak certs shipped in Netscape 4.51 back in 1999, using consumer hardware and a weekend of compute.
- A GitHub repo claiming to publish [Stuxnet's source code](https://github.com/Sadpainy/Stuxnet) turns out to be a reconstruction: its own README says it's an educational reimplementation based on 16 years of public reverse-engineering research (Symantec, Kaspersky, ESET), not the original 2010 code.

## Startups & Industry

- Newly released crash data confirms [a Tesla ran a stop sign and killed 82-year-old Stephen Field](https://electrek.co/2026/09/07/tesla-driver-assist-stop-sign-buena-vista/) in July 2025 with a driver-assist system engaged; Tesla redacted the software-version field that would confirm whether it was Full Self-Driving or Autopilot.
- [Tesla confirmed it is discontinuing Solar Roof tiles](https://electrek.co/2026/09/01/tesla-solar-roof-exit-installers-losses/), telling certified installers it will ship only panels going forward and leaving many with six-figure sunk costs in training and tooling for a product Tesla no longer supplies.
- [Hunter Biden teased a $LAPTOP memecoin launching September 9 on Base](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p19#a260907p19), with some tokens reportedly earmarked for wallets that lost money on the earlier $TRUMP token.
- [Robinhood landed its first IPO underwriting role, in Oura's upcoming IPO](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p20#a260907p20), a foothold that could give it more say over how shares get allocated to its retail customers.

## Research

- Two papers on today's HF list: [teacher-gated on-policy distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.02998) checks a teacher model's reliability per-prompt before trusting its supervision, beating vanilla distillation across six model-and-domain combinations; and [a new benchmark for propagating a single requested edit](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03254) through an AI-generated artifact without the model rewriting everything else.
