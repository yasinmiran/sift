**What matters today:** Mistral closed Europe's largest AI funding round yet, raising €3 billion at a ==€21 billion== valuation led by Samsung as it pushes beyond model-building into its own data centers. [DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-atlas-a-predictive-map-of-every-possible-dna-letter-change-in-the-human-genome/), a 1-petabyte map predicting the molecular effect of every one of the ==9 billion== possible single-letter DNA changes in the human genome, free for researchers. The day also closed out two of yesterday's threads: [Anthropic confirmed it has walked away from its ~$6 billion bid for Decart](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p22#a260907p22), and newly released crash data confirmed [a Tesla stop-sign fatality happened with a driver-assist system engaged](https://electrek.co/2026/09/07/tesla-driver-assist-stop-sign-buena-vista/).

## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic has walked away from talks to acquire Decart for ~$6 billion](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p22#a260907p22) after due diligence on the real-time video and inference-efficiency startup, weeks after the talks were first reported.
- [Mistral raised a €3B Series D led by Samsung at a €21B valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p4#a260908p4), nearly double last year's €11.7B mark, as the French lab expands into building its own data centers to keep pace with American and Chinese rivals.
- Fields medalist Jacob Tsimerman, set to join OpenAI later this month, [launched the Mathematical AI Safety Institute](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p14#a260908p14) to apply higher mathematics to AI alignment problems.
- [Zvi's close read of Astra's system card](https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/09/08/astra-is-hard-to-monitor/) confirms Jakub Pachocki's warning from yesterday's "An Alien Mind": chain-of-thought monitoring, OpenAI's core safety bet, is measurably less effective for Astra than for Sol, and getting worse as models improve.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Broadcom pulled public downloads of VMware's Virtual Disk Development Kit](https://www.virtualizationhowto.com/2026/09/leaving-vmware-just-got-harder-after-broadcom-pulled-vddk-downloads/) without explanation, cutting off a tool migration vendors rely on to move workloads off VMware.
- [Tottenham Hotspur's stadium dropped VMware for HPE Morpheus VM Essentials](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/09/vmware-migration-reduces-tottenham-hotspurs-licensing-fees-by-85-percent/), cutting virtualization licensing costs more than 85 percent and naming Broadcom's post-acquisition pricing as the reason it left.
- [Cloudflare shipped Automatic Key Exchange](https://blog.cloudflare.com/automatic-key-exchange-for-origins/), probing each origin server to pick the right key-agreement algorithm on the first try instead of guessing; HelloRetryRequests fell from roughly 52% to 3.7% across 45 billion daily connections, cutting over 150ms off p90 handshake latency, with post-quantum encryption now rolling out to hundreds of thousands of origins by default.
- [LibreOffice 26.8 set a new download record](https://manualdousuario.net/en/libreoffice-download-record-no-ai/) after the Document Foundation published "Yes, no AI is now a feature," laying out conditions (local control, nothing leaves the device, zero telemetry) that no current AI integration meets.
- [Interconnects tracks a split in open-model licensing](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/latest-open-artifacts-24-motif-3): Western labs (Google, Meta) keep moving to plain Apache 2.0, while Chinese frontier labs are going the other way, Zhipu's GLM-5.3 now requires a security review before large-scale commercial use, following Kimi K3 and MiniMax M3's own revenue-gated terms.

## Security & Privacy

- [The attacker who drained ~4,000 BTC ($320M) from Blockstream's Liquid Network](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p18#a260907p18) returned 3,400 BTC (~$269M) once Blockstream confirmed its bridge nodes were patched, keeping roughly $47M.
- [Grindr agreed to pay £26 million to settle a UK lawsuit](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p21#a260907p21) brought by 12,000 users who alleged the app shared their HIV status with advertisers before 2020; Grindr admits no liability.
- [Researchers found Meta ran 350+ ads containing AI-generated child sexual abuse material this year](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p18#a260908p18), some with morphed images of real children, many promoting "nudification" apps traced to developers and an ad partner in China; the ads reached over 29,000 people, and Meta had removed about half before Wired's report and the rest after.
- [The UK will require Apple and Google to block children from taking, viewing or sharing nude images on their own devices](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p27#a260908p27), citing Apple's existing age-based restrictions as a model.
- [A secretive Border Patrol "Predictive Intelligence Targeting Team" is mining Americans' financial activity](https://www.404media.co/a-secretive-dhs-predictive-policing-unit-is-analyzing-americans-financial-habits-and-pulling-them-over/) and feeding it to local police, who then find a pretext, an obstructed plate, a lane change, to make the stop; one Montana driver was pulled over and charged with DUI after his financial activity flagged him. "Genuine probable cause cannot be synthetically generated," says CDT's Jake Laperruque.
- [New research shows proprietary chain-of-thought traces can be stolen across sessions and users](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/09/stealing-ai-reasoning-traces.html): the encrypted reasoning blocks providers hand back to clients turn out to be interchangeable across models on the same provider, letting an attacker feed a strong model's trace into a weaker, less-guarded one and get it decoded in plaintext, demonstrated against Anthropic, OpenAI and Google.

## Research

- [DeepMind's AlphaGenome Atlas](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-atlas-a-predictive-map-of-every-possible-dna-letter-change-in-the-human-genome/) precomputes predicted molecular effects for all 9 billion possible single-letter changes across the human genome, a 1-petabyte dataset, more than 30 times the size of the AlphaFold Database, free via a web portal, API and Antigravity skill, and ranks each mutation's likely damage with a combined AlphaGenome/AlphaMissense impact score.
- [Mathematician Tristan Buckmaster alleges OpenAI learned of his unpublished Navier-Stokes work with Anthropic's Levent Alpöge](https://www.techmeme.com/260908/p26#a260908p26) and used an internal model to solve the same problem, a claim he compares to Deep Blue's 1997 chess win over Kasparov.
- Two papers on today's HF list: [teacher-gated on-policy distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.02998) checks a teacher model's reliability per-prompt before trusting its supervision, beating vanilla distillation across six model-and-domain combinations; and [a new benchmark for propagating a single requested edit](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03254) through an AI-generated artifact without the model rewriting everything else.
