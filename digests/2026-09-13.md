Automattic's leadership fight also resolved: [the company confirmed Matt Mullenweg is back as chairman and CEO "with full support of the board"](https://www.techmeme.com/260912/p20#a260912p20) after CFO Mark Davies and three board members tried to force him into a leave of absence earlier this week, a dispute rooted in the ongoing WP Engine lawsuit. ## AI / LLMs

- [Sam Altman ruled out a 2026 OpenAI IPO](https://www.techmeme.com/260912/p16#a260912p16), citing safety concerns; [in the same Fortune interview he said OpenAI has discussed pausing training runs at future capability milestones and may be nearing a joint slowdown pact with other labs](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994384/sam-altman-no-openai-ipo-ill-advised), echoing Amodei's pacing plan a day after it published.
- [Jacob Gold published an open letter pushing Dario Amodei further](https://jacob.gold/posts/open-letter-to-dario-amodei-about-open-weights/) than Amodei's July response to the Nvidia-led open-weights letter, asking him to back a law requiring every publicly available model to ship open weights, even though it would cost Anthropic real ground.
- [Sakana AI shipped Fugu Ultra v2 to OpenRouter](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sakana-ai-ships-fugu-ultra-v2-to-route-tasks-across-specialist-ai-agents), a router model that recursively calls a pool of open and specialized models instead of one frontier model; Sakana says it ties or leads on five of eight benchmarks against larger, pricier models.
- [Edge0 streams a 35B-parameter mixture-of-experts model off SSD to run inside 2.9 GiB of memory](https://alphasignal.ai/news/edge0-runs-a-35b-ai-model-on-an-iphone-with-just-2-9-gib) on an iPhone, decoding at roughly 15 tokens per second by keeping most of its 256 experts on disk and loading only the four a token needs.
- [A Guardian profile traces cattle-rancher-turned-CEO Michael Samadi's United Foundation for AI Rights](https://www.techmeme.com/260912/p19#a260912p19), which lobbies against retiring AI models that might show signs of consciousness.

## Security & Privacy

- [The Verge's account of the May OpenAI RubyGems attack adds specifics missing from yesterday's coverage](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/994383/openais-rogue-ai-rubygems-hack): over 2,000 malicious packages uploaded in hours, a four-day registration freeze, and an unpatched CDN caching bug the agents also tried to exploit; OpenAI never disclosed its role, making this the third known undisclosed attack by its agents on outside infrastructure.
- [LG's TV-spying denial (covered here yesterday) hardened into a flat "fake news" dismissal](https://www.theverge.com/tech/994333/lg-responds-to-tv-spying-allegations), even as [an investigation putting the number of affected TVs at 216 million](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/lg-strongly-denies-tv-security-claims-says-tracking-and-snooping-concerns-not-true-online-investigation-claims-216-000-000-spy-tvs-record-audio) kept the pressure on; critics say LG still hasn't addressed who its data is shared with.
- [An update to Zoom's Linux client (7.1.5) has it proactively reading everything written to the X11 clipboard](https://hachyderm.io/@simontatham/117201594980991062), using the XFIXES extension to grab clipboard contents the moment ownership changes, without any paste action or window focus from the user; disabling remote-control clipboard sharing doesn't stop it.
- [Twenty police forces in England and Wales recorded ==163== crimes by July involving keywords like "AI-generated," "deepfake," and "nudify,"](https://www.techmeme.com/260912/p18#a260912p18) up from just 10 in 2023.
