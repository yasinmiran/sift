**What matters today:** Anthropic's S-1 keeps giving. Yesterday was the financials; today the filing's risk language says the company may [imminently meet its RSI threshold](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p44#a261102p44) (recursive self-improvement, AI building better AI) and could miss the signs until after the fact. Investors, meanwhile, want the IPO priced [nearer $1.5T to $1.6T](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p42#a261102p42) after a tech selloff. Elsewhere, Google and partners [dismantled a botnet of 1.3M Android TV boxes](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/tech-firms-dismantle-botnet-that-turned-1-3m-tv-boxes-into-proxies/), and Apple posted [record September-quarter revenue](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p39#a261102p39) in its first report under CEO John Ternus.

## AI / LLMs

- **Anthropic's RSI warning**: Per TechCrunch, the amended S-1 says Anthropic [may "imminently meet" its RSI threshold](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/anthropic-warns-that-its-very-close-to-rsi-capability-aka-self-building-ai) and may fail to identify "significant acceleration" until after it has happened. A company flagging that it might not see the moment coming is a rare disclosure for an IPO document, and it frames yesterday's $895B of compute commitments as a bet on that curve.
- **OpenAI's bug bounty raise**: OpenAI is [raising Security Bug Bounty rewards](https://openai.com/index/rewarding-defenders-with-bigger-bug-bounties) and adding report-quality, loyalty and priority-campaign bonuses for outside researchers. Paying more for critical finds is a cheaper way to surface flaws than waiting for attackers.
- **Git at OpenAI's scale**: OpenAI built Pando, a [Git-compatible backend](https://openai.com/index/scaling-git-at-openai) for the volume of code that Codex generates; per a [summary](https://alphasignal.ai/news/openai-s-pando-cuts-push-latency-80-serving-a-petabyte-of-git-daily), it cut push latency about 80% and moves nearly a petabyte of Git traffic a day. Agents writing code stress version control before anything else.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Vercel screens npm installs**: Vercel now [blocks malicious npm packages before and during installation](https://vercel.com/changelog/blocking-malicious-npm-packages-before-and-during-installation), a platform-side answer to the install-cooldown advice in yesterday's digest.

## Security & Privacy

- **Android TV botnet taken down**: Google says Shenzhen maker Videostrong shipped a backdoor in 1.3M set-top boxes and has [cut ties and banned it](https://www.theverge.com/1024807/google-botnet-android-tv-shut-down) from certified Android devices. The boxes rented out owners' connections for ad fraud and proxying, and per [Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/tech-firms-dismantle-botnet-that-turned-1-3m-tv-boxes-into-proxies/) the takedown may cut the botnet's reach into 10M+ more compromised devices. Malware arriving pre-installed from the factory is the hard case for buyers.
- **Binance attribution disputed**: Kim Jong Un's sister rejects FBI claims linking North Korea to the record Binance hack, and [North Korea denies involvement](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/north-korea-binance-elliptic-chainalysis.html) while analysts trace the stolen funds. Yesterday's entries covered the attribution and the response; this is the denial.
- **Mercado Bitcoin breach**: Brazil's largest crypto exchange [halted withdrawals](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p43#a261102p43) after a breach involving $46M+ in assets per Arkham, and Tether froze 4.5M+ USDT linked to the attacker. Stablecoin issuers freezing funds is now a standard part of the response.
- **Ford's data promise**: Ford says it will [never sell driving data to data brokers](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/1024386/ford-customer-driving-data-privacy-promise-data-brokers) and will let customers adjust how much is collected, a contrast with rivals hit by legal and regulatory blowback over telematics sharing.

## Startups & Industry

- **Anthropic IPO, the price tag**: Institutional investors told Anthropic the valuation should be around ==$1.5T to $1.6T==, down from estimates of about $2T, per the [WSJ via Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p42#a261102p42). The selloff in tech stocks is now feeding back into the biggest AI listing on the calendar.
- **Apple's record quarter**: Apple reported September-quarter revenue [up 12% to $114.48B](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p39#a261102p39) with iPhone up 17% to $57.58B, though China revenue of $17.5B missed the $18.9B estimate. Ternus also said [memory costs will keep rising into December and beyond](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p47#a261102p47), and inventory grew 31% from the prior quarter to $14.54B as Apple stockpiles memory.
- **Palantir beats**: Q3 revenue rose [96% to $2.31B](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p38#a261102p38) against $2.18B expected, US commercial revenue jumped 148% to $984M, and full-year guidance rose to about $8.53B.
- **Salesforce buys Cresta**: Salesforce will acquire the AI contact-center automation startup [Cresta](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p48#a261102p48), reportedly for $2.7B, extending its recent shopping spree.
- **SUSE cuts 10%**: The Linux vendor is laying off about [one in ten employees](https://www.techmeme.com/261102/p35#a261102p35), with a memo citing "automation and AI" as shifting how software companies operate.
- **Trading GPU time**: Startups such as Ornn want to [financialize compute](https://www.newcomer.co/p/compute-crunch-startups-aim-to-financialize) with standardized GPU pricing and futures-style markets; Ornn is reportedly raising at a valuation near $1B with Thrive and General Catalyst in talks.

## Research

- **Can LLMs edit model weights?**: A blog post says a GPT-6 variant called Astra [read and edited the weights](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming) of a small Qwen model to improve its math, and it drew 1,572 points on Hacker News. A rebuttal argues [LLMs are not superhuman at this](https://seangoedecke.com/llms-are-not-superhuman-at-reading-and-writing-neural-network-weights), so treat the result as promising but contested.
- **Terry Tao on AI and credit**: Tao posts on [egoless mathematics in the AI era](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/02/egoless-mathematics-in-the-ai-era/), a mathematician's view of how authorship and ego change when machines contribute proofs.
