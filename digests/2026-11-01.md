## Security & Privacy

- **Binance, the delta**: Yesterday Binance said it had covered 90% of the loss; it now says 95% of the ~$6.3B in stolen tokens is covered and withdrawals [restart in phases from Sunday](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p5#a261101p5). The FBI [attributes the theft to TraderTraitor](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p13#a261031p13), the North Korean group it also blamed for the ~$1.5B Bybit hack, and Elliptic says the [laundering is running on autopilot](https://www.elliptic.co/insights/the-binance-hack-is-laundering-on-autopilot), so recovery of the rest looks unlikely.
- **ChatGPT account takeover**: Parameter Labs chained a [sandboxed-iframe escape in ChatGPT's HTML previews](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/31/how-we-took-over-chatgpt-accounts/) (a virtual-keyboard message handler that inserted raw HTML, then a YouTube endpoint that reflected script past the CSP). A crafted share link could read a signed-in visitor's private data. OpenAI fixed it and paid a $5,000 bounty, which Simon Willison calls cheap for the severity.
- **Backdooring Bash**: Hacktron describes [how the GNU Savannah hosting setup could have been used to backdoor Bash](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/hacking-gnu-savannah), a reminder that a project everyone depends on can sit behind thinly guarded infrastructure.

## AI / LLMs

- **Chatbots and midterm facts**: The Washington Post found chatbots lean on [three obscure AI-built voter sites](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p12#a261031p12) for US midterm answers; Copilot cited the error-prone Ballot Brief in 75% of tests and ChatGPT in 22%. Voters asking an assistant may get answers from a single unvetted source.
- **DeepSeek Super-Agent**: DeepSeek [open-sourced Super-Agent as a research prototype](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p2#a261101p2), a proactive coding agent that can watch group chats for coding issues and suggest fixes, aimed at Claude Code's popularity.
- **Agent playtesting**: Aura open-sourced [Autopilot](https://alphasignal.ai/news/aura-opens-autopilot-an-ai-agent-that-playtests-games-from-plain-english-prompts), an in-editor agent that playtests Unreal, Unity and Godot games from plain-English prompts; Aura claims it beats Claude Code on accuracy and speed in a seven-task benchmark of its own.
- **One photo to 3D**: Sperid Labs released [Idol](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sperid-labs-idol-turns-a-single-photo-into-a-million-gaussian-3d-asset), an Apache 2.0 model that turns one image into up to 1M Gaussian splats (a point-based 3D format) in seconds on consumer GPUs.
