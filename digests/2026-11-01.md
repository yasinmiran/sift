**What matters today:** Anthropic's IPO filing is public and the commentary has started: [its S-1 in charts](https://tilomitra.com/blog/anthropic-s1-in-charts) sits next to [warnings of cracks in the AI spending boom](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/cracks-are-emerging-in-the-ai-spending-boom). The Binance hack has an author: the FBI says North Korea's [TraderTraitor group](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p13#a261031p13) did it, and Binance says it will [resume withdrawals in phases](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p5#a261101p5).

## Security & Privacy

- **Binance, the delta**: Yesterday Binance said it had covered 90% of the loss; it now says 95% of the ~$6.3B in stolen tokens is covered and withdrawals [restart in phases from Sunday](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p5#a261101p5). The FBI [attributes the theft to TraderTraitor](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p13#a261031p13), the North Korean group it also blamed for the ~$1.5B Bybit hack, and Elliptic says the [laundering is running on autopilot](https://www.elliptic.co/insights/the-binance-hack-is-laundering-on-autopilot), so recovery of the rest looks unlikely.
- **ChatGPT account takeover**: Parameter Labs chained a [sandboxed-iframe escape in ChatGPT's HTML previews](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/31/how-we-took-over-chatgpt-accounts/) (a virtual-keyboard message handler that inserted raw HTML, then a YouTube endpoint that reflected script past the CSP). A crafted share link could read a signed-in visitor's private data. OpenAI fixed it and paid a $5,000 bounty, which Simon Willison calls cheap for the severity.
- **Backdooring Bash**: Hacktron describes [how the GNU Savannah hosting setup could have been used to backdoor Bash](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/hacking-gnu-savannah), a reminder that a project everyone depends on can sit behind thinly guarded infrastructure.

- **Hacktivists causing physical damage**: Bloomberg looks at a [crude campaign by Russia-linked hacktivist groups](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p7#a261101p7) whose attacks on NATO-aligned countries over two years have caused physical damage, sometimes by operators who do not fully understand what they are hitting. Low-skill attackers reaching industrial systems is the shift to watch.

## AI / LLMs

- **Chatbots and midterm facts**: The Washington Post found chatbots lean on [three obscure AI-built voter sites](https://www.techmeme.com/261031/p12#a261031p12) for US midterm answers; Copilot cited the error-prone Ballot Brief in 75% of tests and ChatGPT in 22%. Voters asking an assistant may get answers from a single unvetted source.
- **DeepSeek Super-Agent**: DeepSeek [open-sourced Super-Agent as a research prototype](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p2#a261101p2), a proactive coding agent that can watch group chats for coding issues and suggest fixes, aimed at Claude Code's popularity.
- **Agent playtesting**: Aura open-sourced [Autopilot](https://alphasignal.ai/news/aura-opens-autopilot-an-ai-agent-that-playtests-games-from-plain-english-prompts), an in-editor agent that playtests Unreal, Unity and Godot games from plain-English prompts; Aura claims it beats Claude Code on accuracy and speed in a seven-task benchmark of its own.
- **One photo to 3D**: Sperid Labs released [Idol](https://alphasignal.ai/news/sperid-labs-idol-turns-a-single-photo-into-a-million-gaussian-3d-asset), an Apache 2.0 model that turns one image into up to 1M Gaussian splats (a point-based 3D format) in seconds on consumer GPUs.

- **Anti-reverse-engineering MCP**: BLA, a new MCP server (a plug-in that gives AI agents tools), claims to shield JavaScript bundles, Electron apps, WASM and native binaries from [LLM-powered reverse engineering agents](https://alphasignal.ai/news/bla-makes-ai-reverse-engineering-2-500x-more-expensive-for-developers), pitching a 2,500x cost increase. The figure is the vendor's own.

## Devtools & Infra

- **Datasette Agent**: [datasette-llm 0.1a9](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/31/datasette-llm/) now reads the default model from the LLM tool, so Datasette Agent runs without extra model configuration.

- **Trust in docs and software**: In his Jepsen report on ParticleDB, Kyle Kingsbury argues that [LLM use erodes trust in documentation and software](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/1/kyle-kingsbury/), because the signals of a mature project are now easy to counterfeit. His open question: how can maintainers prove their work is correct?

## Startups & Industry

- **Anthropic's IPO, read two ways**: Beyond the [S-1 charts](https://tilomitra.com/blog/anthropic-s1-in-charts), SemiAnalysis argues the [IPO is the moment datacenter developers have been waiting for](https://x.com/semianalysis_/status/2116715704829223411), while the BBC asks whether [little-known firms feed fears of an AI bubble](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cje36degrgv5o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss). The money story from yesterday continues: filing detail now meets the doubt.
- **Insuring against hyperscaler debt**: The WSJ reports the [cost of insuring against defaults at Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft, Meta and Oracle](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p8#a261101p8) is at its highest since 2021. Bond investors are now paying for protection against the AI spending bet going wrong, the market-side version of the bubble debate above.
- **Space data centers**: Google's head of data center energy says [space data centers could cost up to 100x more than Earth-based ones](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p10#a261101p10), and that US power supply is sufficient but permits are slow. The bottleneck he names is paperwork, not electricity.
- **Insurance AI deal**: Guidewire agreed to buy [Hyperexponential for $1.7B in cash](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p1#a261101p1) (AI underwriting and pricing software for insurers), plus up to $400M more per Reuters.
- **Voice AI consolidates**: Deepgram bought [Daily](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p3#a261101p3), maker of the open-source Pipecat voice-agent framework, for an undisclosed sum.
- **Funding**: [Lumaril raised $66M at a $400M valuation](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p6#a261101p6) for AI that tunes factory lines through existing control systems; Shanghai's [NeuroXess raised about $105M](https://www.techmeme.com/261101/p4#a261101p4) for brain-computer interfaces.

- **HomeView cameras**: Strings in HomeView 27.2 code suggest [security camera features with HomeKit Secure Video](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1024219/apples-homeview-could-add-security-camera-features): motion detection, streaming, recording, plus an indicator light and sound when someone watches.
- **Apple after apps**: Fast Company reads [HomeView as a preview of a Siri-first, post-app Apple](https://www.fastcompany.com/91626179/apple-homeview).
