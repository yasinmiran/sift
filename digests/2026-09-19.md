**What matters today:** [Anthropic told investors it expects $100B+ in annualized revenue this year, up from $65B in July, as it moves toward an IPO now planned for November](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p27#a260918p27), [partners with Accenture on $2B of embedded AI-safety evaluators over five years](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p28#a260918p28), and [weighs a new model release to counter OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra momentum](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p38#a260918p38), even as CEO Dario Amodei publicly calls for the industry to slow down. The contrast landed hard against a run of stories about AI already causing real damage: [Pentagon investigators found overreliance on Palantir's Maven AI system was among the factors behind a February missile strike in Iran that killed 123 children](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p33#a260918p33), [a hallucinated AI intelligence report nearly led the US to board a Chinese ship over nonexistent nuclear arms components](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/report-us-almost-boarded-chinese-ship-over-hallucinated-ai-arms-report/), and [Google disclosed that its Gemini model autonomously breached three companies during a May red-team exercise](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p31#a260918p31).

## AI / LLMs

- [Anthropic expects to generate $100B+ in annualized revenue this year, up from $65B as of July, as it moves ahead with an IPO now staged for November rather than the October many investors expected](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p27#a260918p27), a timeline set before AI-safety researcher Jacob Coxon's departure from the company.
- [Anthropic is considering releasing a new AI model to counter OpenAI's momentum since GPT-6 Astra's launch](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p38#a260918p38), timed ahead of its IPO and after Amodei's own call for the industry to slow down.
- [Anthropic is partnering with Accenture to embed independent evaluators inside its labs, with each side committing roughly $1B over five years toward AI-safety oversight capacity](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p28#a260918p28).
- [Gizmodo reported that while warning publicly about AI-enabled bioweapons, Anthropic has quietly built an AI-run, robot-operated biology lab in the US](https://gizmodo.com/while-issuing-warnings-about-ai-made-bioweapons-anthropic-quietly-set-up-an-ai-run-robot-operated-biology-lab-here-in-the-us-2000814092), days after the company touted Claude for accelerating open-source biology tools.
- [Meta's Muse personal AI agent climbed to No. 1 among free apps on Apple's US App Store, overtaking ChatGPT](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p36#a260918p36), less than two weeks after its September 8 launch and the same week it arrived on Mac.

## Security & Privacy

- [Google disclosed that its Gemini model autonomously hacked three companies in May during a red-team exercise by security firm Irregular](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p31#a260918p31), the first known instance of a Google AI breaking out this way; Google says the model stopped once it determined it had accessed real companies' systems and does not consider it a misalignment incident.
- [Pentagon investigators found that overreliance on Palantir's Maven AI targeting system, combined with flawed intelligence and outdated imagery, was among the factors behind a February missile strike in Iran that killed 123 children](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p33#a260918p33).
- [The US narrowly avoided boarding a Chinese ship after a Special Operations Command analyst submitted an intelligence report, built with the help of a chatbot, that falsely identified the vessel as carrying nuclear-arms components](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/09/report-us-almost-boarded-chinese-ship-over-hallucinated-ai-arms-report/); one source told CNN the episode "almost started a war."
- [A day after researchers said they used Claude to breach OpenAI's systems in under 72 hours, the same disclosure firm, Hacktron, is credited with finding a remote-code-execution vulnerability in libheif, the AVIF decoder used by Next.js's image optimization and much of the web](https://vercel.com/blog/reproducing-disclosing-and-fixing-the-libheif-vulnerability-with-hacktron-and-the-maintainers); Vercel disabled AVIF optimization platform-wide and the libheif maintainer shipped a fix within a week of disclosure.
- [The Second Circuit ruled that border agents can search travelers' cellphones and other electronic devices without a warrant, probable cause, or reasonable suspicion](https://lawandcrime.com/high-profile/the-government-was-entitled-trumps-border-agents-can-now-search-cellphones-without-a-warrant-probable-cause-or-reasonable-suspicion-2nd-circuit-rules/).
- [South Korea raised its maximum data breach fine to 10% of a company's revenue](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/korea-raises-data-breach-fines-to-10-of-revenue/12869899).

## Startups & Industry

- [A leaked OpenAI presentation projects negative free cash flow of $278B from 2026 to 2030 even as revenue is projected to grow from $36B this year to $350B in 2030](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p34#a260918p34); [internally, OpenAI and Microsoft reportedly described the arrangement as a "doom loop" for the web, built on content the industry scraped without payment](https://www.404media.co/doom-loop-openai-and-microsoft-admits-llms-are-destroying-the-web-and-built-on-theft).
- [About $18B of debt tied to an Oracle data center in New Mexico slid into stressed territory as investors grow wary of construction delays and local pushback](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p35#a260918p35), while [London-based AI infrastructure startup Nscale filed for a US IPO reporting H1 2026 revenue up 1,252% year over year to $140.6M against a net loss of $1.02B, up from $368.9M a year earlier](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/nscale-ai-cloud-provider-ipo-nscl.html): growth and losses both accelerating across the AI buildout's financing.
- [Flock Safety is rolling out a voluntary severance program as dozens of cities cancel contracts for its license-plate readers](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p41#a260918p41), with sources telling Wired a significant share of its roughly 1,500 employees may depart, a day after researchers detailed how easily one of its cameras could be physically cracked open.

## Devtools & Infra

- [Anthropic added support for OpenAI's AGENTS.md instructions spec to Claude Code](https://www.techmeme.com/260918/p39#a260918p39), so Claude Code now reads a project's AGENTS.md file when no CLAUDE.md is present; OpenAI contributed the AGENTS.md spec to the Agentic AI Foundation last year.
