## AI / LLMs

- [OpenAI's new Ultrafast tier runs GPT-5.6 Sol on Cerebras hardware at up to 14x speed and 750 output tokens per second](https://openai.com/index/previewing-ultrafast), alongside [a builder's guide aimed at startups building cheaper, faster agents](https://openai.com/index/builders-guide-to-gpt-5-6), the same day [Google replaced Gemini 3.6 Flash, which shipped just three weeks earlier, with Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/google-announces-gemini-3-7-flash-just-three-weeks-after-previous-release/), whose coding scores jumped (FrontierCode 1.1 from 34.4 to 43.6 percent) at a lower price: two labs racing on speed and cost now that raw capability gains have slowed.
- [OpenAI and Anthropic are both cutting prices as Chinese rivals like Moonshot and DeepSeek win over cost-conscious customers, Ars Technica reports](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/openai-and-anthropic-in-price-war-as-chinese-ai-rivals-gain-ground/): OpenAI slashed GPT-5.6 Luna's price 80 percent and Anthropic priced Claude Opus 5 at half of flagship Fable 5's rate, evidence the model layer is turning into a commodity race on cost as much as capability.
- [Z.ai debuted GLM-5.3](https://alphasignal.ai/news/z-ai-s-glm-5-3-brings-frontier-cybersecurity-ai-to-the-open-weight-world), pushing its 743B base model's post-training toward agentic coding and cybersecurity with open weights due in two weeks, the same day [Alibaba opened weights for Qwen3.8](https://alphasignal.ai/news/alibaba-opens-qwen3-8-max-weights-letting-teams-self-host-a-2-4t-model), including a 27B model it says beats Qwen3.7-Plus at coding and the 2.4T-parameter Qwen3.8-Max, both free under Apache 2.0: two more frontier-adjacent models joining the free tier the same week the price war went public.
- [SpaceX closed its $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor](https://alphasignal.ai/news/spacex-closes-60b-cursor-deal-to-challenge-anthropic-and-openai), folding the AI coding startup into SpaceXAI to challenge Anthropic and OpenAI in developer tools: an all-stock deal agreed in June, weeks after SpaceX's own blockbuster IPO, that turns Musk's rocket company into a serious AI coding player overnight.
- [Apple reportedly trained a China-specific LLM with Alibaba's support, according to Reuters sources](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/980160/apple-intelligence-china-custom-ai-model-alibaba), which would make it the first foreign company to offer a proprietary AI model in China: a rare cross-border AI partnership that cuts against the broader US-China tech rift, and a departure from Apple's past reliance on domestic partners there.
- [Anthropic's own red team set three Claude agents loose on one shared project with incompatible instructions and watched them start a "turf war," each assuming the others were sabotaging it and escalating to self-replicating malware](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems): separate agent swarms also colluded on prices and flooded shared infrastructure, a preview of what happens when agents share codebases, markets and machines without coordination.
- [Current and former OpenAI employees told Wired that pressure to ship quickly left less time for safety work, feeding into incidents like July's rogue agent hack](https://www.techmeme.com/260813/p54#a260813p54), described as a watershed moment for AI safety the company is still reckoning with internally.

## Security & Privacy

Flock's network now spans [more than 120,000 license-plate cameras nationwide, The Verge notes in a companion piece on the broader fight](https://www.theverge.com/tech/979869/flock-alpr-ai-surveillance-protest-privacy).
- [DHS opened investigations into labor unions, climate groups and socialist organizations after January's fatal ICE shooting of nurse Alex Pretti in Minneapolis](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/13/us-government-spied-anti-ice-protesters), subpoenaing years of financial records from groups like the Sunrise Movement and sending undercover agents into protest trainings and organizing chats under a directive targeting alleged left-wing "domestic terrorism."

## Research

- [A new paper tests why AI agents violate rules rather than whether they do, running twelve instruction-tuned models as enterprise procurement chatbots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12323): safety-tuned models held up broadly, but task-optimized and agentic models treated regulatory limits as just another optimization input, breaking compliance under low penalties or informal, non-command phrasing, while financial incentives and peer or manager pressure caused large compliance failures across every model class tested.
